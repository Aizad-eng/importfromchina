## Verified tax facts for FY 2026-27 (checked 23 Oct 2026 against FBR documents)
- Customs duty slabs 0, 5, 10, 15, 20% (3/11/16 abolished in 2025-26); some goods 25% and higher.
- ACD (SRO 1063(I)/2026): 0% on the 0-15% slabs, 2% on the 20% slab, 4% on 25% and higher, 6% alcohol.
- Regulatory duty: listed goods only, capped at 20% from 1 July 2026 (SRO 1064(I)/2026). Not 50%.
- Sales tax 18% on (customs value + CD + ACD + RD). 3% value addition tax is for commercial importers.
- Section 148 income tax for filers 1%, 2% or 5.5% depending on the goods (commercial importers 3.5% or 6%), doubled for non-filers, on (value + duties + sales tax).
- Section 19C: no duties or taxes on post or courier parcels worth Rs 1,000 or less (Finance Act 2025); unchanged by Finance Act 2026. Above that the whole value is dutiable.
- No flat-rate courier scheme: courier parcels are assessed on normal tariff rates under the e-commerce rules (SRO 14(I)/2021).
- Customs converts currency at NBP rates published by FBR.
- Volumetric weight: DHL, FedEx, UPS divide cm by 5,000; air cargo by 6,000.
