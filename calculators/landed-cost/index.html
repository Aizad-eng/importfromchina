Estimate only. Customs can assess a different value under a valuation ruling, and the rate depends on your item's exact HS code. Sales tax is 18%. Additional customs duty is set automatically from the slab under SRO 1063(I)/2026: 0% up to the 15% slab, 2% on the 20% slab, 4% on 25% and above.

How are duties and taxes worked out on a parcel from China?

Customs works out the tax in a fixed order, each step on top of the last. The customs value is the item price plus shipping and insurance to Pakistan (the CIF value), converted to rupees at the exchange rate customs uses that day.

  1. Customs duty = customs value × the duty rate for the item's HS code. Since the 2025-26 budget the slabs are 0, 5, 10, 15 and 20%, with some goods higher.
  2. Additional customs duty = customs value × 0%, 2% or 4%, depending on the duty slab (SRO 1063(I)/2026, in force from 1 July 2026).
  3. Regulatory duty = customs value × the listed rate, only for goods on the regulatory duty list. It is capped at 20% from 1 July 2026.
  4. Sales tax = (customs value + all customs duties) × 18%.
  5. Income tax under section 148 = (customs value + duties + sales tax) × 1%, 2% or 5.5% for filers on the Active Taxpayer List, and double that for non-filers.

Parcels by post or courier worth Rs 1,000 or less pay nothing under section 19C. Above Rs 1,000 the whole value is taxed, not only the part above the limit.

What can this calculator not tell you?

  • Your exact HS code. The duty slab depends on it. See how to find the HS code for your product.
  • Valuation rulings. For many goods customs uses a minimum value instead of your invoice. See how valuation rulings work.
  • Retail-price goods. Some goods in the Sales Tax Act's Third Schedule are taxed on the printed retail price instead.
  • Phones. Phones also need PTA registration, which is a separate charge. See importing a phone and PTA.
  • Commercial imports. Commercial importers pay higher income tax rates and a 3% value addition tax. This calculator is for one item for your own use.

Frequently asked questions

How much customs duty will I pay on a parcel from China?

It depends on the item's HS code and value. Parcels by post or courier worth Rs 1,000 or less pay nothing. Above that, duties and taxes on a typical consumer item in the 20% slab come to roughly half the customs value for a filer, and less for goods in lower slabs.

Is shipping included in the customs value?

Yes. Customs value is the CIF value: the item price plus shipping and insurance to Pakistan, converted to rupees.

Why does being a filer matter?

Income tax at import under section 148 is doubled for people who are not on FBR's Active Taxpayer List. The other taxes are the same.

Is the Rs 1,000 limit applied to the part above Rs 1,000?

No. If the parcel is worth more than Rs 1,000, duties and taxes apply to its whole value.

Is this calculator's result what customs will charge?

It is an estimate. Customs decides the HS code and can use a valuation ruling instead of your invoice price, so the actual amount can differ.
