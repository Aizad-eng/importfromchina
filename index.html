Who It's For

Right for you, or not?

We are a personal import service, not a trading company. Here's the honest line.

✓This is for you if…

  • You found one product online that isn't sold in Pakistan, or costs far more here.
  • You need a specific part, tool, accessory or gadget and local shops don't have it.
  • You want a gift, hobby item or collectible from a Chinese seller.
  • You tried ordering yourself and your card got declined, or the seller won't ship to Pakistan.
  • You want someone to check the item before it ships, not after it arrives broken.

One item, one price, one parcel. A few different single items are fine too.

✕We are NOT for you if…

  • You want to buy 50, 200 or 1,000 pieces of anything.
  • You are stocking a shop, a Daraz store or an Instagram page.
  • You need wholesale, factory, OEM or private-label orders.
  • You are importing cartons, pallets or containers.

We don't do bulk, full stop. Please don't ask for "just 30 units" either.

FAQ

Questions people ask before their first order

Do you do bulk or wholesale orders?

No. We don't handle bulk, wholesale, OEM or resale stock of any size. We are a single-item service for people who want one specific product from China. If you need quantity, a trading company or freight forwarder is the right fit.

Can I really order just one item?

Yes, that's the whole point. One item is the normal order. If you want two or three different single items, that's fine too; they ship together.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the item's price, weight and customs rate. You get one landed price in rupees within 24 hours before you commit to anything. If it's not worth it, just say no. No charge for a quote.

How long does it take?

7 to 15 days by air from the day the seller hands it over, including Pakistan customs. If the seller needs a few days to dispatch, we tell you before you pay.

How do I pay?

Bank transfer, Raast, JazzCash or Easypaisa, in rupees, after you approve the price. You never pay the Chinese seller or any Chinese platform.

What can't you bring?

Anything illegal or restricted in Pakistan, counterfeit branded goods, weapons, most liquids and aerosols, and some lithium-battery items by air. If you're not sure, send the link and we'll tell you straight.

What if the item arrives faulty?

We test it in China before it ships and you approve the photos, so this is rare. If something is still wrong on arrival, message us the same day with photos and we'll sort it out with you.
