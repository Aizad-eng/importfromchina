About importfromchina.shop

Last updated October 12, 2026
What we do

We get you one item from China that you can't find in Pakistan, and deliver it to your door. You send the product link, we send you one price in rupees, and you pay once before it ships. One item per order. No bulk.

Why this service exists

A lot of things sold in China are never sold in Pakistan: specialist tools, spare parts, niche gadgets, equipment for clinics and workshops. The places that have them, like Alibaba, 1688 and Taobao, are built for bulk buyers or for shoppers inside China. Many sellers won't ship a single piece abroad, 1688 and Taobao take Chinese payment methods only, and then the parcel still has to clear Pakistani customs.

We do all of that for you. You only deal with us, in rupees.

How an order works

  1. Send the link. Paste the product link in our quote form. Alibaba, 1688, Taobao, AliExpress or any Chinese site.
  2. Get one price within 24 hours. One number in rupees. It covers the item, shipping, Pakistan customs and our fee, and we tell you our fee upfront.
  3. Pay once, before it ships. By bank transfer, Raast, JazzCash or Easypaisa. We also give you an expected delivery date in writing.
  4. We buy, ship and clear customs. We buy from a rated seller, ship by air and handle customs clearance.
  5. It arrives at your door. Usually 7 to 15 days after the seller dispatches, anywhere in Pakistan. Nothing to pay on delivery.

A recent order

An audiometric headset (TDH39 / DD45 type), the kind clinics use for hearing tests. No shop in Pakistan stocked it. We found it on Alibaba from a verified supplier with 6 years on the platform, bought it as a single piece and delivered it to the customer in Pakistan.

What we promise

  • One price, no extras. The price we quote is what you pay. If customs charges more than we expected, we pay the difference.
  • Your money back if it goes wrong. If your item hasn't arrived within 10 days after the agreed delivery date, or it arrives wrong, damaged or not working, you get a full refund. See the refund policy.
  • A straight answer. If an item isn't worth importing, or can't be brought in, we tell you before you pay anything.
