China Se Online Shopping Kaise Karein? (Roman Urdu Guide)

By Muhammad Shamshad AslamUpdated October 23, 20268 min read
Quick answer

Haan, Pakistan se China ki online shopping ho sakti hai. Temu aur Shein seedha Pakistan deliver karte hain, AliExpress zyada tar cheezen bhejta hai, jabke Taobao aur 1688 sirf China ke andar bhejte hain, is liye unke liye service chahiye. Shein par JazzCash aur Easypaisa hain, AliExpress par international card chahiye. Rs 1,000 se mehngi cheez par customs duty lag sakti hai.

Kya Pakistan se China ki online shopping ho sakti hai?

Haan, bilkul. October 2026 mein jab humne dekha to Temu, Shein aur AliExpress teeno Pakistan ke address par deliver kar rahe the. Taobao aur 1688 China ke andar ke shoppers ke liye hain, wahan ke seller sirf Chinese address par bhejte hain. Un se cheez mangwani ho to kisi aisi service ki zaroorat hoti hai jo China mein khareed kar Pakistan bheje.

Asal sawal sirf delivery ka nahi. Yeh bhi dekhna hai ke payment kaise hogi, kitna waqt lagega, customs kitna lagega aur cheez ghalat nikli to kya hoga. Neeche har sawal ka chhota jawab hai.

Temu se order kaise karein?

Temu sab se aasan hai, kyun ke iski apni Pakistan site hai aur qeemat rupay mein dikhti hai.

  • Site: temu.com/pk-en ya app, region Pakistan par set ho.
  • Shipping: Temu ke Pakistan shipping page par likha tha ke standard shipping har order par free hai aur waqt 5 se 13 business days hai. Order process hone mein pehle 1 se 3 din lagte hain.
  • Return: Temu ki Pakistan policy ke mutabiq purchase ke 90 din ke andar return ho sakta hai, har order ka pehla return free hai aur courier ghar se pick karta hai. Return request ke baad 14 din mein parcel wapas dena hota hai.
  • Customs: Temu ki Pakistan terms kehti hain ke jahan lagu ho, aap khud importer hain aur duty ka bojh aap par hai.

Facebook ya Instagram par "Temu Pakistan" naam ke pages official nahi hote. Payment sirf Temu ki app ya website ke andar karein. Mazeed detail hamari English guide Does Temu Deliver to Pakistan mein hai.

Shein se kapre kaise mangwayen?

Shein ki alag Pakistan site nahi hai, magar iski global site par Pakistan shipping country ki list mein hai aur rupay mein qeemat dikh sakti hai. Kapron aur joton ke liye yeh achha option hai.

  • Payment: Shein ke How to Pay page par JazzCash, Easypaisa, credit card aur online bank transfer ke alag hissay hain. Wallet chunein, phir apni JazzCash ya Easypaisa app mein payment approve karein.
  • Size: Chinese size aksar chhote hote hain. Listing ka size chart cm mein parhein aur apne naap se milayein.
  • Shipping aur return: Pakistan select kar ke Shein ka shipping aur return page parh lein, kyun ke qawaid har mulk ke liye alag hote hain.

AliExpress se order kaise karein?

AliExpress par sab se zyada variety hai, khaas model, spare parts aur brands ke official stores bhi. Region Pakistan rakhein aur har listing par delivery ki tareekh apne address ke liye dekhein.

  • Waqt: WorldFirst ki July 2026 ki guide ke mutabiq zyada tar orders 15 se 45 din mein Pakistan pohanchte hain, shipping method ke hisaab se.
  • Payment: Isi guide ke mutabiq Visa ya Mastercard sab se bharosemand tareeqa hai, JazzCash aur Easypaisa checkout par seedha support nahi hote, aur cash on delivery bohat kam milta hai.
  • Card setting: Bohat se Pakistani cards par international online payment band hoti hai. Order se pehle bank app se on kar lein. PayPak card bahar ki sites par nahi chalta.

Poora checkout ka tareeqa How to Buy From AliExpress in Pakistan mein hai.

Taobao aur 1688 se kaise mangwayen?

Seedha nahi, kisi service ke zariye. Taobao China ka consumer marketplace hai aur 1688 China ki andar ki market ki website hai jahan factories aur bade seller bechte hain. Dono par qeemat Chinese yuan mein hoti hai, payment Alipay jaise Chinese tareeqon se hoti hai, aur seller sirf China ke andar bhejte hain.

Is liye inka tareeqa yeh hai: aap listing ka link kisi single item service ya agent ko dein, woh China mein khareed kar parcel apne warehouse mein le, phir air se Pakistan bheje aur customs clear kare. 1688 par aksar wohi cheez sab se kam qeemat par milti hai, magar shipping aur service ki fees jod kar hi asal qeemat samajh aati hai.

Customs duty kab lagti hai? Rs 1,000 wala rule kya hai?

Customs Act ki section 19C ke mutabiq, jo Finance Act 2025 mein badli gayi, post ya courier se aane wali cheez ki qeemat Rs 1,000 se zyada na ho to duty aur tax nahi liya jata. Pehle yeh had Rs 5,000 thi. Is se upar duty aur tax lag sakte hain, aur rate cheez ke HS code par depend karta hai.

Duty mein customs duty, kuch cheezon par additional aur regulatory duty, 18 percent sales tax aur advance income tax shamil ho sakte hain. Poora hisaab misaal ke saath hamari Urdu guide چین سے آنے والے پارسل پر کسٹم ڈیوٹی mein hai.

Kaun si cheezen mangwana mana ya mushkil hai?

  • Dawaiyan aur supplements: DRAP ki manzoori chahiye.
  • Drone: pehle se ijazat chahiye.
  • Naqli branded saman: customs zabt kar leta hai.
  • Alag lithium batteries, power bank, perfume aur spray: hawai jahaz mein pabandi hai.
  • Mobile phone: PTA ke DIRBS par registration aur model ke hisaab se tax zaroori hai, warna network par block ho jata hai.

Order karne se pehle kin baaton ka khayal rakhein?

  • Kul qeemat milayein. Item, shipping, bank ka exchange rate aur mumkin customs jod kar Daraz ya local dukaan se muqabla karein.
  • Voltage dekhein. Pakistan mein bijli 220 se 240 volt hai. US model wali 110 volt cheez ke liye converter chahiye.
  • Size chart parhein. Kapron aur joton mein Chinese size chhota hota hai.
  • Protection window yaad rakhein. AliExpress ki buyer protection aur Temu ka return waqt ke saath khatam hota hai.
  • Chinese New Year se pehle order na karein. January ke aakhir se February ke beech hafton ki der aam hai.

Ek cheez ki poori qeemat pehle kaise pata karein?

Agar cheez Taobao ya 1688 par hai, Pakistan ship nahi hoti, ya aap card ke bajaye bank transfer se payment karna chahte hain, to hamare quote form mein product ka link daalein. Link zaroori hai, detail optional. 24 ghante ke andar hum WhatsApp par rupay mein ek qeemat bhejte hain jismein item, shipping, Pakistan customs aur hamari fee sab shamil hain, aur fee pehle se batai jati hai.

Aap shipping se pehle ek hi baar bank transfer, Raast, JazzCash ya Easypaisa se payment karte hain. Cheez aam taur par seller ke bhejne ke 7 se 15 din baad Pakistan mein kisi bhi address par pohanch jati hai, aur delivery par kuch nahi dena hota. Yeh tareeqa takreeban Rs 10,000 ya us se mehngi cheezon ke liye theek hai, kyun ke China se ek cheez ki shipping aam taur par Rs 5,000 se 20,000 hoti hai aur upar se hamari fee. Sasti cheez Temu, Shein ya AliExpress se seedha mangwana behtar hai. Hum ek order mein ek cheez mangwate hain, bulk nahi. Poori tafseel ke liye چین سے سامان کیسے منگوائیں dekhein.

Aam taur par nahi. WorldFirst ki guide ke mutabiq AliExpress ke cross border orders par cash on delivery bohat kam milta hai, aur Temu ki site par humein bhi nazar nahi aaya. Pehle se wallet ya card se payment karein.
