How to Import One Machine From China to Pakistan

By Muhammad Shamshad AslamUpdated November 12, 20267 min read
Quick answer

To import one machine from China to Pakistan, confirm the exact model, voltage and phase, get the seller's full specifications, check the tariff code and any used-goods rule, then ship it by air if it is compact or by sea if it is crated. For your own use you can do this on your CNIC. Duty and 18% sales tax apply on its value.

New to importing? Read our step-by-step guide on how to import from China to Pakistan.

How do you import one machine from China to Pakistan?

You treat it as a single large item with more checks than usual. A machine costs more, weighs more and is harder to return than a gadget, so the work happens before you pay. The order of steps is:

  1. Pin down the exact model, version and options on the listing.
  2. Confirm the voltage, phase, plug and power rating match your supply.
  3. Get the full specification, crate size and gross weight in writing from the seller.
  4. Ask the seller to show the machine running on a live video call.
  5. Check the tariff code and whether the machine is new or used.
  6. Choose air or sea based on the crate size, then arrange clearance and delivery to your door.

For the general import process, see how to import from China to Pakistan. This guide covers what is different about a machine.

Which machines can come as a single item?

Almost any machine can be bought as one unit, but size decides how it travels. Compact machines, such as a sewing machine, a desktop laser engraver, a coffee machine, a 3D printer or a small bench lathe, fit a carton or small crate and can come by air. Large machines, such as a full-size CNC router or a packing line, come in heavy wooden crates and usually travel by sea.

If you only need a part for a machine you already own, that is a different and much simpler job, covered in our guide on importing machine and appliance replacement parts.

What should you confirm with the seller before buying a machine?

Get these answers in writing in the platform chat, not just from the listing title:

  • Exact model and version. Many listings cover several versions under one title, with different motors, bed sizes or controllers.
  • Voltage, phase and frequency. Single-phase or three-phase, and the rated voltage on the nameplate.
  • Power rating. The motor or total wattage, so you know your wiring and breaker can carry it.
  • Plug type. Or whether it is meant to be wired in.
  • What is in the box. Tools, consumables, spare belts or blades, software, and whether a stand is included.
  • Manual and interface language. Whether the control panel and software are available in English.
  • Crate size and gross weight. The packed dimensions decide the shipping cost.
  • Warranty and spare parts. What the seller will send if a part fails, and whether they ship parts abroad.

Will a Chinese machine work on Pakistan's electricity?

Usually yes, because both countries use the same supply. World Standards lists Pakistan and China at 230V single-phase and 400V three-phase, both at 50Hz. Plugs differ: Pakistan uses types C and D, China types A and I, so you may need a plug change or adapter rated for the load.

Two checks matter more than the plug. First, if the machine is three-phase, your workshop needs a three-phase connection, and many homes do not have one. Some machines come in a single-phase version, so ask. Second, a machine built for the US market may be 110V and 60Hz, which needs a transformer and can affect motor speed. Buy the China-market or international version. Ask the seller to confirm the voltage on the nameplate itself, not only in the listing.

How do you check a machine before it ships if you cannot visit?

Ask the seller for a live video call and a list of things to show. Alibaba's own seller blog, which advises buyers on remote checks for new machinery, suggests these points:

  • Send your checklist and ask for the documents before the call.
  • Ask the seller to show the serial number next to something dated that day, so you know the video is live.
  • Ask them to run the machine under load, not just switch it on, and to show the gauges or readings up close.
  • Ask for the actual unit you will receive rather than a showroom model.
  • Ask for an engineer to join if you have technical questions.
  • Keep the chat and notes on the platform, which preserves the record if there is a dispute.

The same blog lists warning signs: many camera cuts, background noise that does not fit the claimed location, and pressure to rush. If you see those, pause the order.

How is shipping worked out for a machine?

Machines are charged by chargeable weight, the higher of actual weight and volumetric weight. Express couriers divide length times width times height in centimetres by 5,000, and air cargo divides by 6,000. A crate adds both weight and volume.

Packed sizeActual weightVolumetric weight by express (divide by 5,000)Volumetric weight by air cargo (divide by 6,000)
50 x 40 x 40 cm carton15 kg16 kg13.3 kg
80 x 60 x 60 cm crate40 kg57.6 kg48 kg
120 x 80 x 100 cm crate150 kg192 kg160 kg

The bottom row is where sea freight usually wins. Sea in a shared container commonly takes 25 to 45 days, and the crate then needs clearing at Karachi Port or Port Qasim and a truck to your city. Ask the seller to quote the crate size before you buy, because it can change the whole decision.

How is a machine taxed at Pakistan customs?

A machine is taxed like any import, by its tariff code. Most machinery falls in chapters 84 and 85 of the Pakistan Customs Tariff, and the exact code depends on what the machine does. Our guide on customs duty from China explains how to read the rates.

When we checked in November 2026, customs duty slabs were 0, 5, 10, 15 and 20%, with higher rates on some goods. Additional customs duty applied on the higher slabs, regulatory duty only on listed goods and capped at 20% from 1 July 2026, and sales tax at 18% on the duty-paid value. Customs values the machine on its price plus freight and insurance, so an expensive crate shipped by sea still pays duty on the shipping cost too.

Can you import a used machine from China?

Sometimes, but check first. The Import Policy Order 2022 has a separate section on the import of used plant and machinery, and its Appendix C lists items that cannot be imported in used or second-hand condition. Used machines are also harder to value, so customs may assess them differently from new ones. If you find a used machine on Xianyu or a second-hand listing, confirm it is allowed in used condition before you pay.

Do you need an NTN to import one machine?

Not if it is for your own use. A single machine for your home or your own workshop can clear on your CNIC through a courier, cargo company or service. If you are importing the machine as a registered business in its own name, for its books and tax records, that business imports on the commercial route with an NTN, a Pakistan Single Window account and usually a customs agent.

What mistakes do people make when importing a machine?

  • Buying a three-phase machine for a single-phase supply.
  • Ordering a 110V US-market version.
  • Not asking for the crate size, then finding shipping costs more than expected.
  • Assuming the listing title covers the exact version they wanted.
  • Skipping the live video call on an expensive machine.
  • Buying used without checking whether it may be imported used.
  • Forgetting that spare parts and consumables will also have to come from China later.

Frequently asked questions

Can I import a single machine from China?

Yes. Most Chinese sellers will sell one unit. Compact machines can come by air, and large crated machines usually travel by sea.

Do Chinese machines work in Pakistan?

Usually, since both countries use 230V single-phase and 400V three-phase at 50Hz. Check the phase matches your supply and avoid 110V US-market versions.

How do I check a machine without visiting China?

Ask the seller for a live video call, with the serial number shown next to something dated that day and the machine running under load.

What duty is charged on machinery from China?

It depends on the tariff code, mostly in chapters 84 and 85. Customs duty, any regulatory duty and 18% sales tax apply on the value including freight.

Can I import a used machine into Pakistan?

Sometimes. The Import Policy Order has rules on used plant and machinery and a list of goods that cannot come in used, so check before buying.

Is air or sea better for one machine?

Air suits compact machines in a carton. For a heavy crate, sea is usually cheaper but commonly takes 25 to 45 days in a shared container.
