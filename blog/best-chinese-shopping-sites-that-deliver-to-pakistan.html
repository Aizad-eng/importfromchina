Best Chinese Shopping Sites That Deliver to Pakistan (2026)

By Muhammad Shamshad AslamUpdated October 23, 20269 min read
Quick answer

Four big Chinese sites delivered to Pakistan directly when we checked in October 2026: Temu, Shein, AliExpress and Alibaba.com. Temu and Shein both list JazzCash and Easypaisa and quote standard delivery of roughly one to three weeks. AliExpress ships most listings to Pakistan but is slower and works best with an international Visa or Mastercard. Alibaba.com suits single pieces only on Ready to Ship or MOQ 1 listings. Taobao, 1688 and Pinduoduo do not deliver to Pakistan, so you need an agent for them.

Which Chinese shopping sites deliver to Pakistan in 2026?

When we checked in October 2026, four of the seven sites people ask about shipped to a Pakistani address on their own: Temu, Shein, AliExpress and Alibaba.com. The other three, Taobao, 1688 and Pinduoduo, are built for shoppers inside China. They take Chinese payment methods and their sellers deliver to Chinese addresses, so from Pakistan you reach them only through someone with an address and account in China.

Shipping to Pakistan is only half the question. The other half is whether you can actually pay, how long it takes, and what happens if the item is wrong. That is where the four sites that ship differ a lot, and where most failed orders come from.

Is Temu the easiest Chinese app to use from Pakistan?

For most people buying something small for themselves, yes. Temu runs a Pakistan version at temu.com/pk-en, prices are in rupees, and its Pakistan shipping page says free standard shipping applies to all orders, with a window of 5 to 13 business days. Order processing takes 1 to 3 days before that, and the final leg is handed to local carriers, so tracking can go quiet for a few days.

Payment is the part Pakistani buyers notice first. The Pakistan site footer shows JazzCash and Easypaisa next to Visa, Mastercard, American Express, UnionPay, Apple Pay and Google Pay. Cash on delivery was not among them.

Returns are generous on paper. Temu's Pakistan policy allows returns within 90 days of purchase, the first return from each order is free, and you must send the package back within 14 days of requesting the return. The policy says the courier picks up from your door. Worn or washed clothing, food and some personal care items cannot be returned.

There is one thing to understand before you order something expensive. Temu's Pakistan terms say that, where applicable, you act as the importer, and any duties collected at checkout are estimates. If customs assesses more, the difference is yours. Our guide on whether Temu delivers to Pakistan covers this in detail.

Does Shein deliver to Pakistan, and how do you pay?

Yes. Shein has no separate Pakistan site, but its global site lists Pakistan as a shipping country. When we checked its shipping page with Pakistan selected, standard shipping showed an estimated window of 7 to 11 business days. The page charged a small fee on orders below about US$14, roughly Rs 4,000 at the rate Shein listed, and showed free standard shipping above that. Shein also notes that processing can take 2 to 4 extra days at times.

Shein's How to Pay page has sections for JazzCash and Easypaisa, with steps to approve the payment request inside your wallet app, alongside credit and debit cards. That makes it one of only two big Chinese sites, with Temu, where a Pakistani wallet works directly.

Returns are tighter than Temu's. The return policy we were served allows returns within 30 days of receipt for most items in new condition, but the only return method listed was a self-return, where you pay the return postage and Shein does not reimburse it. Swimwear, underwear, jewellery and beauty items are among the non-returnable categories. For faulty or damaged items, Shein asks you to contact customer service within 180 days of the order date.

Is AliExpress still worth using from Pakistan?

For a specific part, model or branded accessory, often yes. AliExpress has the widest catalogue of the four sites that ship, including official brand stores that Temu and Shein do not carry. When we opened it with Pakistan as the region in October 2026, prices showed in rupees and the homepage advertised free shipping on Choice items.

The trade-offs are payment and time. A July 2026 guide from the payments firm WorldFirst calls a Visa or Mastercard the most reliable option for Pakistani buyers and says JazzCash and Easypaisa are not directly supported at checkout. Many Pakistani cards ship with international online payments switched off, so turn that on in your banking app first. PayPak cards do not work on foreign sites.

Delivery depends on the shipping method you pick at checkout. The same guide puts most orders to Pakistan at 15 to 45 days. AliExpress's buyer protection page says you can claim a refund if an item does not match its description or arrives damaged, reported within 15 days of receiving it, and that non-arrival is covered within the guaranteed delivery window shown on your order page. Can you buy from Alibaba.com as a normal shopper?

Sometimes. Alibaba.com is a wholesale marketplace for businesses. Most listings show a minimum order quantity and a price that falls as quantity rises. You can buy one piece only where the listing allows it, usually in the Ready to Ship section or on listings with a minimum order of 1.

Payment from Pakistan is easier than on AliExpress for many people. Trade Assurance orders can be paid by credit or debit card, digital wallets or direct bank transfer, and Alibaba holds the payment in escrow until you confirm the order arrived. The catch is shipping. A supplier sending one piece to Pakistan usually uses an express courier, which is quick but often costs as much as a cheap item itself.

Why can't you order from Taobao, 1688 or Pinduoduo directly?

All three are built for the Chinese domestic market. Their sellers price in yuan, take Chinese payment methods such as Alipay, and deliver to Chinese addresses.

  • Taobao launched an English interface in Singapore in September 2024 and later in Malaysia, with international shipping for those markets. We found no official list that includes Pakistan.
  • 1688 is Alibaba Group's Chinese wholesale site. It is where many Taobao, Daraz and AliExpress sellers buy stock, and it is often the cheapest place for the same item.
  • Pinduoduo is run by PDD Holdings, the same group that runs Temu, but it is a separate app for buyers in China. Our guide to Pinduoduo in Pakistan explains how people get items from it.

From Pakistan, the route for these three is an agent or single-item service that buys in China, receives the parcel at a Chinese address and sends it on by air.

Which site is best for what you want to buy?

  • Cheap household, kitchen and lifestyle items: Temu, because shipping is free, wallets work and returns are 90 days.
  • Clothes and shoes: Shein or Temu. Check the size chart on the listing, because Chinese sizes often run small.
  • A specific model, part or branded accessory: AliExpress, ideally from the brand's official store.
  • One piece of equipment from a manufacturer: Alibaba.com, on a Ready to Ship or MOQ 1 listing, if you accept the courier cost.
  • Something you saw on a Chinese video or app: Taobao, 1688 or Pinduoduo, through an agent.

Do you pay customs on parcels from these sites?

It depends on the value. Under section 19C of the Customs Act, as set by the Finance Act 2025, goods arriving by post or courier worth Rs 1,000 or less are duty-free. The budget bill had proposed Rs 500, but the final Act set Rs 1,000. Above that, duty and taxes can apply to the parcel.

How each site handles it differs. Temu's terms make you the importer and say any duty collected at checkout is an estimate. On AliExpress and Shein, any duty assessed is usually collected by the courier or Pakistan Post before release. For how the amount is worked out, see our guide to customs duty from China to Pakistan.

Common mistakes when choosing a Chinese shopping site

  • Shopping on the wrong country version. On Temu, check prices show in Rs before building a cart. A VPN or an old account can put you on another country's site.
  • Trusting a "Temu Pakistan" or "Shein Pakistan" page on social media. These are resellers, not the platforms. Pay only inside the official app or website.
  • Assuming a free returns banner applies to Pakistan. Return rules are set per country. Read the policy page with Pakistan selected.
  • Forgetting the card switch. Turn on international online payments before checkout, not after a decline.
  • Ordering items that cannot fly. Perfumes, aerosols and loose lithium batteries are commonly restricted by air.
