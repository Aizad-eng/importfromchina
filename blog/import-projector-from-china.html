Importing a Projector From China to Pakistan

By Muhammad Shamshad AslamUpdated November 12, 20269 min read
Quick answer

Yes, you can import a single projector from China to Pakistan, including models from Chinese brands such as XGIMI and Dangbei. Compare native resolution and ISO lumens rather than headline claims, buy the global version with licensed streaming apps, and check the 100 to 240V rating. A boxed projector is bulky, so expect the higher end of Rs 5,000 to 20,000 shipping.

Can you import a projector from China to Pakistan?

Yes. A home or portable projector is an ordinary electronic item and one unit can be imported for your home, office or classroom. Several of the best known home projector brands are Chinese, and their full ranges are listed on Chinese platforms long before any local stock appears. What should you look for in a projector?

Look at native resolution, measured brightness, light source, throw ratio and software, in that order. Two well-documented models show how these numbers read. When we checked ProjectorCentral's spec pages in November 2026:

The gap between these two is large, and so is the gap in price. The point is to compare like with like: the same brightness standard, the same kind of resolution figure, and the same screen size at your viewing distance.

What is the difference between native 4K and 4K supported?

Native resolution is what the projector actually draws, and "4K supported" usually means it accepts a 4K signal and scales it down. Many cheap listings say 4K in the title while the panel is 1080p or lower. Some good projectors reach a 4K image through pixel shifting, where a smaller chip shifts the image quickly to show more pixels, as with the 0.47 inch DLP chip in the XGIMI above. Ask the seller in writing for the native resolution and whether pixel shifting is used.

Which screen size will your room give you?

The throw ratio tells you, and the calculation is simple: image width equals the distance from the lens to the wall divided by the throw ratio. With a throw ratio of 1.20:1, a projector 3 metres from the wall gives an image about 2.5 metres wide. With 1.17:1 at the same distance, the image is about 2.56 metres wide. Measure your room before you buy, including where the projector will sit, because a sofa or a ceiling fan can decide that for you.

Short throw and ultra short throw projectors have much lower throw ratios and sit close to the wall, which suits small rooms in flats. Their image is more sensitive to an uneven wall, so a smooth painted wall or a proper screen matters more. Keystone and autofocus features help with placement, but correcting a strongly angled image in software reduces sharpness, so a straight-on position is still best.

What do ISO lumens and ANSI lumens mean?

They are measured brightness figures under recognised test methods, and they are the only brightness numbers worth comparing. ProjectorCentral lists both models above in ISO 21118 lumens. Many cheap projectors quote "lumens" or "LED lumens" with no standard, which can look several times higher than a measured figure. A bright room in a Pakistani afternoon needs far more measured lumens than a dark room at night, so decide where you will watch before choosing. Treat any brightness number without ISO or ANSI next to it as marketing.

Will a Chinese projector play Netflix in Pakistan?

Only if the projector has a licensed Netflix app, which usually means buying the global version. ProjectorCentral says the Dangbei N2 is officially licensed for native Netflix streaming, and lists the XGIMI Horizon S Max as running Android TV. Versions made for mainland China commonly run Chinese-market software without Google services, which means no Play Store and often no licensed streaming apps. Our guide on gadgets that work differently in Pakistan explains China and global software versions in more detail. If you plan to plug in a TV stick or a laptop over HDMI, the built-in software matters less, but confirm the HDMI input and its supported formats.

A TV stick is also the simplest fix if you already own a projector with poor apps. It moves streaming to a device that gets regular updates, and the projector becomes a display. If you game on a console, ask the seller for the input lag figure in game mode, because projectors vary widely and the number is rarely shown on cheap listings. For a classroom or office, check the inputs you need, such as a second HDMI port or a USB port for presentations, before you think about streaming at all.

Will it work on Pakistani electricity?

Most home projectors accept 100 to 240V, so they work on Pakistani mains. Both models above are listed at 100 to 240V. Pakistan runs at about 230V and 50Hz with type C and D sockets, according to the worldstandards.eu table, so you may only need a plug adapter for a type I or type A plug. Where voltage swings are common, a stabiliser or UPS protects the projector's power supply.

Why does a projector cost more to ship?

Because the box is large and padded, so carriers often charge by volumetric weight rather than actual weight. Express carriers such as DHL, FedEx and UPS divide length times width times height in centimetres by 5,000, and air cargo divides by 6,000. As a worked example, a box measuring 40 by 30 by 25 cm has a volumetric weight of 6 kg by the express formula, even if the projector inside weighs 2.2 kg. Shipping one item from China usually costs Rs 5,000 to 20,000, and projectors tend to sit in the upper half of that range. Our guide to the cost to ship one item from China shows how size drives the price. This is why importing makes sense for a projector worth well above Rs 10,000, not a cheap mini projector.

How is a projector taxed at customs?

A projector pays customs duty under its PCT code plus taxes on the customs value. The value is CIF, meaning item plus freight and insurance, converted at NBP rates published by FBR. When we checked in November 2026, customs duty slabs were 0, 5, 10, 15 and 20 percent with some goods higher, additional customs duty was 0 percent on the 0 to 15 percent slabs and 2 percent on the 20 percent slab, regulatory duty applied only to listed goods and was capped at 20 percent from 1 July 2026 under SRO 1064(I)/2026, and sales tax was 18 percent on the value plus duties. Advance income tax under section 148 is also collected, doubled for non-filers. The Rs 1,000 courier exemption under section 19C does not apply to any real projector.

What are the common mistakes?

  • Trusting "4K" in a title without checking the native resolution.
  • Comparing unmeasured "lumens" against ISO or ANSI figures.
  • Buying the China version and losing licensed streaming apps.
  • Ignoring the throw ratio and finding the room is too short for the screen size you want.
  • Expecting the brand's warranty to cover a unit bought in China.
  • Forgetting sound. Built-in speakers are fine for a bedroom, but a living room usually needs a soundbar, so check for an audio output or Bluetooth.
  • Not planning for the fan. Brighter projectors run their fans harder, so check noise if the projector will sit near your seat.

Frequently asked questions

Is it worth importing a projector from China?

It can be for a mid-range or premium model that is not sold locally. Projectors are bulky to ship, so cheap mini projectors rarely make sense.

What does 4K supported mean on a projector?

Usually that it accepts a 4K signal and scales it down to a lower native resolution. Ask the seller for the native resolution in writing.

How many lumens do I need?

It depends on how dark the room is. Compare only ISO or ANSI lumens, and plan for more if you will watch in daylight.

Will a China-version projector have Netflix?

Often not. China-market versions commonly lack Google services and licensed streaming apps. Buy the global version, or use a TV stick over HDMI.

Do Chinese projectors work on 230V in Pakistan?

Most do. Models such as the XGIMI Horizon S Max and Dangbei N2 are listed for 100 to 240V, so you may only need a plug adapter.

Why is shipping a projector expensive?

The padded box is large, and carriers charge by volumetric weight when a box is big for its weight. Expect the upper half of the usual shipping range.
