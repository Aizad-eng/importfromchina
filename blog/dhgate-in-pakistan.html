Does DHgate ship to Pakistan?

We could not verify it either way. We set out to answer this from DHgate's own pages, and when we checked in November 2026 our research tools could not open dhgate.com or its mobile site. Third-party pages we found contradicted each other: some say DHgate sellers ship to Pakistan, one older guide says DHgate does not. None of them are DHgate, and several are years old.

What DHgate itself says, in its App Store description, is that it serves 10 million buyers from 230 countries and regions and offers worldwide delivery on most products. That is a general claim, not a confirmation for Pakistan or for any particular listing. On a marketplace like DHgate, each seller chooses which countries and shipping methods it offers, so the honest answer depends on the listing you want.

This guide sets out what we did verify, how you can check for your own address in a few minutes, and what to do if your item will not ship. For the wider process, read how to import from China to Pakistan.

How can you check if a DHgate listing ships to your address?

Check on the listing and at checkout, because that is where the real answer for your item appears. These steps take a few minutes and cost nothing.

  1. Set your country. Open DHgate in a browser or the app and set the ship-to country to Pakistan.
  2. Open the listing. Look at the shipping section. If Pakistan is not supported, the listing usually says it cannot ship to your country, or shows no shipping method.
  3. Read the shipping methods offered. Note the carrier names and the delivery estimate for each.
  4. Add your full address. Go as far as the payment page without paying. Some restrictions only show when a real address is entered.
  5. Check the total. Look at the shipping charge and whether any duty or tax line is added. Customs in Pakistan may still charge on arrival.
  6. Message the seller. Ask which carrier delivers in Pakistan and whether the tracking number works with a local carrier. Keep the reply.

If the listing does ship, take a screenshot of the shipping method and estimate before paying. If you later need to raise a dispute about late delivery, that record helps.

How would you pay for a DHgate order from Pakistan?

Most likely by international Visa or Mastercard, if your card works there. DHgate's App Store page lists Visa, MasterCard and American Express among its partners. JazzCash, Easypaisa and Raast are Pakistani payment methods and Chinese marketplaces do not normally accept them, and PayPal is not available in Pakistan. Before paying, switch on international online payments in your banking app and check your card limit.

Pay only inside DHgate's checkout. Escrow protection only means something if the money goes through the platform. A seller who asks you to pay by bank transfer or a wallet outside DHgate is removing your protection.

What about counterfeits on DHgate?

This is the biggest risk for a Pakistani buyer, because customs can seize branded copies and you lose the item and the money. The US Trade Representative's 2024 review of notorious markets for counterfeiting and piracy, published in January 2025, named DHgate along with Taobao and Pinduoduo among China-based e-commerce markets. Cheap listings for famous brands on DHgate are often copies, whatever the title says.

For unbranded goods, such as tools, parts, cables, cases and generic gadgets, the counterfeit question matters less. For anything with a famous logo, assume it is a copy unless it comes from an authorised brand store, and do not order it into Pakistan.

Do you pay customs on a DHgate parcel in Pakistan?

If the parcel is worth more than Rs 1,000, expect to. Under section 19C of the Customs Act, post and courier parcels worth Rs 1,000 or less pay no duties or taxes. Above that, customs can charge duty and taxes on the whole value. The amount depends on the product's tariff code, and nobody can promise you the exact figure before the parcel is assessed.

If you receive a message asking you to pay customs duty through a link, do not pay from the message. Ask the carrier for the customs assessment and pay only through the carrier's official channel.

Why do people in Pakistan look at DHgate at all?

Mainly for low prices on small quantities of generic goods and accessories, and for items they found through social media. DHgate describes itself as a cross-border marketplace, and many sellers there also appear on other Chinese platforms. The same or very similar product is often available on AliExpress or from 1688 and Taobao suppliers.

That matters because AliExpress ships most listings to Pakistan, according to our check in October 2026, and Temu has a Pakistan site. What if your DHgate item will not ship to Pakistan?

  • Search AliExpress by the model name or image. Our guide on buying from AliExpress in Pakistan explains payment and delivery.
  • Look for the same product on 1688 or Taobao. These need a buying service, but they cover more sellers.
  • Ask the DHgate seller for another shipping method. Sometimes a seller can add a carrier that serves Pakistan.
  • Use a forwarding address only if you understand the costs. Shipping to a forwarder abroad and then to Pakistan adds a second shipment and a second set of charges.

Banggood is another site people ask about. We looked at it separately in our guide to whether Banggood ships to Pakistan.

What are the common mistakes with DHgate orders?

  • Assuming a listing ships to Pakistan because the site says "worldwide".
  • Ordering branded copies, which customs can seize.
  • Paying a seller outside DHgate's checkout.
  • Choosing the cheapest shipping without checking if it has tracking in Pakistan.
  • Forgetting that customs can charge on arrival even if checkout shows no duty.

Is a cheap DHgate item worth importing on its own?

Often not, even if it does ship. A low listing price can hide a long or untracked delivery, and the parcel can still be charged duty if it is worth more than Rs 1,000. If you use a service or courier instead, shipping one item from China to Pakistan usually costs Rs 5,000 to 20,000 depending on size and type, so a small accessory can end up costing several times its price. A single import makes most sense for items worth around Rs 10,000 or more, or several different items sent together so the shipping is shared.

For a cheap item you need soon, a local seller on Daraz may be the better choice even at a higher price, because you avoid the wait, the customs question and the risk of a parcel that never arrives.
