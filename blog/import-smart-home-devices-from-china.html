Importing Smart Home Devices From China to Pakistan

By Muhammad Shamshad AslamUpdated November 12, 202610 min read
Quick answer

Yes, you can import smart home devices from China to Pakistan, but check the app region, Wi-Fi band and wiring before you pay. Buy global versions, expect most Wi-Fi devices to need 2.4 GHz, add a hub for Zigbee, and check whether a smart switch needs a neutral wire. Shipping of Rs 5,000 to 20,000 suits devices worth Rs 10,000 or more.

New to importing? Read our step-by-step guide on how to import from China to Pakistan.

Can you import smart home devices from China to Pakistan?

Yes. Smart plugs, switches, sensors, locks, robot vacuums, curtain motors and hubs are ordinary electronic goods and can be imported for your own home. Chinese platforms list whole families of devices that never reach Pakistani shops. Read our guide on how to import from China to Pakistan for the basics. This guide is about the four things that decide whether a smart device actually works here: the app and its region, the radio, the wiring, and the power supply.

Which app ecosystem should you buy into?

Pick one ecosystem first, then buy devices that work with it. Mixing brands at random is the most common way to end up with five apps and no automation. The main options are:

EcosystemHow devices connectWhat to check
Tuya / Smart LifeMostly 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi, some Zigbee with a hubMany brands use it, so the same app controls them
Xiaomi Home (Mi Home)Wi-Fi, Bluetooth and Zigbee with a gatewayApp region must match the device's region
Zigbee with a hubLow-power mesh through a hub or USB dongleHub brand and supported devices
MatterWi-Fi, Thread or EthernetA Matter controller, such as Google Home or Home Assistant

The Connectivity Standards Alliance says Matter is deployed across Apple Home, Google Home, Amazon Alexa, Samsung SmartThings and Home Assistant, with consistent setup across brands. A Matter-certified device is the safest bet if you may change apps later.

Will a China-version device work with my app in Pakistan?

Often not, because smart devices are tied to cloud servers by region. Tuya's own mapping of app accounts to data centres, when we checked in November 2026, put Pakistan (country code 92) on its Central Europe data centre, while mainland China accounts map to its China data centre in Shanghai. A device made for the Chinese market may expect the China server. EZVIZ, the camera brand, says its accounts cannot change region once registered. Xiaomi users commonly report that devices bought in mainland China only work with the app region set to mainland China, and that switching the app region hides devices added under another region.

The rule: on Taobao, 1688 or JD.com, ask for the international or global version, and check that the listing names the app you use. Our guide on gadgets that work differently in Pakistan covers region locks across other gadgets.

Why will my smart plug not connect to Wi-Fi?

Most Wi-Fi smart devices only work on 2.4 GHz, and many Pakistani routers broadcast 2.4 and 5 GHz under one name. Tuya's help centre says that unless a device's manual states otherwise, it only supports 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi, and suggests separating the mixed network. During setup, your phone may join 5 GHz while the device can only see 2.4 GHz. Splitting the two bands into separate names, or turning 5 GHz off for a few minutes while pairing, usually fixes it. Wi-Fi also struggles through brick and concrete walls, so a large house may need a mesh system or a Zigbee setup instead.

What about robot vacuums and larger devices?

Robot vacuums, smart locks and air purifiers raise the same region question, plus a few of their own. A China-version robot vacuum may speak Chinese voice prompts and only appear in the app with the region set to mainland China, so ask the seller for the global version in writing. Check the charging dock's input rating for 220 to 240V or 100 to 240V, and the plug type. A smart door lock must also fit your door: measure the door thickness and check whether the lock suits your existing mortise or needs a new one cut. These items are heavy or bulky, so they sit at the upper end of shipping costs, which makes sense mainly for models you cannot buy locally.

When do you need a Zigbee hub?

You need a hub for any Zigbee device, because Zigbee devices do not join Wi-Fi directly. The hub links them to your app and to the internet. Zigbee devices form a mesh, where mains-powered devices pass messages on, so coverage improves as you add powered devices. Sensors on coin cells and switches without a neutral wire usually do not repeat. If you build anything yourself, Espressif's ESP32-C6 is an example of a chip with Wi-Fi 6 on 2.4 GHz plus an IEEE 802.15.4 radio for Thread and Zigbee, which is why it appears in many newer Matter and Zigbee devices.

Does a smart switch need a neutral wire?

Some do, and you must check your switch box before ordering. A smart switch needs a constant trickle of power to keep its radio on. Neutral-wire versions get it from the neutral. No-neutral versions draw a small current through the light itself. Sonoff's ZBM5 Zigbee switch shows the trade-off. In a review, CNX Software reported that it can run with or without a neutral, acts as a Zigbee router with a neutral and an end device without one, needs a minimum load of 3W in no-neutral mode, and is rated for 100 to 240V. The reviewer found a 2W bulb still glowed faintly when switched off. It also needs a Zigbee hub, and it measures 120 by 74 mm, so check that the plate size suits your wall box.

Many switch boxes have only a live wire at the switch. Ask your electrician before ordering, and pick a no-neutral model if there is no neutral, with bulbs above its minimum load.

What happens during load-shedding?

Devices lose power and reconnect when it returns, and what they do next depends on a setting. Many smart switches and plugs have a power-on state option, which sets whether they come back on, stay off or return to the last state. CNX Software notes that the ZBM5 offers a power-on state setting in its apps. Set it deliberately, especially for geysers, motors and anything that should not switch on unattended. Your router and hub should be on a UPS, or automations stop working until the internet returns. Battery-powered sensors and locks keep working, but they still need the hub to report.

Do smart home devices need PTA approval?

For commercial imports, PTA type approval applies. PTA requires type approval for terminal equipment imported for commercial purposes, and lists IoT devices among the equipment that needs it. ProPakistani reported in January 2026 that PTA's revised framework for short range devices and terrestrial IoT, version 1.4, adds rules such as barring the storage of IoT data outside Pakistan without PTA approval, aimed at IoT service licensees. Anything with a SIM, such as a 4G alarm panel, also falls under PTA's DIRBS system. Bringing in devices in quantity for installation jobs is a commercial import, and we do not handle that.

How are smart devices taxed at customs?

Smart devices pay duty and taxes on the customs value under their PCT code, unless the parcel is worth Rs 1,000 or less, which section 19C exempts for post and courier. The customs value is CIF, converted at NBP rates published by FBR. When we checked in November 2026, customs duty slabs ran from 0 to 20 percent with some goods higher, additional customs duty was 0 percent on the 0 to 15 percent slabs and 2 percent on the 20 percent slab, regulatory duty applied only to listed goods and was capped at 20 percent from 1 July 2026, and sales tax was 18 percent on the value plus duties, with advance income tax under section 148 on top.

Frequently asked questions

Do Chinese smart home devices work in Pakistan?

Yes, if you buy the global version that works with your app's region. China-market versions are often tied to the mainland China server.

Why does my smart plug not find my Wi-Fi?

Most Wi-Fi smart devices only support 2.4 GHz. Split your router's 2.4 and 5 GHz networks, or pair with 5 GHz turned off.

Do I need a hub for Zigbee devices?

Yes. Zigbee devices connect through a hub or USB dongle, not directly to Wi-Fi. Mains-powered Zigbee devices also extend the mesh.

Can I fit a smart switch without a neutral wire?

Yes, with a no-neutral model. These need a minimum load, such as 3W on the Sonoff ZBM5, and very small LED bulbs may glow faintly when off.

What happens to smart devices during load-shedding?

They switch off and reconnect when power returns. Set each device's power-on state, and keep the router and hub on a UPS.

Is Matter worth paying extra for?

If you might change apps later, yes. Matter devices work across Apple Home, Google Home, Alexa, SmartThings and Home Assistant.
