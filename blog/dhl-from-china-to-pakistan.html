DHL From China to Pakistan: Delivery Time, Duties and Tracking

By Muhammad Shamshad AslamUpdated November 12, 20269 min read
Quick answer

DHL Express from China to Pakistan is a door-to-door courier that often arrives within about a week of pickup, with DHL clearing Pakistan customs itself. Duty applies to parcels worth more than Rs 1,000, and when we checked in November 2026 DHL Pakistan collected duties in cash at delivery. You track it with the 10-digit waybill number.

How long does DHL take from China to Pakistan?

DHL Express usually gets a parcel from China to a Pakistani address within about a week of pickup, and often in less, provided customs has no questions. DHL does not publish one fixed China to Pakistan time on the pages we could open in November 2026. It gives an estimated delivery date when the shipment is booked, worked out from the origin city, the destination city and the service.

To give a sense of scale, DHL's UK site says it delivers parcels to Pakistan in as little as 2 working days from the UK, and the booking site Eurosender quotes 1 to 3 days for parcels from China to Pakistan. Those figures cover the fast part, the flight and the hub sorting. The slow parts are the two ends:

  • Before pickup. The Chinese seller has to pack the item, create the waybill and hand it to DHL. Some sellers take several days to do this.
  • Pakistan customs. If the paperwork is complete and the value is clear, clearance is routine. If the invoice is missing, the description is vague or the item needs a permit, the parcel waits.
  • Holidays and remote addresses. DHL itself notes that public holidays in Pakistan may affect its estimated transit times. Smaller towns can add a day for the final delivery.

Working days matter here. A parcel picked up on a Thursday in Shenzhen does not move on a Pakistani public holiday, so count working days, not calendar days.

How does a DHL parcel move from a Chinese seller to your door?

A DHL parcel stays inside DHL's own network from pickup in China to delivery in Pakistan, which is why it is faster and better tracked than post. The usual sequence is:

  1. The seller books DHL, prints the waybill and a commercial invoice, and DHL collects the box.
  2. DHL sorts it at a Chinese hub and flies it out on its own or partner aircraft.
  3. It lands in Pakistan, where DHL acts as the clearing agent and presents the shipment to Pakistan Customs.
  4. Customs assesses any duty and taxes. DHL records them against the waybill.
  5. DHL delivers to your address and collects whatever is owed.

The important point for a buyer is step 3. You do not clear the parcel yourself. DHL does it for you, which is convenient, but it also means DHL's own processing charges are added on top of what Customs charges. What duties and taxes do you pay on a DHL parcel?

You pay the normal Pakistani import duties and taxes on the item's customs value, and DHL collects them from you on Customs' behalf. A DHL parcel is not taxed differently from any other import. Customs looks at what the item is (its HS code), its value and the cost of getting it here, then applies customs duty, any additional or regulatory duty that applies to that product, 18% sales tax and withholding income tax.

There is no flat courier rate in Pakistan. Courier parcels are assessed on the normal tariff, so a phone case and a laptop can attract very different amounts. Our guide to customs duty from China to Pakistan explains how the layers stack up for each kind of product.

Duties are paid to the Government, not to DHL. DHL's Pakistan billing help page says plainly that duties and taxes paid to Pakistan Customs are non-refundable, even if the goods are returned, rejected or not what you expected. So check the listing carefully before the seller ships.

Is the duty-free limit for DHL parcels Rs 500 or Rs 1,000?

The limit is Rs 1,000. Section 19C of the Customs Act, as amended by the Finance Act 2025, says no duties and taxes are charged where the value of goods imported through post or courier does not exceed one thousand rupees. Before that amendment the limit was Rs 5,000. The Finance Act 2026 did not change the Rs 1,000 figure.

Some DHL pages about importing into Pakistan still say the duty-free limit is Rs 500. That figure is wrong. It does not match the law that passed, which says Rs 1,000. If you see Rs 500 on a DHL page, rely on the Customs Act instead.

Two cautions apply even below the limit:

  • The value must be honest. Customs can reassess an item it thinks is undervalued, and it can treat several parcels from one sender as one import.
  • Small charges can still appear. DHL Pakistan's billing page says that even when duties and taxes are waived, other regulatory charges such as stamp fee, excise duty and Civil Aviation charges may still apply.

Above Rs 1,000 the whole value is dutiable, not just the amount over the line.

How do you pay DHL duties in Pakistan, and what fees can appear?

When we checked in November 2026, DHL Pakistan said you pay duties and taxes in cash to the DHL courier at the time of delivery. Its billing help page says online payment options are not available in Pakistan because of local regulatory restrictions, that its notification messages contain no payment link or bank details, and that a secure payment link therefore cannot be resent. This matters for scams: a text claiming to be DHL and asking you to pay by link is a warning sign.

The same page lists the kinds of charges that can appear on a DHL import bill. It does not give amounts, and they vary by shipment.

If the parcel is a gift or a sample and you want an exemption considered, DHL says it must be told in advance, and the exemption is subject to Customs approval. For duty questions, the page gives the DHL Pakistan duties and taxes hotline as +92 21 111-345-333 and the general delivery line as +92 21 111-345-111. Keep your waybill number ready when you call.

What documents and ID does DHL need from you?

For a personal parcel, DHL needs your CNIC, and the name on the waybill should match it exactly. DHL's Pakistan import guidance asks for the receiver's CNIC so Customs can verify who the parcel is for, and a misspelled or shortened name can push the parcel into manual handling. Before the seller ships, send them your name exactly as it appears on your CNIC, your full address and a phone number that will be answered.

The seller should also put a commercial invoice in the shipment showing:

  • A plain description of the item, for example "wireless earbuds" rather than "gift" or "accessories".
  • The real price paid and the currency.
  • The quantity and the country of origin.
  • Both addresses and phone numbers.

Keep your order page or payment receipt as well. If Customs questions the value, that is your proof. Commercial shipments are different: an importer bringing in goods for business needs an NTN and goes through formal clearance, which is a separate route from a personal parcel.

How do you track a DHL parcel from China?

You track a DHL Express parcel with its waybill number, which is normally 10 digits with no letters, on DHL's own tracking page or app. The seller gets this number when they book the shipment. Ask for it as soon as they say the parcel has gone.

A typical DHL timeline shows a pickup scan in China, a departure from a Chinese hub, arrival at a transit hub or directly in Pakistan, then customs events, then "with courier for delivery". The customs stage is where most waiting happens. If the tracking shows the parcel held for clearance for more than a couple of working days, call DHL with the waybill number and ask whether a document, a value question or a payment is pending.

A number in a different format, such as 13 characters ending in CN, is not DHL Express. It is a postal number and is tracked through Pakistan Post. If a seller says "DHL" but gives you a postal or 16-digit number, the parcel may be on DHL eCommerce or another network, which is slower.

How is DHL shipping from China priced?

DHL charges on the higher of the real weight and the volumetric weight, and it divides by 5,000 rather than the 6,000 used for air cargo. DHL Express describes the formula as length x width x height in centimetres divided by 5,000, which it also expresses as 1 cubic metre counting as 200 kg. A light item in a big box is therefore charged as a heavy one.

Weight is billed in steps, commonly half a kilogram at a time, so a 200 gram item still pays for the first step in full. That is why DHL is often the most expensive way to ship one small item, and why the box size the seller uses matters so much.

When is DHL worth it for one item from China?

DHL is worth it when speed and tracking matter more than cost, for example a replacement part you are waiting on, or a valuable item you want to follow at every step. It is poor value for cheap or bulky items, where the shipping and DHL's processing charges can be more than the item. Frequently asked questions

Does DHL deliver from China to Pakistan?

Yes. DHL Express carries parcels from China to addresses across Pakistan door to door, and it clears Pakistan customs as part of the service.

Do I pay customs on a DHL parcel from China?

Yes, if the parcel is worth more than Rs 1,000. Below that, section 19C waives duties and taxes, although DHL says small regulatory charges such as stamp fee can still apply.

Can I pay DHL duties online in Pakistan?

Not when we checked in November 2026. DHL Pakistan said online payment was unavailable because of local regulatory restrictions and that duties are paid in cash to the courier at delivery.

Why does a DHL page say the duty-free limit is Rs 500?

That page is out of date or wrong. The Customs Act, as amended by the Finance Act 2025, sets the limit at Rs 1,000 for post and courier parcels.

How many digits is a DHL tracking number?

A DHL Express waybill number is normally 10 digits with no letters. A 13-character number ending in CN is a China Post number, not DHL Express.

Are DHL duties refunded if I return the item?

No. DHL Pakistan says duties and taxes paid to Pakistan Customs are non-refundable even if the goods are returned, rejected or not as expected.
