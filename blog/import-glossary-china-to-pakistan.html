China to Pakistan Import Glossary: 30 Terms Explained in Plain English

By importfromchina.shop teamUpdated October 3, 20269 min read
Quick answer

Buying from China to Pakistan comes with vocabulary from three worlds: the Chinese platforms (Tmall, 1688, Trade Assurance, Taobao code), shipping (CIF, DDP, LCL, volumetric weight) and Pakistan Customs (HS/PCT code, WeBOC, PSW, valuation ruling, CPFTA). This glossary defines thirty of them in one or two plain sentences each, with a table for quick lookup and short notes on the ones that trip up single-item buyers most.

Why learn the vocabulary?

Most confusion when buying from China is about words, not products. A seller writes "MOQ 2, FOB Shenzhen", a forwarder asks for the "chargeable weight", and a friend says you should have used "Trade Assurance". This glossary covers the terms a Pakistani buyer meets on the Chinese platforms, in shipping and at Pakistan Customs. Use the table for a quick lookup, then read the notes on the ones that matter most for one item.

Platform terms worth a closer look

Tmall vs Taobao. Tmall listings sit inside the Taobao app with a 天猫 badge. They are official brand or authorised stores with stricter rules, so prices are higher and the risk of a fake is lower. For a branded item, a Tmall listing is the one to send.

Trade Assurance only applies to orders placed and paid inside Alibaba.com. It covers late shipment and goods that differ from the written order. It does not cover Pakistan duty or return shipping. Details in Alibaba Trade Assurance explained.

Shipping terms worth a closer look

Volumetric weight is where light but bulky items get expensive. A box of 40 x 30 x 25 cm is 30,000 cubic cm, and divided by 6000 that gives 5 kg volumetric. If the item weighs 1 kg, you pay for 5 kg.

LCL and FCL are for bulk. Sea freight has fixed port and documentation fees and a minimum of about one cubic metre, so a single item by sea costs more than by air in almost every case.

DDP is the term to look for. It is the only one where the price you are quoted is the price you pay. The others are compared in Incoterms for single-item buyers.

Customs terms worth a closer look

HS and PCT codes decide the duty rate. The first six digits are international; Pakistan adds digits to make the PCT code in its tariff.

Customs value and valuation rulings. Duty is charged on the CIF value, not the listing price, and for many common goods a valuation ruling sets a minimum value per unit or per kg. A cheap invoice does not help if a ruling applies.

WeBOC and PSW are for commercial importers who file their own declarations under an NTN and STRN. An individual receiving one parcel does not register; the courier or clearing agent files on their behalf against a CNIC.

CPFTA reduces customs duty on many Chinese-origin goods but only with a valid Certificate of Origin, which most single-item shipments do not carry. Sales tax and income tax apply regardless. The calculation is in customs duty from China to Pakistan.

Which terms matter most for one item?

  • MOQ, because it decides whether you can buy one piece at all.
  • Volumetric weight, because it decides what the air freight costs.
  • PCT code and customs value, because together they decide the tax.
  • DDP and landed price, because they are the difference between knowing the total and finding out at the door.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between an HS code and a PCT code?

The HS code is the international six-digit classification. Pakistan extends it to an eight-digit PCT code in its customs tariff, and the PCT code sets the duty rate.

What does MOQ 1 mean?

The seller will sell a single piece. On 1688 it appears as 1件起批. A higher MOQ means you must buy at least that many.

What is volumetric weight and why am I charged for it?

Length times width times height in centimetres divided by 6000 gives the volumetric weight for air. Carriers bill the higher of actual and volumetric weight because space costs as much as mass.

Do I need WeBOC registration to receive one parcel from China?

No. WeBOC and PSW registration are for commercial importers. An individual's parcel is cleared by the courier or agent against a CNIC.

Does CPFTA make my item duty free?

Only for listed goods with a valid Certificate of Origin from China, and only for customs duty. Sales tax and advance income tax still apply.
