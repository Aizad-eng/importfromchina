Can You Use WeChat Pay in Pakistan?

By Muhammad Shamshad AslamUpdated November 12, 20268 min read
Quick answer

No, not for paying anything in Pakistan. WeChat the chat app works here, but WeChat Pay is built for the Chinese mainland. Since July 2023 foreign visitors can link a Visa, Mastercard, JCB or Discover card to pay merchants in China, but foreign cards cannot send transfers or red packets, so you cannot pay a supplier's personal WeChat from Pakistan.

Can you use WeChat Pay in Pakistan?

No. WeChat Pay has no service for paying shops or people in Pakistan, and you cannot load it from a Pakistani bank, JazzCash or Easypaisa. WeChat the messaging app is a different matter. Many Pakistani buyers use it every day to talk to Chinese suppliers. The payment side inside the app is what does not work here.

What WeChat Pay does offer foreigners is a way to pay merchants in China with a linked international card. That helps if you travel to China. It does not help you pay a seller from Pakistan. For the bigger picture of buying from China, see our pillar guide on how to import from China to Pakistan.

What can a foreign card do inside WeChat Pay?

A foreign card in WeChat Pay can buy goods and services from merchants, and very little else. China Briefing reported that Tencent announced the change on 20 July 2023, and the Beijing government's foreigner guide describes the same limits.

How do you add an international card to WeChat Pay?

You add the card from the Wallet section of WeChat, and the service is aimed at people who have arrived in China. China Briefing describes the set-up as happening after entering the Chinese mainland, using a passport or eligible permit, and says the SMS number can be a foreign one. The steps commonly look like this:

  1. Open WeChat and go to Me. Then open Services or Pay and Services, depending on your app version, and then Wallet.
  2. Choose Cards and add a card. Enter the card number, expiry date and security code.
  3. Verify your identity with your passport details when asked.
  4. Confirm the SMS code sent to your phone, and set a six-digit payment password.

The card must be on a supported network. China Briefing listed Visa, Mastercard, JCB and Discover Global Network. A PayPak-only card will not work, and a Visa or Mastercard needs international use switched on in your bank app. Card names should match the WeChat account holder.

What fees and limits apply to foreign cards in WeChat Pay?

When Tencent announced foreign card support in July 2023, China Briefing reported these terms. They may have changed since, so check the app before relying on them:

  • Fees. No fee on transactions under RMB 200, and 3% on transactions above RMB 200. On a refund, the fee is returned in proportion.
  • Limits. RMB 6,000 per transaction, RMB 50,000 per month, and an annual cap reported as 60,000.

Your Pakistani bank adds its own exchange rate, and the payment counts as an international card transaction. That means it falls under the State Bank of Pakistan's USD 30,000 annual card limit per person, and the advance tax on foreign card payments applies.

Can you pay a Chinese supplier through WeChat from Pakistan?

No, and you should not try. Paying a supplier "on WeChat" usually means sending money to their personal account or scanning their personal QR code. Both are transfers, and foreign cards in WeChat Pay cannot make transfers. Even if a friend in China does it for you, the payment sits outside any platform, so there is no buyer protection and no one to mediate if the item never ships.

If a supplier you met on Alibaba asks to move payment to WeChat, keep the order on Alibaba. Our guide on how to send money to China from Pakistan covers the routes that do work.

Is WeChat still useful for buyers in Pakistan?

Yes, as a messaging tool. Many Chinese suppliers prefer WeChat for quick questions, sending pictures of the product and confirming details such as plug type or colour. WeChat's help centre describes translating a chat message by long-pressing it, which turns Chinese text into your app language.

Suppliers often share a WeChat QR code or ID once you have chatted on Alibaba or 1688. Adding them is free, and a quick message there can confirm a detail faster than waiting for a platform reply. The risk is that the conversation slowly becomes the order, with the price and variant agreed only in WeChat messages. Use it to talk, and keep payment and the final order on the shopping platform. Our guide on how to contact Chinese suppliers from Pakistan covers setting up WeChat and what to say in the first message.

Does any Pakistani wallet work with WeChat Pay?

We did not find one when we checked in November 2026. The Pakistani wallet with a China payment link is NayaPay, and that link is with Alipay+, not WeChat Pay. NayaPay's help page explains paying at Alipay+ merchants in China by QR code. JazzCash, Easypaisa and Raast are domestic methods and do not connect to WeChat Pay.

What should you use instead to pay China from Pakistan?

Use the payment method that matches where you are buying:

  • Alibaba or AliExpress. A Visa or Mastercard with international use on, on the site's own checkout.
  • In a shop in China. WeChat Pay or Alipay with a linked card, or NayaPay at Alipay+ merchants.
  • Taobao, 1688, Xianyu or a seller who only takes WeChat. A Pakistan-based service you pay in rupees.

The common thread is that the payment should land somewhere that can give your money back if the item does not arrive: a platform's checkout, or a service with a written refund policy. WeChat Pay with a foreign card is fine for a meal or a taxi in China. It is the wrong tool for paying a supplier you have never met.

How do you get WeChat Pay ready before a trip to China?

Do the card set-up as soon as you arrive, or try it before you leave if the app allows it, and keep a backup. China Briefing describes foreign card set-up as something done after entering the Chinese mainland, so plan for the first hour after landing:

  1. Before you fly, switch on international use on your Visa or Mastercard and raise the online limit in your bank app.
  2. Make sure your Pakistani number can receive SMS abroad, because the card link sends a code.
  3. After landing, add the card in Wallet and make one small purchase to confirm it works.
  4. Keep a second way to pay, such as Alipay with a linked card or NayaPay at Alipay+ merchants.

Remember the transfer limit. A Chinese friend cannot be repaid through WeChat Pay with a foreign card, because person to person transfers are not supported. Settle with them some other way.

What mistakes do people make with WeChat Pay?

  • Assuming a linked card can pay a person. Transfers and red packets are not supported with foreign cards.
  • Asking a friend in China to pay a supplier. It works, but you lose all protection.
  • Using a card in someone else's name. Name mismatches are commonly rejected.
  • Moving the order off the platform because the supplier prefers WeChat.
  • Forgetting the fee above RMB 200. Under the 2023 terms, larger payments carried a 3% fee.
  • Forgetting the Pakistani side. Bank conversion and advance tax still apply to every card payment.
