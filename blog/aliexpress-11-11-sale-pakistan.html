AliExpress 11.11 and Black Friday 2026: Worth Ordering to Pakistan?

By Muhammad Shamshad AslamUpdated October 23, 20269 min read
Quick answer

Yes, if the item is not urgent and the discount is real. AliExpress 11.11 is its biggest sale, but delivery to Pakistan already takes weeks and slows further after the sale. Parcels worth more than Rs 1,000 can attract customs duty. Order during 11.11 for things you can wait a month or more for, and order before or after it for anything with a deadline.

Should I order from AliExpress during 11.11?

Yes, for items you can wait for, and no, for anything you need by a fixed date. The sale can lower the item price, but it does nothing to speed up shipping to Pakistan, and the surge of orders usually makes dispatch and transit slower than normal.

Order during 11.11 if:

  • The item is something you can wait five or six weeks for.
  • You have checked the normal price for a few weeks and the sale price is genuinely lower.
  • The seller lists shipping to Pakistan and you can pay with an international Visa or Mastercard.

Skip the sale if:

  • You need it for a wedding, an exam, a trip or a gift with a date.
  • The saving is small compared with shipping and possible customs.
  • The item is high value, where the delivered price and buyer protection matter more than a percentage off.

When is AliExpress 11.11 in 2026?

AliExpress had not published its 2026 11.11 schedule on its own pages when we checked on 23 October 2026. The anchor date is fixed: Singles' Day is 11 November every year, and AliExpress builds its biggest sale around it.

For a guide to timing, look at last year. AliExpress's own press release for 2025 said its Singles' Day sale ran from 11 November through 19 November, with a Shake and Win promotion running from 8 November. Third-party deal calendars expect a similar pattern in 2026, but we treat those as unconfirmed until AliExpress announces the dates in its app or on its site. Check the app home screen in early November for the official countdown.

When is Black Friday 2026, and does AliExpress run a sale for it?

Black Friday 2026 falls on Friday, 27 November, the day after the fourth Thursday of November, with Cyber Monday on 30 November. AliExpress usually runs a second promotion in this period. In 2025, its 11.11 press release listed a second Shake and Win round running from 27 November to 1 December.

For Pakistani buyers, the Black Friday window has one practical drawback. An order placed in late November meets the tail of the 11.11 backlog, so it can still be in transit in late December. If you are choosing between the two sales for something you need before the new year, 11.11 itself, or an order placed in late October, gives you more time.

How long does AliExpress take to deliver to Pakistan after the sale?

Expect longer than the normal window, which is already measured in weeks. A WorldFirst guide to AliExpress for Pakistani buyers, updated in July 2026, says orders typically arrive within 15 to 45 days depending on the shipping method chosen at checkout. Its carrier table lists China Post at 25 to 45 days, EMS at 15 to 30 days and DHL at 7 to 15 days.

Around 11.11, a very large share of the year's orders are placed in a few days. Sellers take longer to pack and dispatch, Chinese couriers run behind, and air cargo space fills with e-commerce parcels. The slowdown commonly lasts one to three weeks before things return to normal. We explain the pattern in more detail in Chinese New Year and 11.11 shipping delays.

Tracking also tends to go quiet in this period. A parcel can show no update for a week or more between leaving China and reaching a Pakistani carrier. That is usually a queue, not a loss, but it is why you should note the guaranteed delivery date on your order page when you buy.

Do you pay customs on an 11.11 parcel in Pakistan?

You can, if the parcel is worth more than Rs 1,000. Section 19C of the Customs Act, as amended up to 30 June 2026, says no duties and taxes are demanded on goods imported through post or courier where the value does not exceed one thousand rupees, subject to the Board's rules. Above that, duty and taxes can apply.

A sale does not change this. A discounted gadget is still usually worth well over Rs 1,000, so it is likely to be assessed. On AliExpress, any duty is usually collected by the courier or Pakistan Post before release, not at checkout, so the amount is not known when you pay. Do not ask a seller to write a lower value on the parcel. Customs decides the value, and a mis-declared parcel can be held. For how the amount is worked out, see our guide to customs duty from China to Pakistan.

How do you pay for an 11.11 order from Pakistan?

Most Pakistani buyers pay with a Visa or Mastercard that has international online payments switched on. The WorldFirst guide calls cards the most reliable option for Pakistani buyers and says JazzCash and Easypaisa are not directly supported at AliExpress checkout. It also says cash on delivery is rarely available on cross-border AliExpress orders.

Turn on international e-commerce in your banking app a few days before the sale, not at midnight on the day. Some banks also set a low limit on online foreign transactions, and a declined card during a flash deal means losing the price. Our guide on how to buy from AliExpress in Pakistan covers the card settings and checkout in detail.

What protection do you have if the order goes wrong?

AliExpress Buyer Protection applies to sale orders like any other. Its Buyer Protection page says it refunds items that do not arrive, arrive damaged or are not as described, within the protection period. For damaged or not-as-described items, you need to raise it within 15 days of receiving the item. For non-arrival, the cover runs to the guaranteed delivery window shown on your order details page.

That window is the thing to watch in a sale. If your parcel is late, open the dispute before the window closes. A delayed sale order that you leave until after the guaranteed date can be harder to claim. The same page says change-of-mind returns mean you pay the return shipping, which from Pakistan is rarely worth it for a cheap item.

How do you order during 11.11, step by step?

  1. Make a shortlist now. Add the items to your cart in late October and note the normal price, so you can tell a real discount from a raised-then-cut price.
  2. Check Pakistan shipping. Set your region to Pakistan and confirm each listing ships to you, with a shipping method and delivery estimate shown.
  3. Prepare your card. Switch on international online payments and check your limit.
  4. Collect coupons early. AliExpress sale coupons are usually released before the main day and can run out.
  5. Pick the shipping method, not just the price. A slightly dearer tracked method can save weeks after a sale.
  6. Screenshot the order page. Keep the price, the listing and the guaranteed delivery date in case you need to open a dispute.
  7. Budget for customs. If the order is worth more than Rs 1,000, keep cash aside for duty and taxes at delivery.

Common mistakes with AliExpress sales from Pakistan

  • Ordering a deadline item. A wedding outfit or a gift ordered on 11.11 can arrive after the date.
  • Trusting the percentage. Some listings show a high original price to make the discount look larger. Compare against the price you noted earlier.
  • Combining many items into one parcel to save shipping. A bigger parcel pushes the value further past Rs 1,000, so duty is more likely.
  • Missing the dispute window. If the guaranteed delivery date passes, open a dispute promptly.
  • Buying items that cannot fly. Perfumes, aerosols and loose lithium batteries are commonly restricted by air and may be cancelled or held. See lithium battery rules for shipping to Pakistan.
