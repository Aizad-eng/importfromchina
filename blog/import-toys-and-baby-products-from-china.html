Importing Toys and Baby Products From China to Pakistan: One Item

By importfromchina.shop teamUpdated October 3, 20267 min read
Quick answer

A single toy or baby product from China is usually an easy import to Pakistan: building sets, educational toys, ride-ons, strollers, carriers and nursery gadgets ship by air and clear customs without special permits. The buyer's job is safety, because there is no local recourse once it arrives. Check for recognised safety marks, avoid counterfeits, and never import infant formula, medicines or anything a baby swallows.

Can I import a toy or baby item from China for personal use?

Yes. Toys and children's products are allowed into Pakistan and a single unit for your own family does not need a licence or registration. Customs may check children's items against safety expectations and occasional holds happen, but most small parcels clear normally. The real issue is not the border. It is that once the item is in your hands, there is no shop to return it to and no local brand office to complain to, so you have to get the safety judgement right before you pay.

What toys and baby products ship well from China?

  • Building sets and bricks, including the many Chinese brick brands.
  • Educational and STEM toys, puzzles, magnetic tiles, wooden toys.
  • Remote control cars and boats with built-in batteries. Not drones or camera quadcopters, which are routinely held.
  • Plush toys and character items that are not counterfeit brands.
  • Strollers, carriers, baby bouncers and high chairs, with the warning that these are bulky and freight adds up.
  • Nursery gadgets: baby monitors, white noise machines, bottle warmers, sterilisers, night lights.
  • Feeding and bath accessories: silicone bibs, plates, bath seats, towels.

How do I judge toy safety when buying from China?

You cannot test the toy yourself before it ships, so rely on evidence in the listing and from the seller:

  • Safety marks. Look for CE, EN71, ASTM F963 or CPC references in the listing and on the packaging photos. These are claims, not proof, but a seller who exports to Europe or the US under these marks is more likely to meet them.
  • Age grading. Respect the age label. Small parts are a choking hazard under three.
  • Material claims. Food-grade silicone, BPA-free plastic and water-based paint are worth asking about in writing.
  • Seller history. Prefer rated sellers who specialise in toys or baby goods rather than general stores.
  • Ask for photos. The seller can send photos of the actual item and its packaging before dispatch.

What about size and weight for strollers and ride-ons?

Air freight is charged on actual or volumetric weight, whichever is higher. A stroller or a child's ride-on car comes in a big carton, so its chargeable weight is often double its real weight. These items can still be worth importing when the model is not available locally or the local price is very high, but ask for a delivered price before deciding. Small toys and nursery gadgets are light and the freight share is small.

What about duty on toys and baby products?

Toys and children's goods have their own tariff classifications. Customs duty, additional customs duty, sales tax and advance income tax together commonly add 25 to 60 percent of the customs value, and customs may apply a valuation ruling rather than your invoice for common toys. Rates change with budgets and SROs, so confirm the current figure or ask for a delivered price. See how customs duty from China to Pakistan is calculated.

Frequently asked questions

Can I import toys from China to Pakistan for my own child?

Yes. Toys are allowed and a single unit for personal use needs no licence. Check safety marks and age grading in the listing, because there is no local return or recall.

Can I import baby formula from China?

No. Infant formula faces food safety and regulatory holds and you cannot verify storage or authenticity. Buy formula locally.

Can I import a stroller from China to Pakistan?

Yes, but strollers are bulky and air freight is charged on volumetric weight. Ask for a delivered price and compare with local options before deciding.

Are Chinese toys safe?

Many are, especially from exporters who label CE, EN71 or ASTM compliance. Avoid cheap unbranded electrical toys, loose magnets and counterfeits, which have no testing behind them.

Can I import a toy drone for my child?

Not advisable. Drones and camera quadcopters are routinely held by Pakistan Customs without prior permission. RC cars and boats are fine.
