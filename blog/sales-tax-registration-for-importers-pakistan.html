Sales Tax Registration (STRN) for Importers in Pakistan

By Muhammad Shamshad AslamUpdated November 12, 202610 min read
Quick answer

Anyone importing goods commercially in their own name needs a sales tax registration number (STRN) from FBR, because FBR's WeBOC trader registration asks for it alongside the NTN. You apply online on Form 14(1) through IRIS, then complete NADRA biometric verification within 30 days. A one-off personal parcel through a courier does not need an STRN.

What is an STRN?

An STRN is the sales tax registration number the Federal Board of Revenue (FBR) gives a person registered under the Sales Tax Act 1990. It shows that the person is on FBR's sales tax register and, once biometric verification is done, on the Sales Tax Active Taxpayer List. For importers it is one of the identities customs systems use, alongside the NTN.

This guide is about the STRN for commercial importers. If you are bringing in one item for yourself, read the section on personal parcels first, because you probably do not need one. For the overall import process, see how to import from China to Pakistan.

How is an STRN different from an NTN?

The NTN is your income tax registration and the STRN is your sales tax registration. They are separate numbers on the same FBR system, IRIS. A salaried person may have an NTN and never need an STRN. An importer of commercial goods needs both, because FBR's checklist for registering as a trader on WeBOC asks for the NTN certificate and the sales tax registration certificate, and also for active status on both the income tax and sales tax Active Taxpayer Lists.

If you only want to know whether a personal import needs a tax number at all, our guide on whether you need an NTN to import one item answers that.

Do importers have to register for sales tax?

Anyone who wants to file customs declarations for commercial goods in their own name does, in practice. WeBOC user access as a trader requires the sales tax registration certificate or the IRIS Form 14(1) record, and customs collects sales tax at import whether or not the importer is registered.

On the verified rates for 2026-27, sales tax at import is 18 percent of the customs value plus customs duty, additional customs duty and any regulatory duty, and a further 3 percent value addition tax applies to commercial importers. Advance income tax under section 148 of the Income Tax Ordinance is collected at the same time. Our guide on customs duty from China to Pakistan shows how these stack up.

Do you need an STRN to import one item for yourself?

No. When you receive a personal parcel through a courier or agent, the courier files the declaration and identifies you with your CNIC. Section 144 of the Customs Act says that for goods coming by post or courier, the label or declaration on the parcel is treated as the customs entry, and section 19C exempts parcels worth up to Rs 1,000 from duties and taxes altogether.

FBR's rules now spell out the identity point for sea and land cargo. Under SRO 918(I)/2026, from 15 August 2026 cargo declarations at sea ports and land border stations must carry the importer's NTN or FTN where registration is mandatory, and a CNIC or passport number where it is not. Profit reported that this does not apply to declarations filed at airports. The STRN matters when you import goods for business in your own name.

How do you apply for an STRN, step by step?

FBR's registration page, when we checked in November 2026, described a simplified online process:

  1. Log in to IRIS, FBR's online portal, with your credentials. The same process is available through the Tax Asaan mobile app.
  2. Open the Registration menu and choose Form 14(1), the simplified registration form for sales tax.
  3. Enter the tax period and registration type. An individual chooses manufacturer or non-manufacturer. An association or company also gives the CNIC of a member, director or principal officer.
  4. Add business details: business name, start date, capacity, business activity and any branches.
  5. Upload proof: a bank account certificate in the business name, GPS-tagged pictures of the business premises, and the gas or electricity consumer number with a picture of the meter. Manufacturers also add pictures of installed machinery and the industrial meter.
  6. Visit a NADRA e-Sahulat centre within 30 days for biometric verification.

If you do not complete biometric verification, or it fails, FBR removes your name from the Sales Tax Active Taxpayer List. Manufacturers may also face post-registration checks, and if a document is found to be wrong the system can ask for a replacement within fifteen days.

Several papers appear at more than one stage, such as the CNIC, bank documents and utility bills, so keep clean scanned copies together. The premises details you give on Form 14(1) should match the rent agreement or property papers you later submit for WeBOC, because a mismatch slows both.

What comes after the STRN?

An STRN on its own does not let you file customs declarations. FBR's checklist for registering as a trader on WeBOC, when we checked in November 2026, asked for:

  • the proprietor's original CNIC and a request letter on letterhead,
  • a visiting card, two passport-size pictures and the WeBOC external user form,
  • NADRA verification, the NTN certificate and the sales tax registration certificate or IRIS-14(I),
  • the IRIS-181 and IRIS-114 records,
  • a current six-month bank statement showing a last balance of Rs 300,000 and a bank account maintenance certificate,
  • Active Taxpayer List status for both sales tax and income tax, with the principal activity showing as importer or exporter,
  • a valid chamber of commerce certificate, current utility bills and property documents or a rent agreement,
  • an undertaking on Rs 100 stamp paper with two witnesses, and a Rs 500 pay order or cash deposit receipt,
  • a partnership deed or company documents where relevant.

Pakistan Single Window is taking over from WeBOC in stages, starting with goods that need other agencies' clearance. Our guide to Pakistan Single Window covers that step.

What obligations come with registration?

Registration is not a one-time form. A registered person files sales tax returns on the schedule FBR sets and must keep the details on IRIS current, since the Active Taxpayer List is what customs and other businesses check. Falling off the list affects how you are treated at import. Check the current return schedule and any re-verification rules on IRIS rather than relying on an old guide.

What mistakes do new importers make?

  • Registering, then skipping biometrics. The 30-day window is easy to miss, and an inactive status follows.
  • Choosing the wrong registration type. Manufacturer and non-manufacturer registrations carry different checks.
  • Thinking the STRN alone allows customs filing. WeBOC or PSW access is a separate step with its own documents.
  • Registering for one personal parcel. It adds filing obligations you do not need.
  • Using a relative's STRN. The importer of record carries the liability for the declaration.

Frequently asked questions

Do commercial importers in Pakistan need an STRN?

Yes in practice. FBR's WeBOC trader registration asks for the sales tax registration certificate along with the NTN and active taxpayer status.

Which form is used for STRN registration?

Form 14(1), the simplified registration form on FBR's IRIS portal, also available through the Tax Asaan app.

How long do I have for NADRA biometric verification?

FBR's procedure gives 30 days to visit a NADRA e-Sahulat centre. Without it, your name is removed from the Sales Tax Active Taxpayer List.

Do I need an STRN to receive a parcel from China?

No. For a personal parcel the courier files the declaration and identifies you with your CNIC.

Can I file customs declarations once I have an STRN?

Not on its own. You also need a WeBOC or PSW user ID, which has its own document list, including an NTN and a bank statement.

What bank balance does FBR ask for on WeBOC trader registration?

FBR's checklist asks for a current six-month bank statement with a last balance of Rs 300,000.
