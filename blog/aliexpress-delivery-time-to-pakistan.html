AliExpress Delivery Time to Pakistan (2026)

By Muhammad Shamshad AslamUpdated October 23, 202610 min read
Quick answer

When we checked in October 2026, AliExpress listings set to Pakistan showed delivery estimates of roughly 8 to 18 days by AliExpress standard shipping for orders placed that day. Postal routes like China Post commonly take 25 to 45 days, and DHL-type express 7 to 15 days. Customs holds, the Pakistan Post handover and the 11.11 sale are the usual causes of delay.

How long does AliExpress take to deliver to Pakistan?

Most AliExpress orders to Pakistan now show an estimate of about one and a half to two and a half weeks, but real parcels often take longer. On 23 October 2026 we opened several listings with the region set to Pakistan, including a Choice item and items from ordinary stores. Each showed a single shipping option with tracking, named AliExpress standard shipping where the panel gave a name, and the delivery windows ran from 31 October to 10 November. That is about 8 to 18 days from the order date.

That estimate covers the whole trip. It assumes the seller dispatches on time, the parcel clears customs without a hold and the last leg inside Pakistan goes smoothly. Any of those can add days or weeks, which is why many Pakistani buyers still say AliExpress takes a month or more.

How long does each AliExpress shipping method take to Pakistan?

The method you get decides most of the timing. AliExpress sets this per listing, so check the shipping panel on the product page before you pay.

The WorldFirst guide puts most AliExpress orders to Pakistan at 15 to 45 days overall. The shorter estimates we saw on listings are AliExpress's own promise for that day, not a measured average, so treat 8 to 18 days as the best case.

What happened to cheap AliExpress shipping to Pakistan in 2025?

AliExpress cut its budget shipping routes to Pakistan in July 2025. Bol News and TechJuice reported that the platform told sellers routes to Pakistan and Sri Lanka would be handled offline from 7 July 2025, and that low-cost options including AliExpress Standard Shipping and Cainiao could no longer be chosen. AliExpress gave unclear tax policies as the reason. This came at the same time as the duty-free limit for post and courier parcels fell from Rs 5,000 to Rs 1,000.

By October 2026, AliExpress standard shipping was back on the listings we checked, but we did not see the old super-cheap economy options. That is one reason shipping charges of a few hundred rupees now appear on items that used to ship free.

Does AliExpress Choice arrive faster in Pakistan?

Not noticeably, based on what we saw. The Choice item we opened showed the same window, 2 to 10 November, as non-Choice items from ordinary stores. The AliExpress homepage advertised free shipping on all Choice items, yet that single Choice item still showed Rs 627 shipping to Pakistan, so the free shipping rule seems to depend on what is in your cart.

What does the delivery date on the listing actually mean?

It is the window AliExpress expects, and it matters for refunds. AliExpress's Buyer Protection page says you can get a refund if an item does not arrive within the guaranteed delivery window on your order detail page. So the date on your order page, not a forum post, is the date that counts.

Write it down when you order. If the parcel has not arrived by the end of that window, you have grounds to open a dispute.

Why does tracking stop after the parcel leaves China?

Because the parcel changes hands. A postal or standard shipping parcel is tracked by the Chinese side until it departs, then handed to Pakistan Post and Customs on arrival. There can be a gap of days with no new scan while it flies, waits at the office of exchange and goes through customs.

Once Pakistan Post has it, you can usually follow the same number on Pakistan Post's own tracking site, which is ep.gov.pk. Pakistan Post warns that any other tracking link or website claiming to be Pakistan Post does not belong to it.

Why do AliExpress parcels get stuck in Pakistan?

Usually for one of a handful of reasons, most of them on the Pakistan side rather than in China. A 2022 Universal Postal Union report on Pakistan Post's Lahore office of exchange listed the main reasons parcels went undelivered:

  • Incorrect or incomplete address. A missing area, street or phone number can strand a parcel.
  • The addressee had moved.
  • Customs detention. Parcels over Rs 1,000 are assessed and held until duty is paid.
  • Confiscation by Customs or the Anti-Narcotics Force, for restricted or prohibited items.
  • Refusal over high duty. Some people decline the parcel when they see the bill.

Customs holds are the most common surprise for AliExpress buyers. Our guide on what to do when a parcel is stuck at customs covers the next steps, and the AliExpress customs duty guide shows how the bill is worked out.

Is paying for express shipping on AliExpress worth it?

Only for items where time matters more than cost. When a listing offers DHL or a similar express courier, WorldFirst's figures put delivery at 7 to 15 days against 25 to 45 days for China Post. The courier also handles customs clearance through its own channel rather than the postal route.

The trade-off is price. Express shipping for one parcel can cost more than a cheap item itself, and the courier will still ask you for any duty before it delivers a parcel worth over Rs 1,000. On the listings we opened in October 2026, express was not offered at all, so for many items the only choice is the standard route and its window.

Does 11.11 slow down AliExpress orders to Pakistan?

Yes, usually. The 11.11 sale in November produces a surge of orders, so sellers take longer to dispatch and parcels queue at every handover. Chinese New Year, roughly late January to mid February, has a similar effect because many sellers and factories close. If you need something by a date, order well before either period. See our note on Chinese New Year and 11.11 delays.

What should I do if my AliExpress order is late?

  1. Check the guaranteed delivery window on your order detail page. If it has not passed, wait.
  2. Look up the tracking number on Pakistan Post's site if the parcel has reached Pakistan.
  3. If the status shows a customs hold, contact your local post office or the courier named on the tracking. Pakistan Post's helpline is 051-111-111-117.
  4. Message the seller through AliExpress and ask for an update. Keep everything inside the platform.
  5. If the window passes and the parcel has not arrived, open a dispute from the order page before the protection period ends.

Common mistakes with AliExpress delivery times

  • Trusting the estimate as a promise of arrival. It is a window, and customs time can sit on top of it.
  • Waiting too long to dispute. Protection is tied to the window on your order page.
  • Short addresses. Include area, street, nearest landmark and a working mobile number.
  • Paying a link that claims to release your parcel. Pakistan Post says it never collects payment online.
  • Ordering just before 11.11 for a deadline. Build in extra weeks.
