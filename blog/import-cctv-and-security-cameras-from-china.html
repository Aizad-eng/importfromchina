Importing CCTV and Security Cameras From China to Pakistan

By Muhammad Shamshad AslamUpdated November 12, 20269 min read
Quick answer

Yes, you can import a CCTV camera, NVR or small camera kit from China to Pakistan as a single order. Buy the international version, not the China-region one, check PoE, storage and ONVIF support, and watch the operating temperature for Pakistani summers. Expect duty and taxes on the customs value plus Rs 5,000 to 20,000 shipping.

New to importing? Read our step-by-step guide on how to import from China to Pakistan.

Can you import CCTV cameras from China to Pakistan?

Yes. A security camera, a network video recorder (NVR) or a small kit is an ordinary electronic item and can be imported for your own home, shop or office. The reason to import is usually a specific model, sensor or feature that local installers do not stock. Our guide on how to import from China to Pakistan explains the general process.

One limit up front: we do single items, so one camera, one NVR, or a camera with its recorder is fine. Stock for an installation business is a commercial import and belongs with a cargo company or the commercial route.

Which type of camera should you choose?

Choose by how the camera gets power and where its footage is stored. The four common types behave very differently in a Pakistani house with load-shedding and patchy Wi-Fi.

For most homes, a wired PoE camera on a small NVR, with the NVR and network switch on a UPS, keeps recording through a power cut, which Wi-Fi cameras on plain adapters will not.

When you choose an NVR, count three things: channels, PoE ports and drive bays. Channels are how many cameras it can record, and buying a few more than you need today leaves room to add a gate or roof camera later. Built-in PoE ports save buying a separate switch. Drive bays and the supported drive size decide how many days of footage you keep, and higher resolution cameras fill a drive faster. Remote viewing on your phone also needs a working internet connection at the NVR, so plan where the router sits.

Which specs matter on a camera listing?

Resolution, low-light performance, power, storage and weather rating matter most. Take a well-documented camera as a reference. When we checked in November 2026, Reolink's page for its RLC-510A listed:

  • 5 MP resolution at 2560 x 1920.
  • Power over Ethernet to the 802.3af standard at 48V, or a separate 12V 1A adapter.
  • Infrared night vision up to 100 ft from 18 LEDs.
  • A microSD slot, with the page giving 512 GB in one section and 256 GB in its FAQ.
  • IP67 weatherproofing and an operating range of minus 10 to 55 degrees Celsius.

That last line matters here. A camera mounted on a sunny wall in a Pakistani summer can run well above the air temperature, so check the upper operating limit on any camera you pick and mount it under a shade if you can. The conflicting microSD numbers on one official page also show why it is worth asking the seller to confirm the exact figure you care about.

Will a Chinese camera work with my NVR and app?

It will if both speak a common standard, and ONVIF is the one to look for. ONVIF's Profile S covers basic streaming: a Profile S camera sends its stream over the network to a Profile S client, such as an NVR or recording software. ONVIF notes that Profile S is being phased out, with the last date for product conformance submissions on 31 March 2027, so newer products may list other profiles too. Mixing brands through ONVIF usually gives you live view and recording, but brand-only features such as person detection often work only on the same brand's recorder.

If you already have an analogue DVR, match the camera's format (AHD, TVI or CVI) and resolution to what the DVR accepts, or plan to replace both.

What is the China-region problem with cameras?

Cameras made for the mainland Chinese market can be locked to Chinese firmware, apps and cloud servers. Hikvision's own notice for Hong Kong and Macau says products sold there and in mainland China follow the design and certification standards of their own regions, and that it takes no responsibility for compatibility problems from such products. Its regional notices also tie firmware upgrades and warranty to official local channels.

Cloud cameras add an account issue. EZVIZ's support FAQ says an account's region cannot be changed once it is registered, and that you need a new account to use another region. Our guide on gadgets that work differently in Pakistan explains region locks in more detail. The rule is simple: on Taobao or 1688 you usually get the China version, so ask for the international version with English firmware in writing, or buy from an international listing.

Does a CCTV camera need PTA approval?

A wired PoE or analogue camera has no radio, so PTA's radio rules are not usually the question. Wi-Fi and 4G cameras are radio devices. PTA requires type approval for terminal equipment imported for commercial purposes, and its revised framework for short range devices and IoT, version 1.4, was issued in December 2025. ProPakistani reported in January 2026 that the framework bars storage of IoT data outside Pakistan without PTA approval, a rule aimed at IoT service licensees. For a 4G camera, the SIM side falls under DIRBS like any SIM device, so ask the seller for the IMEI and check it by SMS to 8484 before relying on it.

How is a camera taxed at Pakistan customs?

Cameras and recorders pay duty and taxes on their customs value under their PCT code. The customs value is the CIF value, converted at NBP rates published by FBR. When we checked in November 2026, customs duty sat in slabs of 0 to 20 percent with some goods higher, additional customs duty was 0 percent on the 0 to 15 percent slabs and 2 percent on the 20 percent slab, regulatory duty applied to listed goods only and was capped at 20 percent from 1 July 2026, and sales tax was 18 percent on the value plus duties. Advance income tax under section 148 is also collected. Parcels worth Rs 1,000 or less by post or courier are exempt under section 19C, but a camera of any quality is above that.

Is importing one camera worth the shipping cost?

It is for a better camera or an NVR kit, and usually not for a cheap Wi-Fi camera. Shipping from China commonly runs Rs 5,000 to 20,000 per parcel depending on size and type. An NVR with a hard drive is heavy, and a bullet camera box is bulky. A low-priced indoor Wi-Fi camera is often sold locally at a price that importing cannot beat once shipping is added, so single orders work best for items worth around Rs 10,000 or more.

What mistakes do people make with imported cameras?

  • Buying the China-region version and finding the app or firmware will not switch to English or an international account.
  • Mixing brands and expecting smart detection to work across them.
  • Using a cheap PoE injector or switch that does not meet 802.3af, so the camera keeps rebooting.
  • Ignoring the operating temperature for a roof or wall in direct sun.
  • Leaving the NVR and switch off the UPS, so recording stops during load-shedding.
  • Keeping the default password and leaving the camera open to the internet.

Frequently asked questions

Are Chinese CCTV cameras allowed in Pakistan?

Yes. Security cameras are ordinary imports that pay duty and taxes. Wi-Fi and 4G models are radio devices, and SIM-based cameras fall under PTA's DIRBS rules.

Should I buy a PoE or Wi-Fi camera for my house?

PoE is usually better for fixed cameras. One cable carries power and data, and with the NVR and switch on a UPS it keeps recording during load-shedding.

Can I use a Hikvision camera with a Dahua NVR?

Often, for live view and recording, if both support ONVIF. Smart features such as person detection usually work only within one brand.

What is the problem with China-version CCTV cameras?

They can be tied to Chinese firmware, apps and cloud servers. Ask the seller in writing for the international version with English firmware.

Will a camera survive a Pakistani summer outdoors?

Check its operating range. Reolink lists 55 degrees Celsius as the upper limit for its RLC-510A, and a wall in direct sun gets hotter than the air, so shade helps.

Is it worth importing one cheap Wi-Fi camera?

Usually not. Shipping often costs Rs 5,000 to 20,000, so single imports make sense for better cameras or kits worth around Rs 10,000 or more.
