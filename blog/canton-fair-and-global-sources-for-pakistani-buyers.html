Canton Fair and Global Sources: Are They Useful for Pakistani Buyers?

By Muhammad Shamshad AslamUpdated November 12, 20269 min read
Quick answer

The Canton Fair and Global Sources are useful for Pakistani businesses that import commercially and want to meet many Chinese suppliers in person. They are not useful for buying one item for personal use. The Canton Fair runs twice a year in Guangzhou, and Global Sources runs trade shows in Hong Kong and an online marketplace. Both are aimed at trade buyers.

New to importing? Read our step-by-step guide on how to import from China to Pakistan.

Are the Canton Fair and Global Sources useful for Pakistani buyers?

Yes for businesses, no for individuals buying one thing. Both are trade events and platforms where companies meet suppliers, compare products and negotiate orders. If you run a business that imports commercially, a visit can save months of online searching. If you want one product for your home or work, the cost of the trip is far more than the item, and exhibitors expect orders in volume.

This guide explains what each one is, when the next dates are, how buyers register, and how to decide whether it is worth it. If you are new to importing, start with our guide on how to import from China to Pakistan.

What is the Canton Fair?

The Canton Fair, officially the China Import and Export Fair, is a general trade fair held twice a year in Guangzhou, in spring and autumn, with each session split into three phases covering different product groups. The 140th session, in autumn 2026, marked the fair's 70th anniversary.

According to a Xinhua report published by China Daily on 20 October 2026, the first phase of the 140th session had the theme Advanced Manufacturing, covering electronics and home appliances, industrial automation, lighting and electrical equipment, hardware and tools, and new energy vehicles, with more than 12,000 companies on 25,000 booths. It said 189,000 overseas buyers from 214 countries and regions had attended by the end of that phase. Phase two, 23 to 27 October, had the theme Quality Home Life, covering building materials, furniture, household items, gifts and decorations.

A Bastille Post report on 4 November 2026 said the session closed that day with a record 352,000 overseas buyers from 219 countries and regions and 32,000 exhibitors. The same report gave the next session, the 141st, as 15 April to 5 May 2027 in Guangzhou.

What is Global Sources?

Global Sources is a Hong Kong business-to-business company founded in 1971 that connects buyers with Asian suppliers through trade shows and an online marketplace. Its parent company is Clarion Events, backed by Blackstone, according to its Wikipedia entry.

Its main events are the Global Sources Hong Kong Shows at AsiaWorld-Expo. A Global Sources press release from 1 September 2026 said the October 2026 shows ran from 11 to 21 October in two phases, with more than 4,200 Asia-based suppliers on more than 8,000 booths. Phase one, 11 to 14 October, focused on consumer electronics and gaming. Phase two, 18 to 21 October, covered mobile electronics, smart home, security and appliances, lifestyle electronics, and sports and outdoor products.

Can a Pakistani buyer attend the Canton Fair?

Yes. Overseas buyers register online and receive a buyer badge. The Canton Fair's Hong Kong office page says overseas buyers pre-apply for a badge through the Buyer E-Service Tool (BEST) free of charge, and that applications are approved, with collection details emailed, within three working days.

The fair also issues a free invitation, which the same page says is meant for China visa applications, either on paper or as an e-invitation you print. Pakistani passport holders should check the current visa requirements for a business visit with the Chinese embassy or visa centre before booking flights, because visa rules change.

Be wary of anyone selling Canton Fair badges or invitations. The official badge application for overseas buyers is free.

What does a trip actually cost a Pakistani buyer?

More than the badge. Even with free registration, you pay for flights to Guangzhou or Hong Kong, a visa, hotels during the busiest weeks of the year, local transport and often an interpreter. These costs make sense only when you are placing orders large enough to spread them out.

There is also a timing cost. Each phase covers different products, so you must plan your trip around the phase for your category, or stay for more than one.

Using the autumn 2026 session as a guide, a business looking for electronics, appliances, tools, lighting or machinery would have wanted the first phase, Advanced Manufacturing. A business looking for furniture, building materials, household goods or gifts would have wanted the second, Quality Home Life. Categories move between phases from one session to the next, so check the official phase list for the session you plan to attend before booking anything. Global Sources is simpler to plan for an electronics business, since its October 2026 shows were almost entirely electronics and lifestyle products.

Will exhibitors sell one piece?

Rarely, and not to someone abroad. Exhibitors come to meet trade buyers and take orders, often with minimums of hundreds or thousands of units. Some may sell a sample to a buyer at the booth who is clearly preparing a larger order. A request to send one item to Pakistan for personal use is usually ignored, and that is not what these fairs are for.

If you only want one item, the same suppliers are usually on Alibaba, Made-in-China.com, 1688 or Taobao, where some listings sell single pieces. Our guide to Made-in-China.com for Pakistani buyers covers one of those platforms.

What should a business prepare before visiting?

  1. Decide your category and phase, and check the official dates for the session.
  2. Register early and apply for the badge and, if needed, the invitation for your visa.
  3. Know your import route. Commercial imports into Pakistan need an NTN, sales tax registration and a WeBOC account. Our guide to Pakistan Single Window explains the online system used for import filings.
  4. Find your HS code before you negotiate, so you know the duty on what you are buying.
  5. Prepare a short list of target prices and specifications, and business cards.
  6. Plan how you will check suppliers after the fair. A booth does not prove a company is reliable.

How do you check a supplier you met at a fair?

The same way you check any Chinese supplier. Ask for the business licence, look the company up on China's official registry, confirm whether it is a factory or a trading company, and pay through a protected method or a documented contract. Meeting someone at a booth makes a conversation easier, but it does not replace these checks. Our guide on finding reliable Chinese suppliers sets out a checklist.

Can you use Global Sources online instead of travelling?

Yes, for finding suppliers. Global Sources runs an online marketplace where buyers contact manufacturers directly, so a business can shortlist suppliers from Pakistan without flying. It works best for electronics and consumer products. As with any directory, it is the start of supplier research, not proof of reliability, and most suppliers there quote for trade quantities.

For a Pakistani business, online research first and a fair visit second is often the sensible order. Shortlist suppliers online, ask for catalogues and prices, and then use the fair to meet the few that look right. That way the trip is spent on serious conversations rather than walking every hall.

Frequently asked questions

When is the next Canton Fair?

The 141st Canton Fair is scheduled for 15 April to 5 May 2027 in Guangzhou, according to a November 2026 report. Check the official site for phase dates.

Is the Canton Fair free for Pakistani buyers?

The buyer badge and invitation are free for overseas buyers who apply through the fair's online system. Travel, visa and hotels are your own cost.

Can I buy one item at the Canton Fair?

Rarely. Exhibitors take trade orders, and few will sell one piece, let alone ship it to Pakistan.

Is Global Sources the same as the Canton Fair?

No. Global Sources is a Hong Kong company that runs its own trade shows and an online marketplace. The Canton Fair is a separate fair in Guangzhou.

Do I need a visa for the Canton Fair?

Pakistani passport holders should check current requirements with the Chinese embassy or visa centre. The fair issues a free invitation for visa applications.

Do I need an NTN to import what I order at a fair?

Yes, for commercial quantities. Importing for business in your own name needs an NTN, sales tax registration and a WeBOC account.
