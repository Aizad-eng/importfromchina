Import Timeline From China to Pakistan: What Happens Day by Day

By importfromchina.shop teamUpdated October 3, 20267 min read
Quick answer

For one item by air freight: you pay, the order goes to the seller, the seller dispatches in 1 to 3 days to a warehouse in China, the parcel is consolidated and flies within a few days, Pakistan customs takes 1 to 4 working days, and local delivery adds 1 to 3 days. Door to door, 7 to 15 days after dispatch is typical. Most delays come from the seller, not the flight.

Day 0: you confirm and pay

The clock starts when payment lands, not when you first ask for a price. If you order on AliExpress yourself, that is the moment your card is charged. If you use a single-item service, it is the moment you pay the one rupee price, before anything ships. From this point the item is bought from the seller, usually the same day or the next working day in China. China is three hours ahead of Pakistan, so a payment made in the evening in Lahore is often actioned the next Chinese morning.

Days 1 to 3: the seller dispatches

Most rated sellers on 1688, Taobao and Alibaba dispatch in-stock items within 24 to 72 hours. Pre-order items, custom items and anything the listing describes as made to order take longer, sometimes 7 to 15 days before they even leave the seller. This is the stage that varies the most. A seller who is slow to dispatch adds days to every later stage. If you want to know the real timeline, ask about dispatch time before you pay.

Days 3 to 6: the parcel reaches the China warehouse

The seller ships by a domestic Chinese courier to the forwarder's warehouse, usually in Guangdong, Zhejiang or wherever the forwarder is based. Domestic delivery inside China is fast, commonly 1 to 3 days. At the warehouse the parcel is weighed, measured and matched to your order. It then waits for the next consolidated air shipment to Pakistan. Consolidation is why air freight is cheaper than courier: many single parcels share one shipment. The wait for the next shipment is usually a day or two, sometimes longer in busy weeks.

Days 6 to 9: the flight to Pakistan

The consolidated shipment is booked onto a flight to Karachi, Lahore or Islamabad. Flight time itself is a few hours, but cargo space is booked in advance and shipments sometimes wait for the next available flight. Transit through a hub can add a day. The flight is almost never the cause of a late delivery. If your tracking shows the parcel in China for a long time, the problem is upstream, at the seller or the warehouse.

Days 8 to 12: customs clearance at the airport

On arrival the shipment is manifested and a goods declaration is filed through WeBOC or the Pakistan Single Window. Customs assesses duty and taxes, sometimes examines the goods, and releases them once duty is paid. For a routine, correctly declared item this takes 1 to 4 working days. Items that attract attention, such as electronics with unclear value, branded goods or anything with a battery, take longer. Weekends and public holidays in Pakistan do not count. See how customs clearance works at Pakistani airports.

Days 10 to 15: local delivery

Once released, the parcel is handed to a domestic courier for delivery to your address. Karachi, Lahore and Islamabad addresses are usually delivered within 1 to 2 days of release. Smaller cities and towns add a day or two. Remote areas can add more. With a duty-paid service there is nothing to pay at the door, so the courier simply hands it over.

Add the typical column and you get roughly 7 to 15 days from dispatch. Add the delay column and you see why some orders take three weeks. Two things cause most of the slippage: slow sellers and the time of year. Around Chinese New Year, 11.11 and 618 every stage in China stretches. Read when shipping from China slows down.

How can I shorten the timeline?

  • Choose an in-stock listing. Avoid anything marked pre-sale or made to order unless you can wait.
  • Give a complete address and a phone number that is answered. Failed delivery attempts add days.
  • Avoid ordering in the two weeks before Chinese New Year.
  • Pick an item that clears easily: unbranded, no loose battery, clear description.
  • Do not expect air express speed from air freight. Express courier is 5 to 10 days but costs far more. See air freight vs courier for one item.

Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to import one item from China to Pakistan by air?

Typically 7 to 15 days door to door after the seller dispatches, including customs. Add 1 to 3 days for the seller to dispatch an in-stock item.

Which stage of the import takes the longest?

Usually seller dispatch when the item is not in stock, followed by customs when the item needs a valuation check. The flight itself is rarely the issue.

Does the timeline include customs clearance?

Yes. The 7 to 15 day estimate covers arrival, declaration, assessment, duty payment and release at the Pakistani airport.

Why has my parcel been in China for over a week?

Either the seller has not dispatched, the parcel is waiting for the next consolidated shipment, or it is a slow period such as Chinese New Year or after 11.11. Check with whoever placed the order.

Is delivery slower outside Karachi, Lahore and Islamabad?

A little. Major cities are usually delivered within 1 to 2 days of customs release. Smaller towns add a day or two, remote areas more.
