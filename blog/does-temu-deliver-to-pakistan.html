Does Temu deliver to Pakistan in October 2026?

Yes. At the time of writing (October 2026), Temu runs a Pakistan version of its site at temu.com/pk-en, with the region shown as Pakistan and prices in rupees. When we opened it from Pakistan on 11 October 2026, it showed products priced in Rs, a Pakistan shipping page, a Pakistan return policy and Pakistan terms of use.

Older guides, including an earlier version of this one, said Temu did not list Pakistan. That is out of date. ProPakistani reported on 30 September 2024 that Temu had officially started operating in Pakistan, and Temu's Pakistan Terms of Use were last updated on 19 September 2024.

So if you are asking "can Temu deliver in Pakistan" or "is Temu deliver in Pakistan", the short answer is yes, for most items, to most normal addresses. The useful questions now are how long it takes, how you pay, what customs means for your parcel and how to avoid fake "Temu Pakistan" sellers.

How long does Temu delivery to Pakistan take, and what does it cost?

Temu's Pakistan shipping page says free standard shipping applies to all orders, with a delivery window of 5 to 13 business days. Processing takes 1 to 3 days before that, so count from the day the order ships, not the day you pay.

A few details from the same page are worth knowing:

  • The delivery estimate for each package appears after payment, on the order confirmation page and in your order history.
  • The final leg is handed to local carriers, so tracking may not update at every step. A gap of a few days with no scan is common and not a sign the parcel is lost.
  • Some items need a physical address and cannot go to a P.O. box. Large items, valuables and items shipped by private carriers are examples Temu gives.
  • Temu says items cannot be delivered to security-restricted areas such as military bases. If you live inside a cantonment or a gated official colony, use an address outside it.
  • If the parcel has not arrived, or tracking shows delivered but you have nothing, contact Temu customer service within 90 days of the order date.

How do you pay Temu from Pakistan?

The footer of Temu's Pakistan site shows these payment logos: JazzCash, Easypaisa, Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, Maestro, Diners Club, JCB, UnionPay, Apple Pay and Google Pay. Cash on delivery was not among the logos when we checked.

In practice, most people in Pakistan will use JazzCash, Easypaisa or a debit card. If you use a bank card, make sure international or online transactions are switched on in your banking app first, because many Pakistani cards ship with them off. Your bank's exchange rate and any card charges are set by your bank, not Temu.

Refunds go back to the original payment method or to Temu credit, your choice. Temu's Pakistan return policy says refunds to the original method can take 5 to 14 business days, and up to 30 days, depending on your bank or wallet.

Why does the Temu app not show Pakistan for some people?

Temu is one app with many country sites. The country you see depends on the region set in the app or site, not only on where you are. Common reasons you might see the US, UAE or another country instead of Pakistan:

  • A VPN or a company network routes your connection through another country.
  • You created your account earlier while abroad, or while a VPN was on, and it kept that region.
  • You followed a link shared from someone else's country site, so the URL points to their region.

The fix is simple. Turn off any VPN, then change the region to Pakistan in the app's settings or in the country and language selector on temu.com, where the option reads "Pakistan English". Prices should switch to rupees. If they do not, sign out, close the app and open it again.

Can a VPN or a foreign forwarding address get you a cheaper Temu deal?

People ask this because they see a lower price or an item on the US or UAE site that is not on the Pakistan site. It rarely works out, and checkout commonly fails for reasons that have nothing to do with the VPN itself:

  • Address mismatch. Each Temu country site is set up to deliver inside that country, with its own carriers, taxes and return pickups. A Pakistani address on another country's site does not fit that setup.
  • Payment mismatch. JazzCash and Easypaisa appear on the Pakistan site. Other country sites typically show their own local methods, and a Pakistani card on a foreign storefront is more likely to be flagged by fraud checks.
  • Double cost. If you ship to a friend or a forwarder abroad, you pay a second international shipment and still face Pakistan customs on arrival. You also lose Temu's delivery promise and easy returns once the parcel leaves the address you ordered to.

Do you pay customs duty on a Temu parcel in Pakistan?

Sometimes. Two rules matter here.

Temu's own terms. Temu's Pakistan Terms of Use say that, where applicable, you act as the importer, and you authorise Temu to appoint a freight forwarding agent to pay sales tax and customs duties on your behalf. The terms also say any duties collected at checkout are estimates, and if customs assesses more, you are responsible for the difference. Temu's return policy adds that import tax is not refundable if a return is not Temu's fault.

Pakistan's small parcel rule. Section 19C of the Customs Act, as amended by the Finance Act 2025, says that where the value of goods imported through post or courier does not exceed Rs 1,000, no duties and taxes shall be demanded, subject to conditions set by the FBR. As we read it, once a parcel is worth more than Rs 1,000, duty and taxes can apply to its whole value, not only the part above Rs 1,000. Check the current FBR rule if your order is close to that line, because thresholds change with budgets.

What this means for one Temu parcel:

  • Small orders under Rs 1,000 sent by post or courier should not attract duty under section 19C.
  • For larger orders, look at the checkout total. If Temu shows an import tax line, that is its estimate.
  • If a courier asks for extra duty at the door, ask for the customs assessment document before paying anything.
  • Mobile phones are a separate case. They need PTA registration, and the tax depends on the model, whatever the parcel value.

Is a "Temu Pakistan" WhatsApp seller or Facebook page really Temu?

Treat it as a reseller, not Temu. The official way to buy is the Temu app or temu.com with the region set to Pakistan. Temu's own home page carries a security reminder that it will not ask for extra fees by SMS or email, and its Pakistan footer has a "Report suspicious activity" page.

Red flags to watch for:

  • A WhatsApp number or Facebook page calling itself "Temu Pakistan" or "Temu official" that takes orders by chat.
  • Requests to send money to a personal JazzCash, Easypaisa or bank account instead of paying inside the Temu app.
  • A message saying your Temu parcel is held and you must pay a "customs fee" or "release fee" through a link. Check the order in the app, and ask the courier for the customs assessment.
  • Links to look-alike domains that are not temu.com.
  • Prices well below the same item on the official Pakistan site, often with a small advance payment asked first.
  • Shops selling "Temu products" with their own delivery and refund terms. Some are genuine third-party stores, but they are not Temu, so Temu's 90-day return policy does not cover you.

Common mistakes Pakistani buyers make with Temu orders

  • Shopping on the wrong country site. Check that prices show in Rs before you build a cart.
  • Writing a vague address. Local couriers handle the last leg. Add a landmark, the correct city and a phone number you will answer.
  • Ignoring the import tax line. On bigger orders, read the checkout total, and remember customs can assess more.
  • Buying to resell. Temu's terms grant use of its services for personal, non-commercial purposes. For stock, buy from wholesale suppliers instead.
  • Missing the return window. Temu's Pakistan policy allows returns within 90 days of purchase, and you must send the package back within 14 days of requesting the return. Worn or washed clothing and some personal care items cannot be returned.
  • Ordering items that air rules restrict. Perfumes, aerosols and loose batteries often cannot fly. Check before you pay.
