Does Temu Deliver to Pakistan? (And Shein, DHgate, Banggood)

By importfromchina.shop teamUpdated October 3, 20266 min read
Quick answer

At the time of writing, Temu does not list Pakistan as a delivery country and Shein does not deliver to Pakistan either. AliExpress, DHgate and Banggood do ship to Pakistan, slowly. For items you can only find on Temu, Shein, Taobao or 1688, the practical route is a sourcing service that buys the item in China and delivers it to your door.

Why doesn't Temu ship to Pakistan?

Temu expands country by country and sets up payments, logistics and customs handling for each. Pakistan has not been added. When you open Temu from Pakistan, you either cannot select Pakistan as the delivery country or the app redirects you to another region. VPN tricks let you browse but not check out to a Pakistani address.

Can I get Temu or Shein items anyway?

Yes, because almost everything on Temu and Shein is sourced from the same Chinese suppliers who sell on 1688 and Taobao. A sourcing service can usually find the identical item there. The process is:

  1. Send the Temu or Shein link, or a screenshot of the item.
  2. The service finds the same product on a Chinese platform and gives you one price in rupees, delivered.
  3. You pay once, before it ships. It arrives in 7 to 15 days by air.

This also avoids the long post delivery times of AliExpress and the duty surprise at the door. See how to buy from Taobao in Pakistan.

Is DHgate safe for Pakistani buyers?

DHgate is a marketplace of small Chinese sellers aimed at international buyers, with buyer protection similar to AliExpress. It is known for a high share of replica and branded-lookalike goods. Those are seized by Pakistani customs, so only use DHgate for unbranded items. Delivery and payment issues are the same as AliExpress.

What about Banggood, Geekbuying and Gearbest?

Banggood ships to Pakistan and specialises in gadgets, tools and RC hobby items. Geekbuying is similar. Gearbest closed. These sites are fine for electronics, but lithium-battery products often ship by slow surface routes and can be held by customs, and duty is still your problem on arrival.

Which is the best option for one item?

  • Cheap, under PKR 2,000, not urgent, not electronics: AliExpress direct, if your card works.
  • Anything else, or anything from Temu, Shein, Taobao or 1688: a single-item sourcing service. One price, paid once, delivered duty-paid in 7 to 15 days.

Frequently asked questions

Is Temu available in Pakistan?

Not at the time of writing. Temu does not offer Pakistan as a delivery country. You can browse with a VPN but cannot check out to a Pakistani address.

Does Shein deliver to Pakistan?

No. Shein does not ship to Pakistan. Shein-style items are available through sourcing services that buy from the same Chinese suppliers.

Does AliExpress deliver to Pakistan?

Yes, most listings ship to Pakistan, typically in 25 to 60 days by post. Customs duty may be charged on arrival.

Does DHgate ship to Pakistan?

Yes. Delivery takes 20 to 45 days. Avoid branded or replica goods, which customs seizes.

How can I buy from Temu in Pakistan?

Send the Temu link to a sourcing service. They find the same item on 1688 or Taobao and deliver it to your door for one price.
