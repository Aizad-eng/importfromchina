How to Buy From Tmall in Pakistan

By Muhammad Shamshad AslamUpdated November 12, 20268 min read
Quick answer

You cannot have a Tmall order delivered straight to Pakistan, because Tmall checkout needs a Taobao account and accepts only a Mainland China delivery address. You can still buy from Tmall: either someone in China orders it for you and forwards it, or a buying service purchases it to its Chinese address and ships it to Pakistan for you.

New to importing? Read our step-by-step guide on how to import from China to Pakistan.

Can you buy from Tmall in Pakistan?

Yes, but not by ordering to a Pakistani address. Tmall is a shopping site for buyers in China, and its checkout is built around a Chinese delivery address. You can browse it from Pakistan, find the product and even pay with a foreign card, but the parcel has to go to an address in Mainland China first. From there it needs a separate shipment to Pakistan.

We found this out first hand. In October 2026 we ordered a Xiehe vitamin E and C hand cream set from the official 协和旗舰店 (Xiehe flagship store) on Tmall. Checkout asked for a Taobao login. The address form accepted only a Mainland China address, with dropdowns for province, city and district and a phone number starting +86. Payment offered two choices: Renminbi through Alipay, or foreign currency by card.

So the real question is not whether Tmall will sell to you, but how the item gets from a Chinese address to your door. This guide covers that. For the bigger picture of duty, shipping and timing, read how to import from China to Pakistan.

What is Tmall, and how is it different from Taobao?

Tmall is Alibaba Group's business-to-consumer shopping site in China, where registered businesses and brands sell to Chinese shoppers. Taobao, also owned by Alibaba, is a marketplace where small sellers and individuals list items. Tmall began in 2008 as Taobao Mall, a section inside Taobao, became Tmall in 2010 and was split off as a separate Alibaba business in 2011, according to its Wikipedia history.

Why do people in Pakistan want to buy from Tmall?

Mostly for genuine branded products made for the Chinese market that are hard to find in Pakistan. Chinese skincare and cosmetic brands, domestic appliance brands, spare parts for Chinese-market devices and brand accessories are common examples. Tmall's home page promotes a genuine-goods guarantee and a 7-day no-reason return service, both aimed at Chinese shoppers.

The bigger attraction is trust. On Taobao, many sellers offer the same branded item, and some are copies. On Tmall, a brand's official flagship store is a single, identifiable shop, which makes it easier to know who you are buying from.

The Xiehe hand cream set we ordered is a typical case. It is a Chinese brand product sold mainly to Chinese shoppers, so the obvious place to buy a genuine one is the brand's own flagship store. A copy from an unknown seller may cost less, but there is no way to know what is inside the tube. For anything you put on your skin or rely on to work, buying from the brand's own store is worth the extra step.

What does a Tmall flagship store mean?

A flagship store (旗舰店) on Tmall is a store run by the brand itself or by a company the brand has authorised. A 2021 CBNData explainer on Tmall rules says opening a brand flagship store requires a trademark registration certificate or an application acceptance notice, that a third-party operator needs an exclusive authorisation from the rights holder, and that one brand can open only one flagship store in the same category.

When you look at a Tmall listing, check these three things:

  • The store name ends in 旗舰店. 专卖店 and 专营店 are other Tmall store types, not the brand's own flagship store.
  • The store name matches the brand. 协和旗舰店 is the Xiehe flagship store. A name that adds extra words may be a different shop.
  • The listing is from that store. Open the store page from the listing and check it is the same shop.

Our guide to spotting fake listings on AliExpress and Taobao has more on reading store badges.

What happens when you try to check out on Tmall from Pakistan?

You reach the address step and stop, unless you have a Chinese address to give. These are the steps as we saw them in October 2026:

  1. Login. Tmall asked us to sign in with a Taobao account before checkout.
  2. Address. The form had dropdowns for province, city and district inside Mainland China and a +86 phone field. There was no option for Pakistan.
  3. Payment. Two options appeared: pay in Renminbi with Alipay, or pay in foreign currency by card.

The card option is useful, because it means a foreign Visa or Mastercard may be accepted. But a card does not solve the address problem. The seller will only ship to the Chinese address on the order.

Even with a Chinese address, there are two more steps before the item reaches you. Someone has to receive the parcel in China and send it abroad, and the export shipment needs its own label, carrier and customs declaration. Tmall stores sell to Chinese addresses, so asking the store itself to post the item to Pakistan is unlikely to work.

Do you pay customs on a Tmall item in Pakistan?

Usually yes. Under section 19C of the Customs Act, post and courier parcels worth Rs 1,000 or less pay no duties or taxes. Most Tmall items worth importing are above that, and then customs charges duty and taxes on the whole value. Cosmetics, medicines and supplements can need extra checks, so confirm the rules for your item before you buy. Health supplements in particular may fall under DRAP rules.

Is Tmall worth it for one cheap item?

Often not. Shipping one item from China to Pakistan usually costs Rs 5,000 to 20,000 depending on its size and type, before any fee. A hand cream or a small accessory may cost far less than that. Tmall works best for items worth around Rs 10,000 or more, or several different single items sent together so the shipping is shared.

What should you check before asking anyone to buy from Tmall for you?

  • The exact listing link from the official store, not a screenshot.
  • The variant: size, scent, colour, version or plug type.
  • Whether the product is liquid, aerosol or contains a battery, since some items are restricted on flights.
  • Whether instructions or apps are only in Chinese.
  • Whether a Chinese warranty can be used from Pakistan. Usually it cannot.
  • For cosmetics and food items, the shelf life. Ask the seller for the production or expiry date if the listing does not show it, since international shipping adds weeks.
  • Whether labels and ingredient lists are only in Chinese. Use a translation app on the listing photos before you buy.

Frequently asked questions

Does Tmall ship to Pakistan?

Not when we checked in October 2026. Tmall's checkout accepted only a Mainland China address, so an item must go to a Chinese address first and be shipped on from there.

Can I use my Taobao account on Tmall?

Yes. Tmall checkout asks you to sign in with a Taobao account, and one account works for both sites.

Can I pay on Tmall with a Pakistani card?

Tmall offered payment in foreign currency by card as well as Renminbi through Alipay. A card may work, but the order still has to ship to a Chinese address.

Is Tmall more genuine than Taobao?

Official flagship stores on Tmall are run or authorised by the brand, which makes them more reliable for branded goods than random Taobao sellers.

What is Tmall Global?

Tmall Global is Alibaba's channel for foreign brands selling into China. It does not help buyers outside China.
