What is the AYN Thor and who is it for?

The Thor is an Android handheld gaming device from AYN, a Chinese handheld maker. It folds like a clamshell. The top half holds a 6-inch AMOLED touchscreen, and the bottom half holds the controls with a second, 3.92-inch AMOLED touchscreen between them.

It suits you if you:

  • Want a powerful Android handheld with a Snapdragon chip and physical controls.
  • Like the idea of a dual-screen layout.
  • Are willing to wait for a pre-order batch rather than buy from stock.

It does not suit you if you need the device next week, or want something that slips into a trouser pocket. Liliputing lists it at 380g and 150 x 94 x 25.6mm.

How do AYN's pre-order batches work?

AYN sells the Thor in numbered pre-order batches. Each batch has its own shipping start, and AYN's product page names the month each batch begins shipping. You pay when you order, then AYN builds and ships that batch on its own timetable. Retro Dodo reported in mid-2026 that AYN's waits were running at two to three months for one batch, and linked the delay to long lead times on screens.

What this means for you:

  • The order is placed in whichever batch is open when you buy.
  • Dispatch follows AYN's batch schedule, not ours. AYN decides when your unit leaves China.
  • The usual 7 to 15 days to your door start only after AYN dispatches. So total time is the batch wait plus 7 to 15 days.
  • Some versions can be out of stock. AYN's page showed the Lite in black as backordered when we checked.

Does the Thor work in Pakistan, and does it need PTA approval?

It is a Wi-Fi and Bluetooth device. Neither AYN's page nor Liliputing lists a SIM slot or mobile network, so the DIRBS phone registration process is not the usual concern. Check current PTA guidance if you are unsure, because rules change. AYN's page does not mention a charger, so plan to use a good USB-C charger rated 100V to 240V, which covers Pakistan's 220V to 240V supply.

The ports are standard. Liliputing lists a USB 3.1 Type-C port, a 3.5mm headphone jack and a microSD reader on every version, so wired headphones and a locally bought microSD card work without adapters. Bluetooth headphones and controllers pair as on any Android device. Home Wi-Fi works normally, and the Base, Pro and Max support Wi-Fi 7 if your router has it, though they also work with older routers.

What should you check when the Thor arrives?

  1. Version. Go to Settings, then About, and confirm the chip, memory and storage match what you ordered.
  2. Both screens. Check each AMOLED screen on plain white and black backgrounds, and touch every corner of both.
  3. Hinge. Open and close it a few times. It should feel even, with no creaking or grinding.
  4. Controls. Press every button and rotate both Hall sticks fully.
  5. Charging and ports. Charge over USB-C, plug in headphones, and insert a microSD card if you have one.
  6. Warranty details. Keep the order confirmation. AYN states a one-year warranty.
