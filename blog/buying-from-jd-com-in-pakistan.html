Can You Buy From JD.com in Pakistan?

By Muhammad Shamshad AslamUpdated October 23, 20269 min read
Quick answer

JD is still worth knowing: its self-operated listings are a trusted source of genuine electronics, so a buyer in China can order for you and forward the item.

Can you buy from JD.com in Pakistan?

Not directly. When we checked in October 2026, we found no JD service that sells to a Pakistani address. JD.com takes orders for delivery inside China, its overseas shipping arm names a short list of countries that does not include Pakistan, and its international shop, Joybuy, is only for Europe.

You can still get a JD item. Someone in China orders it to a Chinese address and sends it to Pakistan by air. That is worth doing for the right product, because JD is one of the better places in China to buy branded electronics and appliances from a source you can trust.

What is JD.com?

JD.com, known in China as Jingdong (京东), is one of China's largest online retailers. It was founded in 1998 by Liu Qiangdong, is based in Beijing, and is listed on Nasdaq and the Hong Kong Stock Exchange. Reports on its 2025 results put its annual active customers above 700 million.

What makes JD different from Taobao is how it sells. Much of what is on JD is 自营, meaning self-operated. JD buys that stock, keeps it in its own warehouses, sells it directly and issues the invoice. The rest of the site is third-party stores that use JD's platform and ship from their own locations. JD has run its own delivery network since 2007, which is why it is known in China for fast delivery.

Does JD.com or JD Global ship to Pakistan?

No direct option to Pakistan that we could find. JD's overseas consumer shipping is called JD Global Sales (京东全球售). JD's own announcements in 2024 described it as serving Chinese consumers abroad, with direct shipping for customers in the US, Japan and Singapore, and an expansion to Malaysia and Thailand in November 2024. Hong Kong, Macao and Taiwan have a similar service.

The rules are narrow even there. JD's announcements say the service covers self-operated products bought through the JD app and shipped from JD's warehouses in China. Third-party store items generally are not included. We found older third-party guides claiming wider coverage, but none named Pakistan, and none were recent. Before relying on anything, open the JD app, add a product and see whether Pakistan appears as an address option.

What about Joybuy and JD Express?

Neither one lets you shop on JD from Pakistan. They are separate businesses that people often mix up with JD.com.

  • Joybuy is JD's European online shop. It launched in March 2026 in the UK, Germany, France, the Netherlands, Belgium and Luxembourg, and it delivers from warehouses in Europe. It does not serve Pakistan.
  • JD Express International is JD Logistics' courier service. In late 2024 it announced delivery to 11 more Asian countries, and Pakistan was on that list. That means a parcel sent by JD Express can be delivered in Pakistan. It does not mean JD.com sells to Pakistani addresses.
  • JD Worldwide is the reverse direction: a platform for foreign brands to sell into China.

So if someone tells you "JD delivers to Pakistan", ask which part of JD they mean.

What is JD.com good for?

Genuine branded electronics and appliances, mostly. Because self-operated stock is bought by JD from brands or their distributors and sold under JD's own name, it is widely trusted in China for authenticity. That matters for things where fakes are common, such as phones, laptops, storage, chargers, headphones and small appliances.

  • Phones, laptops and tablets. Where a genuine unit from a known source matters more than the lowest price.
  • Memory cards, SSDs and chargers. Items where a fake can look identical to the real one.
  • Models sold only in China. Some Chinese-market versions never reach Daraz or AliExpress.
  • Clear stock and listings. A self-operated listing is one product with set variants, not a pile of sellers using the same photo.

JD is less useful for cheap generic goods, clothes and hobby items, where Taobao and 1688 have far more choice at lower prices.

How do you tell a JD self-operated listing?

Look for 京东自营 (JD self-operated) on the product or the store name. Official brand stores often include 官方旗舰店 (official flagship store) in their name. Third-party stores carry the shop's own name instead. Self-operated orders ship from JD warehouses and third-party orders ship from the merchant, so the delivery details on the listing also give you a clue.

If you send someone a JD link to buy for you, send the full product link, not a screenshot, and say which colour, storage or bundle you want. Our guide on copying a product link from Chinese apps shows how to do this from the JD app.

What should you check before importing a JD item to Pakistan?

Check that the product will work and be supported here, because a genuine item can still be the wrong version.

  • Region version. A Chinese-market phone, tablet or laptop may have Chinese software, a different keyboard layout or missing apps. Our guide on gadgets that work differently in Pakistan covers the common cases.
  • Warranty. A JD warranty is a Chinese-market warranty. Assume you have no local warranty unless the brand says otherwise. See warranty and after-sales when importing from China.
  • PTA for phones. Any phone needs PTA registration in Pakistan, and the tax depends on the model.
  • Batteries and flights. Devices with built-in batteries usually fly. Loose batteries and power banks are commonly restricted.
  • Plugs. Appliances come with Chinese plugs, so check the plug and power rating against what you will use.

How do you get one JD item to Pakistan?

Have someone in China buy it and forward it. These are the steps.

  1. Find the listing on JD. Prefer a 京东自营 or official flagship store listing.
  2. Note the exact variant. Colour, storage, model number and bundle.
  3. Compare once. Check whether the same model is on Daraz or AliExpress at a sensible price. If it is, buying locally may be simpler.
  4. Send the link for one price. Ask for a total in rupees delivered to your door, with nothing left to pay on arrival.
  5. Pay once, then wait for dispatch. JD delivers to the Chinese address, and it is known for same-day or next-day delivery in major Chinese cities. The item is then flown to Pakistan and cleared through customs.

Common mistakes when buying from JD for Pakistan

  • Assuming every JD listing is self-operated. Third-party stores sit on the same site. Check for 京东自营.
  • Using a VPN to force a foreign address. If Pakistan is not an option, it is not an option.
  • Confusing JD Express with JD.com. A courier delivering to Pakistan does not make the shop sell to Pakistan.
  • Buying cheap items from JD. Shipping one item from China usually costs Rs 5,000 to 20,000, so JD makes sense for valuable things, not a phone case.
  • Expecting the Chinese warranty to work here. Plan as if it does not.
