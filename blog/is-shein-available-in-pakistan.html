Yes. Shein is available in Pakistan through its global website and app, though it has no separate Pakistan site. Is Shein available in Pakistan?

Yes, Shein is available in Pakistan. You buy through shein.com or the Shein app with your location set to Pakistan, and Shein ships the order from its global warehouses to your address. When we opened Shein's Shipping Info page in October 2026 with the location set to Pakistan, it showed a standard shipping option with a delivery window and price for Pakistan.

Does Shein have a Pakistan website, app or shop?

No, Shein has no separate Pakistan website. Pakistan is served by the global site and the same Shein app used in other countries. When we checked in October 2026, prices on the site showed in US dollars, and the currency list on Shein's payment page did not include Pakistani rupees.

We found no official Shein shop or Pakistan office on Shein's own pages. Facebook pages, Instagram accounts and WhatsApp numbers named "Shein Pakistan" are independent resellers. Some deliver what they promise, but they set their own prices and return rules, and Shein's policies do not protect you when you pay them. If a page asks you to send money to a personal JazzCash, Easypaisa or bank account, you are not buying from Shein.

Is Shein banned in Pakistan?

No, Shein is not banned in Pakistan as far as Shein's own pages show. Its Shipping Info page lists the countries where it has suspended shipments because of logistics restrictions. In October 2026 that list named Algeria, Bangladesh, French Guiana, Monaco, Samoa, San Marino, Tajikistan, Somalia, the British Virgin Islands, French Polynesia, North Macedonia, Guam and Timor-Leste. Pakistan was not on it.

The same page names Pakistan, with Nigeria, in a link to Shein's On-Time Delivery Service terms for those countries. Shipping lists change, so check the page again if you read this months later.

How long does Shein take to deliver to Pakistan, and what does shipping cost?

Shein's standard shipping to Pakistan takes 7 to 11 business days after 1 to 3 days of processing, when we checked in October 2026. For an order placed on 23 October 2026, the page estimated arrival between 3 and 9 November 2026.

The fee depends on your order value:

  • Below US$14.43: standard shipping cost US$1.08.
  • Above US$14.43: standard shipping was free.

Shein adds three warnings on the same page. Processing can be delayed by 2 to 4 days while it changes its delivery process. Pre-order and customised items take longer. Flights and weather can push the date back. So treat the window as a guide and use the tracking page for the current date.

Shein also runs an On-Time Delivery Service. If you tick the On-Time Delivery box at checkout and the order misses its estimated date, its terms say Shein may give you 100 Shein Points, worth one US dollar, valid for 30 days. The terms exclude delays caused by a buyer not paying taxes or duties on time.

Does Shein deliver to every city in Pakistan?

Not necessarily. Shein delivers to most addresses, but its Shipping Info page says delivery is unavailable in some areas because of logistics restrictions, and that if you cannot type your address during checkout, delivery is not supported there.

If that happens, use an address in the nearest larger city, such as a relative's home or your workplace. Whatever address you use, write it in full with a landmark and a mobile number you answer. Shein's terms also say consignee names, addresses and payer names must be valid for customs, and that Shein will not compensate you if missing or wrong details stop the delivery. Use your real name as on your CNIC.

How do you pay for Shein from Pakistan?

You can pay Shein from Pakistan with JazzCash, Easypaisa or a debit or credit card. Shein's payment page, when we read it in October 2026, had a section for each wallet.

  1. JazzCash: select JazzCash on the order page, open your JazzCash wallet, go to "payment requests" and enter your MPIN to approve.
  2. Easypaisa: select Easypaisa on the order page, open your Easypaisa wallet, go to "my approval" and tap accept.
  3. Card: Visa or Mastercard debit and credit cards work if international online payments are switched on in your banking app. Your bank's exchange rate and card charges apply, because prices are in dollars.

Shein's payment page also describes PayPal, but PayPal does not offer accounts to people living in Pakistan, so ignore that option. Local-only PayPak cards do not work on foreign sites either.

Cash on delivery was not listed for Pakistan on Shein's payment page when we checked. Shein's delivery terms mention cash on delivery orders in general, so check your own checkout screen, but plan to pay upfront. A Facebook page offering "Shein cash on delivery" in Pakistan is a reseller.

Do you pay customs duty on Shein orders in Pakistan?

Sometimes. It depends on the value of the parcel. Section 19C of the Customs Act, as amended by the Finance Act 2025, says no duties and taxes are demanded where goods imported through post or courier are worth Rs 1,000 or less, subject to FBR conditions. Above that, duty and taxes can apply.

Shein's terms put that responsibility on you. They say you authorise Shein and its agents to make customs declarations in your name, and that as the importer you must comply with the laws of your own country. We did not find a Pakistan-specific duty estimate on Shein's checkout pages, so expect any duty assessed to be collected before or at delivery.

A few practical points:

  • A Shein order of several items often goes over Rs 1,000, so do not assume it is duty-free.
  • Splitting one order into several small ones to stay under the limit is risky, and each small order below US$14.43 pays its own shipping fee.
  • If a courier asks for duty, ask for the customs assessment document before you pay.

Can you return Shein items from Pakistan?

Yes, but returns from Pakistan cost you money. Shein's Return Policy, when we read it in October 2026, accepted returns within 30 days of receipt for most items in new condition. The only return method listed was Self-Return, where you pay your own courier and Shein does not reimburse the cost.

  • Not returnable: bodysuits, swimwear, lingerie, jewellery, beauty items, underwear, party and DIY supplies, pet supplies, cosmetics with a broken seal, customised items and anything marked non-returnable.
  • Clothes: must be unworn and unwashed, with original tags and any hygiene sticker intact.
  • Electronics: phones, laptops, tablets and similar items must come back sealed and not activated.
  • Faulty or damaged items: contact customer service within 180 days of the order date.
  • Refunds: to your Shein Wallet or original payment account. Shein says the bank part usually takes 1 to 10 business days.

Because postage to an overseas warehouse can cost more than a cheap top, size carefully and avoid returns where you can. Temu's Pakistan policy is more generous on this point. Our Shein vs Temu comparison sets the two side by side.

What if your Shein parcel is late or missing?

Contact Shein customer service, and watch the deadlines. Shein's shipping page says that if the parcel has not arrived within the estimated time, you should contact customer service within 90 days of the payment date. If tracking says delivered but you have nothing, or items are missing or wrong, the limit is 60 days from the delivery date shown in tracking.

Take pictures of the outer package and label as soon as a parcel arrives, before opening it. If something is missing, those pictures help your claim.

Common mistakes when ordering Shein in Pakistan

  • Paying a "Shein Pakistan" page. Order only on shein.com or the Shein app.
  • Using a card with international payments off. Switch it on first, or pay with JazzCash or Easypaisa.
  • Ordering your usual local size. Measure and use the size chart in centimetres.
  • Forgetting the shipping threshold. Cancelling one item can take an order back under US$14.43.
  • Buying non-returnable items you are unsure about. Swimwear, lingerie, jewellery and beauty items cannot go back.
  • Missing claim deadlines. 90 days from payment for non-arrival, 60 days from delivery for missing items.
