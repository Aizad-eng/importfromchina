Xiaomi China Version vs Global Version: What Pakistani Buyers Should Know

By Muhammad Shamshad AslamUpdated October 23, 202610 min read
Quick answer

The China version of a Xiaomi product is built for mainland China and the global version for everywhere else. China-version phones ship without Google Play, smart home devices are tied to the mainland China server, and Xiaomi Pakistan's warranty covers only products bought in Pakistan. For use in Pakistan, buy the global version unless you know exactly what you are giving up.

What is the difference between Xiaomi's China and global versions?

The China version is built for the mainland Chinese market and the global version for everyone else, and the difference is mostly software, services and support rather than hardware. A China-version phone runs a China build of HyperOS without Google services. A China-version smart home device is set up to work with Xiaomi's mainland China server. Both come with Chinese plugs, and neither is covered by Xiaomi's Pakistan warranty.

Sellers on Taobao, 1688 and JD mostly sell the China version, because that is what Chinese shoppers buy. AliExpress and some Alibaba sellers offer both, with the global version labelled and usually priced a little higher. The China version is often cheaper, which is why it tempts buyers, but the saving only holds if the trade-offs below do not matter for what you are buying.

Will a China-version Xiaomi phone have Google Play?

No. HyperOS China builds ship without Google services because they are restricted in mainland China, and Xiaomi's own apps handle search, messages, calls, contacts and the voice assistant instead. A comparison of the two HyperOS builds by Androidphoria found the features themselves almost identical, but the China build offers only Chinese variants and English as languages and carries more preinstalled apps.

Many apps people use daily in Pakistan are commonly installed from the Play Store, and some rely on Google services for sign-in or notifications, so a China ROM phone can be awkward to live with here. Unofficial ways to add Google services exist but can break after updates. Our guide to gadgets that work differently in Pakistan explains how to read the HyperOS version code to tell which build a phone runs.

Is it safe to buy a China phone flashed with a global ROM?

It has become riskier. Some sellers take China-market phones and install a modified global ROM so they look like global units. In January 2026, tech sites including Gizchina reported that the HyperOS 3 (Android 16) update added a check that compares the software region with the hardware's intended market, and that converted phones were stuck in a boot loop after updating. The same coverage said Xiaomi treats these phones as unofficially modified and would not release a fix.

So a listing that says "global ROM" on a phone that is really a China unit is not the same as a true global version. Ask the seller in writing whether the phone is a genuine global model or a China model with a global ROM. Once it arrives, you can enter the IMEI or serial number on Xiaomi's Product Authentication page at mi.com/global/verify. A genuine global model with a Pakistan-friendly band list is the version to aim for. The full phone picture is in our guide to importing a mobile phone from China and PTA.

Why do Xiaomi smart home devices care about the server region?

Because Xiaomi runs separate cloud regions, and a device generally works only with the region it was sold for. Xiaomi's own support article on devices that cannot be found says it may be because the device is not available in your region, and asks you to make sure the setting under Profile, Settings, Region in the Xiaomi Home app matches the region where the device was bought. Its Malaysia support page adds that a mismatch can stop certain devices from being added.

For a China-version robot vacuum, air purifier, camera or smart plug, that means setting your Xiaomi Home region to Mainland China to add it, while any global-version devices you own belong to the region where they were bought.

There is a newer reason to be careful. XimiTime and Mundo Xiaomi reported in June 2026 that Xiaomi Home version 11.6.602.4004 on Google Play shows a notice saying future non-mainland releases will no longer support mainland China region services, and that users should move to the mainland China version of the app. No end date was given. In practice, a China-version smart device bought today may need the Chinese edition of the app to keep working, which is harder to install and use outside China. For anything app-controlled, the global version is the safer buy.

Does the Xiaomi warranty in Pakistan cover an imported product?

No. Xiaomi Pakistan's warranty page says the limited warranty is valid only in Pakistan and that the product is not eligible for international warranty service, with exceptions for some specific models. It lists one year for phones and tablets and six months for the original battery, adapter and accessories, and service needs the model, serial number or IMEI and the original invoice.

The same page excludes damage caused by improper voltage or power supply and by repairs from anyone other than Xiaomi or its authorised centres. For an imported Xiaomi product, plan on the seller's own promise, the platform's buyer protection and paid repair locally. Our guide to warranty and after-sales when importing from China covers what that looks like in practice.

Does a Xiaomi phone from China need PTA registration?

Yes. Every phone with a SIM used on a Pakistani network is checked against PTA's DIRBS system, whether it is a China or global version. An unregistered phone works for a grace period and is then blocked from calls and mobile data. PTA's DIRBS site lets you check any IMEI by sending it by SMS to 8484, and you find the IMEI by dialling *#06#.

The tax depends on the exact model and changes with policy, so look up the current figure on PTA's channels before you buy. On mid-range and flagship phones it often closes most of the price gap between a China import and a local purchase. PTA describes DIRBS as covering devices with SIM or IMEI functionality, so a Xiaomi tablet or band without a SIM is outside it.

Will the plug and voltage work in Pakistan?

The voltage usually will and the plug usually will not. Pakistan runs at 230V 50Hz with type C and D sockets. China runs at about 220 to 230V 50Hz with type A and I plugs, so a China-version Xiaomi device runs on Pakistani power but needs a plug adapter or a local cable.

For phone chargers, check for a 100 to 240V rating on the label, which almost all have. For high-power appliances such as kettles, rice cookers or heaters, use a good fixed adapter or change the plug properly rather than relying on a loose travel adapter, and never use a US-market 110V version without a step-down converter. Xiaomi's own warranty excludes damage from improper voltage, so this is worth getting right.

How do you check which version a listing is before you buy?

  1. Read the title. Look for "Global Version", "International Version" or "CN Version". No mention usually means China version.
  2. Look at the language list. Chinese and English only points to a China build.
  3. Check the plug in the listing pictures. Angled flat pins (type I) mean a China-market unit.
  4. Ask about the app region for any smart home device: which region does it work with in Xiaomi Home?
  5. For phones, ask whether it is a genuine global model or a China model with a global ROM, and get the answer in writing.
  6. Keep the order page and chat in case the version that arrives is not the one listed.

Common mistakes when buying Xiaomi from China

  • Assuming "global ROM" means global version. Converted phones can fail after HyperOS updates.
  • Buying a China-version smart device for a global Xiaomi Home account. It may not appear in the app at all.
  • Expecting Xiaomi Pakistan to repair an import. Its warranty covers only products bought in Pakistan.
  • Forgetting PTA. Budget for the model's tax before comparing prices.
  • Ordering a power bank or electric scooter without asking. Large batteries need a separate shipping check before you order.

Frequently asked questions

Does a China-version Xiaomi phone have Google Play?

No. HyperOS China builds ship without Google services and use Xiaomi's own apps instead. Unofficial ways to add Google services exist but can break after updates.

Can I use a China-version Xiaomi smart device in Pakistan?

Often, but only with the Xiaomi Home account region set to Mainland China. Xiaomi says the app region should match where the device was bought, and the Google Play app is dropping China region support.

Does Xiaomi Pakistan give warranty on phones bought in China?

No. Xiaomi Pakistan's warranty page says its warranty is valid only in Pakistan and products are not eligible for international warranty, apart from some specific models.

Do I pay PTA tax on a Xiaomi phone imported from China?

Yes, if you use it on a Pakistani network. Both China and global versions need DIRBS registration, and the tax depends on the exact model.

Is a China Xiaomi phone with a global ROM the same as a global version?

No. Reports in January 2026 said HyperOS 3 checks the hardware region, and converted China phones got stuck in a boot loop. Buy a genuine global model.

Will a China-version Xiaomi charger work in Pakistan?

Usually yes on voltage, since most chargers are rated 100 to 240V, but the Chinese type A or I plug needs an adapter for Pakistani C and D sockets.
