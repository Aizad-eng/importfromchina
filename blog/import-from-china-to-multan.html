Can I get one item from China delivered in Multan?

Yes. A single item for your home, farm or work can come from China to a Multan address with no company, licence or customs agent of your own. The parcel travels by air, is cleared through Pakistan Customs, and a courier delivers it in the city. You do not go to the airport or any customs office.

Which airport serves Multan, and does it take international cargo?

Multan International Airport, on the western side of the city in the Multan Cantonment area, is the city's airport, and it does handle cargo. It is run by the Pakistan Airports Authority. Its Wikipedia page describes a cargo complex with a capacity of 10,000 metric tonnes a year, and Pakistan Civil Aviation Authority figures quoted there put cargo handled at 7,276 metric tons for July 2024 to June 2025.

The international routes listed there go to the Gulf, Oman, Qatar and Saudi Arabia. None went to China when we checked in October 2026. For a parcel from China, that means it enters Pakistan at another airport and reaches Multan through the courier network. You do not need to arrange that, and it is already inside the price we quote.

How does a parcel get from China to a Multan address?

In four stages after you order, none of which need you.

  1. The seller in China dispatches the item to the shipping line's warehouse.
  2. It flies to Pakistan with other parcels.
  3. Pakistan Customs assesses it. With our service, duty and taxes are part of the one price you already paid.
  4. A courier carries it to Multan and delivers it. Couriers such as TCS and Leopards have long described their networks as covering all cities and major towns of Pakistan.

Tracking can go quiet between China and Pakistani customs. That is normal. How to track a parcel from China explains what each status means.

What single items suit farm and agriculture work around Multan?

Tools, meters and spare parts are the obvious fit. The district government's website says more than 80 percent of Multan's population earns its living from agriculture in one way or another, and it names cotton and mangoes as the district's best known crops, with the Central Cotton Research Institute based in the city. Some examples of single items that match that kind of work:

  • A refractometer, the handheld meter that reads sugar content in fruit juice in degrees Brix.
  • A soil moisture or soil pH meter.
  • A grain moisture meter.
  • Replacement nozzles, seals or a pump head for a sprayer, matched to the model.
  • A controller or monitor for a solar water pump. Our guide on importing a single solar panel or inverter covers the larger solar items.

These are examples of what one item can be, not a statement about demand. We handle one item, or a few different single items together. If a farm or dealer needs the same item in quantity, that is a commercial import for a cargo company. We do not do bulk or resale.

Does Multan's summer heat matter for a parcel?

It can, for items with batteries. Wikipedia's climate data for Multan describes a hot desert climate, with May and June heat waves pushing temperatures close to 50 °C. A parcel left in a parked car, on a rooftop or outside a gate in that heat is not good for lithium batteries, screens or adhesives.

  • Choose an address where someone is in during the day in summer.
  • Answer the courier's call so the parcel is not left at a gate.
  • Let a device cool to room temperature before charging it if it has been in the sun.

Battery rules for air shipping are separate. Devices with a built-in battery usually fly, but loose batteries and power banks have tighter rules. See lithium battery rules for shipping from China before ordering one.

How should I write a Multan address the courier can find?

Give the colony or town, the street and house number, a landmark and a phone that answers. Specific tips for Multan:

  • Name the residential area clearly, for example Gulgasht Colony, Shah Rukn-e-Alam, Shah Faisal Colony, Zakariya Town or Multan Cantt.
  • Add a landmark such as a known chowk, mosque, school or hospital.
  • Multan Cantt addresses may sit behind checkpoints. Give a gate, reception or meeting point if couriers are not allowed further in.
  • If the item is for a farm outside the city, a city address where someone can receive it is usually simpler than a village address with no house number.
  • Couriers usually call before delivering, so keep the number switched on.

Is it worth importing one item to Multan?

It is worth it for items that are not sold locally, or cost far more locally, and are worth around Rs 10,000 or more. Shipping from China usually costs Rs 5,000 to 20,000 depending on the size and type of item, plus our fee. For something cheap and common, a local shop or Daraz is usually the better choice.

Check the obvious things first: the voltage and plug, the model number for parts, and whether the item is restricted. Medicines, drones and weapon lookalikes have their own rules.
