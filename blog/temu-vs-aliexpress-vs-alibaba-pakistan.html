Temu vs AliExpress vs Alibaba: Which Should You Use From Pakistan?

By Muhammad Shamshad AslamUpdated October 17, 20269 min read
Quick answer

For most people in Pakistan buying something cheap for themselves, Temu is the easiest of the three: a Pakistan site in rupees, JazzCash and Easypaisa, free standard shipping in 5 to 13 business days and 90-day returns. AliExpress has a much wider catalogue and official brand stores, but delivery is slower and you usually need an international card. Alibaba.com is built for businesses buying in quantity, so use it for one item only on Ready to Ship or MOQ 1 listings.

Temu, AliExpress or Alibaba: which should you use from Pakistan?

Short version: use Temu for cheap everyday items, AliExpress for a specific model, part or brand, and Alibaba.com for equipment or a sample from a manufacturer. All three delivered to Pakistan when we checked in October 2026, but they are built for different buyers and they fail in different ways.

The rest of this guide explains why, using what each platform's own pages said when we checked.

What is the real difference between Temu, AliExpress and Alibaba?

Two companies run these three sites. Temu belongs to PDD Holdings, the group that also runs Pinduoduo in China. AliExpress and Alibaba.com both belong to Alibaba Group, but they serve opposite kinds of buyer.

  • Temu is a consumer marketplace with a Pakistan site, rupee prices and its own delivery and return rules for Pakistan.
  • AliExpress is a consumer marketplace where independent sellers, and some official brand stores, sell single pieces to buyers worldwide. Each seller picks its shipping options.
  • Alibaba.com is a business-to-business marketplace. Suppliers list minimum order quantities and price ladders, and buyers pay through Trade Assurance.

So Temu and AliExpress compete for the same shopper. Alibaba.com is a different tool that sometimes overlaps when a supplier will sell one piece.

Which one delivers faster to Pakistan?

Temu publishes the clearest promise. Its Pakistan shipping page lists free standard shipping on all orders with a delivery window of 5 to 13 business days, after 1 to 3 days of processing. The estimate for each package appears on the order confirmation page after payment.

AliExpress has no single number, because every seller chooses its shipping options. A July 2026 guide from WorldFirst puts most AliExpress orders to Pakistan at 15 to 45 days, with faster couriers costing more. Look at the delivery date the listing shows for your address, not at reviews from other countries.

Alibaba.com can be the fastest of the three for one piece, because suppliers usually send single items by express courier. It is also the most expensive way to ship something small. Ask the supplier for the shipping cost to your city before you pay.

Which one is easiest to pay from Pakistan?

Temu, by a distance. Its Pakistan site shows JazzCash and Easypaisa logos alongside the usual cards, Apple Pay and Google Pay. If you have a mobile wallet, you can pay in rupees without touching your card's international settings. Cash on delivery was not shown.

On AliExpress, you will usually need a Visa or Mastercard with international online payments switched on. Many Pakistani cards ship with this off, so a first attempt often fails until you change it in your banking app. PayPak cards do not work on foreign sites, and PayPal is not available in Pakistan.

Alibaba.com's Trade Assurance page says orders can be paid by credit or debit card, digital wallets, direct bank transfer and, for eligible buyers, payment plans. For a one-piece order, a card with international use enabled is the practical option.

Which one has the best buyer protection and returns?

Temu has the clearest Pakistan policy. You can return items within 90 days of purchase, the first return from each order is free, and the courier collects the parcel from your door. You must ship it back within 14 days of requesting the return. Refunds to your original payment method can take 5 to 14 business days, up to 30 days depending on your bank or wallet, and Temu credit is faster. The original shipping fee and import tax are not refunded if the return is not Temu's fault.

AliExpress buyer protection lets you claim a refund if an item does not match its description or arrives damaged, reported within 15 days of receipt. Non-arrival is covered within the guaranteed delivery window shown on your order page. Where a return is needed, sending it back to China from Pakistan rarely makes sense for a cheap item.

Alibaba.com Trade Assurance holds your payment in escrow until you confirm the order arrived, and covers refunds for orders not shipped, lost, defective or incorrect. It is designed for commercial orders, so expect to show evidence. Our guide to Trade Assurance for Pakistani buyers covers how claims work.

Which one is cheapest?

It depends on what you count. For a small item delivered to your door, Temu often wins because shipping is free and the price already shows in rupees. AliExpress listings can be cheaper on the item itself, but add the shipping line at checkout, your bank's exchange rate and card charges, and any duty on arrival.

Alibaba.com usually has the lowest price per piece, but that price is for quantity. For one piece you pay the top tier of the price ladder and then courier shipping, so the total for a cheap item is often the highest of the three. It starts to make sense for higher-value equipment where the item price matters more than the freight.

Can you buy just one item on Alibaba.com?

Yes, on some listings. Filter for Ready to Ship products or look for a minimum order of 1 piece in the price table. If the table starts at 50 or 100 pieces, you cannot buy one from that listing, though you can message the supplier and ask for a sample at a sample price. Our guide on buying one item on Alibaba from Pakistan shows how to find these listings.

Before paying, confirm the voltage and plug for anything electrical. Pakistan uses 220 to 240V at 50Hz, while suppliers sometimes default to a US version.

Which should you pick for your item?

  1. Is it a cheap everyday item? Check Temu first. Free shipping, wallet payment and 90-day returns are hard to beat.
  2. Is it a specific model, part number or brand? Search AliExpress, and prefer the brand's official store.
  3. Is it equipment or a machine from a manufacturer? Look on Alibaba.com for a Ready to Ship or MOQ 1 listing and ask the supplier for courier cost to your city.
  4. Is it on none of them, or excluded from Pakistan? The same item is often on 1688 or Taobao, which need an agent.

Common mistakes when choosing between them

  • Using Alibaba.com like a shop. Most listings are wholesale. A price that looks low is usually the price at 500 pieces.
  • Reading AliExpress delivery reviews from Europe. Fast delivery and free returns run in named markets. Check the estimate for a Pakistani address.
  • Ignoring the duty question on expensive orders. Only parcels worth Rs 1,000 or less are duty-free under section 19C. Above that, see our guide to customs duty from China to Pakistan.
  • Buying to resell on Temu. Temu's terms are for personal use. For stock, use wholesale suppliers.
  • Paying a supplier outside the platform. On Alibaba.com, a payment outside Trade Assurance loses its protection.

Frequently asked questions

Is Temu better than AliExpress for people in Pakistan?

For cheap everyday items, usually yes, because Temu has a Pakistan site, free standard shipping, JazzCash and Easypaisa, and 90-day returns. AliExpress is better when you need a specific model, part or brand that Temu does not stock.

Can I buy a single item from Alibaba.com in Pakistan?

Yes, but only from listings with a minimum order of 1 piece or in the Ready to Ship section. Expect to pay courier shipping, which can cost as much as a cheap item.

Which is faster to Pakistan, Temu or AliExpress?

Temu lists 5 to 13 business days for free standard shipping after 1 to 3 days of processing. AliExpress depends on the seller's shipping option, and a 2026 guide puts most orders at 15 to 45 days.

Are Temu and AliExpress owned by the same company?

No. Temu belongs to PDD Holdings, which also runs Pinduoduo. AliExpress and Alibaba.com belong to Alibaba Group.

Why does my card keep getting declined on AliExpress in Pakistan?

Most often because international online payments are switched off on the card. Turn them on in your banking app, and note that PayPak cards do not work on foreign sites.

Do I pay customs duty on Temu, AliExpress or Alibaba orders?

Parcels worth Rs 1,000 or less are duty-free under section 19C. Above that, duty and taxes can apply on all three, and Temu's terms say any duty collected at checkout is only an estimate.
