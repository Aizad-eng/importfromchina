How Much Does It Cost to Import From China to Pakistan?

By Muhammad Shamshad AslamUpdated November 12, 20269 min read
Quick answer

Importing from China to Pakistan costs the item price plus shipping, plus duties and taxes that commonly add about a fifth to two thirds of the customs value at 2026-27 rates. Customs duty depends on the PCT code, then 18% sales tax and section 148 income tax stack on top. Shipping one item usually runs Rs 5,000 to 20,000.

New to importing? Read our step-by-step guide on how to import from China to Pakistan.

How much does it cost to import from China to Pakistan?

The total cost is the item price, plus shipping and insurance to Pakistan, plus duties and taxes, plus any clearing and delivery charges. The duties and taxes alone can range from nothing on a parcel worth Rs 1,000 or less to well over half the customs value on a high-duty item. This guide breaks each layer down and works through examples using the rates in force for 2026-27. If you are new to the process, start with our guide on how to import from China to Pakistan.

Two warnings before the numbers. First, customs decides the item's tariff code and the value it taxes, and it can use a valuation ruling instead of your invoice, so any figure you work out is an estimate. Second, rates change with budgets and SROs during the year. The rates below are the ones we found when we checked in November 2026.

What are all the cost layers?

There are seven layers, and customs only taxes the first three directly. Everything after the customs value is a percentage of what came before it.

Item priceWhat the seller charges, converted to rupeesSet by the seller
Shipping and insuranceChina delivery plus the international legCommonly Rs 5,000 to 20,000 for one item by air
Customs duty (CD)Percentage of the customs value0, 5, 10, 15 or 20%, some goods 25% or more

Sales taxOn value plus CD, ACD and RD18%
Income tax, section 148On value plus duties plus sales tax1%, 2% or 5.5% for filers, double for non-filers

On top of these sit clearing agent fees, port or terminal charges for cargo, local delivery and your bank's exchange rate and card charges.

How does customs decide the value it taxes?

Customs taxes the CIF value: the cost of the goods plus insurance and freight to Pakistan, converted to rupees. FBR publishes the exchange rates released by the National Bank of Pakistan for this purpose. If a valuation ruling sets a higher minimum value for your type of goods, customs can use that instead. Our guide on customs valuation rulings explains how that works.

The exchange rate is the one on the day customs assesses the goods, not the day you paid the seller, so a weaker rupee between the two dates raises the bill a little. Because freight is part of the value, shipping is taxed too. A Rs 15,000 shipping charge on a 20% duty item adds Rs 3,000 of customs duty before sales tax even starts.

Which rates apply in 2026-27?

The 2026-27 budget kept the customs duty slabs at 0, 5, 10, 15 and 20%, with higher rates on some goods, and cut the other duties. Profit reported on 1 July 2026 that SRO 1063(I)/2026 lowered additional customs duty and SRO 1064(I)/2026 capped regulatory duty at 20%, down from 50%, both from 1 July 2026. Sales tax stays at 18%.

For section 148 income tax, PkRevenue reported FBR's tax year 2027 rates as 1% for Part I goods, 2% for Part II and 5.5% for Part III, which covers most finished consumer goods, with commercial importers paying 3.5% and 6% on Parts II and III. The tax is charged on the import value plus duties and sales tax, and it doubles for people not on FBR's Active Taxpayers List.

What does a parcel worth under Rs 1,000 cost in duty?

Nothing. Under section 19C of the Customs Act, as amended by the Finance Act 2025, no duties and taxes are demanded on goods imported through post or courier worth Rs 1,000 or less, subject to FBR's conditions. Above that, there is no partial allowance: the whole value is dutiable. A Rs 900 phone case by post pays nothing. A Rs 1,200 one is assessed in full.

How much tax does a Rs 30,000 item pay?

Take an item with a CIF value of Rs 30,000, item plus shipping, in the 10% customs duty slab, with no regulatory duty, imported by a filer in the 5.5% income tax category. These are illustration rates, not a ruling for any product.

StepCalculationRs
Customs value (CIF)Item plus shipping30,000

That is about 37% of the customs value, so the item lands at roughly Rs 41,082 before any clearing or delivery charge. A non-filer would pay 11% income tax instead, Rs 4,283, which brings the total taxes to Rs 13,223, about 44%.

The tariff code moves the bill more than anything else, which is why our guide on how to check customs duty on any item starts there.

How much does shipping one item from China cost?

Shipping one item from China to Pakistan usually costs Rs 5,000 to 20,000, depending on size and type. Air shipping is charged on chargeable weight, the higher of actual weight and volumetric weight, so a large light box costs more than a small heavy one. Batteries, liquids and oversized items cost more or cannot fly at all.

This is the layer that makes cheap items poor value. A Rs 3,000 gadget with Rs 6,000 of shipping costs three times its price before duty. Our guide to the cost to ship one item goes through box sizes and examples.

What other charges should you expect?

Expect a few smaller charges outside customs, and plan for them rather than treating them as surprises:

  • Delivery inside China from the seller to the forwarder, often small but not always free.
  • Clearing agent fees on commercial cargo, plus terminal, handling and delivery order charges at the port or airport.
  • Bank and card costs. The bank's exchange rate on a foreign card payment is usually worse than the rate you see online.
  • Local delivery from the port or courier hub to your door.
  • Permit fees where DRAP, PSQCA, PTA or another agency must clear the goods.

Frequently asked questions

How much tax do I pay on items from China in Pakistan?

It depends on the PCT code. In our 2026-27 worked examples, duties and taxes came to about 19% to 64% of the customs value, and nothing on post or courier parcels worth Rs 1,000 or less.

Is shipping included in the value customs taxes?

Yes. Customs taxes the CIF value, which is the goods plus insurance and freight to Pakistan, so shipping attracts duty and tax too.

What is the sales tax on imports in Pakistan?

18%, charged on the customs value plus customs duty, additional customs duty and any regulatory duty.

Do non-filers pay more on imports?

Yes. Section 148 income tax doubles for people not on FBR's Active Taxpayers List, so 5.5% becomes 11% for most finished goods.

How much does it cost to ship one item from China to Pakistan?

Usually Rs 5,000 to 20,000 depending on size and type, charged on the higher of actual and volumetric weight.

Can I calculate the exact duty before ordering?

Only an estimate. Customs decides the tariff code and the value, and may use a valuation ruling instead of your invoice.
