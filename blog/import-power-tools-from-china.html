Importing Power Tools From China to Pakistan

By Muhammad Shamshad AslamUpdated November 12, 20269 min read
Quick answer

Yes, you can import a single power tool from China to Pakistan, corded or cordless. Pick the 220 to 240V version, stay on one battery platform, and know that batteries shipped in the box travel at reduced charge while loose spare batteries are hard to send by air. Heavy tools cost more to ship, so it suits tools worth Rs 10,000 or more.

New to importing? Read our step-by-step guide on how to import from China to Pakistan.

Can you import a power tool from China to Pakistan?

Yes. A drill, impact driver, angle grinder, rotary tool, heat gun or similar tool can be imported as a single item for your own use. Chinese platforms list models, kits and accessories that local shops do not carry. If you have not imported before, read our guide on how to import from China to Pakistan first.

Single items only applies here too. One tool, or a few different tools in one order, is fine. Tools for a hardware shop are a commercial import and need a cargo company or the commercial route.

Which power tools are worth importing one at a time?

Tools that are specialised, or that fit a battery platform you already use, are the best candidates. Typical examples:

  • A bare cordless tool that fits batteries you already own, such as a second impact driver or a small circular saw.
  • Compact tools such as brushless screwdrivers, rotary tools and mini polishers that are rarely stocked locally.
  • Specialised tools such as oscillating multi-tools, cordless grease guns, crimpers or heat guns with digital control.
  • Accessories that match a tool you own: specific blades, sanding pads, collets or chucks.

Heavy bench tools such as table saws or large compressors are possible but often uneconomic, because the box weight drives the shipping cost up.

Cordless or corded is worth thinking about in Pakistani terms. A cordless tool keeps working during load-shedding as long as the battery is charged, and it avoids long extension leads on a building site. A corded tool never runs out of charge mid-job and has no battery to wear out or ship. Many people settle on cordless for drilling and driving, and corded for grinders and heavy sanding where long run time matters.

Should you buy the 220V or 110V version?

Buy the 220 to 240V version for any corded tool. Pakistan runs at about 230V and 50Hz with type C and D sockets, according to the worldstandards.eu table, and China is also listed at 230V and 50Hz. The trap is a listing that also sells a US version at 110 to 120V. A 110V tool plugged into Pakistani mains will burn out. Choose the 220V or EU option in the variant menu and confirm it with the seller in writing.

The plug is a smaller issue. Chinese plugs are often type I or type A, which need an adapter. For a tool with an earth pin, use an adapter that carries the earth connection, or have an electrician fit a local plug. Cordless tools avoid the problem, though their chargers need a 220 to 240V or 100 to 240V input rating.

What specs matter on a cordless tool listing?

Check the battery platform, motor type, torque and what is actually in the box. Kits and bare tools are often shown in the same listing.

The voltage label itself can confuse. DeWalt's page for its 20V MAX battery says the maximum initial voltage, measured without a load, is 20 volts and the nominal voltage is 18. Many brands sell the same kind of pack as 18V. The label is a naming choice, but batteries still only fit their own brand's platform, so match the brand rather than the number.

Can tool batteries be shipped from China by air?

Batteries inside or with a tool can usually be shipped, while loose spare batteries are much harder. Air rules split lithium-ion batteries into three groups. A battery installed in the tool is the easiest. A battery packed in the same box as the tool is allowed, but The Compliance Center notes that from 1 January 2026, lithium-ion batteries packed with equipment above 2.7 Wh must ship at no more than 30 percent state of charge. A battery sent by itself is the hardest group, and many sellers and couriers will not send it to Pakistan by air.

To find a battery's watt-hours, multiply voltage by amp-hours. A nominal 18V, 2 Ah battery is 36 Wh. Our guide to lithium battery shipping rules covers the details. In practice, buy a kit with the battery in the same box, or a bare tool that fits batteries you already have, and expect the battery to arrive with a low charge.

How do you avoid fake branded tools?

Buy brand-name tools only from the brand's official store or an authorised seller, and treat a big price gap as a warning. Famous tool brands are widely copied, and copies often use the same colours with small changes to the name. A Chinese-brand tool sold under its own name is not a fake, and it is often the better buy. Our guide on spotting fake listings explains what to look for. Counterfeit goods can also be seized at customs, so do not order a copy and hope. On a genuine Chinese-brand listing, look for a brand store with a long history, clear specifications in the same units across its range, and spare parts or batteries sold separately, which suggests the brand intends to support the tool.

How are power tools taxed at customs?

Power tools pay duty and taxes on the customs value under their PCT code. The customs value is CIF, meaning the item plus freight and insurance, converted at NBP rates published by FBR. When we checked in November 2026, customs duty slabs were 0, 5, 10, 15 and 20 percent with some goods higher, additional customs duty was 0 percent on the 0 to 15 percent slabs and 2 percent on the 20 percent slab, regulatory duty applied only to listed goods and was capped at 20 percent from 1 July 2026, and sales tax was 18 percent on the value plus duties. Advance income tax under section 148 is collected as well, at a higher rate for non-filers. The Rs 1,000 courier exemption under section 19C will not apply to any real tool.

What does shipping a power tool cost?

Shipping one item from China usually costs Rs 5,000 to 20,000, depending on size, weight and type, and tools sit across that whole range. A compact driver in a small box is at the low end. A kit with a hard case, two batteries and a charger weighs much more, and a mitre saw or compressor can cost more to ship than it is worth. Express carriers charge by the greater of actual and volumetric weight, so a large plastic case adds cost even if it is light. Single imports make most sense for tools worth around Rs 10,000 or more.

What are the common mistakes?

  • Choosing the 110V variant of a corded tool.
  • Buying a bare tool for a battery platform you do not own.
  • Ordering loose spare batteries and then finding nobody will fly them.
  • Paying to ship a heavy case you do not need.
  • Assuming the warranty works in Pakistan. It usually does not, as our warranty guide explains.

Frequently asked questions

Will a Chinese power tool work on Pakistani electricity?

Yes, if it is the 220 to 240V version. Never order the 110V US variant of a corded tool, because it will burn out on Pakistani mains.

Is 20V MAX more powerful than 18V?

Not by itself. DeWalt says its 20V MAX packs are 18V nominal and 20V at maximum without load. Batteries only fit their own brand's platform.

Can I import spare power tool batteries from China?

It is difficult. Batteries shipped on their own are the hardest group to send by air, and many sellers refuse. A kit with the battery in the box is easier.

Why did my tool battery arrive almost empty?

Since 1 January 2026, lithium-ion batteries packed with equipment above 2.7 Wh must ship at 30 percent charge or less. Charge it fully before use.

Is it worth importing a heavy tool like a table saw?

Often not. Shipping is charged by weight or volumetric weight, and a heavy tool can cost more to ship than the saving on the price.

Does the tool warranty work in Pakistan?

Usually not, because many brands limit warranty to the country of sale. Rely on the seller's terms and local repair.
