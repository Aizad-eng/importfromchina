How to Buy From Xianyu (China's Second-Hand App) in Pakistan

By Muhammad Shamshad AslamUpdated October 23, 20269 min read
Quick answer

You cannot buy from Xianyu directly from Pakistan, but you can get an item through someone in China. Xianyu (闲鱼, also called Goofish) is Alibaba's Chinese second-hand app. It is in Chinese, needs Alipay, and sellers almost never ship abroad. A buyer in China pays through the app, the seller posts it to a Chinese address, and it is then sent on to Pakistan by air.

What is Xianyu (闲鱼, Goofish)?

Xianyu is China's biggest app for buying and selling used goods. The name 闲鱼 translates as idle fish, which is why some English guides call it Idle Fish. Its website is goofish.com, which is why you also see the name Goofish. Wikipedia describes it as a classified ads and online shopping app in China for used goods, part of Alibaba Group.

Think of it as a Chinese mix of OLX and a marketplace with built-in payment. Ordinary people sell phones they have upgraded from, cameras, lenses, clothes, bags, books, games and collectibles. Some professional resellers also trade on it. Because many Chinese buyers replace electronics often, there is a large supply of lightly used gadgets at prices well below new.

Who owns Xianyu and how big is it?

Alibaba Group owns Xianyu. It became a separate app in May 2014, growing out of a second-hand section on Taobao. In November 2023 Alibaba named it one of its strategic businesses to run as an independent unit, alongside 1688, DingTalk and Quark.

It is very large. At a late 2024 event Xianyu said it had over 600 million registered users. A tech newsletter that tracks the company, citing QuestMobile, put monthly active users at 209 million in March 2025. Mobile phones, mostly second-hand iPhones, bring in the most sales value, and electronics in general have the most transactions.

How do Xianyu listings work?

Each listing is posted by the seller, usually an individual, with their own pictures, a short description and a price. Unlike a Taobao shop, there is no fixed catalogue. Each item is one thing, in one condition, owned by one person.

  • Prices are often negotiable. Buyers chat with the seller in the app and agree a price before paying.
  • Shipping is domestic. Sellers usually post by SF Express or Cainiao inside China. A travel guide on Xianyu notes sellers almost never ship internationally.
  • Local pickup is common. Many deals happen face to face in the same city, which is no use to a buyer abroad.
  • Condition is the seller's word. Terms like 99新 (99 percent new) are the seller's own description, not a grade checked by anyone.

Read listings with the same care you would on OLX. For tips that apply to Chinese listings generally, see how to spot fake listings on AliExpress and Taobao.

How does payment and Alipay escrow work on Xianyu?

Payment goes through Alipay, and the money is held until the buyer confirms receipt. A Xianyu account has to be linked to Alipay before you can buy. When the buyer pays, Alipay holds the money. The seller ships, and the seller is paid only after the buyer taps confirm receipt in the app.

That escrow step is the main protection on Xianyu, so the most important rule is never to pay outside the app. Scammers ask buyers to send a deposit by WeChat or by a direct Alipay transfer, or to pay a friend. Once money leaves the Xianyu order, the escrow and the dispute process no longer cover it.

If something goes wrong, Xianyu has refund and return workflows, and it has a dispute process it calls Xianyu Small Court (闲鱼小法庭). Whether a return is allowed depends on the case, so do not assume either "no returns" or "easy returns". For a buyer in Pakistan, any return also has to be handled by the person in China who received the item.

Is there an international version of Xianyu?

Not one that serves Pakistan, as far as we could find in October 2026. An English app called Goofish, published by Goofish Pte. Ltd. and first released in September 2024, describes itself as backed by Alibaba. A buying guide that follows the platform says this app is focused on Hong Kong and Macau, and that it is not a worldwide English gateway to mainland Xianyu listings.

The main Xianyu app and goofish.com remain Chinese-only. There is no official English interface, no Pakistan address option, and no Pakistani payment method. If a website claims to be "Xianyu Pakistan", it is not the platform.

Can you buy from Xianyu directly from Pakistan?

No. Four things stop a Pakistani buyer.

  • Account. Signing up needs a Chinese phone number or a linked Alipay account. International numbers work only sometimes.
  • Payment. Buying needs Alipay. JazzCash, Easypaisa, Raast and PayPak do not work.
  • Address. Sellers post to Chinese addresses only.
  • Language. Listings and chats are in Chinese, and condition questions need clear answers.

The route that works is the same one Pakistani buyers use for Taobao: someone in China buys it, receives it and sends it on. Our guide on buying from Taobao in Pakistan explains that route for new goods.

What are the risks of buying used goods from Xianyu?

The biggest risk is that the item is not what the listing says, and you find out in Pakistan, far from the seller. Used goods carry risks that new items do not.

  • Hidden faults. Repairs, replaced parts, worn batteries and missing accessories are not always mentioned.
  • Wrong model or region. A phone or camera may be a Chinese-market version with different bands, software or apps.
  • Account locks. Apple says not to take ownership of a used iPhone or iPad protected by Activation Lock, and to make sure it is erased and removed from the previous owner's account. Ask the seller to confirm this before paying.
  • Counterfeits. Luxury bags, watches and branded trainers on Xianyu can be fakes. Pakistan Customs seizes counterfeit goods.
  • No warranty. A used item from an individual has no seller warranty, and a brand's Chinese warranty rarely helps in Pakistan.
  • Returns are slow. Sending an item back to China costs about as much as sending it here.

Phones add one more step. Any phone brought into Pakistan needs PTA registration, and the tax depends on the model. Read importing a mobile phone from China and PTA before you look at phones on Xianyu.

Which Xianyu items make sense for a buyer in Pakistan?

Items that are valuable, hard to find new, and easy to judge from the listing make the most sense. Cheap items and fragile electronics usually do not.

How does one Xianyu item get to Pakistan?

Through a buyer in China who pays in the app, receives the parcel and sends it on by air. These are the steps.

  1. Find the listing. Browse goofish.com with browser translation, or ask someone with the app to search for you.
  2. Copy the link. Use the share button in the app. Our guide on copying a product link from Chinese apps shows how.
  3. Ask the seller the right questions first. Exact model, faults, repairs, what is included, and for Apple devices whether it is erased and off the seller's account. Asking the seller for extra pictures of marks or the serial label is reasonable.
  4. Get one price. The person or service buying it should give you a total in rupees for the item delivered to your door.
  5. Payment in China goes through the app. The buyer in China pays with Alipay inside the Xianyu order, so escrow applies.
  6. Domestic delivery, then air freight. The seller posts it to a Chinese address, and it is then flown to Pakistan and cleared through customs.
  7. Confirm receipt only when it has arrived in China. Releasing the seller's money too early removes the escrow protection.

Common mistakes when buying from Xianyu

  • Paying a deposit outside the app. This is the classic Xianyu scam. All payment should go through the Xianyu order.
  • Choosing sellers with no history. New accounts with no reviews and a too-good price are a warning sign.
  • Trusting "99新" as a grade. It is the seller's own wording, not a standard.
  • Buying a cheap item. Shipping one item from China usually costs Rs 5,000 to 20,000, so a low-value item rarely works out.
  • Ignoring flight rules. Loose batteries, power banks, perfumes and aerosols are commonly restricted by air.

Frequently asked questions

Does Xianyu ship to Pakistan?

No. Xianyu sellers post to Chinese addresses, and the app needs Alipay and usually a Chinese phone number. Pakistani buyers get items through someone in China who buys and forwards them.

Is Xianyu the same as Goofish?

Yes. Xianyu (闲鱼) is the Chinese name and goofish.com is its website. A separate English Goofish app exists, but buying guides say it focuses on Hong Kong and Macau.

Who owns Xianyu?

Alibaba Group. Xianyu became a standalone app in 2014 after starting as a second-hand section of Taobao.

Is Xianyu safe to buy from?

Payment inside the app is held by Alipay until the buyer confirms receipt, which protects the buyer. The risk comes from paying outside the app and from used items that are not as described.

Can I pay for Xianyu with a Pakistani card or JazzCash?

No. Xianyu needs Alipay. Someone in China pays on your behalf and you pay them in rupees.

Is it worth buying a used phone from Xianyu for Pakistan?

Rarely. A used phone needs PTA registration and tax in Pakistan, may be Activation Locked or a Chinese-market version, and has no warranty here.
