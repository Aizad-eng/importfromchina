Importing Skincare Devices and Cosmetics From China to Pakistan

By importfromchina.shop teamUpdated October 3, 20267 min read
Quick answer

Beauty devices from China, such as LED masks, sonic facial cleansers, microcurrent tools, hair stylers and dermaplaning pens, are good single-item imports to Pakistan because they are light, electronic and ship by air. Creams, serums, liquids and aerosols are a different matter: air carriers restrict many of them and anything medicated falls under DRAP, so parcels are often held. Avoid branded cosmetics at low prices, which are counterfeit and seized.

Which beauty items from China ship to Pakistan without trouble?

Devices. The Chinese market is full of skincare and grooming gadgets that reach Pakistan months later at several times the price, if at all. These travel well because they are solid, light and treated as electronics by customs:

  • LED light therapy masks and wands.
  • Sonic facial cleansing brushes and silicone cleansers.
  • Microcurrent and EMS facial devices.
  • Dermaplaning pens, blackhead vacuums and pore tools.
  • IPL hair removal devices for home use.
  • Hair stylers, straighteners, curlers and hot air brushes (check voltage).
  • Electric scalp massagers, nail drills and lamps, makeup brush cleaners.

Most have built-in rechargeable batteries, which ship by air normally. A few larger IPL units plug into the mains; Pakistan runs 220 to 240V 50Hz and most Chinese devices match, but check the label and avoid 110V US-market versions.

Why are creams and liquids difficult?

Two separate systems get in the way. First, air carriers restrict flammable and pressurised items and limit liquids, so a consolidated air line may simply refuse the parcel. Second, Pakistan regulates cosmetics and anything with a therapeutic claim. Products that claim to treat acne, lighten skin with active drugs, regrow hair or cure anything are treated as drugs and need DRAP registration for commercial import. Personal quantities are still often held. Plain, unmedicated cosmetics in a single unit have a better chance, but there is no guarantee, and we would rather tell you that than take your money for a parcel that does not arrive.

How do I avoid counterfeit cosmetics?

Counterfeit beauty products are among the most common fakes on Chinese marketplaces, and they are worse than other fakes because you put them on your skin. A listing on 1688, Taobao or DHgate for a well-known Western or Korean brand at a quarter of the retail price is not a bargain, it is a counterfeit, and Pakistan Customs seizes counterfeits. Rules of thumb:

  • Buy genuine Chinese brands or unbranded devices, not famous foreign brands.
  • For a brand, buy only from its official store on Tmall or JD, where availability for export can be limited.
  • Treat "original quality" and "1:1" as code words for fake.
  • Prefer rated sellers with a long history in beauty devices. See how to find reliable Chinese suppliers.

Which Chinese beauty device brands do people buy?

Several Chinese brands have built reputations in this space and are sold openly under their own names. Examples commonly bought include Xiaomi ecosystem grooming tools, Dreame and Laifen hair dryers, and device makers that sell on AliExpress and Amazon under their own labels. Availability and model ranges change, so check the current listing rather than relying on a name from a video.

What about duty on beauty devices?

Devices are assessed as electrical appliances or electronics. Customs duty, additional customs duty, sales tax and advance income tax together commonly add 25 to 60 percent of the customs value. Cosmetics have their own classification and often sit in a higher slab with regulatory duty. Rates change with budgets and SROs, so confirm the current rate or ask for a delivered price. What should I check before ordering a skincare device?

  • Voltage if it plugs into the mains. 220 to 240V is right for Pakistan.
  • Plug type. Chinese type A or I plugs need a cheap adapter for Pakistani type C or D sockets.
  • App language and region if the device pairs with a phone.
  • Whether the listing bundles consumables such as gel or serum that could cause the whole parcel to be refused by air.
  • Ask the seller for photos of the exact unit and the manual before dispatch.

Frequently asked questions

Can I import an LED face mask from China to Pakistan?

Yes. LED masks are electronic devices with built-in batteries, ship by air normally and clear customs as electronics.

Can I import skincare creams or serums from China?

It is risky. Liquids and gels face air restrictions and anything medicated falls under DRAP. Single unmedicated items sometimes clear, but holds are common.

Can perfume be shipped from China to Pakistan by air?

Generally no. Perfumes and aerosols are flammable and most consolidated air lines refuse them.

Are cheap branded cosmetics from Taobao or DHgate genuine?

No. Famous brands at a fraction of retail are counterfeit, and Pakistan Customs seizes counterfeits. Buy genuine Chinese brands or unbranded devices.

Will a Chinese hair straightener work in Pakistan?

Most Chinese models are 220V and match Pakistan's 220 to 240V 50Hz supply. Check the label, avoid 110V variants, and use a plug adapter.
