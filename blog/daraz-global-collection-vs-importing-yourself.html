Daraz Global Collection vs Importing It Yourself

By Muhammad Shamshad AslamUpdated October 23, 20269 min read
Quick answer

Daraz Global Collection, shown on Daraz as items that ship from overseas, is usually the easier choice for low-priced items: the listings we opened showed cash on delivery, about one to two weeks' delivery and a price that includes customs. Importing yourself makes sense when the exact item, seller or version is not on Daraz, or when it is worth around Rs 10,000 or more.

What is Daraz Global Collection?

Daraz Global Collection is the part of Daraz where international sellers list products that ship to you from outside Pakistan. Daraz has used the name Global Collection since at least 2019, when it was launched with cash on delivery for these orders. On today's site the same listings carry a label saying the item ships from overseas, and Daraz's policy pages call them Daraz Global products.

You buy them like any Daraz item, in rupees, through the Daraz app or site. The difference is behind the scenes: the seller is abroad, the parcel crosses a border, and some of Daraz's usual policies do not apply in the same way. This guide covers what we found on Daraz's own pages and on two overseas listings we opened on 23 October 2026.

How do you find overseas items on Daraz?

Use the location filter on the search results. When we searched Daraz in October 2026, the Location filter listed the provinces and regions of Pakistan plus two more options, Local and Overseas. Selecting Overseas shows only items that ship from abroad.

On a product page, an overseas item carries a "Ships from overseas" note. The text attached to it, on the listings we opened, said that products shipped by international sellers are delivered at no additional cost, that the stated price includes customs fees and import duties, and that buyers should make sure the design, warranty and return policy meet their needs because they may differ from local products.

How long does Daraz Global take to deliver?

About one to two weeks on the two listings we checked. On 23 October 2026, with Daraz's default Karachi address, an overseas USB desk lamp showed standard delivery by 1 to 7 November, and an overseas keyboard showed 2 to 8 November. That is roughly 9 to 16 days from the day we looked.

Both showed a standard delivery fee of Rs 155. Local listings ship from inside Pakistan, so if you need something quickly, filter for Local instead. Delivery estimates change with the seller, the item and your city, so always read the date range on the listing with your own address selected.

Do you pay customs on Daraz Global orders?

Not separately, according to Daraz. The overseas listing text says the stated price includes customs fees and import duties, so what you see at checkout is what you pay, plus the delivery fee shown.

When you import yourself, customs is your concern. Under section 19C of the Customs Act, as amended up to 30 June 2026, parcels arriving by post or courier are free of duties and taxes only where the value does not exceed Rs 1,000. Above that, duty and taxes can apply, and on many direct orders they are collected at delivery. A service that quotes a delivered price includes customs in that price instead. For how duty is calculated, see our guide to customs duty from China to Pakistan.

Can you pay cash on delivery on Daraz Global?

Yes, on the listings we opened. Both overseas listings showed "Cash on Delivery Available" for the Karachi address. Daraz also accepts cards and wallets at checkout. This is the biggest practical difference from ordering on most Chinese sites yourself, where you usually need an international card and pay in full upfront.

What are the return rules for Daraz Global items?

They depend on the listing, so read it before you buy. Daraz's general returns FAQ says you can request a return within 14 days of delivery, and both overseas listings we opened showed "14 days easy return" with no warranty. But Daraz's refund policy page states that the policy does not apply to Daraz Global products, and its return policy tells buyers to check the product page for the return reasons that apply to overseas items.

There is also a category rule worth knowing. In clothing, shoes, sunglasses and accessories, Daraz's return policy says change-of-mind returns are allowed except for non-DarazMall items shipped from overseas. So a wrong size on an overseas fashion item may not be returnable, while a damaged or incorrect item still can be. Check the Return and Warranty box on each listing.

What does importing it yourself involve?

Importing yourself means buying on a Chinese site and getting the item to Pakistan without Daraz in the middle. There are three common ways:

  • AliExpress directly. Many listings ship to Pakistan. You usually need an international Visa or Mastercard, delivery takes weeks, and customs may be charged on arrival.
  • A shopping agent or forwarder. Useful for Taobao or 1688, which do not ship abroad themselves. You usually pay for the item first and for international shipping later.
  • A single-item import service. You send a link, get one delivered price including customs, and pay once before it ships.

The main reason to do any of these is choice. Daraz overseas listings cover what their sellers chose to list. Alibaba, 1688, Taobao and AliExpress cover far more models, versions and sellers. Our comparison of Daraz and importing from China looks at the wider picture.

How do you decide, step by step?

  1. Search Daraz with the Overseas filter. Note the price, the delivery dates for your address, and the Return and Warranty box.
  2. Search the same item on AliExpress, Taobao or 1688. Check that it is the same model and version, not just the same picture.
  3. Price the import route. Add shipping and likely customs for AliExpress, or ask a single-item service for one delivered price.
  4. Compare delivered totals. Compare the full amount you pay, not the item price alone.
  5. Weigh the return terms. Daraz overseas items with a 14-day return window are easier to send back than anything bought from China.
  6. Choose. If the totals are close, Daraz Global is less hassle. If the exact item is only on a Chinese site, import it.

Common mistakes with Daraz Global orders

  • Assuming local Daraz rules apply. Daraz's refund policy does not apply to Daraz Global products. Read the listing's own return terms.
  • Expecting local delivery speed. The overseas listings we checked took one to two weeks, not two or three days.
  • Ordering a fashion item in a doubtful size. Change-of-mind returns do not apply to non-DarazMall fashion items from overseas.
  • Comparing different versions. An overseas listing and a Taobao listing with the same picture may be different grades or sizes.
  • Forgetting customs on the import side. If you import yourself through a route that does not include it, budget for duty on anything over Rs 1,000.
