Temu Delivery Time in Pakistan: How Long It Really Takes

By Muhammad Shamshad AslamUpdated October 26, 20268 min read
Quick answer

A Temu order to Pakistan usually takes roughly 8 to 22 calendar days from payment. Temu's Pakistan shipping page lists 1 to 3 days of processing, then free standard shipping in 5 to 13 business days, with the last leg handed to a local carrier. Customs, sale seasons, public holidays and remote addresses can add days.

How long does Temu take to deliver to Pakistan?

Temu usually takes about 8 to 22 calendar days to reach a Pakistani address, counted from the day you pay. That range comes from adding the two numbers on Temu's Pakistan shipping page when we checked in October 2026: 1 to 3 days to process the order, then 5 to 13 business days for free standard shipping. If you only want to know whether Temu delivers here at all, our guide on whether Temu delivers to Pakistan covers that. This guide is only about time.

Every Temu order to Pakistan passes through three stages:

  1. Processing. The order is confirmed, packed and handed to the shipping network. Temu says this may take 1 to 3 days.
  2. International shipping. The parcel flies from China and clears Pakistan customs. This sits inside the 5 to 13 business days.
  3. The local leg. A Pakistani carrier takes the parcel to your door. This is also inside the 5 to 13 business days, and it is where tracking often goes quiet.

The clock that matters is the one under each package in your order history. Temu says it shows the delivery time there once a package ships, and that the tracking information has the most up to date delivery date.

What happens during the 1 to 3 days of processing?

Processing is the time between your payment being confirmed and the package being shipped, and nothing moves physically during most of it. Temu's page says that after an order is paid and confirmed, you see the delivery time and cost on the confirmation page, and you get a message that outlines the processing time. You get another message once the order ships.

Two points catch people out:

  • The shipping window starts at dispatch, not at payment. If you count 5 to 13 business days from the day you paid, you will expect the parcel up to three days too early.
  • Each package has its own clock. A cart with several items can ship as separate packages, each with its own tracking number and delivery time. Temu's terms also say several orders may be delivered together in one package, so the reverse can happen too.

What does 5 to 13 business days mean in real dates?

Business days normally mean Monday to Friday, so 5 to 13 business days is roughly one to almost three calendar weeks. Temu's Pakistan page does not define the term, so the safe reading is to skip weekends and public holidays when you count.

Here is a worked example for an order paid on Monday 2 November 2026, using only Temu's published ranges:

This example also falls around 11.11, a heavy shipping period, so the slow end is more likely than the fast end. Use the date Temu shows for your package rather than this table once your order has shipped.

Why does Temu tracking stop updating in Pakistan?

Tracking pauses because the parcel changes hands, usually after it lands in Pakistan, and the next carrier's scans do not always feed back into Temu's tracking. Temu says this plainly on its Pakistan shipping page: orders may be handed over to local carriers for the final leg, so tracking may not be available every step of the way.

In practice a Temu parcel to Pakistan commonly shows a pattern like this:

  • Several scans in China, then a departure scan.
  • A gap of two to five days while it flies, waits for customs and is sorted.
  • A handover note naming a local carrier, sometimes with a new tracking number.
  • One or two local scans, then out for delivery and a call from the rider.

A gap of a few days with no scan is normal and not a sign the parcel is lost. If the tracking shows a local carrier and a second tracking number, check that number on the carrier's own site, which often shows more than Temu's page. Our guide on tracking a parcel from China to Pakistan explains how to read handovers between carriers.

What can delay a Temu parcel to Pakistan?

The common delays are flights, customs, sale seasons, public holidays and the address itself. Temu's page says the actual delivery date may be affected by flight arrangements, weather conditions and other external factors. In Pakistan these usually take one of five forms:

  • Flight space. Parcels wait for cargo space on flights out of China. When space is short, they wait longer before the first long gap in tracking even starts.
  • Customs. Every parcel passes Pakistan customs. Most clear without the buyer doing anything, but a parcel picked for assessment takes longer. The next section covers this.
  • 11.11 and other big sales. Around 11 November, a huge volume of parcels leaves China at once. Orders placed in the weeks around it commonly sit at the slow end of the window. Our guide to Chinese New Year and 11.11 shipping delays explains the yearly pattern.
  • Eid and other public holidays. Many courier and government offices close or run reduced hours for several days around Eid. A parcel that lands just before Eid can sit until offices reopen, and Chinese New Year has the same effect at the China end.
  • Remote or unclear addresses. Parcels for smaller towns often pass through more sorting hubs, and a missing landmark or a phone that is switched off can turn one delivery attempt into three.

Does customs slow down a Temu order in Pakistan?

Usually only by a day or two, unless duty is owed and not paid. Under section 19C of the Customs Act, post and courier parcels worth Rs 1,000 or less pay no duties or taxes. Above that, duty and taxes can apply to the parcel.

Temu's Pakistan terms say that, where applicable, you act as the importer, and that Temu appoints a freight forwarding agent to pay sales tax and customs duties for you. Duties collected at checkout are estimates, and if customs assesses more, you are responsible for the difference. The same terms say Temu is not liable if a product is delayed or denied customs clearance because you did not pay such amounts. So if a genuine courier tells you more duty is due, ask for the customs assessment document, check the order in the Temu app, and settle it quickly. Never pay through a link in an SMS.

How can you make sure the rider finds you the first time?

Give the local carrier everything it needs to deliver in one attempt, because failed attempts are where many Temu delays in Pakistan actually happen. A few habits help:

  • Write house number, street, block or sector, area and city, plus a landmark the rider can ask about.
  • Use a mobile number you answer, and keep it on during the delivery window. Riders here usually call before coming.
  • Avoid security-restricted addresses. Temu's page says items cannot be delivered to security-restricted areas such as military bases.
  • Use a physical address for large items, valuables or anything that may need a signature, which Temu's page says some items do.
  • Tell the guard or family at home that a parcel is coming, so it is not refused.

When should you contact Temu about a late order?

Contact Temu once the date shown for your package has passed, and in any case within 90 days of the order date. Temu's Pakistan shipping page asks you to contact customer service immediately, and within 90 days of the order date, if the package has not been delivered or shows delivered but you have not received it.

Before you write, have the order number, the package's tracking number, the local carrier's name and number if shown, and the last scan date ready. If the date has passed by only a day or two and the parcel is with a local carrier, call the carrier first, because that is often faster. If tracking says delivered and you have nothing, follow the steps in our guide to a Temu order not received in Pakistan.

Can you pay for faster Temu delivery to Pakistan?

Not when we checked. Temu's Pakistan shipping page listed one method, standard shipping, free on all orders, at 5 to 13 business days. It said you may choose standard shipping depending on the items in your order and your location. We did not see an express option for Pakistan in October 2026. If you need something within days, a local seller is the realistic choice, and Temu's own window is worth planning around rather than fighting.
