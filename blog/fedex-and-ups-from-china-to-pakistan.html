FedEx and UPS From China to Pakistan: What to Expect

By Muhammad Shamshad AslamUpdated November 12, 20268 min read
Quick answer

FedEx and UPS both carry parcels from China to Pakistan by air express, door to door. In Pakistan, FedEx is run by Gerry's International as its official service participant, and UPS services are provided through TCS as UPS's authorised service contractor. Expect courier speed, courier prices and normal Pakistani duty on anything worth more than Rs 1,000.

Do FedEx and UPS deliver from China to Pakistan?

Yes, both FedEx and UPS deliver parcels from China to Pakistan as international express shipments. Chinese sellers on Alibaba.com and other export sites commonly offer FedEx, UPS and DHL as the fast shipping choices, especially for samples and single pieces. The parcel is collected from the seller in China, flown through the carrier's network and delivered to your address in Pakistan after customs.

The experience is close to DHL's, with one difference that confuses people: inside Pakistan, neither FedEx nor UPS runs its own branded operation in the way you might expect. Local companies do the work under agreement, and knowing who they are saves time when you need to call someone. For DHL specifically, see our guide on DHL from China to Pakistan.

Who handles FedEx and UPS parcels inside Pakistan?

FedEx parcels in Pakistan are handled by Gerry's International, and UPS parcels are handled by TCS. These are the companies you deal with once the parcel lands.

  • FedEx: Gerry's International. Gerry's describes itself as the Global Service Participant of Federal Express Corporation in Pakistan, owning and managing the operations, offices, vehicles, service centres and hubs. Its site says FedEx began operating in Pakistan in 1989 and lists more than 22 service centres and two operation hubs.
  • UPS: TCS. TCS became UPS's authorised service contractor in Pakistan under an agreement announced in November 2016, so TCS provides UPS international express services here. TCS opened a Karachi international gateway in 2017 and a second one in Lahore in 2018, which it said would streamline import and export shipments in Punjab.

When we tried in November 2026, FedEx's and UPS's own Pakistan pages refused our requests, so we could not confirm the current local import procedures on them. If you need exact current rules, call Gerry's for FedEx or TCS for UPS with your tracking number.

How long do FedEx and UPS take from China to Pakistan?

FedEx and UPS usually deliver from China to Pakistan in a matter of working days once the parcel is collected, similar to DHL, with customs as the main variable. The carrier gives an estimated delivery date when the seller books the shipment, based on the cities and the service level chosen. Express services are faster and dearer. Economy or saver services are slower and cheaper.

Where a parcel starts in China matters a little. FedEx lists Guangzhou Baiyun International Airport as one of its hubs, which suits sellers in Guangdong. A seller in a smaller inland city may add a day for the parcel to reach a hub. What matters more is how fast the seller hands the box over, and whether the invoice is complete enough for Pakistan Customs to release it quickly.

How do customs and duties work on a FedEx or UPS parcel?

Customs and duties work the same way as with any courier: the carrier's local operator clears the parcel as a licensed agent, Pakistan Customs assesses duty and taxes on the item's value, and you pay before or at delivery. The rules come from Pakistani law, not from FedEx or UPS.

  • The Rs 1,000 limit. Under section 19C of the Customs Act, as amended by the Finance Act 2025, no duties and taxes are charged on post or courier parcels worth Rs 1,000 or less. Above that, the whole value is dutiable.
  • Normal tariff rates. There is no flat courier rate. The duty depends on the product's HS code. Our guide to customs duty from China to Pakistan explains the layers.
  • The operator's own charges. Couriers usually add handling or brokerage fees for clearing your parcel. These are separate from government taxes and vary by company.
  • Your CNIC. Couriers clearing personal parcels in Pakistan generally ask for the receiver's CNIC, and the name on the shipment should match it.

If someone sends you a message asking you to pay "customs" through a link, check it against the tracking number and call the operator on a number you looked up yourself.

How are FedEx and UPS prices worked out?

FedEx and UPS charge on chargeable weight, which is the higher of the real weight and the volumetric weight. Like DHL, both divide length x width x height in centimetres by 5,000 for international express, rather than the 6,000 used for air cargo. Weight is billed in steps, so even a very light parcel pays for a full first step.

Here is how the same box comes out at the two divisors. A boxed desk lamp measuring 50 x 20 x 20 cm and weighing 1.2 kg has a volume of 20,000 cubic centimetres:

  • At 5,000 (FedEx, UPS, DHL): charged as 4 kg.
  • At 6,000 (air cargo): charged as 3.33 kg.
  • On the scale: 1.2 kg.

So the courier charges the lamp at more than three times its real weight. Our guide on the cost to ship one item from China to Pakistan works through more examples. Fuel surcharges, remote-area charges and extra handling for batteries can also be added, and they change over time.

How do you track a FedEx or UPS parcel coming to Pakistan?

You track a FedEx parcel on FedEx's tracking page and a UPS parcel on UPS's tracking page, using the number the seller gives you after booking. Ask the seller for it the day they ship. A seller who only says "shipped by express" without a number has not given you anything you can check.

Once the parcel reaches Pakistan, the local operator takes over the delivery. The tracking page normally keeps updating, but if it stalls at a customs or clearance status for more than a couple of working days, call Gerry's for FedEx or TCS for UPS with the tracking number and your CNIC ready. Ask a simple question: is anything pending from me, a document, a value query or a payment?

What should you ask the seller before they book FedEx or UPS?

Ask the seller four things before they book, because each one heads off a common delay.

  1. Who pays the duty. Most sellers ship with duties unpaid, which means you pay in Pakistan. Confirm this so it is not a surprise.
  2. What goes on the invoice. The description should name the item plainly and the value should match what you paid.
  3. The box size and weight. Ask for the packed dimensions, so you can check the volumetric weight yourself.
  4. Whether there is a battery. Items with lithium batteries need to be declared. An undeclared battery can get a parcel held or returned.

Give the seller your name exactly as on your CNIC, your full address and a phone number that will be answered.

Is FedEx or UPS worth it for one item from China?

FedEx or UPS is worth it when you need the item quickly and it is valuable enough that the shipping is a sensible share of the total. For cheap or bulky items, courier pricing usually makes them poor value. Post is cheaper but slow, and consolidated air freight sits in between.

Frequently asked questions

Who delivers FedEx parcels in Pakistan?

Gerry's International, which describes itself as the Global Service Participant of FedEx Express in Pakistan and runs its offices, vehicles and service centres here.

Is TCS the same as UPS in Pakistan?

No, but TCS has provided UPS international express services in Pakistan as UPS's authorised service contractor since an agreement announced in November 2016.

Do I pay customs on a FedEx or UPS parcel from China?

Yes, if it is worth more than Rs 1,000. Duty is set by Pakistan Customs on the item's value and HS code, and the courier's operator collects it with its own handling fees.

Is FedEx or UPS cheaper than DHL from China?

It varies by day, box size and service. All three use a volumetric divisor of 5,000 and similar weight steps, so compare the quotes the seller gets for your exact box.

Can I track a UPS parcel on the TCS website?

Use the UPS tracking number on UPS's own tracking page first. If it stalls in Pakistan, call TCS with the number, since TCS handles UPS shipments locally.

Why did my FedEx parcel stop at customs?

Usually a missing or vague invoice, a value Customs wants to check, a battery or permit question, or duty waiting to be paid. Call Gerry's with the tracking number and your CNIC.
