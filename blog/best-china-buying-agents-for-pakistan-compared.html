Quick answer

The right China buying agent depends on your order. For one item worth Rs 10,000 or more from any Chinese site, a single-item service like ours gives one delivered price. For several Taobao items combined, an international shopping agent such as Superbuy fits, with customs handled by you. For products already in its catalogue with cash on delivery, Markaz fits, though it is aimed at resellers.

What counts as a China buying agent for Pakistan?

A China buying agent is any business that buys from a Chinese seller on your behalf and gets the item to you. In practice, Pakistani buyers meet three models under that name:

  • Single-item import services buy the listing you send, ship it and clear customs for one agreed rupee price.
  • International shopping agents buy on Taobao, 1688 and similar sites, store items in a Chinese warehouse, and ship them on in a second step that you pay for separately.
  • Catalogue marketplaces list China-sourced products in their own app and handle the import when you order.

Freight forwarders and cargo companies are a fourth group, but they normally ship goods you have already paid for rather than buying them. We explain the differences in shopping agent vs freight forwarder vs single-item service.

How does Superbuy work for Pakistani buyers?

Superbuy is an international shopping agent that buys on Chinese platforms, stores your items in its warehouse and ships them to you in a second step. Its fee page says it charges no shopping agent service fee for mainstream platforms such as Taobao, Tmall, JD.com and 1688, and charges a percentage of item value for other platforms. Storage is free for the first 90 days.

Payment comes in two stages. Its fee page says stage one covers the product price, Chinese domestic shipping and any service fee, after which the item goes to its warehouse. The international shipping fee is charged when you submit your shipping order. Payment methods listed include Visa, Wise, Alipay, WeChat Pay and UnionPay.

Pakistan is a listed destination in its shipping calculator. When we ran it on 23 October 2026 for a 1 kg general-goods parcel from its Guangdong warehouse to Pakistan, it showed eleven shipping lines with times from 8 to 11 days on the quickest to 20 to 34 days on the slowest. Every line was marked Tax Excluded, which means Pakistani customs is not part of Superbuy's charge. Some postal lines also showed a declared value cap of USD 130.

That makes Superbuy a fit for buyers who already have an international card, want to combine several small items from different shops into one parcel, and are comfortable handling any customs charge at delivery.

How does Markaz work?

Markaz is a Pakistani marketplace with a China section, and its own pages say it serves both shoppers and resellers, with a separate reseller app. In the China section, you choose from listed products, order in rupees, and Markaz says it pays the supplier, consolidates the shipment, clears customs and delivers. Its pages say there is no minimum order and that the checkout price includes freight and customs.

Payment is cash on delivery in supported cities, which it names as including Karachi, Lahore, Faisalabad, Islamabad, Rawalpindi and Peshawar, or online. It gives about 10 to 17 days for China orders. Damage or mismatch claims are raised within 7 days of delivery through your orders page. The limit is selection: you buy what is in its catalogue. Do you need an agent at all?

Often not, for cheap items. AliExpress ships many listings to Pakistan directly, with prices shown in rupees when we checked in October 2026, and some other Chinese sites deliver too. If the exact item is on a site that ships to Pakistan, you can pay with a card that has international payments enabled, and the item is low-priced, buying directly is usually cheapest.

An agent earns its place when the item is only on Taobao, 1688 or an Alibaba listing that does not ship to individuals, when you cannot pay a foreign site, or when you want customs handled and a known total. Check whether the listing ships to Pakistan before paying anyone to buy it for you.

What about stock or bulk orders?

None of the single-item routes in this guide is right for stock. If you need many units of a product, you need a commercial import: an Alibaba supplier who quotes for quantity, a cargo company or freight forwarder, and registration for commercial import in your own name. We do not handle bulk or resale orders, and catalogue platforms aimed at resellers are a different business model from buying one item for yourself.

How do you choose an agent, step by step?

  1. Work out what you are buying. One item, several small items, or stock.
  2. Find where it is listed. A site that ships to Pakistan, Taobao or 1688, Alibaba, or a Pakistani catalogue.
  3. Check the value. Under about Rs 10,000, a single-item service rarely makes sense once shipping is added.
  4. Ask for the delivered total. Include item, China shipping, international shipping, Pakistan customs and fees.
  5. Count the payments. One payment before shipping, two stages, or cash on delivery. Know which before you start.
  6. Read the refund terms in writing. Look for what happens if the item is late, wrong or damaged.
  7. Check the payee. Pay an account whose name matches the business.

Common mistakes when picking a China buying agent

  • Comparing only the first payment. With two-stage agents, the international shipping bill comes later and can be larger than expected.
  • Missing the Tax Excluded label. If customs is not in the agent's price, budget for duty on parcels worth more than Rs 1,000.
  • Using a single-item service for a cheap item. The shipping can cost more than the product.
  • Asking a single-item service for stock. Use a cargo company and a commercial import route instead.
  • Choosing by reputation claims you cannot check. Compare what each service publishes about price, payments, customs and refunds.
