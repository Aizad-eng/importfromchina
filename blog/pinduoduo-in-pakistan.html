Pinduoduo in Pakistan: What It Is and How to Get Items From It

By importfromchina.shop teamUpdated October 3, 20266 min read
Quick answer

Pinduoduo (拼多多) is a Chinese domestic shopping app owned by PDD Holdings, the same company that runs Temu. It is in Chinese only, needs a Chinese phone number and Chinese payment, and sellers ship only within China, so it does not deliver to Pakistan. Pakistani buyers get Pinduoduo items through an agent with a Chinese address, or by finding the same product on 1688 or Taobao, where most Pinduoduo sellers also source.

What is Pinduoduo?

Pinduoduo, written 拼多多 and often shortened to PDD, is one of the largest shopping apps in China. It grew by selling very cheap everyday goods, farm produce and factory-direct products to Chinese consumers through group buying: two or more people join a 拼单 (team purchase) and get a lower price than buying alone. It is an app first, with a limited website, and it is designed entirely for people living in China. There is no English version and no international storefront.

Is Pinduoduo the same as Temu?

They share a parent. PDD Holdings runs Pinduoduo for China and Temu for buyers outside China. Many of the sellers and factories overlap, and a lot of what appears on Temu was on Pinduoduo first at a lower price. But they are separate apps with separate accounts, catalogues and rules. Temu ships to a list of countries that, at the time of writing, does not include Pakistan. Pinduoduo ships only within China. So a Pakistani buyer cannot use either one directly. Our guide does Temu deliver to Pakistan covers the Temu side.

Does Pinduoduo deliver to Pakistan?

No. The address form accepts only Chinese provinces and cities. Beyond that, three more walls stand in the way:

  • Account. Registration needs a Chinese mobile number for the verification code. Pakistani numbers are not accepted.
  • Payment. Pinduoduo takes WeChat Pay, Alipay and Chinese bank cards. No foreign card, and no JazzCash, Easypaisa or Raast.
  • Language. Everything, including seller chat, is in Chinese.

Why are Pinduoduo prices so low?

Three reasons, and they matter for what you receive. First, group buying: sellers accept thin margins in exchange for volume. Second, many sellers are small factories or traders in the same manufacturing towns that supply 1688, selling direct without a brand or distributor. Third, the platform competes hard on price and pushes sellers to match the lowest listing. The result is that prices are often below Taobao and sometimes below 1688 for the same-looking item, but quality is inconsistent. Two listings with the same photo can ship very different products. The price you see is also usually the group price. Buying alone (单独购买) costs more, and an agent buying one piece for you may pay the single price.

How can I get a Pinduoduo item in Pakistan?

  1. In the Pinduoduo app, open the product and use the share button to copy the link or share text. If you only have a screenshot, that works too. See how to copy and share a product link from Chinese apps.
  2. Send it to an agent on WhatsApp with the variant you want.
  3. The agent either buys it on Pinduoduo through a Chinese account, or finds the same product on 1688 or Taobao from a seller with a better track record, and quotes you one price in rupees for the item delivered to your door, with the fee stated.
  4. You pay once, before it ships.
  5. The seller ships to the agent's address in China, and the agent forwards it by air with customs cleared. Expect 7 to 15 days from dispatch out of China, with nothing to pay on delivery.

What should I watch out for?

  • Lookalike listings. Pinduoduo is known for products that copy a brand's design or name with a letter changed. Pakistan Customs treats these as counterfeits and seizes them.
  • Grade tricks. The lowest price in a listing often belongs to the smallest size, thinnest material or a version without the accessory in the photo. Read the variant list, or have the agent confirm it.
  • Seller reliability. Check sales volume and reviews with photos. Very new shops with a single cheap listing are riskier.
  • Domestic shipping. Free shipping on Pinduoduo means free to a Chinese address. The leg to Pakistan is separate.

Is a Pinduoduo item worth importing to Pakistan?

For a cheap item, usually not on its own. Once air freight and Pakistan customs are added, a product that costs very little in China is rarely worth the total, and the quality lottery makes it worse. Frequently asked questions

Is Pinduoduo available in Pakistan?

No. Pinduoduo is a Chinese domestic app with Chinese-only registration, payment and delivery. It does not ship to Pakistan or any country outside China.

Is Temu the international version of Pinduoduo?

Roughly. Both are run by PDD Holdings and share many sellers, but they are separate apps. Temu does not list Pakistan as a delivery country at the time of writing.

Can I pay Pinduoduo with a Pakistani card?

No. Pinduoduo accepts WeChat Pay, Alipay and Chinese bank cards only. An agent pays on your behalf and you pay the agent in rupees.

How do I buy from Pinduoduo from Pakistan?

Send the product link or a screenshot to an agent. The agent buys it in China, or finds the same item on 1688 or Taobao, and forwards it to you by air with customs cleared.

Why is Pinduoduo cheaper than Temu?

Pinduoduo prices are domestic Chinese prices with group-buy discounts and no international shipping built in. Temu prices include cross-border logistics and local operations.
