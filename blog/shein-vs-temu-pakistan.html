Shein vs Temu in Pakistan: Which One Delivers, and How?

By Muhammad Shamshad AslamUpdated October 23, 20268 min read
Quick answer

Both deliver to Pakistan. Temu runs a Pakistan site with rupee prices, free standard shipping in 5 to 13 business days after 1 to 3 days of processing, and 90-day returns with a free first return picked up from your door. Shein ships to Pakistan through its global site, with standard shipping of 7 to 11 business days, a small fee below a minimum order and free shipping above it. Both accept JazzCash and Easypaisa. We could not find cash on delivery for Pakistan on either.

Do Shein and Temu both deliver to Pakistan?

Yes, when we checked in October 2026. Temu has a dedicated Pakistan site at temu.com/pk-en with prices in rupees, its own Pakistan shipping page, return policy and terms. Shein does not have a separate Pakistan site, but its global site lists Pakistan as a shipping country, offers rupees as a display currency and has Pakistan-specific terms for its on-time delivery service.

So the question is not whether they deliver, but how: what each costs to ship, how long it takes, how you pay, and what happens if something does not fit.

How long does delivery take from Shein and Temu?

Close, on paper. Shein's shipping page, with Pakistan selected, showed standard shipping with an estimated window of 7 to 11 business days. Shein also says processing can be delayed by 2 to 4 days at times, and that pre-order or customised items take longer.

Temu's Pakistan shipping page lists 5 to 13 business days for standard shipping, after 1 to 3 days of processing. The final leg goes to a local carrier, so tracking may not update at every step.

Both count business days, which skip weekends. In calendar terms, plan for roughly two to three weeks from either. Shein also runs a free On-Time Delivery Service that gives Shein points if your order misses its expected date, with separate terms for Pakistan. On both sites, if the parcel never arrives, contact customer service within 90 days: of the order date for Temu, and of the payment date for Shein.

What does shipping cost on each?

Temu is simpler. Its Pakistan page says free standard shipping applies to all orders, with no minimum.

Shein uses a threshold. When we checked with Pakistan selected, standard shipping carried a small fee on orders below about US$14, roughly Rs 4,000 at the rate Shein listed, and was free above that. Shein notes that some items are excluded from free shipping offers, and cancelling part of an order can take you back under the threshold. Check the shipping line at checkout, because thresholds change during sales.

How do you pay Shein and Temu from Pakistan?

Both accept Pakistani mobile wallets, which is rare among Chinese sites. Temu's Pakistan site shows JazzCash and Easypaisa logos next to Visa, Mastercard, American Express, UnionPay, Apple Pay and Google Pay. Shein's How to Pay page has sections for JazzCash and Easypaisa: you choose the wallet at checkout, then approve the payment request inside your wallet app. It also takes credit and debit cards.

If you use a bank card on either, switch on international online payments in your banking app first. Your bank sets the exchange rate and any card charges.

We did not find cash on delivery for Pakistan on either site. Treat any page offering "Shein cash on delivery" or "Temu cash on delivery" in Pakistan as a reseller, not the platform.

Which has better returns from Pakistan?

Temu is more generous. Its Pakistan policy allows returns within 90 days of purchase, and the first return from each order is free, with the courier collecting from your door. You must send the package within 14 days of requesting the return. Worn or washed clothing, or clothing with tags or hygiene stickers removed, cannot be returned. Refunds to your original payment method take 5 to 14 business days, up to 30 days, and the original shipping fee and import tax are not refunded if the return is not Temu's fault.

Shein is stricter on the return policy page we were served. It accepts returns within 30 days of receipt for most items in new condition, but the only method listed was a self-return, where you pay the postage to your logistics provider and Shein does not reimburse it. Bodysuits, swimwear, lingerie, underwear, jewellery and beauty items are among the non-returnable categories. For faulty or damaged items, Shein asks you to contact customer service within 180 days of the order date. The return options in the app can differ by country, so check them on your order before you rely on a return.

For clothes, this makes sizing the real decision. Read our guide to Chinese clothes and shoe sizes before ordering from either.

Do you pay customs on a Shein or Temu parcel?

Possibly, depending on value. Under section 19C of the Customs Act, as set by the Finance Act 2025, post and courier parcels worth Rs 1,000 or less are duty-free. The budget bill had proposed Rs 500, but the Act set Rs 1,000. Above that, duty and taxes can apply.

Temu's Pakistan terms say that, where applicable, you act as the importer and Temu appoints a forwarding agent to pay duties for you. Any duty collected at checkout is an estimate, and you pay the difference if customs assesses more. We did not find a Pakistan-specific customs statement on Shein's pages, so expect any duty assessed to be collected before or at delivery. For how the amount is worked out, see customs duty from China to Pakistan.

Which is better for clothes, and which for everything else?

  • Clothing, shoes and fashion accessories: Shein has the deeper fashion range and size sections such as Curve. Temu also sells clothing, often cheaper, with easier returns.
  • Home, kitchen, tools and gadgets: Temu, which has a much wider general catalogue.
  • Small orders: Temu, because Shein charges shipping below its threshold.
  • Items you might return: Temu, for the 90-day window and free first return pickup.

How do you avoid fake Shein or Temu sellers in Pakistan?

  • Buy only through the official Shein or Temu app or website. On Temu, check that prices show in Rs.
  • Treat Facebook pages and WhatsApp numbers calling themselves "Shein Pakistan" or "Temu Pakistan" as resellers with their own terms. The platforms' return policies do not cover you.
  • Never pay a personal JazzCash, Easypaisa or bank account for a Shein or Temu order. Wallet payments on both sites are approved inside your wallet app after you check out on the platform.
  • Ignore messages asking for a "customs release fee" through a link. Check the order in the app and ask the courier for the customs assessment.

Common mistakes with Shein and Temu orders

  • Ordering clothes in your usual size. Check the size chart on each listing in centimetres.
  • Splitting a Shein order under the threshold. Each small order may pay its own shipping fee.
  • Removing tags before trying clothes on. Both sites refuse returns of clothing without tags or hygiene stickers.
  • Missing Temu's 14-day send-back rule. After you request a return, you have 14 days to hand it over.
  • Ordering perfume or aerosols. These are commonly restricted by air and may not ship.
