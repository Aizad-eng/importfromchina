Import From China to Peshawar: One Item, Delivered

By Muhammad Shamshad AslamUpdated October 23, 20266 min read
Quick answer

Yes, one item from China can be delivered to any address in Peshawar without an import licence of your own. It flies to Pakistan, clears Pakistan Customs and a courier delivers it to your home, shop or workshop. With our service you pay one rupee price once, before it ships, and delivery usually takes 7 to 15 days after the seller dispatches.

Can I get one item from China delivered in Peshawar?

Yes. A single item for your own use, or for your own shop or workshop, can come from China to a Peshawar address without a company or licence in your name. It travels by air, is cleared through Pakistan Customs and a courier brings it to the door. You do not have to visit the airport or a customs office.

Which airport serves Peshawar, and does it handle international cargo?

Bacha Khan International Airport, inside the Peshawar Cantonment and next to PAF Base Peshawar, is the city's airport, and it handles cargo. It is run by the Pakistan Airports Authority. Pakistan Civil Aviation Authority figures quoted on its Wikipedia page put cargo handled at 9,514 metric tons for July 2024 to June 2025, down about 14 percent on the year before.

Its international routes go to the Gulf, Oman, Qatar and Saudi Arabia. None went to China when we checked in October 2026, so a parcel from China enters Pakistan at another airport and reaches Peshawar through the courier network. That leg is already part of the price we quote.

Does my parcel come overland through the Karakoram Highway?

No. A single item through our service is shipped by air, cleared by Pakistan Customs and then delivered to Peshawar by courier. Whatever you have heard about the road link to China, it does not change how a one-item order travels or the window we quote.

How long does delivery to Peshawar take?

Usually 7 to 15 days after the seller dispatches. That is the same window we quote anywhere in Pakistan, and we do not promise a different number for Peshawar. The seller's own handling time comes first and depends on the listing. We give you an expected delivery date in writing when you pay.

Tracking often goes quiet while the parcel is between China and Pakistani customs. That is normal and not a sign that it is lost.

When does importing beat buying at Karkhano?

When the exact model you want is not on the shelves, or when you want it from an official brand store with its normal warranty terms. Karkhano Market, on the western side of the city in Hayatabad, is described on Wikipedia as Peshawar's largest business hub, with electronics, household goods, shoes, clothing and watches among what it sells. For common items, it is often quicker to buy there and you can see the item first.

Importing makes more sense for a specific model, a part with an exact number, or an item from a Chinese brand that no local shop carries. It works best for items worth around Rs 10,000 or more, because shipping from China usually costs Rs 5,000 to 20,000 depending on size and type, plus our fee. Before buying anything with a warranty, read warranty and after-sales when importing from China.

What single items suit Peshawar's workshops and industrial units?

Exact replacement parts and specialist tools. Wikipedia counts 646 industrial units in the Hayatabad Industrial Estate, in the city's western suburbs, though it notes that several are no longer in use. Some examples of single items that fit workshop and factory work:

  • A control board, sensor or display for a machine, ordered by the number on the old part.
  • A specific bearing, seal kit or gear for a machine, matched to its size code.
  • A part for a car or motorbike that local dealers cannot get. Our guide on importing car and bike spare parts shows how to match them.
  • A measuring tool such as a digital caliper, a clamp meter or a thermal camera for electrical work.

These are examples, not a claim about what people in Peshawar order. We bring one item, or a few different single items together. Quantities for a factory or a shop are a commercial import, which we do not handle. We do not do bulk or resale.

What can I not import to Peshawar this way?

The same items that are restricted anywhere in Pakistan. Drones, medicines and supplements, weapon lookalikes, loose lithium batteries, flammable liquids and phones each have their own rules, and some cannot be sent by air at all. The details are in what you cannot import from China to Pakistan.

How should I write a Peshawar address the courier can find?

Give the area, the exact block or street, a landmark and a phone that answers. Specific tips for Peshawar:

  • In Hayatabad, give the phase and sector as well as the street and house number.
  • In the old walled city, around Qissa Khwani and Chowk Yadgar, lanes are narrow and house numbers are not always used. Give the mohalla, the nearest mosque or chowk, and be ready to guide the rider by phone.
  • Peshawar Cantonment addresses can be behind checkpoints. Give a gate or reception point if the courier cannot drive in.
  • For a workshop or industrial unit, give the unit name, the person receiving it and a number for someone at the gate.
  • Keep your mobile on. Couriers usually call before they deliver and return parcels to the depot if nobody answers.
