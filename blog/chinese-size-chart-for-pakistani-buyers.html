Chinese Size Chart for Pakistani Buyers: Clothes and Shoes

By importfromchina.shop teamUpdated October 11, 20269 min read
Quick answer

Chinese clothing sizes are based on your body in centimetres, and a code like 170/88A means height 170 cm and chest 88 cm. Letter sizes from Chinese sellers usually run smaller than UK sizes, so a Chinese L can fit like a UK S. The reliable method is to measure your chest, waist, hips and foot length in cm, then match those numbers to the seller's own cm chart, not the letter. The tables below show how the codes compare with UK, EU and US charts.

Why do Chinese sizes seem to run small?

Because the labels mean different things. A UK or US letter size is whatever a brand decides M or L should be. Chinese sizing under the national standard GB/T 1335 is built from body measurements: height and chest (or waist) in centimetres, plus a body-shape letter. Many Chinese sellers then attach S, M, L and XL to those codes, and their L is often cut for a body that a UK brand would call S or M.

The fix is simple. Ignore the letter, measure yourself in centimetres, and compare those numbers with the seller's chart.

What does a size like 170/88A mean?

It has three parts:

  • 170 is height in cm. It sets the garment length.
  • 88 is chest girth in cm for tops (or waist girth for trousers). It sets the width.
  • A is the body type. The standard uses four types, Y, A, B and C, set by the difference between chest girth and waist girth.

Adult sizes step in fixed increments: 5 cm for height, 4 cm for chest and 2 cm for waist. So 175/92A is one step up from 170/88A in both length and width.

Trousers and jeans: what do the waist numbers mean?

Trousers from Chinese sellers usually come in one of two styles. Some use the GB/T style code, where the second number is the waist in cm, such as 170/74A. Others use waist numbers like 28, 30 or 32, which follow the inch sizes used on jeans worldwide. One inch is 2.54 cm, so:

Chinese shoe listings sometimes give a length in millimetres instead of a size, such as 240 or 250. That is roughly 24 or 25 cm. Some sellers mean the foot, others the inside of the shoe, so if a listing does not say, allow a few millimetres extra or ask.

How do you measure yourself in cm?

  1. Chest or bust: over thin clothing, arms relaxed at your sides, tape around the fullest part of the chest and level all the way round.
  2. Waist: around your natural waist, the narrowest part, without pulling the tape tight.
  3. Hips: around the widest part of the hips and seat.
  4. Height: barefoot against a wall.
  5. Foot length: tape paper to a hard floor, stand on it with your weight even, mark the heel and the tip of the longest toe, and measure between the marks. Do both feet and use the longer one. Measuring later in the day gives a more realistic figure.
  6. Garment check: lay a shirt that fits you well flat, measure across the chest from armpit to armpit, and double it. Compare that with a seller chart that lists garment measurements.

Save all of these in your phone's notes. You will use them for every order.

The most important question is whether the chart shows body measurements or garment measurements. A garment that measures 胸围 100 cm fits a body chest smaller than 100 cm, because clothes need room to move, and how much smaller depends on the cut. Compare a garment chart with your flat-lay shirt measurement, not your body. If the chart does not say which it is, ask the seller.

Should you size up?

If you fall between two sizes, size up. Nike's own chart says the same for shoes. For Chinese clothing, read the negative reviews too. If several buyers write that it runs small (偏小), go up one more. Loose fits and oversized cuts are the exception, so check whether the listing describes the style as oversized before sizing up.

Common sizing mistakes

  • Ordering your UK letter size. A Chinese M is often a UK XS or S.
  • Matching body measurements to a garment chart without allowing for ease.
  • Using the shoe number instead of the foot length. CN 40 is not guaranteed to fit like every EU 40.
  • Measuring over a jumper or pulling the tape tight.
  • Assuming you can swap sizes. Returning to China from Pakistan is rarely worth the cost, so get it right first time.

Frequently asked questions

What does 170/88A mean on Chinese clothes?

It means the garment is cut for a height of 170 cm and a chest of 88 cm, with an A body type, the common middle shape.

Is a Chinese L the same as a UK L?

Usually not. A Chinese L is often cut for a 92 cm chest, which sits around UK S on many UK charts.

How do I convert Chinese shoe sizes?

Measure your foot length in cm and match it to the seller's chart. Use the tables as a starting point, since brands differ by up to half a size.

Should I size up when ordering clothes from China?

If you are between sizes, yes. Also read the reviews, and go up again if buyers say it runs small.

What is 胸围 on a size chart?

It means chest or bust measurement. Check whether the chart gives body or garment measurements.

Can I exchange a wrong size from China?

Rarely in a practical way. Sending an item back to China from Pakistan usually costs too much, so measure carefully first.
