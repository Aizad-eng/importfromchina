How to Order From Alibaba in Pakistan, Step by Step

By Muhammad Shamshad AslamUpdated November 12, 20269 min read
Quick answer

To order from Alibaba in Pakistan, open a free buyer account, find a supplier that offers Trade Assurance, agree the exact item, price and shipping to your city in Alibaba's Message Center, then pay on Alibaba.com by card or bank transfer to Alibaba's own account. Track the parcel, pay any duty customs assesses, and confirm delivery only after checking the item.

How do you order from Alibaba in Pakistan?

You order from Alibaba in Pakistan the same way buyers anywhere do: you agree terms with a supplier on Alibaba.com and pay through Alibaba, not to the supplier directly. The platform is open to individuals, and you do not need a company to place an order. What changes for a Pakistani buyer is the payment step, the shipping cost to Pakistan and the customs bill at the end.

This guide is the full ordering process from start to finish. If your only question is whether a supplier will sell you a single piece, and what that costs to ship, read buying one item from Alibaba in Pakistan first. For the wider picture of duties, shipping and paperwork, see our guide on how to import from China to Pakistan.

Here is the whole order in eight steps:

  1. Create a free buyer account on Alibaba.com and set Pakistan as your country.
  2. Search for the product and shortlist suppliers that show Trade Assurance.
  3. Check the minimum order, the price for your quantity and the supplier's profile.
  4. Use Start order for a ready-to-ship listing, or Contact Supplier to ask questions and get an order drafted.
  5. Agree model, variant, quantity, shipping method and delivery address in writing inside Alibaba.
  6. Pay on Alibaba.com by card or by bank transfer to the account details Alibaba shows you.
  7. Track the parcel and deal with the courier or customs when it lands in Pakistan.
  8. Check the item, then confirm delivery or raise a claim within the protection period.

What do you need before you place the order?

You need four things ready before you message a supplier, because missing any of them is what usually stops an order halfway.

  • An Alibaba.com buyer account. It is free. Use an email you check often, since supplier replies and order updates go there.
  • A way to pay. Alibaba's Trade Assurance payment page lists credit and debit cards, digital wallets and bank transfers, with payment possible in more than 40 currencies when we checked in November 2026. For most people in Pakistan that means a Visa or Mastercard with international online use switched on in the banking app. Our guide on Alibaba Trade Assurance covers paying from Pakistan in more detail.
  • A full delivery address and a working mobile number. Couriers in Pakistan normally call before delivery, and a missing street or block number causes delays.
  • Exact product details. Model number, size, colour, plug type and voltage. China and Pakistan both use 220V, but some listings are built for other markets.

How do you find a supplier that will actually sell to you?

Search for the product, then narrow the results until only suppliers who can sell your quantity with protection are left. On Alibaba's own How It Works page, buyers are told to assess sellers by their badges, such as Gold Supplier, Verified Supplier and Trade Assurance Supplier, and to keep all communication inside the Message Center.

A practical shortlist looks like this:

  • Trade Assurance shown on the listing. Without it you have no Alibaba payment protection.
  • A minimum order you can meet. The price table shows the minimum. "1 piece" is what a single buyer needs. "1 set" or "1 carton" can mean many units.
  • Years on the platform, ratings and reviews. Read the low ratings, not just the score.
  • Response time and clear answers. A supplier who answers a technical question precisely is easier to deal with if something goes wrong.

Shortlist three suppliers, not one. Prices and shipping quotes for the same item can differ a lot, and a second option saves time if the first stops replying.

Should you click Start order or Contact Supplier?

Use Start order when the listing is ready to ship and the price and shipping are already shown for your country. Use Contact Supplier when anything needs confirming first. The two routes lead to the same protected checkout, but they start differently.

What should you agree with the supplier before paying?

Agree everything that affects what arrives and what it costs, and get it written into the order itself, not only in chat. If there is a dispute later, Alibaba looks at the order and the messages, so vague promises do not help you.

  • The exact item. Model, version, colour, size, plug and any accessories in the box.
  • Quantity and unit price. Confirm the price applies to your quantity, not to the minimum shown in the headline.
  • Shipping method and cost to your city. Express courier, air freight or post, and which company carries it.
  • Trade terms. Most single-parcel orders are delivered to your door with duty paid by you on arrival. If a supplier quotes EXW or FOB, shipping to Pakistan is not included in that price.
  • Dispatch date. Trade Assurance protects agreed shipping dates, so a written date matters.
  • Packing. For fragile items, ask how it will be packed.

Ask the seller for clear pictures of the exact variant if the listing photos are generic. That is a normal request and a reliable supplier will not mind.

How do you pay for an Alibaba order from Pakistan?

You pay inside Alibaba.com, at checkout, and nowhere else. Alibaba's Trade Assurance payment page says plainly that buyers should never pay outside the platform, and that bank transfers should go to the official bank details Alibaba provides for the order. Alibaba's How It Works page adds that you must pay on Alibaba.com for the order to qualify for Trade Assurance.

  1. Open the order from your Alibaba account and check the total, the items and the shipping line.
  2. Choose card or bank transfer. If paying by card, make sure international online payments are enabled and your card limit covers the amount.
  3. If paying by bank transfer, use only the beneficiary details shown on Alibaba's payment page for that order, never details sent in chat, by email or on a proforma invoice.
  4. Keep the payment receipt and the order number.

Alibaba's payment page also says a fee applies to each successful transaction, and your own bank may add charges for foreign currency payments. If a supplier offers a discount for paying their account directly, treat that as a warning sign. Our guide to Alibaba scams Pakistani buyers should know about explains why.

How does the order get shipped to Pakistan?

The supplier ships it, usually by international express courier for a single parcel, and gives you a tracking number in the order. Alibaba also offers its own logistics service, Alibaba.com Logistics, which its Trade Assurance page says has real-time tracking for most countries and regions and delay compensation if delivery misses the scheduled date.

For one parcel, shipping is often the expensive part. Express couriers price by actual or volumetric weight, whichever is higher, so a light but bulky box can cost more than you expect. Ask the supplier to quote two shipping methods so you can compare them before paying.

What happens at Pakistan customs?

The courier clears the parcel through Pakistan Customs, and you pay whatever duty and taxes customs assesses before delivery. Under section 19C of the Customs Act, post and courier parcels worth Rs 1,000 or less pay no duties or taxes. Above that, the whole value is dutiable. Customs works on the value of the goods plus shipping and insurance, so expect duty and sales tax on top of the price you paid.

A few points worth knowing:

  • The courier usually contacts you with the amount due before delivery. Ask for the customs assessment if the figure seems high.
  • Some goods need permission, such as medicines and drones, and branded copies can be seized.
  • Ordering many units of the same thing is a commercial import. That route needs an NTN, sales tax registration and a WeBOC account through Pakistan Single Window, and is a different process from a personal parcel.

When should you confirm delivery, and when should you open a dispute?

Confirm delivery only after you have opened the box and checked that the item matches the order. Alibaba's How It Works page says Trade Assurance protects your order for 30 days after you receive it. If the item is wrong, damaged or not working, raise the issue with the supplier first inside Alibaba, then escalate to Alibaba if you cannot agree.

For a strong claim, keep the order page, the chat, the courier receipt and your own pictures of the parcel and item as received. Do not confirm delivery to be polite because the supplier asks you to.

What are the most common ordering mistakes?

  • Paying outside Alibaba for a discount, which removes your protection.
  • Trusting the headline price when it applies only to large quantities.
  • Not checking the shipping line at checkout, where the default method may be slow or costly.
  • Forgetting customs in the budget, since duty is paid on arrival.
  • Agreeing changes only in chat and never getting them into the order.
  • Confirming delivery early, before checking the item.

Frequently asked questions

Can an individual order from Alibaba in Pakistan?

Yes. Alibaba.com accounts are free and open to individuals. You only need a company and import registration if you are importing commercial quantities in your own name.

Can I pay Alibaba with JazzCash or Easypaisa?

Not directly. Alibaba's checkout lists cards, digital wallets and bank transfers, and Pakistani buyers usually pay with an international Visa or Mastercard.

Does Alibaba deliver to my door in Pakistan?

Usually yes for parcels. The supplier ships by courier to your address, and the courier clears customs and collects any duty before delivery.

How long do I have to complain about an Alibaba order?

Alibaba says Trade Assurance protects an order for 30 days after you receive it. Raise problems inside Alibaba well before that.

Is it safe to pay a supplier's bank account if they ask?

No. Alibaba says to never pay outside the platform and to use only the bank details Alibaba gives for the order.

Do I pay customs on an Alibaba order?

Yes, if the parcel is worth more than Rs 1,000. Above that, customs charges duty and taxes on the whole value, and the courier collects it.
