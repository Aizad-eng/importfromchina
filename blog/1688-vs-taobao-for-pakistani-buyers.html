1688 vs Taobao: Which Should Pakistanis Use?

By Muhammad Shamshad AslamUpdated October 23, 20268 min read
Quick answer

For one item, Taobao is usually the better choice, because its listings sell single pieces at retail and its range of consumer goods is wider. 1688 can be cheaper for the same generic product, but many listings set minimum quantities and price tiers built for businesses. Neither delivers to Pakistan or takes Pakistani payment, so for both you need someone in China to buy for you.

1688 or Taobao: which should Pakistanis use?

Use Taobao when you want one finished consumer item, and use 1688 when the same generic item is listed there with a one-piece minimum at a lower price. Both belong to Alibaba Group and both are built for buyers inside China, but they work differently for someone who wants a single piece.

The short answer by situation:

  • Clothes, shoes, bags, hobby items, small brands: Taobao.
  • A generic gadget, tool or home item with a 1-piece minimum: check 1688 first, then compare with Taobao.
  • Raw materials, packaging or made-to-order goods: 1688 only, and these usually have real minimums of dozens or hundreds, so they are not for single buyers.
  • Anything branded: Taobao's brand stores, or a local authorised seller. Cheap branded goods on either site are usually fakes.
  • Something you can buy on Daraz or Temu at a fair price: buy it locally. Shipping one item from China costs more than the savings on cheap goods.

What is the difference between 1688 and Taobao?

1688 is a wholesale marketplace and Taobao is a retail one. Alibaba Group describes 1688.com as China's leading domestic wholesale marketplace, founded in 1999 and known as the product source for Chinese e-commerce, where wholesale suppliers sell at wholesale prices to small businesses and, more and more, to individual buyers. It describes Taobao, founded in 2003, as China's leading retail e-commerce platform for Chinese consumers, with individual Taobao stores alongside Tmall brand flagship stores.

In practice that means:

  • On 1688 you deal with factories and wholesalers. Listings show a starting quantity and a price ladder that falls as quantity rises.
  • On Taobao you deal with shops selling to the public. One piece is the normal order, and the price you see is the price for one.

Many of the same products appear on both, because Taobao shops often source from the same factories that list on 1688.

Can you buy just one piece on 1688?

Often yes, on stock items, but not on every listing. A WorldFirst help page on 1688 says many products have low minimums and that you can order as few as one or two items. Its August 2026 buying guide says many suppliers accept one to two units for standard stock items, and that minimums vary by supplier and product.

What you commonly see on a 1688 listing:

  • A one-piece minimum. Common on finished goods from trading companies. You pay the highest step of the price ladder, which is still often low.
  • A two or three piece minimum. Common on cheap accessories. Sometimes the seller agrees to one piece, sometimes not.
  • A mixed-order minimum. The seller wants a minimum total across several products. This does not work for one item.
  • Real factory minimums. Raw materials, packaging and custom goods with minimums of 50, 100 or more. These are not for a single buyer, and no agent can change that.

Taobao avoids this whole question. Listings are priced per piece, so if it is in stock, you can buy one.

Is 1688 cheaper than Taobao for one item?

Sometimes, but the gap is smaller than people expect when you buy one piece. 1688 prices are wholesale, and the WorldFirst guide reports buyers seeing savings of up to 40 percent against the same supplier's Alibaba.com listing. But the headline 1688 price is usually the price for large quantities. At one piece you pay the top tier, which can land close to the Taobao price.

For a buyer in Pakistan, the item price is also not the biggest number. Shipping one parcel from China, plus customs and the buying service fee, often costs more than the difference between the two sites. So pick the listing with the better seller and the exact variant you want, not the one that is a few yuan cheaper.

Where 1688 does win clearly is on generic items such as tools, phone accessories, home goods and replacement parts, where a factory listing with a one-piece minimum can undercut every Taobao shop selling the same thing.

Which has more variety for personal shoppers?

Taobao, by a wide margin, for anything with style, size or brand. Alibaba Group lists Taobao stores selling fashion, handicrafts and local specialties alongside Tmall flagship stores for brand-name products. That is where you find small Chinese clothing labels, hobby gear, collectibles, kitchen tools in many designs and items that never reach Pakistani shops.

1688 is stronger for plain, functional goods made in large numbers. Its listings tend to be factory-style, with less attention to sizes, styling and product pictures, because the buyer is expected to be a shop or a business.

Can you order from 1688 or Taobao directly from Pakistan?

No, not in a practical way, for either site. Both are built for buyers in China, and the overseas routes they have opened do not include Pakistan.

  • Taobao: its App Store listing, when we checked in October 2026, named the regions where its English version is available, including Malaysia, Singapore, Australia, Thailand and Cambodia. Pakistan was not among them, and we found no official direct delivery to Pakistan.
  • 1688: a WorldFirst help page says 1688 has expanded to overseas buyers in Japan, South Korea, Taiwan, Hong Kong, Macau and Vietnam. Its UK buying guide says most 1688 suppliers ship within China only and that payment for foreign buyers runs through a WorldFirst account set up for business use.
  • Payment: Pakistani cards, JazzCash, Easypaisa and Raast do not work on either site.

So for both, the route is the same: someone with a Chinese account and address buys the item, receives it in China and sends it on. Our step-by-step guides for buying from 1688 in Pakistan and buying from Taobao in Pakistan cover each site.

Which is safer to buy from, 1688 or Taobao?

Neither is risky if you choose the seller carefully, but the warning signs differ. On 1688, look for years on the platform, a repeat-buyer rate and a high sales count, and treat very new stores with a single product as a gamble. On Taobao, look at the shop's ratings, the number of sales on that exact listing and buyer reviews that mention size or quality.

On both sites, a famous brand at a fraction of its normal price is almost always a copy, and Pakistan Customs can seize counterfeit branded goods. Stick to unbranded goods, genuine Chinese brands or official Tmall brand stores.

Common mistakes when choosing between 1688 and Taobao

  • Comparing the 1688 quantity price with the Taobao single price. Compare the price for one piece on both.
  • Picking a listing with a minimum you cannot meet. Check the starting quantity before you fall for a price.
  • Ignoring the variant menu. One listing can hold dozens of colours, sizes and grades at different prices. Name the exact one.
  • Chasing a small saving on a cheap item. Shipping to Pakistan outweighs it. For cheap goods, Temu or Daraz is usually better.
  • Buying a cheap "branded" item. It is a copy, and customs can seize it.

Frequently asked questions

Is 1688 or Taobao better for buying one item?

Taobao, in most cases, because every listing is priced per piece. 1688 can be cheaper when a listing has a one-piece minimum, so it is worth comparing both.

Is 1688 cheaper than Taobao?

Often, for generic goods, because 1688 prices are wholesale. At a single piece you pay the top price tier, so the gap with Taobao is usually smaller than the listing suggests.

Does 1688 have a minimum order?

Many stock listings sell from one or two pieces, but some need more, and factory listings for materials or custom goods have minimums of dozens or hundreds.

Can I use Taobao in English from Pakistan?

Not officially. When we checked in October 2026, Taobao's App Store listing named the regions with its English version, and Pakistan was not one of them.

Do 1688 or Taobao ship to Pakistan?

No direct delivery that we could confirm. Most 1688 suppliers ship within China only, and Taobao is built for buyers in China, so you need a buyer there.

Can I pay 1688 or Taobao with JazzCash or a Pakistani card?

No. Neither accepts Pakistani wallets or cards for normal orders. A buyer in China pays the seller and you pay that service in rupees.
