How to Ask a Chinese Seller for a Sample

By Muhammad Shamshad AslamUpdated November 12, 20269 min read
Quick answer

Ask in the listing's chat, or use the sample option on the product page if there is one. Name the exact model and variant, say you want one sample, and ask for the sample price, shipping cost and dispatch date. Expect to pay for the sample and its courier. Pay through the platform, never by direct transfer, and plan for customs in Pakistan above Rs 1,000.

How do you ask a Chinese seller for a sample?

You ask on the platform, either by ticking the sample option on the listing or by messaging the seller. Alibaba's guide to requesting samples says that sometimes a sample is as simple as checking a box on the product page, where the supplier has already said it will send samples, and that otherwise you need to make the request yourself. It also notes that most suppliers will not suggest a sample first, so do not wait to be offered one.

For many single-item buyers in Pakistan, the sample is the order: you want one piece to use. The steps are the same either way. For how this fits the whole process, see our pillar guide on how to import from China to Pakistan.

What types of samples can you ask for?

Alibaba's guide describes four kinds of sample, and they differ a lot in time and cost:

TypeWhat it isSpeedSuits a single-item buyer?
Factory sampleAlready made to the maker's own specification, no changesCan ship very quicklyYes, this is usually what you want
Production sampleTaken from items already in mass productionQuick if production is runningYes, often the same as a stock unit
Custom sampleBuilt to your specificationSlow, with a high fail rate and several roundsRarely, and it costs more
Virtual sampleA rendering or 3D modelFastNo, you cannot use it

Alibaba also warns that samples may differ from mass-produced goods, because one-off units can be made on different equipment or with different materials. For a factory or production sample taken from stock, that gap is small.

What should a sample request include?

Give as much detail as you would for a full order. Alibaba's guide recommends describing the product fully and including the shipping address. For one sample, put these in the message:

  • The listing link and the exact model number.
  • The variant: colour, size, voltage, plug type and any accessories.
  • Quantity: one sample.
  • Where it is going: a Pakistani address, or a China address if someone will forward it.
  • Questions: sample price, shipping cost, dispatch date and the packed size and weight.

English: Hello. I would like to buy 1 sample of this product: [link]. Model [model], colour [colour], 220V with European plug. What is the sample price and the shipping cost to [Pakistan or China address]? When can you ship it? Please also tell me the package size and weight. I will order and pay on the platform.

Simple Chinese: 您好，我想买一个样品：[链接]。型号[型号]，颜色[颜色]，220V，欧规插头。请问样品费多少？寄到[巴基斯坦／中国地址]运费多少？什么时候可以发货？也请告诉我包装尺寸和重量。我会在平台上下单付款。

Who pays for a sample and its shipping?

Usually you do. Alibaba's guide says the seller sets the sample price, that some charge a lot because of the work involved and some charge nothing in the hope of a larger order later, and that shipping small parcels over long distances is usually expensive. For a cheap item, the courier cost can be several times the sample price.

The guide also says buyers can ask for the sample cost to be deducted from a later, larger order. That only applies if a larger order is genuinely coming. If you want one piece for your own use, say so and pay the sample price. Hinting at a large order you will not place is not honest, and suppliers often see through it.

Is a sample the same as buying one piece?

Often it is the same item at a different price. For stock products, a factory sample and a single unit come off the same shelf. The difference is the price: some suppliers charge a sample price above their single-unit tier, and some Ready to Ship listings sell one piece at a fixed listed price without any sample request. Alibaba says Ready to Ship listings show one non-negotiable price and the shipping fee upfront, and must leave the facility within 15 days. If the listing allows a quantity of one, buying one piece is often simpler than asking for a sample.

How do you pay for a sample safely?

Pay on the platform with a method it offers. Alibaba's sample guide recommends paying with a method on Alibaba.com rather than a third-party payment platform. Its Trade Assurance page says orders made with a supplier on Alibaba.com and paid through Alibaba.com are covered, with the money held in escrow until you confirm the product arrived. Ask the supplier to send the sample as an Alibaba order with the price, variant and dispatch date written on it.

Do not send the sample fee by WeChat, direct Alipay transfer or bank transfer to a personal account, even if the amount is small. A request to pay a "small sample fee" outside the platform is one of the easiest ways for a fake seller to collect money.

Do you pay customs on a sample in Pakistan?

Yes, unless it is worth Rs 1,000 or less. Under section 19C of the Customs Act, as changed by the Finance Act 2025, no duties or taxes are charged on post or courier parcels worth Rs 1,000 or less. Above that, the whole value is dutiable, and a sample is treated like any other parcel. Courier parcels are assessed at normal tariff rates for that product under the e-commerce rules, not a flat courier rate. A "free" sample still has a value for customs.

How long does a sample take to reach Pakistan?

It depends on the sample type and the shipping method. A factory sample from stock can leave within days, a Ready to Ship listing must leave within 15 days under Alibaba's rules, and a custom sample can take weeks with several rounds of changes. After dispatch, express courier to Pakistan is the fastest option and also the most expensive for one small parcel. Customs clearance in Pakistan adds time on top. Ask the supplier for a dispatch date in writing and count from that, not from the day you paid.

What should you check when the sample arrives?

Check it against the order on the day it arrives, while you can still raise a problem. Keep the outer box and packing until you are satisfied. Then go through:

  • Model number and labels on the item and box, against the listing.
  • Variant. Colour, size, voltage and plug type should match what was written on the order.
  • Accessories. Cables, adapters, manuals and any agreed extra.
  • Damage. Note any marks or breakage in transit.
  • Basic function. Switch it on or use it as intended.

If something is wrong, tell the seller in the platform chat first, with clear pictures and the order number. On Alibaba, a Trade Assurance order that arrives defective, incorrect or damaged is eligible for a refund claim. A message on WeChat alone leaves no record the platform can use.

What mistakes do people make when asking for samples?

  • Asking for a free sample while planning to buy only one.
  • Not naming the variant, and receiving the default plug or voltage.
  • Paying the sample fee off the platform.
  • Forgetting the packed size. A large box costs more to fly than the item suggests.
  • Asking for a custom sample when a stock unit would do.
  • Ignoring customs. Anything above Rs 1,000 is dutiable.

Frequently asked questions

Do Chinese sellers give free samples?

Some do, usually to buyers they expect to place a large order. Alibaba's guide says sample prices are set by the seller, and shipping is usually expensive. For one piece for your own use, expect to pay.

How do I say sample in Chinese?

Sample is 样品 (yangpin), and the sample fee is 样品费. A simple request is 我想买一个样品 (I would like to buy one sample).

Can I get a sample shipped directly to Pakistan?

Many Alibaba suppliers can ship a sample to Pakistan by express courier. 1688 and Taobao sellers usually ship only inside China, so their samples need a forwarder or a buying service.

Is a sample covered by Trade Assurance?

Trade Assurance covers orders made with a supplier on Alibaba.com and paid through Alibaba.com. Ask the supplier to send the sample as an Alibaba order and pay there.

Will I pay customs on a sample from China?

Yes, if it is worth more than Rs 1,000. Post and courier parcels at or below Rs 1,000 are free of duties and taxes under section 19C. Above that the whole value is dutiable.

Is it better to buy one piece than to ask for a sample?

If the listing allows a quantity of one, buying it directly is often simpler. A sample request makes sense when the listing shows only a high minimum order.
