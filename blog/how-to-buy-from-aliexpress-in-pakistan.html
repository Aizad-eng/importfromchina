How to Buy From AliExpress in Pakistan (And Why Orders Fail)

By importfromchina.shop teamUpdated October 3, 20267 min read
Quick answer

AliExpress does deliver to Pakistan for most listings, but buyers hit four problems: Pakistani cards get declined at checkout, delivery takes 30 to 60 days, parcels get held at customs for duty, and there are no local returns. If you only want one item, a Pakistan-based sourcing service that buys it for you and delivers duty-paid in 7 to 15 days is usually faster and less stressful.

Does AliExpress ship to Pakistan?

Yes. Most AliExpress sellers offer shipping to Pakistan through AliExpress Standard Shipping, Cainiao or China Post. Some listings exclude Pakistan, which you only discover at checkout. Delivery is by ordinary post or a consolidator, not a courier like DHL, so it is slow and tracking often stops at "departed country of origin".

How do I order from AliExpress from Pakistan?

  1. Create an account with your Pakistani address. Use the exact format your local post office recognises, including the area and city.
  2. Check the listing ships to Pakistan. Set your country in the top bar and look for "Ships to Pakistan" under the price.
  3. Pay with a Visa or Mastercard that has international transactions enabled. Call your bank first to switch this on, or the payment fails silently.
  4. Wait. Typical delivery is 30 to 60 days. Keep the tracking number.
  5. If the parcel is held at customs, Pakistan Post or the courier contacts you to pay duty before release.

Why does my card get declined on AliExpress?

This is the most common failure. Reasons, roughly in order:

  • International transactions are switched off on the card by default. Most Pakistani banks require you to enable them through the app or a phone call.
  • The bank blocks the merchant category or the country as a fraud precaution.
  • The card is a local-only debit card, often PayPak, which does not work on foreign sites at all.
  • AliExpress itself flags a first order from a new account in Pakistan and rejects it.

Pakistani buyers cannot use PayPal, because PayPal does not operate in Pakistan. JazzCash, Easypaisa and Raast are not accepted on AliExpress.

Do I pay customs duty on AliExpress orders in Pakistan?

Often, yes. Pakistan has no meaningful duty-free threshold for commercial parcels. Small, low-value items sometimes pass without assessment, but anything that looks like electronics, cosmetics or clothing is likely to be held and assessed. You then pay duty, sales tax and sometimes a handling fee before the parcel is released. The amount is not known until the parcel arrives, which is the part most buyers dislike. What happens if the item is wrong or never arrives?

AliExpress buyer protection lets you open a dispute if the item does not arrive within the promised window or does not match the listing. Refunds for non-delivery are usually granted. Refunds for wrong or faulty items often require you to ship it back to China at your own cost, which for a cheap item is not worth doing. There is no local return or exchange in Pakistan.

Frequently asked questions

Is AliExpress available in Pakistan?

Yes, AliExpress accepts Pakistani accounts and addresses, and most sellers ship to Pakistan. Payment and delivery time are the usual problems, not availability.

Which Pakistani cards work on AliExpress?

Visa and Mastercard debit or credit cards with international transactions enabled. PayPak cards do not work. Enable international use through your bank's app before ordering.

How much is customs duty on AliExpress parcels in Pakistan?

It depends on the item. Duty, sales tax and advance income tax together commonly add 25 to 60 percent of the item value. You find out the exact figure only when the parcel is assessed.

Can I track an AliExpress parcel in Pakistan?

Partly. Tracking works until the parcel leaves China and often resumes once Pakistan Post scans it. The gap in between can be weeks.

Is it cheaper to use AliExpress directly or a sourcing service?

For a very cheap item that clears customs unnoticed, AliExpress direct can be cheaper. For anything worth more than a few thousand rupees, a service that delivers duty-paid in 7 to 15 days usually costs about the same once you add duty and saves weeks and the payment hassle.
