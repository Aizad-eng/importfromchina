AliExpress Item Won't Ship to Pakistan? Your Options Explained

By Muhammad Shamshad AslamUpdated October 17, 20269 min read
Quick answer

When an AliExpress item will not ship to Pakistan, the seller has usually not included Pakistan in that listing's shipping options, or the item, such as a loose battery or a liquid, cannot go by the methods the seller uses. Check that your ship-to country is Pakistan and your address is complete, try other variants, then look for the same item from another seller. If nobody ships it, Temu, Daraz or a single-item import service buying from 1688 or Taobao are the realistic routes.

What does it mean when AliExpress says an item can't be delivered to Pakistan?

On AliExpress, shipping is set per listing by the seller, not by AliExpress for the whole site. When a listing does not cover your destination, the product page or checkout shows a message that the item cannot be delivered to your selected address, and you cannot pay. The exact wording varies between the app and the website.

The message is about the listing and your destination. It is not about your card, your balance or your account standing. Payment problems show up later, at the payment step, and look different. If your card is the issue, see our guide on how to buy from AliExpress in Pakistan.

AliExpress does deliver to Pakistan for most listings. When we checked in October 2026, the AliExpress site opened from Pakistan showed prices in rupees. So one blocked listing does not mean AliExpress has stopped serving Pakistan.

Why do AliExpress sellers not ship some items to Pakistan?

Sellers build shipping templates in their seller panel. According to a marketplace integration guide for AliExpress sellers, when a seller creates a template they pick a delivery method and choose which countries it is available for. Pakistan is simply left out of some templates. Common reasons, which AliExpress does not list officially, are:

  • Carrier coverage and cost. The seller's usual logistics partner may not serve Pakistan at a price that works for a cheap item.
  • Restricted contents. Loose lithium batteries, power banks, liquids, aerosols, strong magnets and chemicals face air transport rules. Many sellers turn off destinations rather than deal with them.
  • Size and weight. Bulky items can exceed international parcel limits.
  • Stock held outside China. Some listings offer stock in warehouses in other countries. Those options commonly serve only their own region.
  • Seller choice. Some sellers only target a few markets and never add new ones.

How do you check your AliExpress settings for Pakistan?

  1. At the top of the site, or in the app's settings, set the ship-to country to Pakistan. Prices should show in rupees.
  2. Open your address book and check the default address. Use your full house and street details, area, city and province, and a mobile number you answer.
  3. Avoid special characters and Urdu script in the address fields.
  4. Reload the product page after changing anything. The shipping section updates only after a refresh.
  5. Try the desktop site if the app keeps showing an old setting, or log out and back in.

If other listings ship to you fine, your settings are not the problem. Move on to the listing itself.

A good habit is to check the shipping section on the product page before you compare prices or read reviews. Once Pakistan is set, the page shows whether the item ships, the shipping cost and an estimated delivery date. Each listing sets its own, so a cheap item with a high shipping line can cost more delivered than a dearer one that ships free.

Does the variant or the ships-from option change anything?

Often, yes. A single AliExpress listing can hold many variants, and they do not always share the same stock or shipping. A plug type, a bundle with a battery, or a larger size can each have different shipping rules. Some listings also offer a choice of where the item ships from. An option dispatched from a warehouse in Europe or elsewhere is commonly limited to that region, while the China option may cover Pakistan.

Click through every variant and every ships-from option before you give up on a listing. If one combination ships to Pakistan and has the parts you need, that is the simplest fix.

Can you ask the seller to ship to Pakistan?

You can message the seller through AliExpress and ask whether they can add a shipping method for Pakistan. Some will, especially for higher-value items. Keep a few rules in mind:

  • Ask them to make the change on the listing so you can check out normally on AliExpress.
  • Do not pay outside AliExpress, whatever the seller suggests. Buyer protection only covers orders placed and paid on the platform.
  • Get the delivery estimate and shipping method in writing in the chat, so you have a record for any dispute.

Is the battery the real problem?

If every seller of an item refuses Pakistan, check whether it has a loose battery. Air rules treat a power bank, a spare battery or a tool sold with a separate battery pack very differently from a device with the battery built in. Our guide to lithium battery shipping rules explains the three categories. Often you can buy the device without the spare battery and source the battery locally.

Also check whether the item is one Pakistan bans or restricts, such as drones, counterfeit branded goods or medicines. No seller or service should ship those without the right permission.

How do you get the same item another way, step by step?

  1. Save the AliExpress link, plus screenshots of the variant, size, model number and what is in the box.
  2. Search Temu Pakistan and Daraz with the plain product description. If you find it at a sensible price, that is usually the easiest route.
  3. Search 1688 or Taobao by image. Many AliExpress sellers also sell on those Chinese sites. Our guide on image search on Taobao and 1688 shows how.
  4. Compare listings by specs, not photos. Check dimensions, material and included parts.
  5. Get a delivered price in rupees for each route, including shipping and customs, and compare totals.

Common mistakes when an AliExpress item won't ship

  • Assuming it is a card problem. The shipping block appears before payment. Fixing your card will not change it.
  • Not checking every variant. One plug type or bundle may ship when another does not.
  • Paying a seller off-platform. That removes buyer protection completely.
  • Ignoring customs. Post and courier parcels worth Rs 1,000 or less are duty-free under section 19C of the Customs Act. Above that, duty can apply. See customs duty from China to Pakistan.
  • Importing a cheap item at high shipping cost. Compare the delivered total with a local price first.

Frequently asked questions

Why does AliExpress say this item can't be delivered to my address in Pakistan?

The seller's shipping options for that listing do not include Pakistan, or the item cannot travel by the methods the seller uses. It is not a card or account problem.

How do I change my AliExpress shipping country to Pakistan?

Set the ship-to country at the top of the website or in the app settings, then reload the product page. Prices should show in rupees once Pakistan is selected.

Can I ask an AliExpress seller to ship to Pakistan?

Yes, message the seller through AliExpress and ask them to add a shipping method for Pakistan. Never agree to pay outside AliExpress, because that removes buyer protection.

Why won't any AliExpress seller ship a power bank to Pakistan?

Power banks count as loose lithium batteries under air rules, which makes them hard and costly to ship. Devices with a built-in battery are much easier.

What can I do if an item on AliExpress doesn't ship to Pakistan?

Try other variants and sellers, then check Temu Pakistan and Daraz. If nobody ships it, a single-item import service can buy the same item from 1688 or Taobao and deliver it.

Is it worth importing a cheap AliExpress item through a service?

Usually not. Shipping one item from China typically costs Rs 5,000 to 20,000, so a service makes sense for items worth around Rs 10,000 or more.
