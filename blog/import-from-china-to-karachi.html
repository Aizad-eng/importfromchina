How to Import One Item From China to Karachi

By importfromchina.shop teamUpdated October 3, 20266 min read
Quick answer

Karachi is Pakistan's main gateway for goods from China. A single item flies into Jinnah International Airport, clears Pakistan Customs, and a local courier delivers it to your Karachi address, commonly within 7 to 15 days after the seller dispatches. Heavy or bulky goods arrive by sea through Karachi Port or Port Qasim, but for one item air is almost always the practical route. A single-item service gives you one rupee price for all of it, paid once.

Why is Karachi the easiest city to receive goods from China?

Karachi handles the largest share of Pakistan's imports. Jinnah International Airport has the biggest air cargo volume in the country, and the two seaports, Karachi Port and Port Qasim, receive the containers that carry bulk goods from China. Most consolidated air lines from Guangzhou, Shenzhen and Yiwu land in Karachi first. For a Karachi buyer, that means the parcel does not need a second road leg after landing. It clears customs in the city and goes straight to a local courier.

How does one item travel from China to Karachi?

  1. Seller dispatch. The seller on 1688, Taobao, Alibaba or AliExpress sends the item to a consolidation warehouse in China.
  2. Air cargo to Karachi. The parcel is loaded with others and flown into Jinnah International Airport.
  3. Customs clearance. Pakistan Customs assesses the parcel. For an individual, the courier or agent clears it using your CNIC.
  4. Local delivery. A Pakistani courier delivers to Clifton, DHA, Gulshan, North Nazimabad, Bahria Town Karachi or wherever you are in the city.

Should I use sea freight because Karachi has the ports?

Almost never for one item. Sea freight is priced per cubic metre, with a minimum of roughly 1 CBM, and fixed port and documentation fees make anything under that uneconomical. Sea LCL also takes 25 to 45 days. Living next to the port does not change the maths. The exception is a genuinely heavy single item, such as a piece of gym equipment or a large appliance, where air freight by weight becomes expensive. Even then, the port fees often cancel the saving. Ask for a delivered price both ways before deciding.

How long does delivery to Karachi take?

At the time of writing, 7 to 15 days door to door after dispatch is typical for consolidated air freight, including customs. Karachi addresses are sometimes at the quick end of that range because there is no onward road leg. Allow extra time around Chinese New Year, late January to mid February, and in the weeks after the 11.11 and 618 sales when Chinese logistics are slower.

What do Karachi buyers commonly order from China?

Karachi has Saddar's electronics market, Tariq Road for fashion and a strong trading culture, so local stock is wide. People still import when they want a specific model, a better grade or something not sold locally. Common examples:

  • Electronics and accessories: earbuds, smartwatches, chargers, keyboards, camera gear.
  • Specialist and professional tools: workshop tools, lab and medical devices for personal use, fishing gear.
  • Home items: small appliances, lighting, organisers, decor seen on Taobao.
  • Fashion, bags and shoes in specific styles and sizes.
  • Hobby items: model kits, RC parts (not drones), board games.

What does it cost to get one item to Karachi?

The item price, the freight and Pakistan customs duty and taxes. Duty and taxes depend on the item's tariff classification and commonly add 25 to 60 percent of the customs value. Rates change with budgets and SROs, so confirm the current figure or get a delivered price. Freight is based on actual or volumetric weight, whichever is higher. What do I need to receive a parcel in Karachi?

  • A complete address with area, block, street and house or flat number. Karachi addresses are long; include a landmark.
  • A mobile number that answers calls from unknown numbers. Couriers call before delivery.
  • Your CNIC number for customs clearance.
  • Someone at home, or an agreed alternative address, because couriers attempt delivery during working hours.

Frequently asked questions

How long does a parcel from China take to reach Karachi?

By consolidated air freight, commonly 7 to 15 days door to door after dispatch, including customs. Karachi is often at the quicker end because the parcel lands in the city.

Is sea freight cheaper for a single item because Karachi has ports?

Rarely. Sea is priced per cubic metre with a minimum of about 1 CBM plus fixed port fees, so a single small item costs more by sea than by air and takes 25 to 45 days.

Do I need to visit the airport or port in Karachi to collect my item?

No. The courier or agent clears customs with your CNIC and a local courier delivers to your door.

Is there anything to pay on delivery in Karachi?

Not with a single-item service. You pay one price before shipping. Ordering direct from AliExpress or Alibaba can mean a duty bill from the courier on arrival.
