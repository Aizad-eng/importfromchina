Do I Need an NTN to Import One Item for Personal Use?

By Muhammad Shamshad AslamUpdated October 23, 20268 min read
Quick answer

No. To receive one item for personal use by courier or post, you normally need only your CNIC, and the courier or agent files the customs declaration. An NTN is needed when you import commercially in your own name, because a Pakistan Single Window subscription requires an NTN, FTN or STRN. For an individual, the NTN is the CNIC number once you e-enrol on FBR's IRIS portal.

Do you need an NTN to import one item for personal use?

No, not for one item for yourself sent by courier or post. Your CNIC is what the courier asks for, and the courier or its clearing agent files the customs declaration under its own registration. An NTN becomes necessary when you import goods commercially in your own name, file your own goods declarations or subscribe to the Pakistan Single Window.

That said, an NTN is not hard to get for an individual. The FBR uses your 13-digit CNIC as your NTN once you e-enrol on its IRIS portal. So the question is less about whether you can get one and more about whether your import needs one.

What does a courier ask for when you receive a personal parcel?

A copy of your CNIC, plus the shipping paperwork. DHL Pakistan's import guide, dated 30 July 2026, lists these documents for individual recipients:

  • CNIC copy, for identity and parcel verification. DHL says the name on your airway bill must match your CNIC exactly.
  • Airway bill, the transport document showing weight, size and route.
  • Commercial invoice, or a purchase receipt, as proof of the value.
  • Letter of authorisation, with a CNIC copy, only if someone else collects the parcel for you.

No NTN appears on that list. The same guide separately says commercial importers must have an NTN to import goods, and lists NTN and STRN among the documents for business imports.

Who actually files the customs declaration for your parcel?

The courier, Pakistan Post or the agent handling the shipment, not you. DHL's guide says DHL Express Pakistan handles customs clearance on your behalf as a licensed clearing agent, and contacts you by email or SMS if customs needs duty or extra documents.

This is why an individual does not need a customs login. The declaration is filed in the clearing agent's system, using your CNIC and the invoice to identify the consignee and the value. When do you need an NTN to import?

When the import is commercial and you are the importer of record. DHL's guide lists what a commercial importer is expected to provide:

  • A commercial invoice on letterhead, with buyer, seller and HS codes
  • A packing list
  • The bill of lading or airway bill
  • NTN, which it calls mandatory for filing customs declarations for imports
  • STRN, the sales tax registration number
  • The most recent sales tax return
  • A certificate of origin, if you want trade agreement benefits
  • A financial instrument issued by an authorised bank

The other trigger is filing in your own name on the Pakistan Single Window. PSW's subscription page lists an NTN, FTN or STRN as a requirement to subscribe. If you plan to import goods to sell, that is the route, and it is a different job from receiving one item for yourself.

What is the difference between NTN, STRN, WeBOC and PSW?

They are four separate things that often get mixed up. This table shows what each one is and whether a person receiving one personal item needs it.

Is your CNIC already your NTN?

Effectively, yes, once you register. The FBR's income tax registration page says that for individuals, the 13-digit CNIC will be used as the NTN or registration number, and that individuals are treated as registered when they e-enrol on the IRIS portal. E-enrolment gives you a login and password.

Registration and filing are different. Being registered gives you an NTN. Filing your income tax return is what puts you on the Active Taxpayers List, which affects some withholding tax rates, including the advance tax on foreign card payments explained in our guide to tax on international card payments. Neither is needed to receive a personal parcel.

If you do need to import in your own name, how do you register on PSW?

You subscribe on psw.gov.pk. These steps follow PSW's subscription page when we checked in October 2026:

  1. Get your NTN first by e-enrolling on IRIS. Note the email address you used, because PSW expects the same email as in IRIS.
  2. Check your SIM. PSW needs the SIM number of a mobile connection registered in your own name.
  3. Enter your details on the subscription form: NTN, FTN or STRN, CNIC, mobile number and email. A company also enters its SECP registration number or CUIN.
  4. Pay the fee. PSW states a one-time, non-refundable subscription fee of Rs 500, paid through a system-generated payment slip ID.
  5. Complete biometric verification at a NADRA e-Sahulat franchise, with your CNIC and application ID. Only the subscriber can do this step.
  6. Set your password from the link PSW emails with your user ID, then register with customs from inside the portal.

Our guide to the Pakistan Single Window covers what you can do once you are in, including the Single Declaration and bank linking.

Does the value of the item change whether you need an NTN?

It changes the duty, not the NTN question. Section 19C of the Customs Act, as amended by the Finance Act 2025, says that where the value of goods imported through post or courier does not exceed Rs 1,000, no duties and taxes shall be demanded, subject to conditions set by the Board. Before that amendment the limit was Rs 5,000. The customs part of FBR's budget 2026-27 salient features, which we read in October 2026, did not list a change to it.

Above Rs 1,000, customs duty and taxes can apply to a personal parcel, but they are assessed and collected through the courier or agent. You still do not need an NTN. Couriers do not always agree on the figure in their own material. DHL's July 2026 guide gives Rs 1,000 in one place and Rs 500 in another, so go by the law as amended.

What if your item is a phone, medicine or other regulated product?

Then the extra requirement is usually a permit or registration, not an NTN. DHL's guide gives two examples. Mobile devices beyond a personal quantity need clearance from the Pakistan Telecommunication Authority, and unapproved devices are blocked by its DIRBS system. Medicines and medical devices fall under the Drug Regulatory Authority of Pakistan, and DHL says individuals may bring in up to a 90-day supply of medication for personal use without a permit. Check the current rule with the regulator for anything in these categories.

Quantity can also change the route. A personal import starts to need commercial paperwork when the goods look like trade rather than personal use. One item, or a few different single items for you or your family, is personal. Several identical units, a range of sizes of the same product, or goods invoiced to a business look commercial, and commercial goods need commercial clearance under an NTN. We explain where customs draws that line in our guide to personal use vs commercial import.

Common mistakes about NTNs and personal imports

  • Registering for sales tax just to receive one parcel. It is not needed for a personal item and brings filing duties of its own.
  • A name on the airway bill that does not match the CNIC. DHL warns this halts processing.
  • Typing the CNIC wrongly on the order. The courier uses it to identify you.
  • Assuming an NTN makes the parcel duty-free. Duty depends on the goods and their value, not on whether you have an NTN.
  • Using a personal-parcel route for stock you plan to sell. Commercial goods need commercial clearance.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an NTN to receive a parcel from China?

No, not for a personal parcel. The courier or agent clears it using your CNIC. An NTN is needed for commercial imports in your own name.

Is my CNIC my NTN?

For individuals, yes once you register. The FBR says the 13-digit CNIC is used as the NTN after you e-enrol on its IRIS portal.

Do I need to register on WeBOC or PSW to import one item?

No. The courier or clearing agent files the declaration. A PSW subscription, which needs an NTN, FTN or STRN, is for people filing their own declarations.

What documents does DHL ask for on a personal parcel in Pakistan?

DHL's July 2026 guide lists a CNIC copy, the airway bill and a commercial invoice, plus a letter of authorisation if someone else collects it.

Is there a duty-free limit for personal parcels?

Under section 19C, as amended in 2025, goods worth Rs 1,000 or less sent by post or courier are not charged duties and taxes, subject to the Board's conditions.

When does an import need an NTN?

When it is commercial, meaning goods for trade imported in your own name. Commercial clearance needs an NTN, usually an STRN, and a fuller set of documents.
