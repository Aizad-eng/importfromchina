How to Buy From 1688 in Pakistan (One Item, Without a Chinese Account)

By importfromchina.shop teamUpdated October 3, 20267 min read
Quick answer

1688.com is Alibaba's wholesale site for the Chinese domestic market. It is in Chinese, takes only Chinese payment methods, and sellers ship only within China, so you cannot order from Pakistan directly. Pakistani buyers use an agent who has a Chinese account and address, buys the item, receives it in China and forwards it by air. Many 1688 listings sell single pieces, and prices are typically 20 to 40 percent below Alibaba.com.

What is 1688 and how is it different from Alibaba.com?

1688.com is owned by Alibaba Group, but it is not Alibaba.com. Alibaba.com is the English-language site built for foreign buyers, with Trade Assurance, card payments and sellers who quote export prices. 1688 is the Chinese-language site that Chinese shop owners, Taobao sellers and small factories use to buy stock. The same supplier often lists the same product on both sites, and the 1688 price is typically 20 to 40 percent lower because it is a domestic price with no export margin built in. That price gap is the whole reason Pakistani buyers ask about 1688.

Can I order from 1688 directly from Pakistan?

In practice, no. Four things block a direct order:

  • Language. Listings, filters, seller chat and the checkout are all in Chinese. Browser translation lets you read, but not negotiate or confirm a variant.
  • Registration. An account needs a Chinese mobile number for verification, and foreign numbers are unreliable.
  • Payment. 1688 accepts Alipay and Chinese bank cards. Pakistani Visa, Mastercard, JazzCash, Easypaisa and Raast do not work.
  • Shipping. Sellers ship to Chinese addresses only. There is no option to enter a Pakistani address.

Some foreign buyers get around the first two with a Chinese friend's account. The last two still stop them. The normal route is an agent.

Does 1688 have a minimum order quantity?

Often, but less than people fear. Every 1688 listing shows a 起批量 (minimum batch) and a price ladder that falls as quantity rises. What you commonly see:

  • 1件起批 (from 1 piece). Very common for finished goods, especially from trading companies and stock holders. You pay the top tier of the price ladder, which is still usually below AliExpress.
  • 2 or 3 pieces minimum. Common on cheap accessories. An agent can sometimes ask the seller to sell one, and sometimes cannot.
  • 混批 (mixed batch). The seller accepts any mix of items as long as the total meets a value or piece count. Useless for a single item.
  • 拿样 (sample). Some factory listings allow a one-piece sample at a higher price. For a single-item buyer, the sample price is the real price.
  • Genuine factory MOQs of 50, 100 or 500 on raw materials, packaging and made-to-order goods. These are not for single buyers, and no agent can change that.

So the realistic question is not whether 1688 has MOQs but whether the specific listing you found sells one piece. Most consumer goods do.

How does a Pakistani buyer get one item from 1688?

  1. Find the listing and copy the link or the share code from the 1688 app. If you cannot find it, send a photo instead. See how to copy and share a product link from Chinese apps.
  2. Send the link to an agent on WhatsApp with the exact colour, size or model you want.
  3. The agent checks stock and the minimum quantity, and sends you one price in rupees for the item delivered to your door in Pakistan, with the fee stated.
  4. You pay once, before anything ships, by bank transfer, Raast, JazzCash or Easypaisa.
  5. The agent buys it with Alipay, the seller ships it to the agent's address in China, and the agent forwards it by air with customs cleared.
  6. It reaches you in roughly 7 to 15 days after it leaves China. Nothing to pay on delivery.

How do I find an item on 1688 without reading Chinese?

  • Open 1688.com in Chrome and let it translate the page. Searching in English returns poor results, so translate the product name into Chinese with Google Translate first and paste that.
  • Use the camera icon in the 1688 app to search by photo. A screenshot from AliExpress, Daraz or a video usually finds the same item.
  • Many AliExpress and Daraz sellers buy from 1688. If you already have a link to the item elsewhere, an agent can usually locate the 1688 source for you.

What should I watch out for on 1688?

  • Variants. One listing can hold dozens of colours, sizes and grades at different prices. Name the exact one.
  • Seller signals. Look for years on the platform, the 回头率 (repeat buyer rate) and badges such as 实力商家 (verified strength merchant). Prefer sellers with high volume.
  • Pre-sale listings marked 预售 ship days or weeks after ordering.
  • Branded goods at a fraction of retail are replicas and will be seized by Pakistan Customs.
  • Domestic shipping. The listing price usually excludes shipping within China to the agent's warehouse. A good agent includes this in the one price you are quoted.

Is 1688 right for a single-item buyer?

Yes, when the listing sells one piece and the item is not something you can get locally at a fair price. The saving over AliExpress and Daraz is real on gadgets, accessories, tools, home goods and hobby items. It is not for buying 30 units to resell, and we do not handle bulk at all. Frequently asked questions

Does 1688 ship to Pakistan?

No. 1688 sellers ship only to addresses in China. A Pakistani buyer needs an agent with a Chinese address who receives the item and forwards it.

Is 1688 cheaper than Alibaba?

Usually. The same supplier's price on 1688 is typically 20 to 40 percent lower than on Alibaba.com, because 1688 is a domestic Chinese price without an export margin.

Can I buy just one piece on 1688?

Often, yes. Many listings show 1件起批, meaning from one piece. Others need 2, 3 or more. Factory listings for made-to-order goods have real MOQs that cannot be waived.

Can I pay 1688 with a Pakistani card?

No. 1688 accepts Alipay and Chinese bank cards only. An agent pays with Alipay and you pay the agent in rupees.

How long does a 1688 order take to reach Pakistan?

The seller usually ships to the agent within 1 to 3 days. Air freight to your door, including customs, then takes about 7 to 15 days. Allow 2 to 3 weeks in total.
