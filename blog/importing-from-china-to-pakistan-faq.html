Importing From China to Pakistan: 30 Common Questions Answered

By Muhammad Shamshad AslamUpdated November 12, 20268 min read
Quick answer

Importing from China to Pakistan is legal, and most people do it without a company or a trip to China. Personal parcels clear on a CNIC, duty and 18% sales tax apply above Rs 1,000, and phones need PTA registration. This page answers 30 common questions in one or two paragraphs each, with links to detailed guides.

New to importing? Read our step-by-step guide on how to import from China to Pakistan.

Is importing from China to Pakistan allowed?

These three questions come up first, and the short answer to all of them is that a personal import is simple and legal.

1. Is it legal to import from China to Pakistan? Yes. Goods that are not banned or restricted by the Import Policy Order 2022 can be imported from China once duties and taxes are paid. Counterfeit branded goods are prohibited under section 15 of the Customs Act.

2. Do I need a company to import from China? No. An individual can receive personal goods on a CNIC. Even commercial imports can be made as an individual with an NTN, without forming a company.

3. Do I need an NTN to import one item? No. An NTN is needed to import commercially in your own name, because a Pakistan Single Window subscription requires an NTN, FTN or STRN. For a personal parcel, the courier or service files the declaration.

Which Chinese sites can you buy from?

Where you buy decides whether the seller ships to Pakistan and how you pay.

4. Which Chinese sites ship directly to Pakistan? Temu, AliExpress and Shein ship here for many listings, and Alibaba suppliers arrange international shipping. ProPakistani reported in July 2025 that AliExpress stopped offering its budget routes for Pakistan, so check the options at checkout.

5. Can I order from Taobao or 1688 to Pakistan? Not directly. Taobao and 1688 sellers ship within China only and take Chinese payment methods. You need an agent or a single-item service to buy and send the item.

6. Can I buy just one piece on Alibaba? Often yes. Some suppliers list Ready to Ship items with a minimum order of one, and many sell single samples. Courier shipping for one piece can be expensive.

How do you pay Chinese sellers from Pakistan?

Payment is where many first orders stall, so it helps to know the options before you choose a site.

7. Can I pay with JazzCash or Easypaisa on Chinese sites? Not on Alibaba, AliExpress, Taobao or 1688. You can use them to pay a Pakistani agent or service in rupees, which then pays the seller.

8. Does a Pakistani card work on Chinese sites? A Visa or Mastercard debit or credit card with international use enabled often works on Alibaba, AliExpress and Temu. Taobao and 1688 generally need Chinese payment methods.

9. Is there tax on paying a Chinese site by card? Yes. Banks collect advance income tax on foreign card payments under section 236Y of the Income Tax Ordinance. Your bank shows the current rate.

How much does importing from China cost?

The item price is only the start. What matters is the landed price.

10. How much does it cost to ship one item from China? Usually Rs 5,000 to 20,000, depending on size and type, before duty and taxes. That is why importing one item makes most sense for things worth around Rs 10,000 or more.

11. What is a landed price? Everything you pay until the item is in your hands: product, shipping, insurance, duty, taxes and any fees. Compare the landed price with a local shop's price, not the product price alone.

12. Is it cheaper to buy from China than in Pakistan? Sometimes. For items with a big local markup, or not sold here, importing can win. For everyday items already stocked locally, a local shop is often cheaper and faster.

What duty and tax do you pay on imports from China?

Duty is the part of the cost people most often guess wrong. Our customs duty guide has the full calculation.

13. Is there a duty-free limit for parcels from China? Only a small one. Section 19C of the Customs Act, as amended by the Finance Act 2025, waives duties and taxes on post or courier parcels worth Rs 1,000 or less. Above that the whole value is dutiable.

14. What are the duty rates? When we checked in November 2026, customs duty slabs were 0, 5, 10, 15 and 20%, with higher rates on some goods. Regulatory duty applies to listed goods only and was capped at 20% from 1 July 2026. Sales tax is 18% on the duty-paid value.

15. How does customs decide what my item is worth? Customs uses the price paid plus shipping and insurance, converted at the exchange rate FBR publishes. For many goods, valuation rulings set a minimum value that customs applies if the declared price is lower.

How long does importing from China take?

Time depends on the shipping method and on the season in China.

16. How long does air shipping take? Express courier commonly takes 5 to 10 days door to door. Consolidated air freight commonly takes 7 to 15 days including customs. Add the seller's own dispatch time.

17. How long does sea shipping take? A shared container commonly takes 25 to 45 days, and a full container somewhat less. Sea suits large, heavy goods, not single small items.

18. When are delays most likely? Around Chinese New Year, when many factories and logistics firms close for roughly two to four weeks between late January and mid February, and after the 11.11 and 618 sales.

What can't you import from China?

Most goods are fine. A short list causes most of the trouble, and our guide on what you cannot import from China has the full version.

19. Which goods are banned or restricted? Counterfeit branded goods, weapons and realistic replicas are not allowed. Medicines and supplements need DRAP approval, and drones need prior permission. Some goods cannot be imported in used condition.

20. Can I import a phone from China? Yes, but it must be registered with PTA through DIRBS and the tax on the model paid, or it will be blocked on Pakistani networks. You can check a device by sending its 15-digit IMEI to 8484.

21. Can I ship a power bank or batteries from China? Loose lithium batteries and power banks are restricted on flights, so many air routes refuse them. Devices with a built-in battery usually ship normally.

How does customs clearance work for a parcel from China?

Clearance happens in Pakistan, and for personal parcels someone else usually files it.

22. Who clears customs on a personal parcel? The courier, Pakistan Post or the service handling it. An express courier usually pays the duty and collects it from you, with a handling charge, before delivery. You show your CNIC.

23. What if my parcel is held at customs? Usually customs needs documents or a duty payment. Reply quickly with the invoice and proof of payment. Prohibited goods, such as counterfeits, are not released by paying duty.

24. How does commercial clearance work? A commercial importer, or their licensed customs agent, files a goods declaration on Pakistan Single Window. PSW subscription needs an NTN, FTN or STRN, NADRA biometric verification and a one-time Rs 500 fee, when we checked in November 2026.

What if something goes wrong with an order from China?

Most orders arrive as expected. When they do not, your options depend on how you paid.

25. What if the item arrives wrong or faulty? If you paid on a platform, open a dispute there with photos straight away. Alibaba's Trade Assurance covers orders that are not shipped and missing or defective items, subject to its terms.

26. Can I return an item to China? It is possible but slow and costly, because return shipping is international and duty paid in Pakistan is not usually refunded. Checking the listing carefully before paying is the better protection.

27. Does a Chinese warranty work in Pakistan? Often not in practice. Many Chinese warranties expect the item to be sent back to the seller. Ask before buying whether the seller will send spare parts abroad.

How does a single-item import service work?

A single-item service buys the item in China, ships it and clears it for you. This is how ours works.

28. How do I get a price? Send the product link through our quote form. Within 24 hours we reply with one price in rupees covering the item, shipping, Pakistan customs and our fee, which is stated upfront.

29. When and how do I pay? Once, before it ships, by bank transfer, Raast, JazzCash or Easypaisa. Nothing is due on delivery. We give an expected delivery date in writing, usually 7 to 15 days after the seller dispatches, and we refund in full if the item has not arrived within 10 days after that date or arrives wrong, damaged or not working.

30. Do you handle bulk orders? No. We handle one item, or a few different single items together, for personal or professional use. For quantities, use a cargo company or the commercial route. For the full process either way, read how to import from China to Pakistan.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to travel to China to import?

No. You can find products online, message sellers, pay through the platform or a Pakistani service, and have goods shipped and cleared without travelling.

Can I import from China for personal use?

Yes, with just your CNIC. Duty and 18% sales tax apply once a parcel is worth more than Rs 1,000.

Is Temu available in Pakistan?

Yes. Temu has a Pakistan site and delivers here. Like any parcel worth more than Rs 1,000, a Temu order can be assessed for duty.

Can I import a used item from China?

Many used items can be imported, but the Import Policy Order lists goods that cannot come in used, so check before you buy.

Do I pay anything on delivery with a single-item service?

Not with ours. You pay one price once, before the item ships, and it already includes shipping, customs and our fee.

Where should a first-time importer start?

Pick the item first, check it is allowed, work out the landed price, then choose the route that fits its value, size and urgency.
