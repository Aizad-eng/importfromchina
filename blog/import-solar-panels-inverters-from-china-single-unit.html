Can You Import One Solar Panel or Inverter From China to Pakistan?

By importfromchina.shop teamUpdated October 3, 20267 min read
Quick answer

A single inverter from China is a realistic import to Pakistan, because it is compact enough for air freight and the saving on a specific model can justify the cost. A single solar panel rarely is, because it is large, fragile and heavy, which makes air expensive and sea uneconomical for one piece. Lithium batteries are restricted by air. Duty and tax rules on solar equipment in Pakistan change often, so always confirm the current position before paying.

Why do Pakistanis want to import solar equipment from China?

Pakistan's solar boom is built on Chinese panels, inverters and batteries, and almost everything sold in the local market started as an import. Buyers look at Alibaba or 1688 prices, compare them with local quotes, and wonder why they should not import directly. For a full rooftop system, the answer is that installers import by the container and the economics favour them. For one specific unit, the answer depends on which unit.

Is a single solar panel worth importing?

Usually not. A typical residential panel is around two metres by one metre, weighs 20 to 30 kilograms and has a glass face. That combination creates three problems:

  • Air freight is charged on volumetric weight. A panel's size pushes the chargeable weight far above its actual weight, so air freight for one panel often costs more than the panel.
  • Sea freight has a minimum. Sea is priced per cubic metre with a minimum of roughly 1 CBM plus fixed port and documentation fees. One panel does not fill that, and the fixed fees land on one item.
  • Breakage. A single panel travels without the pallet and crate protection of a bulk shipment. If it cracks, there is no practical return route to China.

Local wholesalers in Karachi, Lahore and Islamabad sell panels one at a time at prices that reflect container-scale freight. For a single panel, buying locally is nearly always the better choice.

Is a single inverter worth importing?

Often, yes. An inverter is a boxed unit that weighs a few kilograms to perhaps 15 or 20 kilograms for a larger hybrid model, and it packs into a compact carton. That suits air freight. People import one when they want a specific brand or model that local sellers do not stock, a particular feature such as a certain battery communication protocol or a Wi-Fi monitoring module, or a newer version than the local market carries. Things to check before you order:

  • Voltage and frequency. Pakistan is 220 to 240V 50Hz. Most Chinese inverters are built for that, but confirm the model is not a 110V or 60Hz export variant.
  • Grid compliance. If you plan to net meter, your DISCO may require approved inverter models. A grey import may not be accepted.
  • Warranty. A Chinese seller's warranty usually means shipping the unit back to China. Treat it as no warranty in practice.
  • Firmware and app. Some models sold for the Chinese domestic market ship with Chinese-only apps or regional servers.

Can I import a lithium battery for my solar system?

By air, generally no. Loose lithium batteries and large battery packs are restricted on aircraft and most consolidated air lines refuse them. The alternatives are special battery lines, which are slower and not always available, or sea freight, where a single battery runs into the same minimum charge problem as a panel. Combined with the weight of a 48V lithium pack, this is a bulk importer's item. Buy batteries locally. Read what you cannot import from China to Pakistan for the wider battery rules.

What about duty and tax on solar equipment?

This is where you must be careful. Pakistan has changed the tax treatment of solar panels, inverters and batteries several times in recent budgets, moving items between exempt and taxed and adjusting sales tax. We do not quote rates here because they can be out of date by the time you read this. Check the current Customs tariff and the latest Finance Act or SRO for the specific HS code, or ask for a delivered price that already includes whatever applies. Customs value is the CIF value, and customs may apply a valuation ruling rather than your invoice. See how customs duty from China to Pakistan works.

What should I ask the Chinese seller before buying an inverter?

  • Confirm the exact model code, output rating and input voltage range in writing.
  • Ask for the packed dimensions and gross weight, because freight is calculated on them.
  • Ask whether the unit is the international version with an English app.
  • Ask for photos of the unit and the carton before it ships. The seller can provide these.
  • Prefer rated sellers on Alibaba or 1688 with a history of inverter sales.

Frequently asked questions

Can I import one solar panel from China to Pakistan?

You can, but it is rarely economical. The panel's size makes air freight expensive and sea freight has a minimum charge of about 1 CBM. Buying one panel locally is usually cheaper.

Can I import a single solar inverter from China?

Yes. An inverter is compact enough for air freight and a specific model can be worth importing. Check voltage, grid approval for net metering, and accept that the warranty is effectively void.

Can lithium solar batteries be shipped from China by air?

Generally no. Loose lithium batteries are restricted by air. Sea or special battery lines are the alternatives, which do not suit a single unit.

How much is customs duty on solar equipment in Pakistan?

It changes often with budgets and SROs. Check the current tariff for the specific HS code or ask for a delivered price that includes the current duty and taxes.

Will a Chinese inverter work on Pakistan's grid?

Most are built for 220 to 240V 50Hz, which matches Pakistan. Confirm the model is not a 110V or 60Hz variant and check your DISCO's approved list if you plan to net meter.
