Temu Item Not Shipping to Pakistan? Here's What to Do

By importfromchina.shop teamUpdated October 17, 20269 min read
Quick answer

If a Temu item will not ship to Pakistan, first check that your app or site is set to Pakistan with prices in rupees, that the link is from temu.com/pk-en, and that your address is a normal physical address outside restricted areas. If it still cannot be ordered, the item is usually not offered on the Pakistan site or cannot travel by air. Then look for the same item from another Temu listing, on AliExpress, or from the Chinese supplier through a single-item import service.

Why won't a Temu item ship to Pakistan?

Temu does deliver to Pakistan. It runs a Pakistan version of its site at temu.com/pk-en, and its Pakistan Terms of Use were last updated on 19 September 2024. But Temu is one app with many country sites, and each country site has its own catalogue, carriers and rules. An item you saw on the US or UAE site, or in a TikTok video, may simply not be offered for Pakistan.

Temu's own Pakistan shipping page says you may choose standard shipping "depending on the items in your order and your location". So two things decide whether an item ships: what the item is, and where it is going. In practice the problem is almost always one of these:

  • Wrong region. Your app or browser is set to another country, often because of a VPN or an account created abroad.
  • Foreign link. Someone shared a link from another country's Temu site. The listing exists there but not on the Pakistan site.
  • Not in the Pakistan catalogue. The seller or Temu has not made that listing available for Pakistan.
  • Address limits. Some items need a physical address, and no item can go to a security-restricted area.
  • Air and import rules. Items with loose batteries, aerosols or flammable liquids often cannot fly, and some goods are banned or restricted by Pakistan.

Temu does not publish one list of every reason a listing is unavailable, so treat the table below as a checklist rather than an official rulebook.

How do you check that your Temu region is set to Pakistan?

This fixes more "not shipping" problems than anything else. When we checked in October 2026, Temu's Pakistan pages showed a country and language selector reading "Pakistan English".

  1. Turn off any VPN, proxy or office network that routes your connection abroad.
  2. Open the country and language selector on temu.com, or the region setting in the app, and choose Pakistan.
  3. Check that prices now show in Rs and that page addresses contain /pk-en.
  4. If prices still show another currency, sign out, close the app fully and open it again.
  5. Search for the item again from inside the Pakistan site rather than reopening the old link.

If your account was created years ago while you were abroad, it may keep pulling you back to that region. Changing the region in settings usually works. Creating a fresh account on the Pakistan site is the last resort.

What if someone sent you a Temu link from another country?

A Temu link carries its country in the address, such as /us or /ae. Opening it in Pakistan may show you that country's listing, which you cannot buy for a Pakistani address. The same product, or something made in the same factory, is often on the Pakistan site under a different listing and price.

Search the Pakistan site using the product's plain description, not the seller's long title. Two or three key words plus a size or model number work best, for example "magnetic phone mount 15W" rather than the full listing name. Compare photos, dimensions and the parts included before you assume it is the same thing.

Can your address stop a Temu item from shipping?

Yes. Temu's Pakistan shipping page sets out a few address rules worth knowing:

  • P.O. boxes are allowed in general, but large products, valuables, perishables, age-restricted products and items sent by private carriers may need a physical address or a signature.
  • Sellers can say in a listing that they do not ship to P.O. boxes.
  • Items cannot be delivered to security-restricted areas such as military bases.

If you live in a cantonment or an official colony with restricted access, enter a family member's or office address outside it. Write the full street, area, city and a phone number you answer, because Temu hands the last leg to local carriers.

Which items are hard to ship from Temu to Pakistan?

Some products cannot travel the way Temu's standard shipping works, whatever the site says about the price. The usual problem groups are:

  • Loose lithium batteries and power banks. Air rules treat a power bank as a battery, not a gadget. Devices with a battery built in, like earbuds, are much easier. See our guide to lithium battery shipping rules.
  • Aerosols, perfumes, nail polish and flammable liquids. These are commonly refused for air transport.
  • Very large or heavy items. Furniture and bulky equipment can exceed what a standard parcel route accepts.
  • Items Pakistan bans or restricts. Drones, counterfeit branded goods, weapon lookalikes and medicines are the common ones. Our list of what you cannot import from China to Pakistan explains each.

If your item falls into one of these groups, no setting will make it ship. A different route can sometimes work for the battery and size cases, but not for banned goods.

Should you use a VPN or a foreign forwarding address?

People try this when an item is on the US or UAE site but not on the Pakistan one. It rarely works out:

  • Each country site is built to deliver inside that country. A Pakistani address does not fit another country's checkout.
  • A Pakistani card on a foreign storefront is more likely to be flagged by fraud checks.
  • If you ship to a friend or a parcel forwarder abroad, you pay a second international shipment and still face Pakistan customs on arrival.
  • Once the parcel leaves the address you ordered to, Temu's delivery promise and its return pickup no longer help you.

The cleaner route is to find the same product from the Chinese supplier and have it brought to Pakistan directly.

How do you find the same item outside Temu?

Many products on Temu come from the same Chinese factories and traders who sell on 1688 and Taobao, often with more colours or sizes.

  1. Save the Temu link and a clear screenshot of the item, its size or model, colour and what is in the box.
  2. Search AliExpress with the same key words. Set the ship-to country to Pakistan and check the shipping line before you get attached to a listing.
  3. Search 1688 or Taobao by image, using the camera icon in their search bars with your screenshot.
  4. Compare the listings carefully. Same photo does not always mean same product, so check dimensions, materials and included parts.
  5. Work out a delivered price in rupees for each route, including shipping and any customs, before you choose.

Common mistakes when a Temu item will not ship

  • Blaming the item before checking the region. A VPN or old account is the most common cause.
  • Buying a lookalike in a hurry. A cheaper listing with the same photo may be a smaller size or missing a part.
  • Paying a "Temu Pakistan" chat seller. Treat any WhatsApp or Facebook seller using the Temu name as a reseller, not Temu.
  • Ignoring customs. Post and courier parcels worth Rs 1,000 or less are duty-free under section 19C of the Customs Act. Above that, duty can apply. See customs duty from China to Pakistan.
  • Forgetting returns. Temu's Pakistan return policy allows returns within 90 days of purchase. A forwarded or third-party route loses that.

Frequently asked questions

Why does Temu say an item cannot be shipped to my address in Pakistan?

Usually the listing is not offered on Temu's Pakistan site, your region is set to another country, or the item needs a physical address or cannot fly, such as a loose battery or aerosol. Check the region first, then the address.

How do I change my Temu country to Pakistan?

Turn off any VPN, then pick Pakistan in the country and language selector on temu.com or in the app's region setting. Prices should switch to rupees and page addresses should contain /pk-en.

Can I order from the US Temu site and ship to Pakistan?

Not in a useful way. Each Temu country site delivers inside that country, and forwarding from abroad adds a second shipment, Pakistan customs and the loss of Temu's return route.

Why can't I buy a power bank on Temu in Pakistan?

Air rules treat a power bank as a loose lithium battery, which is much harder to ship than a device with a built-in battery. Many routes simply exclude them.

Where else can I buy a Temu item that won't ship to Pakistan?

Try another Temu Pakistan listing, AliExpress, Daraz, or the same item on 1688 or Taobao through a single-item import service. For items under about Rs 10,000, a local option is usually cheaper.

Can someone buy a Temu item for me if it doesn't ship to Pakistan?

Yes. A single-item import service can usually find the same product from the Chinese supplier and deliver it, with one price in rupees paid before it ships. It suits items worth around Rs 10,000 or more.
