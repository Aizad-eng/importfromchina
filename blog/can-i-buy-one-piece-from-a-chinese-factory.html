Can you buy just one piece from a Chinese factory?

In many cases, yes. Chinese factories are set up for bulk orders, but a large number of them also sell single units, either as samples for buyers who want to check quality first or as stock items that are already made and sitting in a warehouse.

Whether a particular factory will sell you one piece depends mostly on one question: does the item already exist, or does it have to be made for you? An item that is already in stock can usually be sold singly. An item that needs new production, a custom colour or your logo usually cannot, or only at a high sample price.

This guide is about the factory side of the question: why minimum orders exist, which items you can realistically get as one piece and how to ask. If you already know you want an Alibaba listing, our step-by-step on buying one item from Alibaba in Pakistan covers checkout and courier costs.

What is an MOQ, and why do factories set one?

MOQ stands for minimum order quantity. It is the smallest number of units a supplier will sell in one order. Factories set one because a lot of their costs are fixed per production run, not per unit:

  • Machine setup. Changing moulds, dies or settings takes hours, whether the run is 10 units or 10,000.
  • Materials. Fabric, plastic and components are often bought in rolls, bags or reels with their own minimums.
  • Printing and packaging. Custom boxes and labels have their own print minimums.
  • Admin. Quoting, packing and export paperwork take the same time for a small order as a large one.

This is also why you see tiered pricing. The more you buy, the lower the price per unit. A single piece sits at the top of the price table, and that is normal, not a sign that you are being overcharged.

Does it matter if the seller is a factory or a trading company?

For one piece, less than people think. On Alibaba, suppliers choose labels such as manufacturer or trading company themselves, so the label alone does not prove much. Sourcing guides point to better clues: a narrow product range suggests a real factory, while a catalogue running from furniture to cosmetics suggests a trader.

Trading companies are often the easier route for a single unit. They hold stock from several factories, are used to small orders and usually answer messages faster. A factory might sell you one piece of its main product, but it will rarely break a carton of a cheap accessory for you.

What matters more is that the seller is established, has trade history and reviews, and lets you pay through the platform. Those checks protect you far more than a factory label does.

How do you buy one piece from a factory on Alibaba?

  • Look at the price table. If it shows a price for 1 piece, or a range starting at 1, you can order one. Check the unit is a piece, not a set, box or carton.
  • Use Ready to Ship. Alibaba's guide says these items are in stock, with prices and shipping costs shown upfront, and dispatched from the supplier's warehouse within about 7 to 15 days. Sellers need business verification to use the label, and these orders come with Trade Assurance when paid through Alibaba.
  • Request a sample. Some product pages have a sample option. Otherwise message the supplier, describe what you want, and ask for a sample price including shipping.
  • Expect a higher price. Alibaba says sample prices vary widely by seller, and that shipping for one small package is usually relatively high.

If you later order in bulk, you can ask for the sample cost to be deducted. Alibaba's guide says this is a request, not automatic.

Can you buy one piece on 1688?

Often, yes. 1688 is Alibaba Group's Chinese wholesale site, and many of its suppliers are factories and wholesalers. Many listings accept very small orders, often one or two pieces, with tiered prices that fall as quantity rises. Listings that offer 一件代发, meaning the supplier ships single units on a buyer's behalf, are a good sign that one piece is fine.

The catch is that 1688 is built for buyers inside China. It mostly uses Chinese payment methods and ships within China, so a buyer in Pakistan needs someone there to pay and receive the item. Our guides on how to buy from 1688 in Pakistan and on using a 1688 buying agent explain the options.

How do you ask a factory to sell you one piece?

A clear, short message gets a better answer than a vague one. This works well:

  1. Name the exact listing. Paste the link and the model number.
  2. Say it is one unit for your own use. Do not pretend to be a bulk buyer. Factories can tell, and a straight answer is faster.
  3. Give the variant. Colour, size, voltage, plug type, any accessories.
  4. Ask for the one-piece price and dispatch date. Ask whether it is in stock or needs to be made.
  5. Ask about the packaging size and weight. You need this to estimate shipping.
  6. Pay only through the platform. Keep the order on Alibaba so the protection applies.

If the answer is that the MOQ is 50 and they cannot sell one, ask whether they have a trading partner or a stock unit. If not, look for the same item from another supplier. The same product is often made or sold by several.

What does one piece cost compared with the bulk price?

More than the headline price. Three things add up.

  • The unit price. The single-piece tier is the highest in the table. Some suppliers charge a sample price above that.
  • Shipping. Shipping one item from China to Pakistan usually costs Rs 5,000 to 20,000, depending on its size and type. In bulk, that cost is shared across many units. For one piece, it is all yours. See our breakdown of the cost to ship one item from China.
  • Customs. Post and courier parcels worth Rs 1,000 or less are duty-free under section 19C. Above that, duty and taxes apply depending on the product. See our guide to customs duty for rates.

So a factory item listed at a low bulk price can land in Pakistan at several times that. It is still often worth it for equipment, tools and parts that are not sold here, or that sell locally for much more.

What are the common mistakes when buying one piece from a factory?

  • Reading the MOQ wrong. Min. order 1 set or 1 carton may mean many units.
  • Using the bulk price in your sums. The 1,000-piece price is not the price for one.
  • Asking for a logo or custom colour on one unit. That turns a stock sale into a custom sample.
  • Paying off the platform. A discount for paying by direct transfer removes your protection.
  • Ignoring plugs and voltage. Check the variant suits Pakistan before you order.
  • Forgetting packaging size. Factory packing for one machine can be large, and large boxes cost more to fly.
  • Trying to order 30 units as one. That is a small bulk order, and it needs commercial import, not a single-item route.

Frequently asked questions

Will a Chinese factory sell me just one item?

Often yes, if the item is already made and in stock. Many Alibaba suppliers sell single units through Ready to Ship listings or as samples, and many 1688 listings start at one or two pieces. Custom or logo items usually need a minimum order.

What does MOQ mean on Alibaba and 1688?

MOQ means minimum order quantity, the fewest units a supplier will sell in one order. Factories set it because machine setup, materials and packaging cost the same whether the run is small or large.

Can I order a sample from a Chinese factory without placing a bulk order?

Yes. Many suppliers sell samples to buyers who want to check quality first. Sample prices vary by seller and shipping for one small parcel is relatively high, so ask for the full price including shipping before you pay.

Is it better to buy one piece from a factory or a trading company?

For one piece, a trading company is often easier because it holds stock and is used to small orders. Supplier labels on Alibaba are self-declared, so trade history, reviews and paying through the platform matter more than the label.

Why is the single-piece price so much higher than the bulk price?

Because fixed costs such as setup, packing and paperwork are spread over fewer units, and suppliers use tiered pricing where the price per unit falls as quantity rises. For one piece, shipping is also not shared with other units.

Can I buy one piece from a Chinese factory and have it delivered to Pakistan?

Yes. Alibaba sellers can ship one piece by courier, though it is expensive. 1688 and Taobao ship only within China, so you need a service in China. We buy single items and deliver them anywhere in Pakistan for one price in rupees.
