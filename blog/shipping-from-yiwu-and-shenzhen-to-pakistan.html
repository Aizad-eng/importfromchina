Shipping From Yiwu and Shenzhen to Pakistan

By Muhammad Shamshad AslamUpdated November 12, 20268 min read
Quick answer

Yiwu and Shenzhen ship to Pakistan very differently. Yiwu is an inland market city for small goods, so items travel by road or rail to a coastal port such as Ningbo or to an airport. Shenzhen is a coastal electronics centre with its own major port and airport. For one item from either, the seller ships by courier or to a forwarder, and it flies to Pakistan.

How do Yiwu and Shenzhen differ for buyers in Pakistan?

Yiwu is an inland trading city known for small everyday goods sold mainly by the carton, while Shenzhen is a coastal city known for electronics, with its own large port. That difference shapes how goods leave each city and what usually goes wrong.

How does a parcel leave Yiwu?

A parcel leaves Yiwu by road, rail or air to reach an international gateway, because Yiwu has no seaport and its own airport is a regional one. Yiwu Airport mainly has domestic flights, including to Guangzhou, Shenzhen, Beijing and Chengdu, so international parcels usually travel onward through a larger hub.

For one item, the usual path is simple: a seller on Alibaba, 1688 or Taobao based in Yiwu ships it by Chinese domestic courier to a forwarder's or buying service's warehouse, or books an express courier directly. From there it flies to Pakistan with other parcels. Yiwu also has well-known freight rail links, including the long Yiwu to Madrid line, but those serve Central Asia and Europe, not single parcels to Pakistan.

Which port handles sea cargo from Yiwu?

Sea cargo from Yiwu is commonly trucked or railed to Ningbo-Zhoushan or Shanghai and shipped from there. Ningbo-Zhoushan, on the coast of Zhejiang across Hangzhou Bay from Shanghai, has been described as the world's busiest port by cargo tonnage. Containers from these ports reach Karachi Port or Port Qasim, with Shanghai to Karachi taking roughly 18 to 20 days port to port on forwarder estimates.

Sea freight is for commercial quantities. Our guide on sea freight from China to Pakistan, LCL vs FCL explains shared and full containers. It is not a sensible way to move one item.

Can you get one item from a Yiwu seller?

Sometimes. Yiwu's market stalls mostly sell in cartons and set minimum quantities, so asking a stall for one piece usually fails. Many Yiwu-area sellers also list on online platforms, where single pieces or small orders are often possible. The same product is frequently sold by sellers in other cities too.

So the practical approach for one item is to find the product on a platform listing, rather than through a market stall, and buy from a seller who sells single pieces. We only handle single items, so if you want cartons from Yiwu, that is a job for a cargo company or a sourcing agent under the commercial route.

What is Shenzhen known for, and why does it matter for shipping?

Shenzhen is known for electronics, and its Huaqiangbei area in Futian District has one of the largest electronics markets in the world, with tens of thousands of businesses. Many of the gadgets Pakistani buyers look for, from earbuds and dash cams to development boards and phone accessories, are made or sold by Shenzhen companies.

That matters for shipping because electronics often contain lithium batteries, which airlines restrict. The item itself is usually fine to fly if the battery is built into the device and declared properly. Loose batteries and power banks are much harder. A Shenzhen seller's habits on battery declaration can decide whether a parcel moves smoothly or gets refused.

How do Shenzhen electronics with batteries ship to Pakistan?

Electronics with built-in batteries usually ship by air when they are declared and packed correctly, while loose lithium batteries and power banks face strict limits and are often refused for air transport. Ask the seller three things before they ship:

  1. Is the battery built into the device, packed with it, or a separate spare?
  2. What is the battery's watt-hour rating?
  3. Will the shipment be declared as containing lithium batteries?

A seller who says "no need to declare" is creating risk for you. Undeclared batteries are a common reason parcels are held or sent back. Our guide on lithium battery rules for shipping from China to Pakistan explains the categories and limits.

Which port and airport serve Shenzhen?

Shenzhen is served by the Port of Shenzhen and Shenzhen Bao'an International Airport. The Port of Shenzhen has several port areas, including Yantian in the east, and it handled about 30 million TEU of containers in 2022, making it the third largest port in China. Its neighbour Guangzhou has its own cargo airport, Baiyun, and its own seaport at Nansha, so Pearl River Delta sellers have several gateways within a short drive.

For a single parcel, the airport and port matter less than who the seller hands it to. Express couriers collect across Shenzhen and sort at their hubs in the Pearl River Delta. Forwarders with warehouses in the area group parcels and fly them out.

What mistakes do people make when shipping from Yiwu or Shenzhen?

The common mistakes are about minimums, batteries and paperwork:

  • Expecting a Yiwu stall to post one piece. Most stalls sell by the carton and do not ship abroad themselves.
  • Buying a power bank as a "free gift" add-on. It can stop the whole parcel flying.
  • Accepting "gift" on the invoice. Pakistan Customs values the item anyway, and a vague description slows it down.
  • Ignoring the box size. Many Yiwu goods are light but bulky, and couriers charge by volume.
  • Assuming China-only free shipping covers Pakistan. It does not.
  • Buying a China-only electronics version. Some Shenzhen gadgets come in China-market versions with Chinese-only apps or different plugs.

What should you send a Yiwu or Shenzhen seller before they ship?

Send the seller your delivery details and four short requests before they ship, because most delays start with the paperwork, not the flight.

  • Your name exactly as on your CNIC. Couriers in Pakistan match the receiver's name to the CNIC during clearance.
  • Your full address and a phone number that will be answered. Local delivery riders call before they come.
  • A plain item description and the real value on the invoice. "Bluetooth speaker, 1 piece" clears faster than "gift".
  • The battery details. Built in, packed with the device or separate, and whether it will be declared.
  • The packed box size and weight. So you can check the volumetric weight before you agree the shipping charge.

For Yiwu goods, the box size request matters most, because many small commodities are light but bulky. For Shenzhen goods, the battery question matters most.

Do you pay customs on items from Yiwu or Shenzhen?

You pay Pakistani customs on items from Yiwu or Shenzhen in exactly the same way as on items from anywhere else in China, because duty depends on the product and its value, not the city. Under section 19C of the Customs Act, as amended by the Finance Act 2025, post and courier parcels worth Rs 1,000 or less are free of duties and taxes. Above that, the whole value is assessed on the normal tariff for that product, with sales tax and other layers on top.

Electronics from Shenzhen are worth a specific warning. Phones and other devices with a SIM slot need PTA registration after they arrive, on top of customs, and wireless gadgets can raise questions at clearance. Check the rules for your exact device before you order.

Frequently asked questions

Does Yiwu have an international airport for cargo?

Yiwu has a regional airport with mainly domestic flights, so international parcels from Yiwu usually travel onward through larger hubs.

Which port is closest to Yiwu for sea freight?

Yiwu is inland, so sea cargo usually goes through Ningbo-Zhoushan or Shanghai, then by ship to Karachi Port or Port Qasim.

Can I buy one item from the Yiwu market?

Market stalls usually sell by the carton. For one item, buy from a seller who lists single pieces online and have it shipped by courier or a single-item service.

Can Shenzhen electronics with batteries be shipped to Pakistan?

Usually yes, if the battery is built into the device and declared. Loose batteries and power banks are heavily restricted by air.

What is Huaqiangbei?

Huaqiangbei is an area in Futian District, Shenzhen, with one of the world's largest electronics markets and tens of thousands of businesses.

Is shipping from Shenzhen faster than from Yiwu?

Often slightly, because Shenzhen is on the coast next to major courier hubs. For one item, the seller's handling time and Pakistan customs matter more than the city.
