Alibaba vs 1688: Which Is Better for Pakistani Buyers?

By importfromchina.shop teamUpdated October 3, 20267 min read
Quick answer

Alibaba is built for international buyers: English interface, card payments, Trade Assurance protection, and suppliers who export. 1688 is Alibaba's domestic Chinese platform: prices are typically 20–40% lower and MOQs smaller, but it is in Chinese, accepts only Chinese payment methods, and ships only within China. Pakistani buyers use Alibaba for direct orders and 1688 through a sourcing agent who handles payment and export.

What is the difference between Alibaba and 1688?

Both are owned by Alibaba Group, but they serve different markets.

Why are 1688 prices lower?

Three reasons. First, 1688 sellers price for the Chinese domestic market, with no export margin built in. Second, many Alibaba listings are from trading companies who buy on 1688 and resell at a markup. Third, 1688 competition is intense, so wholesalers operate on thin margins. For common products such as phone accessories, home goods and fashion items, the same factory often lists the same product on both sites at very different prices.

Can I buy from 1688 directly from Pakistan?

In practice, no. You would need a Chinese phone number to register, a Chinese payment method, Chinese reading ability to communicate with sellers, and a Chinese address to receive the goods. Sellers do not export, do not provide customs paperwork, and do not speak English.

This is why 1688 buying in Pakistan goes through a sourcing agent. The agent places the order, pays in RMB, receives the goods at a warehouse in China, inspects them, consolidates several orders, and exports the shipment to you with proper documents. Even after the agent's fee, 1688 pricing is usually well below Alibaba for the same item.

When should I use Alibaba?

  • You want to deal with the supplier directly in English.
  • You need custom manufacturing, private labelling or OEM work with detailed specifications.
  • You want Trade Assurance so payment is released only after you confirm delivery.
  • Your order is large enough that the supplier's export pricing is still competitive.

When should I use 1688?

  • You are buying ready-made stock items in small to medium quantities.
  • Price per unit matters more than customisation.
  • You want to test several products with small orders before committing.
  • You are working with a sourcing agent anyway.

How does Alibaba Trade Assurance protect me?

When you pay through Alibaba, the money is held until you confirm receipt or the agreed delivery date passes. If the goods do not arrive or do not match the contract, you can open a dispute and Alibaba mediates. Protection only applies to orders paid through the platform. Suppliers who ask you to pay outside Alibaba are removing your protection, and that request itself is a warning sign.

What about Alibaba suppliers who are actually middlemen?

Many are. Look for "Manufacturer" rather than "Trading Company" in the supplier profile, check whether the business licence scope includes production, and ask for factory photos or a video call. Trading companies are not necessarily bad: they often handle small orders and mixed products better than factories. Just be aware you are paying their margin.

Which platform is better for a new Pakistani importer?

For a first order, Alibaba with Trade Assurance is the safest way to go direct. For the best prices and smallest MOQs, 1688 through an agent wins. Most of our clients end up using 1688 for stock products and Alibaba only for custom manufacturing. Read our guide to finding reliable suppliers before placing a first order on either platform.

Frequently asked questions

Is 1688 cheaper than Alibaba?

Yes, usually by 20 to 40 percent for the same product, because 1688 prices are for the Chinese domestic market and many Alibaba sellers are trading companies that source from 1688.

Does 1688 ship to Pakistan?

No. 1688 sellers ship only to addresses in China. Pakistani buyers use a sourcing agent with a Chinese warehouse who receives, consolidates and exports the goods.

Can I pay 1688 with a Pakistani card?

No. 1688 accepts Alipay and Chinese bank payments only. An agent pays the seller on your behalf and you pay the agent in PKR.

Is Alibaba safe for buyers in Pakistan?

Alibaba is safe when you pay through Trade Assurance, verify the supplier, and start with a small order. Paying outside the platform or to a personal account removes your protection.

What is Alibaba's minimum order quantity?

Each supplier sets their own MOQ. It is commonly 100 to 1,000 units for factories, though many suppliers accept smaller trial orders at a higher unit price.
