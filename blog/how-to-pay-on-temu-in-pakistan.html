How to Pay on Temu in Pakistan: JazzCash, Easypaisa and Cards

By Muhammad Shamshad AslamUpdated October 26, 20269 min read
Quick answer

You pay on Temu in Pakistan inside the Temu checkout, before the order ships, with an international card such as Visa or Mastercard. JazzCash and Easypaisa were listed on Temu's Pakistan site in mid October 2026 but not on 26 October, so check your checkout screen. There is no cash on delivery. Switch on e-commerce and international use for your card first, expect a small advance tax if your bank treats it as a foreign payment, and never pay anyone outside the app.

Which payment methods does Temu accept in Pakistan?

Temu's Pakistan site accepts mobile wallets and international cards, but not cash on delivery. When we checked its Pakistan pages between 21 and 23 October 2026, the methods shown were JazzCash, Easypaisa, Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, Maestro, Diners Club, JCB, UnionPay, Apple Pay and Google Pay. When we looked again on 26 October 2026, the logo strip in the footer of Temu's policy pages showed the card brands, Apple Pay and Google Pay, without the wallet logos. Footers change, so the checkout screen for your own order is the final word on what you can use.

How do you pay Temu with JazzCash or Easypaisa, step by step?

You select the wallet at checkout, enter the wallet's mobile number and approve the payment on your phone. The steps are the same for both wallets:

  1. Top up the wallet first with the full cart total, not just the item price. If a tax line appears at checkout, it is part of the total.
  2. Build your cart on the Pakistan site, with prices in Rs, and tap checkout.
  3. Choose JazzCash or Easypaisa in the payment section.
  4. Enter the mobile number registered to the wallet.
  5. Approve the request the way the screen tells you, usually through a prompt in the wallet app or by entering your wallet PIN. Keep the phone in hand, as approval requests usually time out after a short while.
  6. Wait on the Temu screen until it confirms the order. Then check that the order appears in "Your Orders" and that the wallet shows one deduction, not two.

The wallet route suits people without an international card. Our guide on ordering from China without a credit card covers other sites where that is harder.

How do you pay Temu with a debit or credit card, step by step?

You enter the card at checkout and confirm the bank's one-time code, after switching on online and international use in your banking app. In order:

  1. Open your banking app and switch on e-commerce or online transactions, and international transactions, for the card. The next section explains why both matter.
  2. Check the card's daily online limit is above your cart total.
  3. At Temu checkout, choose card and enter the number, expiry date and security code exactly as printed.
  4. Complete the 3D Secure step. Your bank sends a one-time code by SMS or email, or asks you to approve in its app.
  5. Wait for Temu's confirmation screen, then check your bank SMS for the amount actually charged.
  6. If you want, switch international use off again afterwards. Turn it back on before any future order.

Apple Pay and Google Pay work only if your bank lets you add your card to the phone wallet. Check with your bank before relying on them.

Which card settings must be switched on?

Two settings must be on: e-commerce use and international use. Many Pakistani debit cards come with one or both turned off, and the payment is declined without a clear reason on Temu's side.

HBL's DebitCard page is a good example of how banks handle this. When we checked in October 2026, it listed HBL Mobile features to enable or disable e-commerce transactions and to enable or disable international transactions, and to manage POS and e-commerce limits. It listed a daily purchase and e-commerce limit of Rs 250,000 on its standard Visa, Mastercard and UnionPay debit cards. Most other banks offer similar switches in their apps. It also said 3D Secure sends a one-time password by SMS or email, so the mobile number and email on your bank record must be current.

Why international use, when Temu shows prices in rupees? Because the card payment may still be processed as a payment to a merchant abroad. If your bank treats it that way, a card with international use switched off will be declined.

Do you pay extra tax or fees when you pay Temu by card?

Possibly, depending on how your bank processes the payment. If your bank treats a Temu card payment as money sent abroad, it collects advance income tax under section 236Y. FBR's withholding tax rate card, updated to 30 June 2026 under the Finance Act 2026, lists 0.5% for people on the Active Taxpayers List and 1% for those who are not, on amounts remitted abroad through credit, debit or prepaid cards.

On a Rs 10,000 card payment, that is Rs 50 for a filer or Rs 100 for a non-filer. It shows as a separate line on your bank statement, not on Temu's receipt. Your bank may also apply its own charges or exchange rate on foreign transactions. Those are set by your bank, so check its schedule of charges.

Temu's Pakistan terms add one more point: payment processors may charge fees for purchases, and those processing fees will be disclosed to you via Temu. Look at the final total before you approve.

Why is there no cash on delivery on Temu in Pakistan?

Temu does not explain it, but its terms are built around paying first. They say that to make a purchase you must provide a valid payment method, and that your payment obligation is fulfilled once payment of the agreed price is received. Free shipping and 90-day returns depend on Temu holding the money before the parcel leaves.

The practical result: any page offering "Temu cash on delivery" in Pakistan is a reseller, not Temu. It sets its own prices and return terms, and Temu's return policy does not cover what you buy from it.

Why did your Temu payment fail, and how do you fix it?

Most failed Temu payments in Pakistan come from the bank or wallet side, not from Temu. Work through these in order:

  • Online or international use is off. Switch both on in your banking app, then retry.
  • The daily limit is too low. Raise the e-commerce limit in your app or split the cart into two orders.
  • No one-time code arrived. Update your mobile number or email with the bank, and check that your phone can receive SMS from short codes.
  • Not enough balance. Include any tax line in the amount you load into the wallet.
  • The wallet approval expired. Start again and approve within the time shown.
  • A PayPak-only card. PayPak was not among the listed brands. Use a Visa, Mastercard or UnionPay card, or a wallet.
  • A VPN or the wrong region. Turn off the VPN and check prices show in Rs.
  • Temu held the payment. Its terms say it may decline, freeze or hold a transaction for suspected fraud. Contact customer service from inside the app.

If money left your account but no order appeared, do not pay again straight away. Wait a few hours, check "Your Orders", and contact customer service with the bank SMS.

How do you spot a fake Temu payment request?

A real Temu payment only happens inside the Temu checkout, so any request to pay elsewhere is a red flag. Temu's own home page carries a security reminder that it won't ask for extra fees via SMS or email, and its Pakistan footer has a "Report suspicious activity" page.

  • A message saying your parcel is held and you must pay a customs or release fee through a link.
  • A WhatsApp or Facebook seller calling itself "Temu Pakistan" asking you to send money to a personal JazzCash, Easypaisa or bank account.
  • A request for your card's one-time code by phone. No genuine company needs it.
  • A link to a look-alike domain that is not temu.com.

Customs can genuinely assess more duty than Temu estimated, because Temu's terms make you the importer. If a real courier says duty is due, ask for the customs assessment document and check the order in the app before paying anything. Parcels worth Rs 1,000 or less pay no duties or taxes under section 19C of the Customs Act. For more on the wider risks, see is Temu safe in Pakistan.

Where does your money go if Temu refunds you?

A refund goes back to the method you paid with, or to Temu credit if you choose it. Temu's Pakistan return policy says refunds to the original method can take 5 to 14 business days, and up to 30 days, depending on your bank or wallet. Temu credit is faster and does not expire, but it typically cannot be cashed out and can only be spent on Temu. The full process is in how to return a Temu order in Pakistan.
