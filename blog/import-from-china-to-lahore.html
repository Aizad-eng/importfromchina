How to Import One Item From China to Lahore

By importfromchina.shop teamUpdated October 3, 20266 min read
Quick answer

A single item from China reaches Lahore by air. It flies into Allama Iqbal International Airport in Lahore or into Karachi and then travels by road, clears Pakistan Customs, and a local courier delivers it to your door. Door to door, this commonly takes 7 to 15 days after the seller dispatches. A single-item service gives you one rupee price for the whole journey, paid once, before it ships.

Can I get a single item from China delivered in Lahore?

Yes. Lahore is one of the three cities in Pakistan with an international airport that handles cargo, alongside Karachi and Islamabad. That means a parcel from China can land directly in Lahore, or land in Karachi and come up by road. Either way, the last stretch is a local courier bringing it to your address in DHA, Johar Town, Gulberg, Model Town, Bahria Town or anywhere else in the city. You do not need a company, an import licence or a customs agent of your own for one item.

How does the item physically travel from China to Lahore?

  1. Seller dispatch in China. The seller on 1688, Taobao, Alibaba or AliExpress sends the item to a consolidation warehouse in China, typically in Guangzhou, Shenzhen or Yiwu.
  2. Air cargo to Pakistan. The parcel is loaded with other parcels onto a flight. Some lines fly into Lahore directly. Many fly into Karachi, which has the largest cargo volume, and the parcel then moves to Lahore by road.
  3. Customs clearance. Pakistan Customs assesses the parcel. For an individual, clearance is done by the courier or agent using your CNIC, not by you in person.
  4. Local delivery. A Pakistani courier such as TCS, Leopards, M&P or a similar network delivers to your door.

You do not see most of this. What you see is tracking, then a knock on the door.

How long does delivery to Lahore take?

At the time of writing, air freight door to door, including customs, commonly takes 7 to 15 days after the seller dispatches. Add the seller's own handling time, usually 1 to 3 days, and a little more for pre-order items. Ordinary post from AliExpress is a different story and takes 25 to 60 days. Around Chinese New Year, late January to mid February, expect delays on top of this because factories and sellers close for 2 to 4 weeks.

Does it matter whether it lands in Lahore or Karachi?

Not much for you. A parcel that lands in Karachi and travels by road to Lahore usually arrives within the same 7 to 15 day window. Karachi has more flights and more cargo capacity, so lines often route through it even for Lahore deliveries. What matters more is that customs is cleared properly and that the courier has a correct address and a working phone number.

What do Lahore buyers commonly order from China?

Lahore has a large electronics and accessories market in Hall Road and a big fashion and home goods market, so people in Lahore tend to import things that are not stocked locally or that carry a big markup here. Common examples:

  • Gadgets and accessories: earbuds, smartwatches, chargers, mechanical keyboards, phone gimbals.
  • Specialist tools and hobby gear: soldering stations, 3D printer parts, camera accessories, cycling components.
  • Home and kitchen devices: small appliances, LED lighting, smart home sensors.
  • Fashion and shoes in a specific style or size seen online.
  • Specific items from Taobao, 1688 or Temu-style listings that never reach Daraz.

If the item is a phone, remember PTA registration applies. If it has a loose lithium battery, is a liquid or a medicine, read what you cannot import from China to Pakistan first.

What does it cost to get one item to Lahore?

Three things make up the cost: the item, the freight, and Pakistan customs duty and taxes. Duty and taxes depend on the type of item and commonly add 25 to 60 percent of the customs value. Rates change with budgets and SROs, so confirm the current rate or ask for a delivered price. Freight for air is charged on actual or volumetric weight, whichever is higher, so a light but bulky item costs more than its weight suggests. Our shipping cost and time guide explains the maths.

What do I need to receive a parcel in Lahore?

  • A full address with area, block or sector, house number and a landmark if the street is hard to find.
  • A mobile number that answers. Couriers in Lahore call before delivery and return the parcel to depot if they cannot reach you.
  • Your CNIC number for customs clearance. Keep a copy handy in case the courier asks for it.
  • Patience with tracking gaps. Tracking often goes quiet between leaving China and clearing customs in Pakistan.
