What Is MOQ (Minimum Order Quantity) on Alibaba and 1688?

By Muhammad Shamshad AslamUpdated October 23, 20265 min read
Quick answer

MOQ is short for minimum order quantity, the smallest number of units a seller will accept in one order. On Alibaba.com it appears as the minimum order on each listing, and on 1688 as 起批量. An MOQ of 1, shown on 1688 as 1件起批, means you can buy a single piece.

What does MOQ mean?

MOQ means minimum order quantity: the lowest number of units a supplier allows a buyer to purchase at one time. Alibaba's own seller guide defines it this way and says sellers set it themselves when they post a product. A listing with an MOQ of 50 will not let you check out with 49.

MOQ is a rule of the seller, not of the platform. Two sellers of the same item can show very different minimums.

Why do Chinese suppliers set an MOQ?

Because small orders cost them time for little profit. Alibaba's seller guide says suppliers use MOQs to avoid spending resources on orders that earn little, and notes that the more of something a factory makes, the less each unit costs. Many sellers on Alibaba.com are manufacturers set up for large runs, so the minimum reflects how their production works.

One Alibaba seller blog also notes that some suppliers list a high MOQ mainly to cut down on casual enquiries. So a high number is not always the seller's final word, but treat it as the rule unless the seller says otherwise in writing.

Where do you see MOQ on Alibaba and 1688?

On both sites it sits next to the price, usually as part of a price ladder where the unit price drops as quantity rises.

Read the ladder carefully. The big headline price is often the price for the highest tier. One piece usually costs the top of the ladder. WorldFirst's 1688 guide makes the same point: tiered pricing means lower unit costs only for larger orders, though it also notes that many 1688 listings allow orders of one or two items. Can you buy one piece when the MOQ is higher?

Sometimes. These are the realistic routes for someone who wants one item for their own use.

  1. Find another listing with MOQ 1. The same item is often listed by several sellers. Search by image on 1688 or Taobao to find them. See how to search Taobao and 1688 by image.
  2. Ask for a sample. Alibaba's sample guide says some product pages have a sample option you can tick, and otherwise you can request one by message. A factory sample is a finished unit made to the maker's standard specification, which is exactly one ready-made piece.
  3. Check Taobao or AliExpress. Consumer sites sell single pieces as normal, often from the same factories.
  4. Ask the seller directly. For stock items, some sellers will sell one piece at a higher price. Get the agreement in writing.

Expect the single-piece price to be higher than the tier price, and expect shipping for one item to be a larger share of the total. Our guide can I buy one piece from a Chinese factory goes into this in more depth.

Does MOQ include shipping or customs?

No. MOQ only sets the quantity. The listed price is normally the factory price before shipping, and Pakistan customs is separate again. For one item, duty can apply to anything worth more than Rs 1,000 arriving by post or courier, under section 19C of the Customs Act. Frequently asked questions

What does MOQ 1 mean?

The seller will sell a single piece. On 1688 it appears as 1件起批, and on Alibaba.com as a minimum order of 1.

What does 起批量 mean on 1688?

It means minimum order quantity. The number next to it is the fewest units you can buy from that listing.

Is the MOQ price the price I pay for one piece?

Usually not. The headline price is often for the largest quantity tier. A single piece is normally charged at the top of the price ladder.

Can I ask an Alibaba supplier to lower the MOQ?

You can ask. Some sellers will sell one piece of a stock item or a sample at a higher price. Get the agreement in writing before paying.

Does Taobao or AliExpress have an MOQ?

Generally no. They are consumer sites, so single pieces are normal, which makes them a good place to look when an Alibaba listing has a high MOQ.
