What is the Anbernic RG 477M and who is it for?

The RG 477M is an Android handheld gaming device from Anbernic, a Chinese maker of handhelds. It looks like a compact game console with a screen in the middle, a D-pad and buttons, and two joysticks. Inside, it runs Android 14 on a phone-class MediaTek Dimensity 8300 chip, so it plays Android games and apps with physical controls instead of a touch screen.

It suits you if you want:

  • A premium, metal-bodied handheld that still fits a jacket pocket.
  • Proper controls for Android games instead of playing on a phone screen.
  • A 4:3 screen shape, which reviewers note suits classic-style games well.

It does not suit you if you want something light. Anbernic lists it at 354g, and a hands-on review called it heavier than many buyers expect.

Anbernic rates the 5300mAh battery for about 7 hours. Real battery life depends on the game, the screen brightness and whether you use 120Hz. Lowering the refresh rate and brightness for less demanding games is the simplest way to stretch it on a long journey.

How much storage do you need, and which microSD card?

The 8GB model has 128GB of internal storage, and the 12GB model has 256GB. Android games and apps vary a lot in size, so start with the internal storage and add a card only when you need it. The RG 477M takes microSD cards up to 2TB, according to Anbernic.

  • Buy a card from a known brand from a shop that will replace it if it is faulty.
  • After inserting it, check the capacity shown in Android's storage settings matches the label. Fake-capacity cards report a bigger size than they really hold.
  • Let the device format the card before you use it, and copy your own files to it after that.

This is the other reason to pick the version without a bundled SD card. You stay in control of the card's brand, size and warranty, and you are not paying extra for something you can buy locally.

What comes in the box, and do I need a charger?

Anbernic lists the RG 477M, a USB-C charging cable, a screen protector, a user manual and a silicone protective case. No wall adapter is listed. To charge at the full rated 27W, use a USB-C charger that supports 9V output, such as a USB Power Delivery charger of 27W or more. A lower-powered USB-C charger should still charge it, just more slowly.

Pakistan's mains supply is 220V to 240V. Many USB-C chargers are rated 100V to 240V, so check the label before you plug in, and use a proper adapter rather than forcing a Chinese or US plug into a loose socket.

What do reviewers say you should know before buying?

  • Weight. The reviewer found it heavy for its size, close to Anbernic's RG Slide.
  • Fan noise. The retrohandhelds.gg review found the fan comes on often and is noticeable. Play with headphones if that bothers you.
  • Digital triggers. The shoulder triggers are digital, not analog. Racing games that rely on gradual throttle feel less precise.
  • Tall sticks. The joysticks sit high. The reviewer swapped the caps for lower ones.
  • Controls and build. The same review rated the D-pad and face buttons well and found the rounded metal body comfortable.

Does the RG 477M work in Pakistan, and does it need PTA approval?

It connects over Wi-Fi 6E and Bluetooth 5.3. Anbernic's spec sheet lists no SIM slot or mobile network, so it is not a mobile phone and the DIRBS phone registration process is not the usual concern. Rules change, so check current PTA guidance if you are unsure. Home Wi-Fi in Pakistan works normally, and Bluetooth controllers and headphones pair as with any Android device. With USB-C DisplayPort output it can also connect to a TV or monitor with a suitable USB-C to HDMI adapter.

What should you check when it arrives?

  1. Box contents. Console, USB-C cable, screen protector, manual and silicone case.
  2. Correct version. Go to Settings, then About, and confirm the memory and storage match what you ordered.
  3. Screen. Look for dead pixels on a plain white and a plain black background, and confirm the 120Hz option in display settings.
  4. Controls. Press every button, move both sticks in full circles and tilt the device to confirm the gyroscope responds.
  5. Charging. Plug in a USB-C charger and confirm the battery percentage rises.
  6. Wi-Fi and Bluetooth. Join your network and pair a headset.
  7. Fan. Play a demanding game for ten minutes and listen. The reviewer found the fan noticeable, so some noise is normal, but grinding or rattling is not.
  8. Screen protector. Anbernic includes one. Apply it in a clean, dust-free room before you start carrying the device around.

Common mistakes when buying the RG 477M

  • Choosing a bundled SD card option instead of buying a branded card locally.
  • Paying for 12GB when 8GB would do, according to the reviewer.
  • Assuming a wall charger is in the box. Anbernic lists only a cable, so use your own 27W-capable USB-C charger.
  • Confusing it with older, cheaper Anbernic models such as the RG35XX series, which are very different devices.
  • Forgetting the weight. It is a solid metal device, not a featherweight.
