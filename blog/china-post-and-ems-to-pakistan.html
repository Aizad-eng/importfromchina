China Post and EMS to Pakistan: Times, Tracking and Pakistan Post

By Muhammad Shamshad AslamUpdated November 12, 20269 min read
Quick answer

China Post and EMS parcels reach Pakistan through the postal network: China Post flies them out, Pakistan Customs assesses them, and Pakistan Post delivers them and collects any duty in person. EMS is the faster, better tracked tier. Neither publishes a fixed China to Pakistan time we could find, so expect weeks for ordinary post. Track it on ep.gov.pk.

What is the difference between China Post and EMS?

China Post is China's national postal operator, and EMS is the express mail service that national posts run, including China Post and Pakistan Post. A China Post parcel and an EMS parcel both travel through the postal system, not through a private courier, but EMS is the faster, priority tier with tracking at more points.

In practice you meet three kinds of postal items from China:

  • EMS. Express mail with a tracking number starting with E. It is handed to Pakistan Post's EMS network on arrival.
  • Registered or tracked small packets. Light items sent as letter post with tracking. Many low-cost online orders travel this way.
  • Parcel post. Heavier items sent as ordinary postal parcels, slower than EMS.

All of them end with Pakistan Post. That is the key difference from DHL, FedEx or UPS, where one company carries the parcel from end to end. Our guide on Pakistan Post vs courier for parcels from China compares the two systems in full. This page goes deeper into the postal side.

How long do China Post and EMS take to reach Pakistan?

We could not find an official China Post or Pakistan Post delivery standard for items from China to Pakistan when we checked in November 2026. What can be said honestly is this: EMS is usually the quickest postal option, and ordinary small packets and parcel post commonly take several weeks door to door, much longer than a courier.

Postal time is made of stages that each add days:

  1. The seller posts the item and China Post accepts and sorts it.
  2. It waits for space on a flight, often bundled with other mail.
  3. It arrives at an office of exchange in Pakistan, where Pakistan Customs examines dutiable items.
  4. Pakistan Post sorts it and sends it to your local post office or delivers it.

Postal routes to Pakistan can also be disrupted by events outside anyone's control. In May 2025, for example, Hongkong Post announced that mail delivery to Pakistan, which had been affected by events in the region, had returned to normal. When a route pauses, items already in the system simply wait.

How do you read a China Post tracking number?

A China Post tracking number is 13 characters long and ends in CN, following the Universal Postal Union's S10 standard. The format is two letters for the service, eight digits for the serial number, one check digit, and the two-letter code of the country that issued it. The first letter tells you what kind of item it is:

Starts withServiceWhat to expect
E (EA to EZ)EMS express mailFastest postal tier, tracked through Pakistan Post's EMS system
C (CA to CZ)Parcel postHeavier parcels, slower, tracked
R (RA to RZ)Registered letter postSmall packets with registration
L (LA to LZ)Tracked letter postSmall packets with tracking
V (VA to VZ)Insured letter postSmall insured items
U (UA to UZ)Other letter postMay have little tracking once it leaves China

So EA123456789CN would be an EMS item issued by China Post. If your number does not follow this pattern at all, it is a courier or consolidator number and is tracked on that company's own site, not on Pakistan Post's.

Where do you track a China Post or EMS parcel in Pakistan?

Once the item is in Pakistan, you track it on Pakistan Post's official site, ep.gov.pk, using the same 13-character number. Pakistan Post calls this its Express Mail Track and Trace System. It says ep.gov.pk is its only tracking site, and its other official site is pakpost.gov.pk. Any other site claiming to be Pakistan Post tracking is unofficial.

Before the item leaves China, the China Post or seller's tracking shows acceptance and export scans. Then expect a quiet period. A postal item can show nothing for days or weeks between leaving China and its first Pakistani scan. That gap is normal for post and is the main reason people worry. What does Pakistan Post do when the parcel arrives?

Pakistan Post receives the item from China, presents dutiable items to Pakistan Customs, then delivers it or holds it for collection. According to Pakistan Post, Customs examines all dutiable inbound articles and sets the duty based on the value of the goods. Pakistan Post does not decide the duty. It only collects it on Customs' behalf.

Depending on your city and the item, one of three things usually happens:

  • The item is delivered to your door with nothing to pay.
  • The item is delivered with duty to pay, which you pay to Pakistan Post.
  • You receive a notice to collect it from a post office, where you pay any duty and show your CNIC.

Pakistan Post's helpline is 051-111-111-117. Have your tracking number and CNIC with you when you call or visit.

Do you pay customs duty on a China Post parcel?

You pay customs duty on a China Post parcel only if its value is above Rs 1,000. Section 19C of the Customs Act, as amended by the Finance Act 2025, says no duties and taxes are charged on goods imported through post or courier where the value does not exceed one thousand rupees. Before that change the limit was Rs 5,000.

Above the limit, the whole value is assessed on the normal tariff for that product, and Customs can treat several items from the same sender as one import. Because Pakistan Post collects duty through its own manual system, paying is done in person, not online. How do you spot a fake Pakistan Post payment message?

Any message asking you to pay Pakistan Post through a link is fake. Pakistan Post says it has no online system or call centre to collect any amount from customers, that it never asks for online payment through its website, and that you should not pay through any link, phone call, cash app or card. It also says it is not responsible for money lost to such fraud.

Signs of a scam message:

  • A link to a site that is not ep.gov.pk or pakpost.gov.pk.
  • A small "redelivery" or "customs" fee to pay by card.
  • A tracking number that does not match the one the seller gave you.
  • Pressure to pay within hours or lose the parcel.

If in doubt, call 051-111-111-117 or visit your post office with the real tracking number.

What can you do if a postal parcel goes missing?

If a postal item stops moving for a long time, first check it on ep.gov.pk, then file a complaint with Pakistan Post and, separately, raise it with the seller or platform. Pakistan Post's EMS page says complaints about loss or non-delivery must be made within 30 days, and that compensation is limited and based on actual loss. So do not wait months before acting.

  1. Confirm the number format and that it ends in CN.
  2. Check the last scan date on ep.gov.pk.
  3. Call the helpline or use Pakistan Post's complaints page, quoting the number.
  4. Open a dispute with the platform before its protection window closes, keeping screenshots.

When should you choose post over courier for one item?

Choose post for light, cheap items you are not in a hurry for, where the lower shipping cost matters more than speed. Choose a courier or a single-item service when the item is valuable, needed by a date, or something you would hate to lose track of in a long gap.

Frequently asked questions

Is China EMS the same as China Post?

EMS is the express tier of the postal system, run by China Post on the Chinese side. Both are postal services, but EMS is faster and better tracked than ordinary China Post packets.

Can I track a China Post parcel on Pakistan Post's website?

Yes, once it reaches Pakistan. Enter the same 13-character number ending in CN on ep.gov.pk, Pakistan Post's official tracking site.

Why has my China Post tracking not updated for two weeks?

Postal items often go quiet between leaving China and the first Pakistani scan while they wait for flights and customs. Act if there is no movement for a long time, within Pakistan Post's 30-day complaint window.

Do I have to pay duty to receive a China Post parcel?

Only if it is worth more than Rs 1,000. If duty is due, you pay Pakistan Post in person, at delivery or at the post office, never by an online link.

Does Pakistan Post send payment links for customs?

No. Pakistan Post says it never asks for online payment and has no online system or call centre to collect money. Any such link is a scam.

What does a tracking number starting with L or U mean?

Under the postal S10 format, a number starting with L is tracked letter post and one starting with U is other letter post, which may have little tracking after it leaves China.
