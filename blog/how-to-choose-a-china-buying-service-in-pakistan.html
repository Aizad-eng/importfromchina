How to Choose a China Buying Service in Pakistan: A Checklist

By importfromchina.shop teamUpdated October 17, 20269 min read
Quick answer

Choose a service that gives you one total price in rupees before you pay, covering the item, international shipping, Pakistan customs and its fee; states its fee upfront; takes payment to an account in a business name you can check; puts its refund terms in writing; and handles your kind of order, one item or bulk. Ask for an expected delivery date and what happens if it is missed. For a single item worth around Rs 10,000 or more, these checks matter more than the lowest headline rate.

What kind of service do you actually need?

Before checking any business, decide which type fits your order. A freight forwarder only ships goods you have already paid for. A shopping agent buys for you and usually bills shipping separately. A sourcing agent handles bulk and factory orders. A single-item service buys, ships and clears one item for one price. Our comparison of shopping agents, forwarders and single-item services explains each.

The checklist below works for all of them, but the right answers differ. A bulk buyer expects staged payments. A single-item buyer should expect one.

The checklist: twelve things to confirm before you pay

  1. Is the price a delivered total? It should cover the item, shipping inside China, international shipping, Pakistan customs and delivery to your city. If any part is "billed later", you do not yet know the cost.
  2. Is the fee stated? You should know what the service earns, either as a stated amount within the total or as a clear rule.
  3. How many payments will there be? One, or a first payment and a second after weighing? Get it in writing.
  4. Who pays if customs charges more than expected? This is the most common surprise. A good answer is "we do".
  5. Is anything collected at the door? The answer should be no, or a stated amount.
  6. What is the expected delivery date? Ask for a date, not just "two to three weeks".
  7. What happens if it does not arrive? Look for a clear point after which you get a full refund.
  8. What happens if it arrives wrong, damaged or not working? Ask how to report it, how soon and what evidence is needed.
  9. What if the seller cancels or is out of stock? You should get your money back quickly or be offered an alternative you can refuse.
  10. Which platforms can it buy from? Alibaba, 1688, Taobao and AliExpress each need different accounts. Check yours is covered.
  11. Does it handle your quantity? Some services take single items only. Others want a minimum order value.
  12. Will it tell you before you pay if an item cannot be shipped? Batteries, liquids, drones, medicines and branded replicas cause problems. A good service flags them up front.

How can you check that a business is real?

A few checks take minutes.

  • Tax registration. FBR runs an Online Verification Portal through its IRIS system, and publishes Active Taxpayer Lists for income tax and sales tax. If a business gives you its NTN, you can look it up. A registered business is not automatically a good one, but a business that will not share any registration detail is a warning.
  • Company registration. Private limited companies are registered with the SECP. Sole proprietors are not, so a missing SECP record does not mean a scam on its own.
  • Account title. Before you send money, check that the name on the receiving account matches the business you are dealing with. If your banking app shows the account title, read it.
  • A written policy. A refund policy on a website, with a date on it, is a stronger commitment than a promise in a chat.
  • Consistency. The business name on the website, the payment account and the messages should all match.

How do you compare quotes fairly?

Quotes often arrive in different shapes. One service gives a per kilogram freight rate. Another gives item plus commission. A third gives one delivered price. To compare, turn each into the same thing: the total you will have paid when the item is in your hands.

  • Add shipping inside China if it is not included.
  • Remember that air freight is charged on the greater of real and volumetric weight, so a bulky box costs more than its weight suggests.
  • Add Pakistan customs if it is billed later. Parcels by post or courier worth Rs 1,000 or less are duty-free under section 19C of the Customs Act. Above that, duty and taxes apply, and our customs duty guide explains how they are worked out.
  • Add any delivery or handling charge in Pakistan.

The result is your landed price. Our guide on what a landed price is walks through the idea. If one quote is far below the others, find out which of these lines it leaves out.

Also check the value. Shipping one item from China usually costs Rs 5,000 to 20,000, so for a single item, a buying service makes sense when the item is worth around Rs 10,000 or more.

What payment method is safest?

Bank transfer and Raast leave a clear record with the receiving account's details, which helps if you ever need to raise a complaint. JazzCash and Easypaisa transfers also leave a record. Whichever you use:

  • Pay a business account, not a third person's personal account.
  • Keep the receipt and the conversation where the price was agreed.
  • Pay once against a stated total, rather than in unplanned extra instalments.
  • Never send money to a different account because a message says the account has "changed" without confirming through a channel you already trust.

The same logic applies if you buy on Alibaba yourself. Trade Assurance only protects orders paid through Alibaba.com, and a supplier asking for a direct transfer is asking you to give that up.

What refund terms should a good service offer?

Read the refund policy before you pay, not after. Strong terms answer each of these in writing:

  • Cancelling before the service buys.
  • The seller cancels or the item is out of stock.
  • The parcel does not arrive by a stated point after the agreed date.
  • The item arrives wrong, damaged or not working, and how to report it.
  • Customs charges more than expected.
  • Customs seizes the item, and whose mistake it was.
  • You change your mind after purchase, which normally is not refundable for a one-off international order.
  • A fault that appears later, which is usually a warranty matter with the seller or brand.

For what is realistic on returns to China, see refunds and returns when importing from China.

What are the common mistakes?

  • Choosing on the per kilogram rate alone. It leaves out the item, the China leg and often customs.
  • Paying a deposit with no total. You have committed without knowing the cost.
  • Not asking about customs. This is where most surprise bills come from.
  • Sending a cheap item alone. The shipping can exceed the product's price.
  • Asking a single-item service for stock, or a bulk agent for one piece. Each is built for its own kind of order.
  • Accepting promises only in chat. Ask for the policy in writing.
  • Ignoring restricted items. A service cannot get a branded replica or an undeclared battery through customs for you.

Frequently asked questions

How do I know if a China buying service in Pakistan is trustworthy?

Check that it gives one delivered price before you pay, states its fee, takes payment into an account in its own business name and has written refund terms. Ask for its NTN and look it up on FBR's online verification.

What should a China buying service include in its price?

The item, shipping inside China, international shipping, Pakistan customs, delivery to your city and its own fee. Anything billed later means the quote is not the final cost.

Is it safe to pay a buying service before my item ships?

It is normal practice, as long as you pay a verifiable business account against one agreed total and the refund terms are in writing. Keep the receipt and the conversation.

Why are China buying service quotes so different from each other?

They often leave out different things, such as China delivery, customs or a second shipping bill. Convert every quote into the total you will have paid on delivery before comparing.

Should I choose the cheapest China buying service?

Only after comparing delivered totals and refund terms. A low first quote that adds customs and shipping later can end up costing more than a higher all-in price.

Is a buying service worth it for a cheap item from China?

Usually not. Shipping one item from China commonly costs Rs 5,000 to 20,000, so a service makes sense for items worth around Rs 10,000 or more.
