Cheapest Way to Get One Item From China to Pakistan

By importfromchina.shop teamUpdated October 3, 20267 min read
Quick answer

For one item, the cheapest route depends on value and weight. Very cheap, light items are cheapest on AliExpress direct if you can wait 30 to 60 days and your card works. For anything worth more than a few thousand rupees, a single-item sourcing service that uses consolidated air freight and includes customs in one price is usually cheaper than Alibaba courier shipping and far more predictable than AliExpress, with delivery in 7 to 15 days.

What are the ways to get one item from China?

  1. AliExpress, DHgate or Banggood direct. Order yourself, wait for the post, pay duty if assessed on arrival.
  2. Alibaba direct with courier. Buy one piece, pay DHL or FedEx rates, pay duty to the courier.
  3. Someone travelling. A friend or relative brings it in their baggage.
  4. Single-item sourcing service. Send the link, pay one rupee price once, receive it duty-paid in 7 to 15 days.

The sourcing service's single price usually lands close to the Alibaba-plus-courier total, and below it for light items, because consolidated air freight costs less per kilogram than retail courier rates. The difference is you know the total before you commit.

When is AliExpress direct genuinely cheapest?

  • The item costs under about PKR 2,000 to 3,000.
  • It is small and light, so it travels as a packet and is less likely to be assessed.
  • You do not mind waiting one to two months or the chance it never arrives.
  • Your card works. See how to pay Chinese sellers from Pakistan.

When does a sourcing service win on cost?

  • The item is on Taobao or 1688, where the price is 20 to 40 percent lower than AliExpress. Those savings often cover the service fee and shipping.
  • The item weighs more than half a kilogram, where courier rates climb fast.
  • The item is clearly dutiable (electronics, beauty devices, tools), so you would pay duty anyway and prefer to know the amount upfront.

What about the hidden costs people forget?

  • Currency conversion. Cards charge 2 to 4 percent on foreign transactions, plus the bank's exchange rate.
  • Clearance fees. Couriers add a fixed fee for customs handling on top of the duty.
  • Failed orders. A declined card or a cancelled AliExpress order costs time, and refunds take weeks to return to a Pakistani card.
  • Returns. There is no cheap way to return anything to China from Pakistan. Getting the right item the first time is the saving.

Frequently asked questions

What is the cheapest way to ship one item from China to Pakistan?

For very cheap, light items, ordinary post via AliExpress. For anything else, consolidated air freight through a single-item service is cheaper than courier and includes customs in one price.

Is it cheaper to import one item or buy it on Daraz?

Often cheaper to import if the item is not common on Daraz or carries a big markup there. For everyday items that Daraz stocks, Daraz is usually cheaper and faster. See our Daraz comparison guide.

How much does a sourcing service charge for one item?

A fee on top of the item, shipping and duty, stated upfront as part of one price. There is no separate shipping or customs bill.

Can I avoid customs duty on a single item?

Not legally. Small post parcels sometimes pass unassessed, but that is luck, not a rule. A duty-paid service removes the uncertainty.

How long does the cheapest option take?

Post via AliExpress takes 30 to 60 days. Air freight through a service takes 7 to 15 days door to door.
