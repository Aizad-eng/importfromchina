Customs Duty on Shein Orders in Pakistan: What to Expect

Do you pay customs duty on Shein orders in Pakistan?

You might. Shein orders come into Pakistan as post or courier parcels, and Pakistan's rule for those is simple at the bottom end: under section 19C of the Customs Act, a parcel worth Rs 1,000 or less pays no duties or taxes. Above Rs 1,000, the whole value can be charged, not just the part above Rs 1,000.

Rs 1,000 is a low line for a Shein order. When we checked on 11 November 2026, Shein's free-shipping threshold for Pakistan was US$14.43, about Rs 4,000 at the rate Shein listed. So most Shein orders that qualify for free shipping are worth well over Rs 1,000 and can attract duty and tax.

"Can" is the key word. Whether a particular parcel is stopped and charged, and how much, is decided by customs when it arrives. In Pakistan that is hard to predict, which is why this guide explains how it works rather than promising a number.

Who is responsible for customs on a Shein order?

You are. Shein's Terms and Conditions (section 2.5, Customs) say that, as the importer, you are responsible for complying with all laws and regulations in your own country. The same section says you authorise Shein and its affiliates to make and amend customs declarations and instruct customs agents in your name.

The section also sets a rule that matters in Pakistan: all consignee names, addresses and payer names must be valid, and if missing or incorrect information stops the shipment or customs clearance, Shein will not compensate you. Use your real name as on your CNIC, the same name on the payment if you can, and a full address with a phone number you answer.

What does customs look at when it charges a parcel?

Customs works from the declared value of the goods and the type of goods. For a parcel above the Rs 1,000 line, the charges can include:

  • Customs duty, at a rate that depends on the item's tariff heading. Pakistan's main slabs are 0, 5, 10, 15 and 20%, with some goods higher.
  • Additional customs duty, 0% on the 0 to 15% slabs and 2% or more on higher ones.
  • Regulatory duty, on listed goods only.
  • Sales tax of 18% on the value plus those duties.
  • Income tax under section 148, which depends on the goods and is doubled for people not on the Active Taxpayers List.

Customs converts the dollar value at NBP rates published by FBR, and there is no flat courier rate: courier parcels are assessed on normal tariff rates. Clothing, shoes and accessories, which make up most Shein orders, often fall into higher-duty headings than, say, books or many machines.

Can you calculate the exact duty on a Shein parcel before you order?

No. In Pakistan, customs decides the value it accepts, the heading it uses and whether to charge at all when the parcel arrives, and two similar parcels can be treated differently. Any figure you work out in advance, including from our rough duty estimator, is a rough guess, not the bill. Our main guide on customs duty from China to Pakistan explains why in more detail.

What you can control is the size of the risk. A smaller order is a smaller amount at stake if duty is charged.

Can you avoid duty by splitting a Shein order?

Splitting a large order into several small ones to stay under Rs 1,000 each rarely works with Shein and is not worth trying. Most single Shein items with shipping are near or above Rs 1,000 already. Shein can also pack several orders into one parcel: its On-Time Delivery terms mention cases where "multiple orders" are shipped "in one package", and customs looks at the parcel in front of it. And deliberately breaking up a shipment to dodge duty is the kind of thing the de minimis rule is not meant for.

Order what you need, in one order, and expect that duty may apply.

Does unpaid duty delay a Shein parcel?

Yes. A parcel that customs has charged waits until the amount is paid. Shein's On-Time Delivery terms also exclude delays "caused by users failing to pay related taxes or duties on time", so you would not get the late-delivery points in that case. If you are asked to pay, do it promptly through the official route the courier gives you. Our guide on a parcel stuck at customs in Pakistan covers what to do if it sits there.

How do you spot a fake Shein customs fee message?

Fake "customs fee" texts are common. Before you pay anything:

  • Do not pay through a link in an SMS or WhatsApp message until you have checked it with the courier through its official website or helpline, not through numbers in the message.
  • Check the order in the Shein app. If it is still in China or in transit, no Pakistani duty is due yet.
  • Ask the courier for the assessment document and the official payment route, and check the amount on it.
  • Do not send money to a personal JazzCash, Easypaisa or bank account.

Is a Shein order personal use or a commercial import?

A few items for yourself are personal use. Many of the same item, in different sizes or colours, looks like stock for resale and can be treated as a commercial import, with different rules. Our guide on personal use vs commercial import explains where that line sits.
