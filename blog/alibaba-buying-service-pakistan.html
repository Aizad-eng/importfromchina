Is There a Service That Buys From Alibaba for Me in Pakistan?

By importfromchina.shop teamUpdated October 17, 20269 min read
Quick answer

Yes. Several kinds of service in Pakistan will buy from Alibaba.com for you: sourcing agents for bulk, shopping agents, and single-item import services for one piece. You send the listing link, the service confirms the one-piece price with the supplier, pays through Alibaba, and gets the item shipped to Pakistan and cleared. You pay in rupees by bank transfer, Raast, JazzCash or Easypaisa instead of a foreign card. For one item worth around Rs 10,000 or more, a service that quotes one delivered price is usually simplest.

Can't I just buy on Alibaba myself?

You can. Alibaba.com is open to buyers in Pakistan, and its Trade Assurance checkout accepts credit and debit cards, digital wallets and bank transfers. Some listings, often from trading companies, sell a single piece. Our guide on buying one item on Alibaba from Pakistan covers that route step by step.

People look for a buying service when one of these gets in the way:

  • The card fails. Pakistani cards often need international use switched on by the bank, and some still get declined.
  • The shipping quote is huge. For one piece, suppliers commonly quote express courier, which can cost more than the item.
  • The duty is unknown. A courier clears the parcel and bills you duty and taxes on arrival, and you only learn the amount then.
  • The supplier is slow or vague. Getting a clear answer on variant, voltage or plug type takes back and forth.
  • It is simply a lot of work for one purchase you will not repeat.

What does a buying service do on Alibaba that you would otherwise do yourself?

A decent service takes over the parts that cost time and the parts that cost money.

  1. Confirms the listing sells one. A price ladder that starts at "1 set" or "1 carton" may hide many units. The service asks the supplier directly.
  2. Pins down the variant. Model number, colour, plug type and voltage are confirmed with the supplier and written into the order, because Trade Assurance protects what is written in the order rather than what was said in chat.
  3. Chooses where it ships. Instead of express courier to Pakistan, the supplier can ship to a warehouse in China. From there the item goes to Pakistan with other parcels by consolidated air freight, which is much cheaper per kilogram. See air freight vs courier for one item.
  4. Pays through Alibaba. Payment goes into Trade Assurance from the service's account, so you never need a working foreign card.
  5. Handles Pakistan customs. Depending on the service, customs is included in the price or billed after clearance.

Who holds Trade Assurance when a service buys for me?

The service does. Trade Assurance holds the payment in escrow and releases it to the supplier after the product is received and confirmed. Alibaba says buyers can claim a refund if an order has not shipped, goes missing or arrives with defects. That protection belongs to the account that placed the order.

So when a service buys for you, your protection is the service's own refund terms, not Alibaba's. That is not worse by default, but it means the service's written policy is the thing to read before you pay. Ask what happens if the supplier never ships, if the item arrives wrong, and if customs charges more than expected. For how Trade Assurance itself works, read Alibaba Trade Assurance explained.

One warning applies either way. Trade Assurance only covers orders paid through Alibaba.com. A supplier who offers a discount for a direct bank transfer is asking you to give up that protection. A buying service that pays suppliers off the platform carries the same risk, and you should ask how it pays.

What if I do not have an Alibaba link yet?

Alibaba has a Request for Quotation feature, usually called RFQ. You post what you need, with specifications and quantity, and suppliers send quotes. It is designed around trade orders with quantities and specifications, so a request for one piece may not get many useful replies.

For a single item, it is usually faster to find the listing yourself. Search Alibaba with the product name and model number, or search by image on Alibaba, 1688 or Taobao using a screenshot. Our guide on searching by image shows how. Once you have a link, any buying service can work with it. We need a link to quote.

Which Alibaba items are worth buying through a service?

The best fits are higher-value items that are hard to find in Pakistan: test and measurement equipment, specialist tools, machine parts, studio and audio gear, and niche electronics. We recently sourced one TDH39 or DD45 type audiometric headset from a verified Alibaba supplier and delivered it in Pakistan. That is the kind of order this route suits.

The value matters. Shipping one item from China usually costs Rs 5,000 to 20,000, so a single-item service makes sense for items worth around Rs 10,000 or more. For a cheap phone case, the shipping alone defeats the point. Look on a site that already ships to Pakistan or buy locally.

What happens to warranty and after-sales?

Alibaba advertises extras on Trade Assurance orders, such as on-site installation, maintenance and replacement parts for eligible products, and a local Easy Return for defective items in eligible countries. These depend on the product and the buyer's country, so do not assume they reach Pakistan. Check the listing and the order page if they matter to you.

In practice, a single item bought from China has a different kind of after-sales than one bought from a local shop. If it arrives faulty, that is a refund or replacement question for whoever bought it, the service or you. If it develops a fault months later, it becomes a warranty claim with the supplier or the brand, which usually means sending details and photos and waiting for a reply rather than walking into a service centre. Before buying, ask the supplier what warranty applies outside China and whether spare parts can be ordered later. For equipment you depend on, that answer can matter more than a small price difference.

How can I tell a buying service is paying through Alibaba?

You do not need to see their account, but you can ask sensible questions. Will the order be placed as a Trade Assurance order? Will the variant and specification be written into it? Which shipping method will the supplier use, and to where? A service that buys through Alibaba properly can answer these simply. Vague replies are a reason to keep looking.

What should I send a buying service?

  1. The Alibaba link, copied from the browser or the app's share button.
  2. The exact variant: model, colour, size, plug type, voltage, and any accessory bundle.
  3. The quantity, which for a single-item service is one.
  4. Your city, so delivery is priced to the right place.
  5. Anything unusual about the item, such as a built-in battery, a liquid, a magnet or a brand name. These change whether and how it can ship.

What are the common mistakes?

  • Reading "Min. order: 1 set" as one piece. A set can be many units. Ask.
  • Paying a supplier directly to save a little. You lose Trade Assurance completely.
  • Comparing the listing price with a delivered price. The listing excludes shipping, customs and any fee.
  • Ordering branded goods at replica prices. Pakistan Customs seizes counterfeits, and restricted goods such as drones and medicines need approvals first.
  • Forgetting voltage and plugs. Pakistan runs on 220 to 240 volts. Some Alibaba items are built for 110 volt markets.
  • Asking a single-item service for 30 units. That is a sourcing agent's job.

Frequently asked questions

Is there someone in Pakistan who can buy from Alibaba for me?

Yes. Sourcing agents, shopping agents and single-item import services all buy from Alibaba.com for Pakistani customers. You send the listing link and pay them in rupees, and they pay the supplier through Alibaba.

Can I pay for an Alibaba order with JazzCash or Easypaisa?

Not on Alibaba itself. Alibaba takes cards, wallets and bank transfers in foreign currency. A Pakistani buying service can accept JazzCash, Easypaisa, Raast or bank transfer and then pay the supplier.

Do I still get Trade Assurance if a service buys from Alibaba for me?

Trade Assurance belongs to the account that placed the order, so it protects the service. Your protection is the service's own refund terms, so read them before you pay.

Is it cheaper to use a buying service than to order from Alibaba directly?

For one piece it often is, because a service can ship by consolidated air freight instead of express courier. Compare the final delivered price, including customs, not the listing price.

Can an Alibaba buying service get me one piece from a supplier with a high MOQ?

Sometimes a supplier agrees to sell one sample, sometimes not. Factory listings for made-to-order goods usually have real minimums that no service can change.

How long does an Alibaba order take to reach Pakistan through a service?

With consolidated air freight, commonly 7 to 15 days after the supplier dispatches. Add the supplier's own preparation time, which varies by listing.
