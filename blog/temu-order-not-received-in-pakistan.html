Temu Order Not Received in Pakistan: What to Do

By Muhammad Shamshad AslamUpdated October 26, 20269 min read
Quick answer

If your Temu order has not arrived in Pakistan, or tracking says delivered but you have nothing, contact Temu customer service promptly and within 90 days of the order date. First check every package in the order, the local carrier's tracking and your household. Then report it in the support center as missing items or delivered but not received.

What should you do first when a Temu order has not arrived?

Work out which problem you have before contacting anyone, because Temu handles a late parcel, a parcel marked delivered and a parcel with missing items in different ways. Open "Your Orders", tap the order and look at each package separately. Then follow this order:

  1. Check the delivery date shown under each package. If it has not passed, the parcel is probably still on its way.
  2. Read the last tracking scan. Note any local carrier name and second tracking number.
  3. If it says delivered, ask everyone at the address, the guard and close neighbours.
  4. Call the local carrier with the tracking number.
  5. Report it to Temu in the support center, with the right option for your case.

Temu's Pakistan shipping page asks you to contact customer service immediately, and within 90 days of the order date, if a package has not been delivered or shows delivered when you have not received it. If you are not sure Temu delivers to your area at all, start with does Temu deliver to Pakistan.

Is your Temu order actually late?

It is only late once the date shown for that package has passed. Temu's Pakistan page lists 1 to 3 days of processing, then 5 to 13 business days of standard shipping, which works out to roughly 8 to 22 calendar days from payment. Around 11.11 or Eid, the slow end is common.

A tracking page that has not moved for a few days is not, on its own, a lost parcel. Temu says orders may be handed to local carriers for the final leg, so tracking may not be available every step of the way. Our guide to Temu delivery time in Pakistan has a worked calendar example and explains each gap.

What if Temu tracking says delivered but you have nothing?

Search locally for a day, then report it in Temu's support center as "package shows delivered but not received". Temu's return policy describes exactly this route: find the order in the support center, select "package shows delivered but not received" and apply for a refund, or contact customer service or the seller.

Before you report it, check the places a Pakistani parcel usually ends up:

  • A family member, servant, tenant or office colleague who signed for it.
  • The security guard, gate office or reception of your building or society.
  • A neighbour with a similar house number or on a similarly named street.
  • The local carrier's office, if the rider marked it delivered after a failed call.

When you report it, give the order number, the package's tracking number, the time the tracking says it was delivered and a short note of who you checked with. Stick to facts. Temu's policy says it reviews refund requests and treats falsified evidence as abuse.

What if the parcel arrived but items are missing?

First check whether the missing item is in a separate package, then report it as a missing item. Orders with several items often ship in more than one package, each with its own tracking number and delivery date, so an item missing from the first parcel may simply be in the second.

If every package has arrived and something is still missing, Temu's policy says to find the order in the support center and select "missing item/parts" to apply for a refund, or contact customer service or the seller. It helps to:

  • Keep the outer packaging and the shipping label until the case is closed.
  • Take a clear photo of the label and of what was inside, in case customer service asks for it.
  • Note whether the parcel looked opened or resealed when it arrived.

A wrong or damaged item is a return, not a missing item. Our guide on how to return a Temu order in Pakistan covers that route.

How do you check with the local carrier?

Use the local tracking number if the Temu tracking shows one, and call or message the carrier directly. Once a parcel reaches Pakistan, the local carrier knows more than Temu's tracking page does. Ask it three things:

  • Is the parcel at a local office, out for delivery, or marked delivered?
  • If delivered, at what time and to whom, and was a signature or phone confirmation taken?
  • If not delivered, why, and can it be redelivered or collected?

Write down the name of the person you spoke to and what they said. If the carrier confirms it never delivered, pass that to Temu customer service. If the tracking shows no local carrier at all and the date has passed, go straight to Temu.

What if you entered the wrong address?

Contact Temu customer service straight away, and call the local carrier as soon as one appears in tracking. Temu's shipping page asks you to give a correct and current address. Once a parcel is moving, the rider's call is often your best chance to fix a wrong house number or street, which is why a phone number you answer matters.

Some addresses fail by design. Temu says items cannot be delivered to security-restricted areas such as military bases. It also says large items, valuables, perishables, age-restricted products and items sent by private carriers may need a physical address or signature, so a P.O. box can fail for those. If you live inside a cantonment or a guarded colony, use an address outside it on your next order.

Can customs hold a Temu parcel in Pakistan?

Yes, though for most small Temu parcels it is rare. Post and courier parcels worth Rs 1,000 or less pay no duties or taxes under section 19C of the Customs Act. Larger parcels can be assessed for duty.

Temu's Pakistan terms say that, where applicable, you act as the importer, that duties collected at checkout are estimates, and that if additional amounts are assessed, you are responsible for them. They also say Temu is not liable if a product is delayed or denied customs clearance because you did not pay such amounts. So a parcel held for unpaid duty is your problem to sort out, through the courier handling it. Ask for the customs assessment document before paying. Our guide on a parcel stuck at customs in Pakistan explains what that document should show.

Is a message asking for a Temu customs or release fee real?

Treat it as a scam unless the courier physically holding your parcel can show a customs assessment. Temu's home page carries a security reminder that Temu won't ask for extra fees via SMS or email. Scam messages commonly arrive just when a parcel is overdue, which is why they work.

  • Do not click links in SMS or WhatsApp messages about held parcels.
  • Do not send money to a personal JazzCash, Easypaisa or bank account.
  • Never share a one-time code from your bank or wallet.
  • Check the order status yourself in the Temu app.
  • Report the message through the "Report suspicious activity" page in Temu's Pakistan footer.

What is the 90-day window, and what does each case need?

What happens after you report a missing Temu order?

Temu reviews the case and, if it agrees, refunds you or helps with the seller. Refunds go to your original payment method, which can take 5 to 14 business days and up to 30 days depending on your bank or wallet, or to Temu credit, which is faster but typically cannot be cashed out. Check the refund status on the order details page. If the parcel turns up after you were refunded, contact customer service rather than keeping both.

Frequently asked questions

What do I do if my Temu order says delivered but I did not get it?

Check with your household, guard and neighbours, then report it in Temu's support center as package shows delivered but not received, within 90 days of the order date.

How long do I have to report a missing Temu parcel?

Temu's Pakistan shipping page asks you to contact customer service immediately and within 90 days of the order date.

Why did only part of my Temu order arrive?

Orders with several items often ship as separate packages, each with its own tracking number. If every package has arrived and something is still missing, report it as a missing item.

Can I get a refund if my Temu parcel is lost?

Yes, if Temu agrees after reviewing the case. The refund goes to your original payment method or to Temu credit.

It can, mainly for parcels worth more than Rs 1,000 where duty is due. Temu's terms make you the importer, so you pay any extra duty customs assesses.
