Is AliExpress Available in Pakistan? (2026 Status)

By Muhammad Shamshad AslamUpdated October 23, 20266 min read
Quick answer

Yes. AliExpress is available in Pakistan in October 2026: the website and app accept Pakistani addresses, show prices in rupees when your region is set to Pakistan, and most listings ship here. What is limited is payment, because JazzCash and Easypaisa are not accepted at checkout, plus some listings that exclude Pakistan and the cheaper shipping routes AliExpress cut in 2025.

Is AliExpress available in Pakistan right now?

Yes. When we opened aliexpress.com in October 2026, the site set the region to Pakistan with English and PKR in the header, showed every price in rupees and listed shipping to Pakistan on the product pages we opened. There is no separate Pakistan website. You use the global site or app with Pakistan selected as your country.

What has changed is not access but cost and choice. Budget shipping was cut in 2025, the duty-free limit for parcels fell to Rs 1,000, and paying still needs the right kind of card.

How do you set AliExpress to Pakistan?

Use the country and currency selector in the site header. On the desktop site in October 2026 it sat next to the app download link and showed a flag with the language and currency, EN and PKR in our case. Choose Pakistan as the ship-to country and PKR as the currency, then save. If you use the app, check the same country setting there before ordering.

Set this before you search. Shipping options, delivery estimates and whether a listing ships to you at all depend on the country selected, so a listing that looks fine on another country setting may not ship to Pakistan.

Does AliExpress show prices in Pakistani rupees?

Yes. With the region set to Pakistan, the homepage and listings showed prices like Rs 533 or Rs 11,455, and shipping was shown in rupees too. Product pages also carried the note "Tax excluded, add at checkout if applicable", so the listed price is not always the final amount.

Your bank may still treat the payment as a foreign transaction and add its own fee, so the amount on your statement can differ slightly from the checkout total.

Is the AliExpress app available in Pakistan?

Yes, on both Android and iPhone. The Google Play listing, opened with Pakistan as the country, showed the AliExpress Shopping App by Alibaba Mobile with 500 million plus downloads. Apple's Pakistan App Store listed AliExpress Shopping App as free, from Alibaba.com Singapore E-Commerce Private Limited.

Download it only from those stores. Apps or APK files shared on social media pages calling themselves AliExpress Pakistan are not the official app.

Can you pay for AliExpress from Pakistan?

Yes, with a Visa or Mastercard that allows international online payments. WorldFirst's July 2026 AliExpress guide for Pakistan calls a Visa or Mastercard the most reliable checkout option and says JazzCash and Easypaisa are not directly supported at checkout. It also notes cash on delivery is rarely offered on cross-border orders.

If your card is declined, check your banking app for a setting that turns on international or e-commerce use, and confirm the card is Visa or Mastercard rather than a local-only PayPak card. Our guide on ordering from China without a credit card covers the alternatives.

What is not available on AliExpress for Pakistan?

A few things, and you usually find out at checkout:

  • Some listings. Not every listing offers shipping to Pakistan, and you may only see this when you enter your address. Our guide to AliExpress items not shipping to Pakistan explains why and what to try.
  • The cheapest shipping options. Bol News reported that AliExpress took low-cost routes to Pakistan, including AliExpress Standard Shipping and Cainiao, offline from 7 July 2025, citing unclear tax policies. Standard shipping was back on listings we checked in October 2026, but not the old economy options.
  • Local wallets. JazzCash, Easypaisa and Raast cannot be used directly to pay.
  • Easy returns. Sending an item back to China costs more than most cheap items are worth, even when the policy allows it.

Do you pay customs on AliExpress orders in Pakistan?

Only above Rs 1,000. Parcels by post or courier worth Rs 1,000 or less are duty-free under section 19C of the Customs Act. Above that, the whole parcel is assessed for customs duty, sales tax and income tax, and Pakistan Post or the courier collects it before delivery. Our AliExpress customs duty guide has worked examples.
