Importing Sewing and Embroidery Machine Parts From China to Pakistan

By Muhammad Shamshad AslamUpdated November 12, 20269 min read
Quick answer

Yes, sewing and embroidery machine parts are among the most practical things to import from China to Pakistan as single items. Hooks, bobbin cases, presser feet, servo motors, control boards and embroidery machine screens are small and often valuable. Order by the exact machine model and part number from the maker's parts list, and match the needle system and the voltage.

New to importing? Read our step-by-step guide on how to import from China to Pakistan.

Which sewing and embroidery machine parts do people import from China?

The most common orders are parts that wear out or fail and are hard to find locally in the exact version: rotary hooks and bobbin cases, feed dogs, needle plates, presser feet, servo motors, control boxes, embroidery machine mainboards, display panels and trimmer parts. Many industrial sewing machines and their parts are made in China, and many Chinese embroidery machines are sold in Pakistan, so the parts trail usually leads back to Chinese suppliers.

Small parts with a clear part number make the best single imports. A whole new industrial machine is heavy and usually better bought from a local dealer. For the basics of buying from China as an individual, see our guide on how to import from China to Pakistan.

How do you find the exact part number?

Use the maker's parts list for your exact model. For JUKI industrial machines, JUKI runs a WebPartsList on its industrial site with model search and parts search, grouped by apparel and non-apparel machines. JUKI also publishes parts list PDFs for popular models such as the DDL-8700 series. Household machine makers print part numbers in their manuals or parts diagrams.

  1. Copy the model from the nameplate. Include every suffix. A DDL-8700 and a DDL-8700-7 are different machines with different parts lists.
  2. Find the part in the parts list. Note the number exactly as printed.
  3. Search that number on Taobao, 1688, AliExpress or Alibaba. Many Chinese sellers list the original number in the title.
  4. For Chinese embroidery machines, read the controller label. Many use a Dahao BECS series control system, and the model printed on the controller matters more than the machine's brand name.
  5. Ask the seller to confirm fit in writing for your machine model and part number.

Which needle system does your machine use?

Household machines almost all use the 130/705 H system, while industrial machines use whatever system the maker specifies. Schmetz says 130/705 H is used by 99 percent of home sewing machines, and that there are hundreds of different needle systems for industrial machines. Its FAQ also warns that some systems that look close are not interchangeable, such as DCx1 and BLx1, because their shank diameters and lengths differ.

Needle size is the blade diameter in millimetres times 100, so a 0.80 mm needle is size 80. Schmetz lists the matching Singer sizes:

7010Fine fabrics
8012Light to medium fabrics
9014Medium fabrics
10016Heavier fabrics, denim

Schmetz also notes that very large sizes such as 150/22 and 160/23 are not made for home machines because they would damage them. Needles are cheap, though, so they make sense only as part of a larger order, not on their own.

Will a Chinese servo motor or control box work on Pakistani power?

Usually yes, if it is a single phase 220 V model. Pakistan's mains supply is 230 V at 50 Hz, with type C and type D sockets common. Servo motors for lockstitch machines are commonly listed in 220 V single phase versions, which suit Pakistani homes and small workshops. Some industrial motors and machines are three phase, which needs a three phase supply.

Before you order, check:

  • Voltage and phase. The listing should say 220 V single phase if you are running it from a normal socket.
  • Power rating. Compare it with your old motor's label.
  • Mounting. Bolt pattern under the table and pulley size.
  • Plug. Chinese plugs may need a simple adapter. Our guide to voltage and plugs for appliances from China explains plug types in more detail.

A direct drive machine with an integrated motor and control box needs the exact matching control box. A generic one rarely fits.

What about embroidery machine boards, screens and software?

Match the controller model and the board version, not just the machine brand. Many embroidery machines sold under different brand names use the same family of Chinese control systems, but boards differ between controller models and software versions. A board that looks the same can carry different firmware.

  • Copy the model and version printed on the controller and on the board itself.
  • Note the head count and needle count of your machine.
  • Ask the seller whether the board ships with firmware for your machine or needs setting up.
  • Keep your design files backed up before any board is changed.

Screens and keypads usually match by controller model. Main boards are where people most often get the wrong version, so take your time here.

Genuine, compatible or clone parts: what is the difference?

Genuine parts come from the machine maker, compatible parts are made by another factory to fit, and clones copy the brand's markings. Compatible hooks, feet and bobbin cases for popular industrial machines are made in large numbers in China and many work well. A part with a copied logo may be counterfeit, and counterfeit goods can be seized at customs. "Fits JUKI DDL-8700" is fine. A JUKI logo on an unbranded part is not.

For precise parts such as rotary hooks, quality matters a lot, because a poor hook can cause skipped stitches and thread breaks. Read reviews, ask about the material, and pay a little more for a known parts brand when the part is critical.

What does a sewing machine part cost to ship and clear?

Shipping a parcel from China usually costs around Rs 5,000 to 20,000 depending on size and type, so small parts make sense when they are worth around Rs 10,000 or more, or when several different parts travel together. A servo motor or a mainboard is a good fit. A single presser foot on its own is not.

For customs, post and courier parcels worth Rs 1,000 or less pay no duties or taxes under section 19C of the Customs Act. Above that, customs duty is charged by tariff code in FY 2026-27 slabs of 0, 5, 10, 15 and 20 percent, with some goods higher, and sales tax of 18 percent applies on the value plus duties, along with withholding income tax.

What should you check when a sewing machine part arrives?

Compare the new part with the old one before you fit it. Put them side by side and check the part number, the shape, the screw holes and any markings. For a hook or bobbin case, check that the bobbin type matches the one your machine uses. For a motor or control box, read the label for voltage and phase before you plug it in.

  • Fit the part and turn the machine slowly by hand first, so the needle and hook timing can be seen.
  • Run it at low speed on scrap fabric before full speed.
  • For an embroidery board, keep the old board until the new one runs your designs properly.
  • Keep the packaging and the seller's confirmation of fit until you are sure the part works.

If something is wrong, contact the seller or us straight away with the details. A part that arrives wrong or damaged is easier to sort out quickly than weeks later.

What are the common mistakes with sewing machine parts?

  • Ordering by machine brand without the full model and suffix.
  • Assuming household and industrial needles are interchangeable.
  • Buying a three phase motor for a single phase supply.
  • Ordering an embroidery board without matching the controller model and version.
  • Shipping a cheap part on its own, where shipping costs more than the part.
  • Buying parts with copied brand logos.

Frequently asked questions

Can I import one sewing machine part from China to Pakistan?

Yes. Hooks, bobbin cases, servo motors, control boxes and embroidery boards are practical single imports, especially when worth around Rs 10,000 or more.

Where do I find JUKI part numbers?

JUKI runs a WebPartsList on its industrial site with model search and parts search, and it publishes parts list PDFs for popular models such as the DDL-8700 series.

Do household and industrial machines use the same needles?

No. Home machines almost all use the 130/705 H system, while industrial machines use the system their maker specifies, and similar looking systems are not always interchangeable.

Will a Chinese servo motor work in Pakistan?

A 220 V single phase servo motor generally suits Pakistan's 230 V, 50 Hz supply. Check the mounting, pulley and plug, and avoid three phase models unless you have three phase power.

How do I order the right embroidery machine board?

Copy the controller model and board version printed on your current parts, note the head and needle count, and ask the seller to confirm firmware for your machine.

Can you import parts in quantity for a garment factory?

No. We only handle single items or a few different single items. For quantities, use a cargo company or the commercial import route.
