Sea Freight From China to Pakistan: LCL vs FCL

By Muhammad Shamshad AslamUpdated November 12, 20269 min read
Quick answer

LCL means your goods share a container with other shippers and you pay per cubic metre or tonne; FCL means you rent a whole 20 or 40 foot container. For China to Pakistan, LCL usually suits shipments up to around 15 cubic metres and FCL suits larger loads. Sea freight from Shanghai to Karachi takes roughly 18 to 20 days port to port.

What is the difference between LCL and FCL?

LCL (less than container load) means your cartons share a container with other shippers' goods, while FCL (full container load) means one shipper books a whole container. With LCL, a forwarder collects goods from several customers at a warehouse in China, packs them into one container, ships it, and separates them again at a warehouse in Pakistan. With FCL, the container is loaded at the supplier or a depot, sealed, shipped and delivered to the importer still sealed.

The practical differences follow from that:

  • Cost. LCL is priced by the space you use. FCL is priced per container, whether it is full or not.
  • Time. LCL adds days at both ends for consolidation and separation. FCL moves as soon as it is loaded.
  • Handling. LCL cartons are handled more times, so there is more chance of damage. FCL stays sealed.
  • Customs. An LCL container holding goods for many importers can be slowed if Customs examines anyone's goods inside it.

Sea freight is a commercial route. For a single item it is slow and poor value, which we explain at the end.

How is LCL sea freight priced?

LCL is priced per revenue ton, where one revenue ton is 1 cubic metre or 1,000 kg, whichever is higher. This is called the weight or measure rule. Light, bulky goods are charged on volume. Dense, heavy goods are charged on weight.

A worked example: 0.6 cubic metres of goods weighing 900 kg is 0.6 by volume and 0.9 by weight, so the charge is for 0.9 revenue tons. Forwarders often apply a minimum, such as one revenue ton, so very small shipments pay for at least that much.

To find your volume, multiply each carton's length, width and height in metres and add them up. Ten cartons of 0.5 x 0.4 x 0.4 m come to 0.8 cubic metres. Ask the supplier for the packing list with carton sizes and gross weights before you request quotes, because the freight rate is meaningless without the volume.

What sizes do FCL containers come in?

The common dry containers are the 20 foot, the 40 foot and the 40 foot high cube. Volume and maximum payload are the two limits, and light goods fill the space long before they reach the weight limit. Mainfreight publishes these typical figures:

ContainerInternal size (L x W x H)Cubic capacityMax payload
20 ft general purpose5.89 x 2.35 x 2.36 m33 cubic metres21,700 kg
40 ft general purpose12.05 x 2.35 x 2.36 m66 cubic metres26,500 kg
40 ft high cube12.05 x 2.35 x 2.69 m76 cubic metres26,500 kg

You never use the full capacity in practice. Cartons do not fit perfectly, and pallets and loading gaps take space. Exact figures vary slightly between container owners.

When does FCL make more sense than LCL?

FCL usually becomes better value once a shipment is large enough that paying per cubic metre costs more than a whole container. iContainers, an online freight forwarder, describes LCL as ideal for volumes under 15 cubic metres and says that below about 15 cubic metres LCL usually costs less than a whole 20 foot container. Above that, compare quotes for both.

Price is not the only reason to choose FCL. It also suits goods that are fragile, valuable, heavy or awkward, because the container stays sealed from loading to delivery and your goods are not mixed with anyone else's. LCL suits smaller loads where saving on freight matters more than a few extra days.

How long does sea freight from China to Pakistan take?

Sea freight from Shanghai to Karachi takes roughly 18 to 20 days port to port, according to iContainers' estimates, with several sailings a week on that lane. Door to door takes longer, because you add:

  • Trucking from the factory to the Chinese port or LCL warehouse.
  • Waiting for the next sailing and, for LCL, for the container to fill.
  • Possible transhipment at a hub port, which adds days.
  • Unloading, customs clearance and, for LCL, separation at a warehouse in Pakistan.
  • Trucking to your city if it is not Karachi.

So plan for several weeks from the supplier's door to yours, more around Chinese New Year and other holidays when factories and ports slow down.

Which Pakistani ports receive sea freight from China?

Sea freight from China arrives at Karachi Port or Port Qasim, both in Karachi. Goods for Lahore, Islamabad, Faisalabad and other inland cities are either cleared in Karachi and trucked onward, or moved under customs control to a dry port inland and cleared there.

Main Chinese departure ports for Pakistan include Shanghai, Ningbo, Shenzhen and Guangzhou's Nansha. Which one you use depends on where the supplier is. A supplier in Zhejiang often ships from Ningbo or Shanghai, and one in Guangdong from Shenzhen or Nansha. Our guides on shipping from Guangzhou and from Yiwu and Shenzhen cover the city side.

What charges come on top of the ocean freight?

The ocean freight rate is only part of the cost. Ask for a quote that lists every charge, because these are commonly added:

  • Origin charges. Trucking in China, export clearance, terminal handling and, for LCL, warehouse fees.
  • Destination charges. Terminal handling in Karachi, delivery order fees and, for LCL, warehouse separation charges.
  • Customs duty and taxes. Assessed on the customs value under Pakistan's tariff.
  • Clearing agent's fee. For filing the goods declaration and handling the clearance.
  • Storage and container charges. If goods are not cleared and collected within the free days, the port, terminal or shipping line starts charging per day. iContainers puts free time at around 5 to 7 days on average.

Delays in paperwork are what turn a cheap sea freight rate into an expensive one, because daily charges keep running until the container is cleared.

What do you need to clear a sea shipment in Pakistan?

To clear sea freight in your own name, you need to be a registered importer with an NTN, usually a sales tax registration, and a Pakistan Single Window account, and you file a goods declaration through a clearing agent. Pakistan Single Window says subscribing needs an NTN, FTN or STRN, CNIC biometric verification at NADRA, a SIM registered in your name and a one-time Rs 500 fee.

The documents are the commercial invoice, the packing list, the bill of lading and any permits the goods need. If the goods qualify, a certificate of origin lets you claim reduced duty under the free trade agreement. Our guide to the China Pakistan Free Trade Agreement explains how that works, and our guide to customs duty from China to Pakistan covers the tax layers. Pakistan Single Window says it now offers traders the WeBOC functions, and some declarations must go through its Single Declaration.

What mistakes push up the cost of LCL and FCL?

Most extra costs in sea freight come from missing information or slow paperwork, not from the ocean rate itself.

  • Quoting without a packing list. A rate per cubic metre means little until you know the real volume and weight.
  • Comparing freight rates only. One quote can look cheap because it leaves out origin or destination charges.
  • Late documents. If the invoice, packing list or bill of lading is not ready when the ship arrives, daily storage and container charges start.
  • Wrong product descriptions. A vague or wrong description can lead to examination, reassessment or worse.
  • Forgetting the certificate of origin. Without it, eligible goods lose any reduced duty under the free trade agreement.

Can one item go by sea from China to Pakistan?

One item can technically travel as a tiny LCL shipment, but it rarely makes sense. The minimum charge, the port and warehouse fees at both ends and the clearing agent's fee are much the same for one carton as for many, and it takes several weeks. For a single item, air is faster and usually cheaper in total.

Frequently asked questions

What does LCL mean in shipping?

LCL means less than container load. Your goods share a container with other shippers' goods and you pay for the space you use, usually per cubic metre or tonne.

What does FCL mean in shipping?

FCL means full container load. You book a whole 20 or 40 foot container and pay per container, whatever is inside it.

How many cubic metres fit in a 20 foot container?

A 20 foot general purpose container holds about 33 cubic metres, and a 40 foot high cube about 76, though you rarely fill the full space.

How long does a ship take from China to Karachi?

Shanghai to Karachi takes roughly 18 to 20 days port to port. Door to door takes longer once you add trucking, consolidation, customs and delivery.

Is LCL cheaper than FCL?

For small shipments, yes. iContainers says LCL usually costs less than a 20 foot container below about 15 cubic metres. Above that, compare both.

Can I clear a sea shipment without an NTN?

Clearing commercial sea freight in your own name needs an NTN and a Pakistan Single Window account. Personal single items normally come by courier or air instead.
