What Is CIF Value? How Pakistan Customs Values Your Parcel

By Muhammad Shamshad AslamUpdated October 23, 20265 min read
Quick answer

CIF value is the value Pakistan Customs taxes an import on: the price paid for the goods plus the cost of freight and insurance to Pakistan. Section 25 of the Customs Act starts from the price actually paid and adds transport and insurance. For some goods, a valuation ruling sets a minimum value instead.

What is CIF value?

CIF stands for Cost, Insurance and Freight. As a customs value, it means the price of the goods plus the cost of getting them to Pakistan, plus insurance. Pakistan Customs works out duty and taxes on this figure, not on the price you see on the listing.

CIF is also the name of an Incoterm, where the seller pays freight and insurance to the destination port. The two meanings are linked but separate. Whatever term you bought on, EXW, FOB, DDP or anything else, customs still builds the value on a CIF basis. Our guide to Incoterms for single-item buyers covers the trade term.

How is CIF value different from FOB and C&F value?

Each one adds a little more of the journey. You will see all three on quotes and in customs documents.

  • FOB value is the price of the goods loaded at the Chinese port or airport, with no international freight.
  • C&F value, cost and freight, adds the freight to Pakistan.
  • CIF value adds insurance on top of that.

The gap between C&F and CIF is usually small, because insurance on one parcel costs little. The gap between FOB and CIF can be large for one item, because freight for a single parcel is often a big share of the item's price. That is why an FOB or EXW price on an Alibaba listing understates what customs will tax.

How does section 25 of the Customs Act set the value?

Section 25 of the Customs Act, 1969 sets the rules. Under section 25(1), the customs value is the transaction value, meaning "the price actually paid or payable" for the goods when sold for export to Pakistan. FBR calls this the first and most important method of valuation.

Section 25(2) then adds costs that are not already in the price, including:

  • Transport to the port, airport or place of importation in Pakistan.
  • Loading, unloading and handling linked to that transport.
  • Insurance.
  • Other items such as packing and some commissions and royalties, which rarely apply to one personal item.

Delivery inside Pakistan after arrival is not added. So the customs value stops at the border: item plus freight plus insurance.

What does a CIF value look like for one item?

A simple example in rupees, with made-up figures to show the method only.

PartAmount (Rs)
Price paid to the seller30,000
Freight from China to Pakistan8,000
Insurance300
CIF value38,300

Customs duty is charged on the Rs 38,300, not the Rs 30,000. Sales tax and other taxes are then charged on the CIF value plus the duties, which is why the total tax bill grows in steps. The rates depend on the product's PCT code. Our guide to customs duty from China to Pakistan shows the full calculation.

What if customs does not accept the invoice price?

Then it moves to other methods, in order. If the transaction value cannot be used, section 25 sets out fallbacks: the value of identical goods, then similar goods, then a deductive value based on prices in Pakistan, then a computed value from production costs, and finally a flexible fallback method.

For many common goods, a valuation ruling cuts this short. These are issued under section 25A by the Directorate General of Customs Valuation and fix a value per unit or per kilogram, often by country of origin. FBR says section 25A is meant to support section 25, not override it, and that most assessments stay under section 25. A ruling works as a floor. If your genuine invoice is higher, the higher value applies. Our guide on how customs valuation rulings work shows how to find one.

What does CIF value mean for your parcel?

  • Shipping raises the duty base. A more expensive freight route means a slightly higher customs value.
  • A low invoice does not help. Under-declaring is an offence, and a valuation ruling can override it anyway. Parcels with doubtful values are often held. See a parcel stuck at customs.
  • Small parcels have one exemption. Goods by post or courier worth Rs 1,000 or less are not charged duties and taxes under section 19C. Above that, the CIF value is taxed in full.

Frequently asked questions

What does CIF value mean in Pakistan customs?

It is the price paid for the goods plus freight and insurance to Pakistan. Customs duty and taxes are calculated on this value.

Is shipping cost included in the customs value?

Yes. Section 25(2) of the Customs Act adds transport to the port or airport of importation, plus insurance, if they are not already in the price.

Is delivery inside Pakistan part of the CIF value?

No. Freight after the goods arrive in Pakistan is left out, so the value stops at the border.

Can customs use a higher value than my invoice?

Yes. If the invoice cannot be accepted, section 25 allows other methods, and a valuation ruling under section 25A can set a minimum value for some goods.

Do I pay duty on CIF value for a cheap parcel?

Not if it arrives by post or courier and is worth Rs 1,000 or less, under section 19C. Above that, duty and taxes are charged on the full customs value.
