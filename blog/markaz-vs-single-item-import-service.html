Markaz vs a Single-Item Import Service: Which Do You Need?

By Muhammad Shamshad AslamUpdated October 23, 20269 min read
Quick answer

It depends on where the item is listed. Markaz suits you if the product is already in its catalogue and you want cash on delivery, with China orders taking about 10 to 17 days by its own pages. A single-item import service suits you if you have a link from Alibaba, 1688, Taobao or AliExpress and want one fixed rupee price, paid once before shipping.

What is Markaz?

Markaz describes itself as Pakistan's online marketplace, with products from local sellers and from factory-direct suppliers in China. When we checked its pages in October 2026, the site had two main shopping areas: a local shop with Pakistani sellers, and a China section where Markaz lists products from Chinese suppliers and handles the import for you.

Everything in this guide about Markaz comes from its own pages: the homepage, the China section, the About page, its terms and conditions, and two posts on its own blog written by its co-founders. We have not ordered through Markaz, so we describe only what it says about itself. Policies change, so check its site before you order.

Who is Markaz aimed at?

Markaz is aimed at two groups: shoppers in Pakistan, and resellers. Its homepage addresses people shopping for themselves across fashion, beauty, electronics, home and kids categories, and it also has a separate reseller app and tools for people who sell its products on to others. Its About page and terms make clear that resale is a large part of what the platform is built for. Its conditions of sale say products may be bought for personal use or for commercial resale.

That matters for one reason. A platform built around a catalogue that many sellers share works best for popular, ready-to-ship products. If you want something specific that is not in that catalogue, you need a different route.

How does ordering from China on Markaz work?

You choose a product from the Markaz catalogue and order it in rupees, and Markaz does the rest. The China section says Markaz pays the supplier in China, consolidates the shipment, clears Pakistani customs and delivers to your door. Its pages say there is no minimum order, so a single piece is fine.

You find products by browsing the China section or by image search, which lets you search with a picture of the product. When we checked, its pages did not mention ordering from a link you found on another site such as 1688 or Taobao. The China section's FAQ says each product page shows the expected arrival date for that item.

On price, Markaz says the price shown at checkout already covers the supplier payment, international freight, customs duties and last-mile delivery. A post on its blog from June 2026 calls the checkout price the all-in price and says you do not pay separate duties on arrival.

How do payment and delivery work on Markaz?

Markaz's main payment method is cash on delivery, with online payment also offered. Its China section says cash on delivery is available in supported cities, and names Karachi, Lahore, Faisalabad, Islamabad, Rawalpindi and Peshawar. The pages we read did not list which cities are excluded, so check at checkout if you live elsewhere.

For delivery times, Markaz's homepage gives about 3 to 5 days for local orders and about 10 to 17 days for China orders. A June 2026 post on its blog says that 10 to 17 day window runs from placing the order until the parcel is in your hands, across four stages: supplier pickup in China, international freight, customs clearance and last-mile delivery by a local courier.

What does a single-item import service do differently?

A single-item import service starts from your link, not from its own catalogue. You find the exact product on Alibaba, 1688, Taobao, AliExpress or another Chinese site, send the link, and the service buys that listing from that seller, ships it by air, clears it through customs and delivers it.

The price works differently too. Instead of a catalogue price, you get one quote for your specific item that covers the item, shipping, Pakistan customs and the service's fee. You pay that once before the item ships. There is no cash on delivery in this model, because the service has already paid the seller, the freight and the duty for you.

The trade-off is cost for cheap things. Shipping one item from China usually costs Rs 5,000 to 20,000 depending on size and type, so a single-item service makes sense mainly for items worth around Rs 10,000 or more. Which one should you choose?

Choose by where your item is listed and how you want to pay. Both routes deliver China-sourced products to a Pakistani address with customs handled, so the deciding factors are selection, payment and price certainty.

Choose Markaz if:

  • The exact product you want is already in the Markaz catalogue.
  • You want to pay cash on delivery and you live in one of its supported cities.
  • The item is low-priced, where separate international shipping for one piece would cost more than the product.
  • You are happy to pick from what is listed rather than from a specific seller.

Choose a single-item import service if:

  • You have a link to a particular listing on Alibaba, 1688, Taobao or AliExpress, and you want that exact seller and version.
  • The item is specialist: a tool, a spare part, a piece of equipment, an audio product or anything with few sellers.
  • The item is worth around Rs 10,000 or more, so a fixed delivered price matters more than a low headline price.
  • You prefer to know the full cost in writing before anything is bought, and to pay once.

If the item is cheap and already on a site that ships to Pakistan, you may need neither. Our guide on whether it is cheaper to buy from China or Pakistan walks through that check.

What happens if something goes wrong?

Both routes have a claims process, but the details differ. Markaz's China section says that if a product arrives damaged, defective or does not match the listing, you raise a claim through your orders page within 7 days of delivery, and that claims are handled case by case because the stock comes from the original supplier, with Markaz mediating. Its homepage advertises a 7-day return window with buyer protection. Its terms say an order is accepted when it is dispatched, and that sellers can cancel before dispatch.

With a single-item service, the terms should be in a written refund policy you can read before paying. Ours gives a full refund if the item has not arrived within 10 days after the agreed delivery date, or if it arrives wrong, damaged or not working. The full wording is on our refund policy page. Whichever route you use, keep the order page, the listing and any messages until the item has arrived and works.

How do you decide for one item, step by step?

  1. Find the exact product. Note the model, colour, size or plug type you need.
  2. Search the Markaz catalogue. If the same product is listed, note its checkout price and the expected arrival date on the product page.
  3. Find the listing on a Chinese site. If it is not on Markaz, or only in a different version, find it on Alibaba, 1688, Taobao or AliExpress and copy the link.
  4. Get a delivered quote. Send the link to a single-item service and ask for one total in rupees.
  5. Compare like for like. Compare the two delivered totals, the delivery windows and the return terms for the same version of the item.
  6. Check the payment fit. Cash on delivery or a single payment before shipping. Pick the one you are comfortable with.

Common mistakes when choosing between them

  • Comparing different versions. Two listings with the same picture can be different grades, sizes or plug types. Match the specification before comparing prices.
  • Assuming every city gets cash on delivery. Markaz names supported cities. Check yours at checkout.
  • Sending a cheap item through a single-item service. Shipping one piece from China can cost more than a low-priced product. Use a catalogue or local option instead.
  • Ignoring the claim window. Markaz asks for claims within 7 days of delivery. Open the parcel and check it promptly.
  • Expecting stock quantities from a single-item service. Single-item services handle one item per order, not stock.

No. Markaz sells products from its own catalogue of local and China-sourced listings. A single-item import service buys the exact listing you send as a link from a Chinese site and delivers it for one agreed price.

Does Markaz offer cash on delivery for China orders?

Yes, according to its China section, in supported cities including Karachi, Lahore, Faisalabad, Islamabad, Rawalpindi and Peshawar. It also mentions online payment.

How long does Markaz take to deliver from China?

Markaz's own pages say most China orders arrive in about 10 to 17 days from the order, and local orders in about 3 to 5 days. Each product page shows an expected arrival date.

Can I send Markaz a link from 1688 or Taobao?

Its pages describe browsing its catalogue or using image search. When we checked in October 2026, we did not see an option to order from a link on another site.

Who is Markaz aimed at?

Its pages address both shoppers in Pakistan and resellers, with a separate reseller app. Shoppers can buy single items, since it states there is no minimum order.

When is a single-item service the better choice?

When you want a specific listing that is not in a catalogue, the item is worth around Rs 10,000 or more, and you prefer one fixed delivered price paid once before shipping.
