Daraz vs Importing Directly From China: Which Is Cheaper?

By importfromchina.shop teamUpdated October 3, 20266 min read
Quick answer

Daraz is usually the better choice for common, cheap items because delivery takes days and returns are possible. Importing directly from China wins when the item is not on Daraz, when the Daraz price carries a large markup, or when you need a specific model, variant or quality that local sellers do not stock. For a single item, a sourcing service gives you China prices with one rupee total and 7 to 15 day delivery, without the hassle of ordering from China yourself.

Where does Daraz stock come from?

A large share of the electronics, accessories, home goods, beauty tools and fashion sold on Daraz is bought from the same Chinese suppliers on 1688, Taobao and Alibaba that you can reach yourself. Daraz sellers import in small bulk, add their margin, platform commission and return costs, and list the item locally. The markup over the China price is often 50 to 150 percent on gadgets and accessories, less on bulky or heavy goods where shipping dominates.

When is Daraz the better option?

  • Common items that many sellers stock. Competition keeps the markup low.
  • Cheap items under a few thousand rupees. Import shipping and duty would cost more than the saving.
  • You need it this week. Daraz delivers in 2 to 7 days.
  • You want a return option. Daraz has a return policy. Importing from China does not.
  • Heavy or bulky goods. Freight per kilogram makes single-item import expensive.

When is importing from China the better option?

  • The item is not on Daraz at all, or only in the wrong colour, size or version.
  • The Daraz price is far above the China price for a specialist or niche item with few local sellers. Equipment, specialist tools, hobby gear and medical or lab devices are typical.
  • Quality matters. Daraz listings sometimes sell the lowest-grade version of a product. Buying from a chosen seller on 1688 or Taobao lets you pick the grade.
  • It is a specific listing you saw in a video or on a Chinese site, and you want that exact one.

How do I check if importing is worth it?

  1. Find the item on Daraz and note the price.
  2. Find the same item on AliExpress or send the Daraz link to a sourcing service and ask for a delivered price.
  3. Compare the delivered China price with the Daraz price. If the saving is under about 20 percent, Daraz is the easier choice. If it is 30 percent or more, or the item is not on Daraz, import it.

Frequently asked questions

Is Daraz cheaper than importing from China?

For common, cheap items, usually yes, once you add shipping and duty to the China price. For niche, specialist or higher-value items, importing is often 30 percent or more cheaper.

Does Daraz sell Chinese products?

Most of the electronics, accessories, beauty tools and home goods on Daraz are sourced from Chinese suppliers and resold with a markup.

Can I return an item imported from China?

Not in the way you can on Daraz. There is no cheap return route to China. A sourcing service can take up a wrong or faulty item with the seller for you.

How long does Daraz take compared to importing?

Daraz delivers in 2 to 7 days. Importing takes 7 to 15 days through a service or 30 to 60 days through AliExpress post.

Should I import to sell on Daraz?

That is a bulk business and needs commercial import. We only handle single items for personal use, not resale stock.
