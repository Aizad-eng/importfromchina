Shein Delivery Time in Pakistan: How Long It Really Takes

By Muhammad Shamshad AslamUpdated November 11, 20267 min read
Quick answer

A Shein order to Pakistan usually arrives about 9 to 15 days after you order. When we checked Shein's Shipping Info page on 11 November 2026 with Pakistan selected, it listed 1 to 3 days of processing, then Standard Shipping in 7 to 11 business days, and gave an order placed that day an arrival window of 20 to 26 November. Shein warns that processing can slip by 2 to 4 days, and customs or a hard-to-find address can add more.

How long does Shein take to deliver to Pakistan?

Shein usually takes about 9 to 15 calendar days to reach a Pakistani address, counted from the day you order. That is the window Shein's own Shipping Info page showed on 11 November 2026 with the location set to Pakistan: an order placed that day was due to arrive between 20 and 26 November. If you only want to know whether Shein delivers here at all, our guide on whether Shein is available in Pakistan covers that. This guide is only about time.

The page builds that window from two numbers:

  1. Processing, 1 to 3 days. Shein confirms, picks and packs the order at its global warehouses. The page labels this "Ship from Global (1-3 days for processing)".
  2. Standard Shipping, 7 to 11 business days. The parcel travels to Pakistan, clears customs and is delivered to your door.

Standard Shipping was the only method listed for Pakistan when we checked. We did not see an express option.

What does 7 to 11 business days mean in real dates?

Business days normally mean Monday to Friday, so read 7 to 11 business days as roughly one and a half to two and a half calendar weeks after the parcel leaves the warehouse. Shein's own arrival dates are a little tighter than a strict business-day count, so the date on your order is the one to plan around.

Why can a Shein order take longer than the estimate?

Shein lists the main reasons itself, on the same Shipping Info page:

  • Processing delays. The page says Shein is changing its delivery process, so there may be a 2 to 4 day delay in processing orders.
  • Pre-order and customised items. If the order includes special items such as pre-order or customised products, the page says the estimated delivery time will be longer.
  • Flights and weather. The actual date can be affected by flight arrangements, weather and other outside factors.

Three more things slow parcels in Pakistan in practice:

  • Sale seasons. Big sale days such as 11.11 and Black Friday fill outgoing flights, so the slow end of the window is more likely.
  • Holidays. Courier and customs offices run reduced hours around Eid and other public holidays.
  • Address problems. A missing landmark or a phone that is switched off can turn one delivery attempt into three.

Does customs slow down a Shein order in Pakistan?

It can. Under section 19C of the Customs Act, post and courier parcels worth Rs 1,000 or less pay no duties or taxes. Since Shein's free-shipping line alone is about Rs 4,000, most Shein orders are above that, so duty and taxes may apply. How much is decided by customs when the parcel arrives, and it is hard to predict, so treat any figure you see online as a rough guess.

Shein's Terms and Conditions say that, as the importer, you are responsible for complying with the laws of your own country, and that you authorise Shein to make customs declarations in your name. The same section says all consignee names, addresses and payer names must be valid, and that Shein will not compensate you if missing or wrong details stop delivery or customs clearance. Shein's On-Time Delivery terms also exclude delays caused by a buyer not paying taxes or duties on time. So use your real name as on your CNIC, and if a genuine courier tells you duty is due, check the order in the Shein app and settle it quickly. Never pay through a link in an SMS.

What do you get if Shein delivers late?

Possibly 100 Shein Points, worth one US dollar, but only if you ticked the On-Time Delivery box at checkout. Shein's Shipping Info page names Pakistan, with Nigeria, in a link to its On-Time Delivery Service terms. When we read those terms in November 2026, they said:

  • The service applies to all delivery options, including free shipping, but you must tick "On-Time Delivery" at checkout.
  • If the order misses its estimated date, Shein may give 100 points "at SHEIN's sole discretion", issued within 2 business days of the missed date.
  • The points expire after 30 days and can cover up to 70% of a later order's price.
  • Each order qualifies only once, however many packages it ships in.
  • It does not apply to delays from customs rule changes, wrong or incomplete addresses, unpaid duties, or a failed delivery because nobody was available.

It costs nothing to tick, so tick it. Just do not expect more than a dollar back.

When should you contact Shein about a late order?

Contact Shein once the date shown on your order has passed. Shein's Shipping Info page sets two deadlines:

  • If the package has not arrived within the estimated time, contact customer service within 90 days of the payment date.
  • If tracking says delivered but you have nothing, or items are missing or wrong, contact them within 60 days of the delivery date shown in tracking.

Before you write, have the order number, the tracking number and the last tracking update ready. If the parcel is already with a courier in Pakistan, call the courier first, because that is often faster. Take photos of the parcel and label as soon as it arrives in case something is missing.

One more date to know: Shein's page asks you to tap "Confirm Delivery" within 6 months of shipment, after which the button turns grey and you can no longer use it to earn points.

How can you make sure your Shein parcel finds you the first time?

Most of the delay you can control is at the last step. A few habits help:

  • Write house number, street, block or sector, area and city, plus a landmark the rider can ask about.
  • Use a mobile number you answer, and keep it on during the delivery window. Riders here usually call before coming.
  • If Shein will not accept your address at checkout, its page says delivery is not supported in that area. Use an address in the nearest larger city, such as a relative's home or your workplace.
  • Tell the guard or family at home that a parcel is coming, so it is not refused.

Frequently asked questions

How many days does Shein take to deliver in Pakistan?

Shein's Shipping Info page lists 1 to 3 days of processing and then 7 to 11 business days of Standard Shipping. For an order placed on 11 November 2026 it showed arrival between 20 and 26 November, so about 9 to 15 days.

Does Shein have express shipping to Pakistan?

Not when we checked on 11 November 2026. The only method listed for Pakistan was Standard Shipping, at US$1.08 below US$14.43 and free above it.

Is Shein shipping free to Pakistan?

On orders above US$14.43, about Rs 4,000 at Shein's listed rate, when we checked in November 2026. Below that, Standard Shipping cost US$1.08.

Does Shein offer cash on delivery in Pakistan?

No. Shein's shipping data for Pakistan showed cash on delivery as not supported for Standard Shipping in November 2026. Pay with JazzCash, Easypaisa or a card.

What happens if my Shein order to Pakistan is late?

If you ticked On-Time Delivery at checkout, Shein may give 100 Shein Points, worth US$1, valid for 30 days. If the parcel still has not come, contact Shein within 90 days of the payment date.

Can customs delay my Shein parcel in Pakistan?

Yes. Parcels worth over Rs 1,000 can attract duty and taxes, and an unpaid amount holds the parcel. Customs decides the amount on arrival, so it cannot be known exactly in advance.
