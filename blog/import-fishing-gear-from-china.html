Importing Fishing Gear From China to Pakistan

By Muhammad Shamshad AslamUpdated November 12, 20269 min read
Quick answer

Yes, you can import fishing gear from China to Pakistan, and reels, braided line, lures, terminal tackle and travel rods are the most practical single items. Long one-piece rods are awkward and expensive to fly, so choose multi-piece or telescopic rods. Match the reel size and line rating to your rod, and get any angling licence your province requires.

New to importing? Read our step-by-step guide on how to import from China to Pakistan.

What fishing gear do Pakistani anglers buy from China?

Anglers mostly buy spinning and baitcasting reels, braided and fluorocarbon line, lures, hooks and terminal tackle, multi-piece and telescopic rods, and electronics such as bite alarms and small fish finders. Much of the tackle sold worldwide is made in China, and Taobao, 1688, AliExpress and Alibaba carry everything from unbranded lures to well-known reel brands.

The most sensible single imports are items that are small, specific and not easy to find locally: a particular reel model, a good braid in your chosen strength, or a travel rod that packs short. For the general process of buying from China as an individual, see our guide on how to import from China to Pakistan.

How do you choose a spinning reel size and gear ratio?

Pick the size for your target fish and line, then the gear ratio for how fast you want to retrieve. West Marine's reel guide explains that spinning reel sizes run from about 1000 to 30000, that the numbers are not standard across brands but are consistent within a brand's range, and that a 2500 from one brand is roughly comparable to a 2500 from another. Its advice when torn between two sizes is to go with the larger reel.

The gear ratio is how many times the rotor turns for one turn of the handle. West Marine notes that most spinning reels sit between 4.8:1 and 6.2:1, while baitcasters often reach 8.1:1 or higher. Spool diameter also affects how much line comes back per turn, so compare the retrieve per crank where the listing gives it.

How do you match rod, reel and line?

Match the reel to the rod type, and the line to the rod's rating. A spinning reel goes on a spinning rod, not a baitcasting rod. The table shows what to read on a listing and why it matters.

West Marine also notes that braid holds much more line than mono at the same strength, because it is thinner. If you switch to braid, the same spool takes more line.

Why are long rods a shipping problem?

Couriers charge on size as well as weight, and a long tube has a large volumetric weight. DHL's formula is length times width times height in centimetres, divided by 5,000, and you pay the higher of that or the actual weight. Here is what that means with illustrative tube sizes:

  • A one-piece rod in a 200 x 10 x 10 cm tube works out at 4 kg volumetric, even if the rod weighs a few hundred grams.
  • A two-piece rod in a 110 x 10 x 10 cm tube is about 2.2 kg.
  • A telescopic or four-piece travel rod in a 60 x 10 x 10 cm box is about 1.2 kg.

For a single rod, a multi-piece or telescopic model is usually the sensible choice. Our explainer on volumetric weight shows the calculation in more detail.

Which lines, lures and small tackle are worth ordering?

Line, lures and terminal tackle are cheap per piece, so they make sense added to a larger order rather than on their own. They are light and pack small, so they add very little to shipping.

  • Braided line. Braid is much thinner than mono at the same strength, so a spool holds more of it. Order the strength in pounds or kilograms that matches your rod's line rating, and check the length on the spool.
  • Leader material. Many anglers add a mono or fluorocarbon leader to braid. Order it in the strength you use.
  • Lures. Check the weight in grams against your rod's casting weight range, and the hook size.
  • Hooks, swivels and snaps. Sizes differ between brands, so compare with what you already use.

A reel with a spool of braid and a few lures is a sensible single parcel.

Freshwater or saltwater: what changes?

Saltwater gear needs more protection from corrosion. West Marine recommends looking for a sealed gearbox and drag, shielded stainless or anti-rust bearings, and a corrosion-resistant body such as properly anodised aluminium or graphite. It also advises rinsing every reel with fresh water after saltwater use, even sealed ones.

For sea fishing off Karachi, Gwadar or the Makran coast, read the listing for words like sealed, saltwater rated or the bearing material. A cheap freshwater reel used in the sea can seize up quickly.

Which fishing items are difficult to ship by air?

Batteries, sprays and some liquids are the problem items. Electronics with built-in batteries, such as bite alarms and many small fish finders, usually ship without trouble. Electric reels with large separate battery packs, spare lithium batteries and power banks are restricted. Our guide to lithium battery shipping rules explains the limits.

Fish attractant sprays, scented liquids in bottles and aerosol lubricants can be refused for air shipping. Ask before adding them to an order. Lures, hooks, line, swivels and weights are ordinary goods and ship normally.

Do you need a licence to fish in Pakistan?

In many waters, yes, and the rules are set by each province. Punjab's Department of Aquaculture and Fisheries lists angling licence categories under the Punjab Fisheries Rules 1965, as amended up to 2016. When we checked in November 2026, its page listed separate licences for rod and line fishing, for waters reserved for rod and line, with seasonal, monthly and daily options, and a provincial angling licence.

Punjab also imposed a 10-year ban on commercial fishing in many provincial waters from 1 September 2022 to 31 July 2032, as Dawn reported in May 2022. Rod and line angling was allowed with weekly or monthly permits, between sunrise and sunset, with a daily catch limit. Check the current rules with your provincial fisheries department before you fish, since importing gear does not give you the right to use it anywhere.

What duty and tax applies to fishing gear?

Post and courier parcels worth Rs 1,000 or less pay no duties or taxes under section 19C of the Customs Act. Above that, customs assesses the whole value by tariff code. In FY 2026-27 customs duty is charged in slabs of 0, 5, 10, 15 and 20 percent, with some goods higher, sales tax is 18 percent on the value plus duties, and withholding income tax is added. Shipping from China usually costs around Rs 5,000 to 20,000 depending on size and type, so the best single orders are worth around Rs 10,000 or more, such as a good reel, or a reel plus line and lures together.

What are the common mistakes with fishing gear from China?

  • Ordering a long one-piece rod and paying for a large volumetric weight.
  • Pairing a spinning reel with a baitcasting rod.
  • Loading line heavier than the rod is rated for.
  • Using a freshwater reel for sea fishing.
  • Adding sprays, scents or spare batteries to an air parcel.
  • Assuming a famous brand name on a very cheap listing is genuine. Counterfeit goods can be seized by customs.
  • Fishing without the licence or permit your province requires.

Frequently asked questions

Can I import a fishing reel from China to Pakistan?

Yes. Reels are one of the most practical fishing items to import as a single piece, because they are small and valuable.

What does reel size 2500 or 4000 mean?

It is the spool and body size within a brand's range. Numbers are not standard across brands, but similar sizes from different brands are roughly comparable.

Why is shipping a fishing rod so expensive?

Couriers charge on volumetric weight, and a long tube has a large volume. Multi-piece or telescopic rods pack much shorter and cost far less to ship.

Can fish finders be shipped by air?

Units with built-in batteries usually ship fine. Spare lithium batteries, power banks and large separate battery packs are restricted.

Do I need a fishing licence in Punjab?

Yes, in most waters. Punjab's fisheries department lists angling licences under the Punjab Fisheries Rules, and rod and line permits apply in waters under the 2022 to 2032 ban.

Can you import tackle for my shop?

No. We only handle single items or a few different single items. For shop stock, use a cargo company or the commercial import route.
