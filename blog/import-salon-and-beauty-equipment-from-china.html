Importing Salon and Beauty Equipment From China to Pakistan

By Muhammad Shamshad AslamUpdated November 12, 20269 min read
Quick answer

Yes, salon and beauty equipment can be imported from China to Pakistan one piece at a time, but size and power decide whether it makes sense. Dryers, clippers, steamers, nail lamps and trolleys travel well. Hydraulic and barber chairs, shampoo units and treatment beds are heavy and costly by air. Laser and IPL machines may need DRAP checks before you order.

New to importing? Read our step-by-step guide on how to import from China to Pakistan.

What salon equipment do people import from China?

Salon owners and beauticians usually import tools and smaller machines that are hard to find locally in a specific model: professional hair dryers, clippers and trimmers, hair steamers and processors, facial steamers, UV and LED nail lamps, nail drills, wax heaters, sterilising cabinets, magnifying lamps, trolleys and, less often, chairs and beds. Chinese makers sell all of these on Alibaba, 1688, Taobao and AliExpress, many as single pieces.

Buying one item for your own salon is professional use, which is fine. Equipping a whole new salon with many chairs and stations is a different job, better done through a cargo company by sea. For the basics of buying anything from China as an individual, read our guide on how to import from China to Pakistan.

Which items are worth flying and which are too bulky?

Small electrical tools are worth flying, while chairs, beds and shampoo units usually are not. Air couriers charge the larger of actual weight and volumetric weight. DHL's formula divides length times width times height in centimetres by 5,000. The examples below use illustrative carton sizes to show how quickly bulk adds up.

A hydraulic chair is also heavy in actual weight because of its steel base and pump. Shipping a normal parcel from China usually costs around Rs 5,000 to 20,000 depending on size and type, and big furniture-style items go well past that.

Will Chinese salon equipment work on Pakistani power?

Most will, if you buy the 220 to 240 V version. Pakistan's mains supply is 230 V at 50 Hz, with type C and type D sockets common. China also uses 220 V, so equipment made for the Chinese market usually matches the voltage, though the plug may need a simple adapter.

Check these points on the listing:

  • Voltage. Many listings offer 110 V and 220 V versions. Select 220 V before you copy the link. A 110 V dryer or wax heater on 230 V will be damaged.
  • Power in watts. Professional dryers and steamers draw a lot of power. Make sure your salon's wiring and sockets can carry it, especially if several run at once.
  • Plug type. A US flat-pin plug or a Chinese angled plug needs an adapter or a plug change by an electrician.
  • Battery tools. Cordless clippers have built-in lithium batteries, which usually ship without trouble, but check the charger is rated 100 to 240 V.

Do laser, IPL or other treatment machines need DRAP approval?

They may, and you should check before ordering. The Drug Regulatory Authority of Pakistan regulates medical devices under the Medical Devices Rules, 2017. DRAP's medical devices page links to an "Is it a medical device?" section and publishes lists of enlisted and registered devices, with Class A devices enlisted and Class B, C and D devices registered. Whether a particular aesthetic machine counts as a medical device depends on what it does and what the seller claims it does.

Machines sold for hair removal by laser or IPL, skin treatment with energy, or anything marketed with a medical claim are the ones to check. A device that needs DRAP clearance is not a simple personal parcel, and customs can hold it. Our guide on importing medical and lab equipment and DRAP explains the process in more detail. Simple tools such as dryers, clippers and nail lamps are not in this group.

What should you check on a listing before ordering?

Read the full specification, not just the title. For salon equipment the details that matter are:

  • Exact model and variant, including voltage and plug.
  • Motor details for clippers and dryers, where the listing gives them, so you can compare models.
  • Chair details: pump type, base material, maximum load, and whether it ships assembled or in parts.
  • Lamp details for nail lamps: power and whether it cures both UV and LED gels.
  • Spare parts: blades, filters, bulbs and attachments. Ask whether the seller sells them separately.
  • Manual language: ask for an English manual if the item is complex.
  • Carton size and gross weight, so you know the shipping cost before you decide.

Which salon products cannot be shipped by air?

Liquids, sprays and flammable products are the main problem. Hair sprays and other aerosols, nail polish, acetone removers, some gel products and many chemical treatments can be refused for air shipping. Creams and liquid cosmetics also raise their own issues. Our guide to cosmetics and skincare devices from China covers that side. For equipment, the safe rule is to order the machine and buy consumables locally.

What duty and tax applies to salon equipment?

Normal import duty and taxes apply once the parcel is worth more than Rs 1,000. Under section 19C of the Customs Act, post and courier parcels worth Rs 1,000 or less pay no duties or taxes. Above that, customs duty is charged by tariff code in FY 2026-27 slabs of 0, 5, 10, 15 and 20 percent, with some goods higher. Additional customs duty is 0 on the 0 to 15 percent slabs and 2 percent on the 20 percent slab, regulatory duty applies to listed goods only and was capped at 20 percent from 1 July 2026, and sales tax of 18 percent applies on the value plus duties, with withholding income tax on top.

What should you check when salon equipment arrives?

Read the rating label before you plug anything in. It should show 220 V or a 220 to 240 V range and 50 Hz or 50/60 Hz. Then check the plug and use a proper adapter or have an electrician fit a local plug if needed.

  • Count the attachments, combs, blades, filters and bulbs against the listing.
  • Run dryers and steamers briefly on a low setting first, and watch for unusual smell or noise.
  • For a chair, follow the assembly order in the manual and tighten every base bolt before anyone sits in it.
  • Keep the carton and packing until you are sure everything works.

If the item arrives wrong, damaged or not working, our refund policy explains what happens next. Report problems straight away with clear details.

What are the common mistakes with salon equipment from China?

  • Copying a link with the 110 V variant selected.
  • Flying a chair or bed when the shipping cost is more than the saving.
  • Ordering a laser or IPL machine without checking DRAP first.
  • Adding sprays, polish or removers to an air parcel.
  • Not checking that the salon's wiring can carry a high wattage dryer or steamer.
  • Choosing a tool with no spare blades or filters available.

Frequently asked questions

Can I import one piece of salon equipment from China?

Yes. Dryers, clippers, steamers, nail lamps and trolleys are practical single imports. Chairs and beds are possible but usually cost more to fly than they save.

Will a Chinese hair dryer work in Pakistan?

Yes, if you buy the 220 to 240 V version. Pakistan runs at 230 V and 50 Hz. You may need a plug adapter, and high wattage dryers need suitable wiring.

Does a laser hair removal machine need DRAP approval?

It may. DRAP regulates medical devices under the Medical Devices Rules, 2017, so check whether the machine counts as a medical device before you order.

Is it worth importing a salon chair from China by air?

Usually not. A hydraulic chair is heavy and bulky, so air shipping often costs more than the saving. A cargo company by sea suits chairs and beds better.

Can nail polish and hair spray be shipped by air?

They can be refused because they are flammable or pressurised. Order the equipment from China and buy these consumables locally.

Can you import equipment for a full salon setup?

No. We only handle single items or a few different single items. For a full fit-out, use a cargo company or the commercial import route.
