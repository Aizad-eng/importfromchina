Can I get one item from China delivered in Sialkot?

Yes. A single item for your own use or your own workshop can come from China to a Sialkot address without a company registration or licence in your name. The parcel travels by air, is cleared through Pakistan Customs and a courier delivers it. You do not need to go to the airport or the dry port.

Which airport serves Sialkot, and does it handle international cargo?

Sialkot International Airport, near Sambrial to the west of the city, is the city's airport, and it handles cargo. It is unusual in Pakistan because it was built and is owned privately. Dawn called it the first privately constructed and owned airport in the country, and Wikipedia says the Sialkot business community built it. It is operated by Sialkot International Airport Limited.

Pakistan Civil Aviation Authority figures quoted on its Wikipedia page put cargo handled at 9,873 metric tons for July 2024 to June 2025. Its international routes go to the Gulf, Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, Oman, Qatar and Bahrain. None went to China when we checked in October 2026, so a parcel from China enters Pakistan at another airport and reaches Sialkot by the courier network. That leg is part of the price we quote.

Do I need the Sialkot Dry Port for one item?

No. Wikipedia says Sialkot's dry port was set up in 1985 by the city's chamber of commerce as the country's first dry port, to give the city's traders faster customs services. It serves commercial cargo. A single item for your own use is shipped by air as a parcel, cleared through customs and delivered by courier, so you never deal with the dry port.

How long does delivery to Sialkot take?

Usually 7 to 15 days after the seller dispatches. That is the window we quote anywhere in Pakistan, and we do not promise a separate number for Sialkot. The seller's own handling time comes first and varies by listing. We give you an expected delivery date in writing when you pay.

Plan around Chinese holidays. Chinese New Year falls on 6 February 2027 and many sellers stop dispatching for days or weeks around it.

What tools and parts suit Sialkot workshops as single items?

Precise tools and exact replacement parts are the best fit. Dawn described Sialkot in 2021 as the world's largest centre of surgical instrument manufacturing and the largest producer of hand-sewn footballs, and the city is also known for its sports goods and leather industries. Some examples of single items that fit workshop work:

  • A digital caliper or micrometer of a specific brand and range.
  • A grinding or polishing attachment, matched to the shaft size of your machine.
  • A replacement part for an industrial sewing machine, ordered by its part number.
  • A part for a heat press or a leather skiving machine, matched to the model.
  • A control board, sensor or display for a machine, ordered by the number on the old part.

These are examples of what one item can be, not a claim about what workshops order. For more on finding the right part, see importing machine and appliance replacement parts. Some goods have their own rules, so check what you cannot import from China to Pakistan before ordering anything unusual.

What if my workshop needs the same part in quantity?

Then it is a commercial import, and we are not the right route. We bring one item, or a few different single items together, for personal or professional use. Raw material, stock or the same part by the dozen belongs with a cargo company or a clearing agent through the commercial route. We do not do bulk or resale.

How should I write a Sialkot address the courier can find?

Give the area, the street, the house or unit number, a landmark and a phone that answers. Specific tips for Sialkot:

  • Sialkot Cantonment, to the north of the city, has wide planned streets but can be behind checkpoints. Give a gate or reception point if the courier cannot drive in.
  • In the dense old city, give the mohalla, the nearest mosque or chowk, and be ready to guide the rider by phone.
  • For a workshop, give the unit name, the name of the person receiving it and a number for someone at the gate, so the parcel is not left with someone who does not know who it is for.
  • If you are in Sambrial, Daska or another nearby town, write the town name clearly as well as the district.
  • Keep your mobile on. Couriers usually call before they deliver.
