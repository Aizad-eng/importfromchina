Can You Use Alipay in Pakistan?

By Muhammad Shamshad AslamUpdated November 12, 20269 min read
Quick answer

Not as an everyday wallet. Alipay does not run a consumer service for people living in Pakistan, and you cannot top it up with JazzCash, Easypaisa or a Pakistani bank account. Its international version lets visitors link a Visa or Mastercard to pay merchants in China, and NayaPay users can pay at Alipay+ shops in China by scanning a QR code.

Can you use Alipay in Pakistan?

You can download Alipay in Pakistan, but you cannot use it the way people in China do. There is no way to load it from a Pakistani bank, no Pakistani shops that take it, and no rupee balance. What Alipay offers foreigners is a way to pay merchants in China with a linked international card. That matters to Pakistani buyers in two situations: when visiting China, and when trying to pay a Chinese site such as Taobao that expects Alipay.

People searching this usually want to buy from Taobao, 1688 or Xianyu, which are built around Alipay. If that is you, our pillar guide on how to import from China to Pakistan explains the full route, and the sections below explain where Alipay fits.

What is the difference between Alipay, Alipay+ and Easypaisa?

These three names get mixed up, and they do different things. The table sets out what each one is, as we found it in November 2026.

Can you open an Alipay account with a Pakistani number?

Guides for foreign visitors say Alipay's international version accepts sign-up with a foreign mobile number, but we did not find an official country list that names Pakistan. When Alipay announced the change in July 2023, the Pudong government's English site reported that travellers with international bank cards and non-local phone numbers could use the app to pay. The practical check is the country code list on the sign-up screen.

Signing up is not the hard part. The account is useful only once a card is linked, and even then it is set up to pay merchants, not to hold money or receive it.

Which cards can you link to Alipay from Pakistan?

The July 2023 announcement named Visa, Mastercard, Diners Club International and Discover as the card networks overseas users could link. So a Pakistani Visa or Mastercard with international use switched on is the card to try. A PayPak-only card will not link.

Two points we could not confirm from any official page when we checked in November 2026: whether a virtual card from a Pakistani fintech app links reliably, and what spending limits apply to a Pakistani card inside Alipay. Our guide to SadaPay and NayaPay cards on Chinese sites covers what those apps do confirm.

Remember that a payment through Alipay with a Pakistani card is still an international card payment. The State Bank of Pakistan's USD 30,000 annual limit per person and the advance tax on foreign card payments apply.

Can you pay Taobao with Alipay from Pakistan?

Paying can work, but delivery is the real obstacle. A Wise guide published in January 2024 says Alipay accepts international Visa, Mastercard and JCB cards for Taobao and Tmall purchases of physical goods, with a flat 3% fee on the card portion. The same guide says second-hand goods, pre-sale goods and Tmall International orders do not accept international cards.

Most Taobao sellers ship only to addresses inside China, so after you pay, the parcel still needs a Chinese address and someone to send it on to Pakistan. Our guide on how to buy from Taobao in Pakistan covers the options. 1688 and Xianyu are harder again, because they expect a verified Chinese Alipay account and domestic delivery.

Can you send money to a Chinese seller's Alipay?

Not from Pakistan in any normal way. The 2023 change was about paying merchants with a linked card, not about sending money to other people, and there is no Pakistani bank or wallet route that tops up an Alipay balance. Travel guides consistently report that person to person transfers fail with a foreign card.

A seller who asks you to send a deposit by direct Alipay transfer, outside the shopping app, is also asking you to give up the buyer protection that Taobao's own checkout provides. That request is a common pattern in scams.

How does NayaPay work with Alipay+ in China?

NayaPay is the Pakistani wallet that connects to Alipay+, and it lets you pay in shops in China by QR code. Business Recorder reported in December 2025 that NayaPay rolled out QR payments through Alipay+, the wallet gateway run by Ant International, starting in more than 50 countries.

NayaPay's help page explains how it works. You either tap the scanner icon at the top right of the Home screen and scan the merchant's Alipay+ QR, or open Quick Pay and show your Alipay+ QR for the merchant to scan. China, Hong Kong and Japan are among the places listed. The page describes in-person payments at merchants, restaurants and attractions. It does not list fees or limits, and it does not describe paying online shopping sites such as Taobao.

Does Easypaisa use Alipay?

Easypaisa is linked to Alipay's owner, but it is not Alipay. Profit reported in January 2025, when Easypaisa received approval to operate as Pakistan's first digital retail bank, that Ant Group had invested USD 184.5 million for a 45% stake in Telenor Microfinance Bank, Easypaisa's parent, and that Easypaisa uses Alipay's technology. We found nothing showing that an Easypaisa account can pay Taobao, 1688 or an Alipay merchant in China.

How should you set up Alipay before visiting China from Pakistan?

Set it up at home, before you fly, so that a failed card link does not leave you stuck at a shop counter. A sensible order is:

  1. Switch on international use on a Visa or Mastercard in your banking app, and check the daily online limit.
  2. Install Alipay and sign up with your Pakistani number while you can still receive SMS codes easily.
  3. Link the card and approve the bank's one-time code.
  4. Keep a second option. A second card from a different bank, or NayaPay for Alipay+ merchants, helps if the first card is declined.
  5. Carry some cash within the limits. Dawn reported in November 2022 that the SBP limits foreign currency cash for adult travellers to USD 5,000 per visit and USD 30,000 a year.

Every payment made this way counts towards the SBP's yearly card limit, and your bank's exchange rate applies, so small purchases add up.

What mistakes do people make with Alipay in Pakistan?

  • Expecting to top it up. There is no route from JazzCash, Easypaisa or a Pakistani bank into an Alipay balance.
  • Paying a seller by direct transfer. It skips the shopping app's protection.
  • Using someone else's verified account. Borrowing a friend's Chinese Alipay leaves you with no claim if something goes wrong.
  • Forgetting delivery. Paying Taobao is half the job. Most sellers still ship only inside China.
  • Using a PayPak card. It will not link.

Frequently asked questions

Is Alipay available in Pakistan?

Not as a local wallet. You can install the app, but you cannot fund it from a Pakistani bank or wallet, and Pakistani shops do not take it. It is mainly useful for paying merchants in China with a linked card.

Can I link my Pakistani Visa or Mastercard to Alipay?

The July 2023 change allowed overseas users to link Visa, Mastercard, Diners Club and Discover cards. Switch on international use first. PayPak cards will not link.

Can I use NayaPay in China?

Yes, at Alipay+ merchants. NayaPay's help page says you scan the merchant's Alipay+ QR from the Home screen or show your own QR in Quick Pay.

Is Easypaisa connected to Alipay?

Ant Group, which owns Alipay, holds a stake in Easypaisa's parent bank, and Easypaisa uses Alipay's technology. Easypaisa is still a separate Pakistani service and is not a way to pay Taobao.

Can I pay Taobao with Alipay from Pakistan?

Taobao takes international Visa, Mastercard and JCB cards through Alipay for many orders, but most sellers ship only inside China, so delivery to Pakistan needs a forwarder or a buying service.

Can I send money to a Chinese seller's Alipay from Pakistan?

Not through any normal Pakistani bank or wallet route, and a direct transfer outside the shopping app removes buyer protection. Pay on the platform or through a service.
