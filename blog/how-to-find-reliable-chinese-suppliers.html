How to Find Reliable Chinese Suppliers and Avoid Scams

By importfromchina.shop teamUpdated October 3, 20268 min read
Quick answer

To find a reliable Chinese supplier, verify the business licence, confirm whether they are a factory or trading company, check years on the platform and transaction history, order a sample before bulk, pay through Trade Assurance or a 30/70 split, and get a pre-shipment inspection with photos or video. Never pay 100% upfront to a personal account, and treat prices far below the market as a warning sign.

Where do reliable suppliers come from?

  • Alibaba.com: verified suppliers with trade history, Trade Assurance and reviews. Best for direct orders and custom work.
  • 1688.com: domestic wholesalers and factories at lower prices, accessed through a sourcing agent. Best for stock items.
  • Made-in-China.com and Global Sources: more industrial and B2B suppliers, often with audited profiles.
  • Trade fairs such as the Canton Fair in Guangzhou, if you can travel.
  • Sourcing agents with existing supplier relationships, who have already filtered out the bad ones.

How do I verify a supplier before paying?

Run through this checklist. A supplier that fails two or more items is not worth the risk.

  1. Business licence. Ask for a copy. Check the registered company name matches the name on the invoice and the bank account. A mismatch is the single biggest red flag.
  2. Years on the platform. Prefer suppliers with 3+ years and consistent order volume. New accounts are not automatically bad, but they carry more risk.
  3. Transaction history and reviews. On Alibaba, look at the number of transactions in the last 6 months and read negative reviews specifically.
  4. Factory or trading company. Ask directly. Request factory photos, a video tour, or a video call from the production floor. A trading company is acceptable if the price is right, but you should know which you are dealing with.
  5. Product expertise. Ask technical questions about the product. A genuine manufacturer answers quickly and precisely. A middleman stalls or gives vague answers.
  6. Response quality. Clear, prompt, specific communication in the quote stage predicts how they will behave when there is a problem.

What payment terms are safe?

  • Alibaba Trade Assurance: Pay on the platform. Funds are released after you confirm delivery. Use this for any first order on Alibaba.
  • 30% deposit, 70% before shipment: Standard for larger orders with established suppliers. Release the balance only after inspection photos or a third-party inspection report.
  • Through a sourcing agent: You pay the agent in Pakistan. The agent pays suppliers after goods are received and checked at their warehouse. This moves the risk from you to the agent.

Never pay 100% upfront to an unverified supplier. Never pay to a personal bank account, a Western Union number, or an account whose name differs from the company. These are the three most common ways money is lost.

Should I order a sample first?

Yes, for any order above a few hundred dollars or any product where quality varies. A sample costs the unit price plus express courier, usually a small sum compared with a bad bulk order. Compare the sample against the listing photos and against competitor products in Pakistan. If the supplier refuses to send a sample at any price, move on.

How do I make sure the bulk order matches the sample?

  • Put the agreed specification, materials, colour, packaging and quantity in writing on the proforma invoice.
  • Keep the approved sample as the reference and tell the supplier the bulk order will be compared against it.
  • Request pre-shipment photos and video of the actual goods, packed, with the carton labels visible.
  • For orders above a few thousand dollars, use a third-party inspection at the factory before the balance is paid.
  • Count and inspect on arrival in Pakistan before selling anything.

How does a sourcing agent reduce the risk?

An agent in China receives the goods at their warehouse before anything is shipped to you. They open cartons, count, check quality against the order, and send you photos. If the goods are wrong, they go back to the supplier before you have paid for freight and duty. The agent also pays the supplier, so your money does not go to an unverified Chinese account. For buyers who cannot visit factories, this is the most practical protection available. Read more about what a China sourcing agent does and how Alibaba and 1688 compare.

Frequently asked questions

How do I know if a Chinese supplier is legitimate?

Check the business licence, confirm the company name matches the bank account, look at years and transaction volume on the platform, ask for a factory video, and order a sample. Suppliers that refuse any of these are not worth the risk.

Is it safe to pay a Chinese supplier by bank transfer?

Only for verified suppliers you have ordered from before, and only to the company's registered account. For first orders use Alibaba Trade Assurance or pay through a sourcing agent.

What is a gold supplier on Alibaba?

A paid membership tier, not a quality guarantee. It shows the supplier has invested in the platform and passed basic verification. Still run the full checks.

Can I visit the factory in China?

Yes, with a business visa. Most Pakistani buyers do not, and instead rely on video calls, third-party inspections, or a sourcing agent who visits on their behalf.

What should I do if a supplier sends the wrong goods?

If you paid through Trade Assurance, open a dispute with evidence. If you used an agent, the agent handles the return before shipping. If you paid directly by transfer, recovery is difficult, which is why payment method matters.
