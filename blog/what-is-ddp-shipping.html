What Is DDP Shipping? Meaning for Pakistani Buyers

By Muhammad Shamshad AslamUpdated October 23, 20265 min read
Quick answer

DDP shipping is a delivery term, short for Delivered Duty Paid, where the seller or service delivers the goods to your address with import clearance done and duties and taxes already paid. For a buyer in Pakistan it means the price you agree is the full cost, with nothing to pay the courier on delivery.

What does DDP mean?

DDP stands for Delivered Duty Paid, one of the Incoterms 2020 rules published by the International Chamber of Commerce. Under DDP, the seller delivers when the goods are placed at the buyer's disposal, cleared for import, at the named destination. Until that point the seller carries the cost and the risk.

In practice the seller, or whoever sold you the delivered price, has to:

  • Pay for transport all the way to the named place, usually your address.
  • Handle export clearance in China and import clearance in Pakistan.
  • Pay the import duties and taxes that Pakistan Customs assesses.
  • Carry the loss if the goods are lost or damaged before delivery.

DDP puts more responsibility on the seller than any other Incoterm. Unloading at your end is technically the buyer's job, which for one parcel just means taking it from the courier.

What does DDP leave out?

DDP fixes who pays. It does not fix what the goods are worth, whether they match the listing, or what happens if the seller fails. Incoterms do not deal with breach of contract, so a refund or replacement depends on the seller's own policy or the platform's protection, not on the letters DDP.

It also does not make an item legal to import. Restricted goods, such as medicines that need DRAP approval or phones that need PTA registration, follow their own rules whatever the delivery term.

How is DDP different from DAP, CIF and "free shipping"?

The difference is who pays Pakistan customs. This table covers the terms a single-item buyer is most likely to see.

DDP, Delivered Duty PaidSeller or serviceNothing
DAP, Delivered at PlaceYouDuty, taxes and any clearance charge
CIF, Cost, Insurance and FreightYouClearance, duty, taxes and delivery from the port

"Free shipping" is the one that catches people. It usually means the seller pays the freight, not the duty. On Taobao and 1688 it often means free delivery inside China only. Our guide to Incoterms for single-item buyers compares EXW, FOB, CIF, DAP and DDP line by line.

Does DDP mean no customs duty is charged?

No. Pakistan Customs still assesses duty and taxes on a DDP parcel. The only change is who pays them. Duty is worked out on the customs value, which under section 25 of the Customs Act starts from the price paid and adds the cost of transport and insurance to Pakistan. That is the CIF value.

The one real exemption for small parcels is the de minimis limit. Under section 19C of the Customs Act, as amended by the Finance Act 2025, goods arriving by post or courier worth Rs 1,000 or less are not charged duties and taxes. It was Rs 5,000 before. Above that, duty applies whether the term is DDP or not. More on that in the de minimis limit in Pakistan.

What should you check before trusting a DDP price?

A DDP price is only as good as the duty worked into it. Before you pay, check these points.

  1. Is it written down? The quote or listing should say duties and taxes are included, not only shipping.
  2. Is the address the named place? DDP to Karachi airport is not DDP to your house in Multan.
  3. Is the declared value real? A DDP line that keeps duty low by declaring a fake low value can lead to the parcel being held. Customs can also apply a valuation ruling that sets a minimum value for some goods.
  4. Who handles a hold? If customs stops the parcel, the DDP seller should deal with it, not you.

Why does DDP suit someone buying one item?

Because it is the only term where the quoted price equals the landed price. You do not need a clearing agent or a WeBOC registration, and you do not get a call from a courier asking for duty before release. For one parcel, setting any of that up yourself costs more than it saves.

Frequently asked questions

What does DDP stand for in shipping?

Delivered Duty Paid. It is an Incoterms 2020 rule where the seller delivers to the named place with import clearance done and duties and taxes paid.

Do I pay anything on delivery with DDP?

No duty or taxes. Under DDP those are paid by the seller or service before the parcel reaches you, so nothing should be collected at your door.

Is DDP the same as free shipping?

No. Free shipping usually means the seller pays the freight only. DDP means the seller also pays Pakistan customs duty and taxes and handles clearance.

Is customs duty still charged on a DDP parcel?

Yes, unless the parcel is worth Rs 1,000 or less and arrives by post or courier. DDP changes who pays the duty, not whether it is charged.

What is the difference between DDP and DAP?

Both deliver to a named place. Under DAP you clear customs and pay duty and taxes yourself. Under DDP the seller does both.
