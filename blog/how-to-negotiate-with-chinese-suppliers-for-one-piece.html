How to Negotiate With Chinese Suppliers When You Want One Piece

By Muhammad Shamshad AslamUpdated November 12, 20269 min read
Quick answer

Be direct: say you want one piece for your own use, paste the listing link and model, and ask for the one-piece price, stock and dispatch date. Do not pretend to be a large buyer. On a single piece the room to negotiate is usually in shipping, variant, accessories and timing rather than a big discount, and Alibaba's Ready to Ship prices are fixed.

Can you negotiate with a Chinese supplier for just one piece?

Yes, but the negotiation looks different from the price haggling people imagine. One piece is the smallest, least profitable order a supplier can take, so your goal is first to get a clear yes, then to get the right variant, a fair shipping cost and a firm dispatch date. A small discount is a bonus. If you push one piece like a container order, many suppliers simply stop replying.

What can you realistically negotiate on a single item?

Some terms are worth asking about on one piece and some are not. On Alibaba, check first whether the listing is Ready to Ship. Alibaba's own explanation says Ready to Ship listings show one non-negotiable price upfront, show the shipping fee upfront and must leave the facility within 15 days, while customised products can be negotiated.

How should you open the conversation?

Open with the listing, the exact variant and a clear quantity of one. Say it is for your own use. That is honest, and it tells the supplier this is a quick stock sale, not a long project. Send it from the listing's chat so the product is attached.

English: Hello. I want to buy 1 piece of this item for my own use: [link]. Model [model], colour [colour], 220V with European round-pin plug. Is it in stock, and what is the price for 1 piece?

Simple Chinese: 您好，我想买一件这个产品，自己用：[链接]。型号[型号]，颜色[颜色]，220V，欧规插头。请问有现货吗？一件多少钱？

Pakistan uses 220 to 240 volt power and round-pin plugs, so asking for 220V and a European plug avoids receiving a 110V or flat-pin version.

How do you ask for a better price without sounding rude?

Ask once, politely, and give a reason the supplier can say yes to. Good reasons on one piece are that you are ready to pay today, that you will take the item as it is in stock, or that you found the same model a little cheaper from another seller. A line such as "I can pay today if the price is [amount]" (如果价格是[金额]，我今天就可以付款) works better than repeating "best price?".

Keep the tone friendly. Thank the supplier for checking stock, and use their name if they gave one. Small courtesies go a long way on a low-value order, where the supplier is doing you a favour by breaking a carton. If the supplier says no, accept it and look at shipping instead. A cheaper courier option or delivery to a China address often saves more than a discount on the item. Do not keep asking. On a low-value order, a supplier who feels pushed will often stop answering.

What if the supplier says the minimum order is higher?

Ask one follow-up, then move on if the answer is still no. The follow-up worth sending is whether they have a stock unit, a display unit or a sample they can sell:

English: I understand the minimum order. Do you have 1 piece in stock or a sample I can buy?

Simple Chinese: 我理解起订量。请问有一件现货或者样品可以卖吗？

The same product is often made or sold by several suppliers, so a no from one is not the end. Our guide to what MOQ means explains how to read minimum order tables on Alibaba and 1688.

How do you confirm the deal before paying?

Put everything agreed into the platform order before you pay. On Alibaba, ask the supplier to send the order through Alibaba with the final price, the variant, a quantity of one, the shipping method and the dispatch date written on it. Before you press pay, check the order against this list:

  • The product link and model number match the listing you discussed.
  • Colour, size, voltage and plug type are written out, not left as "default".
  • Quantity is 1, and the unit price is the one you agreed.
  • Any included accessory is named on the order.
  • The shipping method and the address, in China or Pakistan, are correct.
  • The dispatch date is a date, not "soon".

To ask for this in Chinese, send: 请把我们谈好的价格、型号、颜色、插头和发货日期写在订单里 (please write the agreed price, model, colour, plug and dispatch date on the order).

Alibaba's Trade Assurance page says protection applies to orders paid through Alibaba.com, with the payment held in escrow until you confirm delivery. A deal agreed on WeChat and paid by direct transfer has none of that.

Why does one piece cost more than the listed price?

The headline price on many listings is for large quantities, and one piece is charged at the highest tier. Some suppliers charge a sample price above that. Courier shipping for one parcel is also expensive, because nobody else shares the cost. In Pakistan, post and courier parcels worth Rs 1,000 or less are free of duties and taxes under section 19C of the Customs Act. Above that, the full value is dutiable at the rate for that product.

What should you avoid when negotiating for one piece?

  • Pretending to be a large buyer to get a lower price. It is dishonest, and the price for one does not change.
  • Asking for a logo or custom colour on one unit. That turns a stock sale into custom work.
  • Taking a discount for paying off the platform. You lose Trade Assurance.
  • Haggling hard over a small amount. You may lose the seller over the cost of a cup of tea.
  • Agreeing details only by voice note. Get them in writing on the order.

Frequently asked questions

Do Chinese suppliers negotiate on a single piece?

A little, sometimes. One piece already sits at the highest price tier, and Ready to Ship prices on Alibaba are fixed, so shipping, variant and accessories are usually easier to negotiate than the item price.

How do I say I want one piece in Chinese?

Write 我想买一件 (I want to buy one piece) or ask 可以只卖一件吗？ (Can you sell just one piece?). Add the listing link and model so there is no confusion.

Should I tell the supplier the item is for my own use?

Yes. It is honest and it tells them this is a quick stock sale. Pretending to be a large buyer does not lower the one-piece price.

Can I get a free item if I promise a larger order later?

Do not make promises you will not keep. Some suppliers give free samples to genuine large buyers, but for one piece for your own use, expect to pay the item and shipping.

What does 包邮 mean?

包邮 means free shipping, usually only inside China. It does not cover international shipping to Pakistan.

Is it safe to accept a discount for paying directly?

No. Paying outside Alibaba removes Trade Assurance, so a small discount is not worth losing your protection.
