Can You Import From China Without Going There?

By Muhammad Shamshad AslamUpdated November 12, 20267 min read
Quick answer

Yes. Most people who import from China to Pakistan never travel there. You find the product online, talk to the seller through the platform's chat, pay by card, through Alibaba checkout or through a Pakistani service, and a courier or cargo company brings it in. What you lose is seeing the item in person, so remote checks before paying matter more.

New to importing? Read our step-by-step guide on how to import from China to Pakistan.

Can you import from China without going there?

Yes. Buying from China without visiting is the normal way to do it, for one item or a large order. The products are listed online, the sellers answer messages, payment works across borders and customs clearance happens in Pakistan whether you have been to China or not. A visit is a choice, not a requirement.

Every step that people imagine needs a trip has a remote version: finding the product, judging the seller, seeing the actual item, paying and getting it shipped. The rest of this guide goes through each one. For the full process from start to finish, see our main guide on how to import from China to Pakistan.

What does travelling to China actually give you?

A trip gives you three things: you can handle the product, you can meet the seller face to face, and you can see many suppliers in a few days. For someone placing large factory orders, those can be worth a ticket, a visa and a week of hotels.

For one item, or a few items for yourself, a trip rarely makes sense. The travel cost is far higher than any saving on the product, and most of what a visit offers can be done by message and video call. The one thing you cannot do remotely is touch the item. That gap is real, and the checks below are how you close as much of it as possible.

How do you find Chinese products without visiting?

You search the same sites Chinese buyers use. Alibaba.com is in English and built for international buyers. 1688 and Taobao are Chinese domestic sites with lower prices but delivery only within China. AliExpress and Temu sell to Pakistan directly. JD.com and Xianyu, a second-hand site, add more choice.

Image search helps when you only have a photo. Taobao and 1688 both let you upload a picture and find matching listings, which often turns up the same product from several sellers at different prices. Once you find it, copy the exact product link, because the link is what any agent or service needs to buy the right item.

How do you talk to a Chinese seller from Pakistan?

Use the chat built into the platform. Alibaba, AliExpress, Taobao and 1688 all have a message button on the listing, and many sellers reply within hours. Write short, simple sentences and include the model number and variant you mean. On the Chinese sites, a translation app handles both directions well enough for product questions.

A first message that gets a useful reply looks like this: the model number, the exact colour or size, one or two specific questions, and a request for real photos of that variant. For example: "Model X200, black, 220V version. Is it in stock? What is the packed size and weight? Please send real photos of this exact unit." Short, numbered questions get answered. Long paragraphs often get a copy of the listing back.

Keep the conversation on the platform. Alibaba's own seller blog points out that its built-in tools preserve message history, which helps if there is a dispute later. A seller who quickly pushes you to WeChat or another app for a better price or a different payment method is removing that record, and that is where most remote scams start.

How can you check a seller and a product remotely?

You check the seller with records and the product with evidence from the seller. Neither needs a visit.

  • Store history. Years on the platform, number of sales and the rating are visible on the store page. A store a few months old selling expensive goods is a warning.
  • Business licence. A Chinese company's licence can be checked on China's official company registry. Our guide on how to verify a Chinese seller walks through it.
  • Verification badges. On Alibaba, a Verified Supplier has been assessed by independent firms such as SGS, Intertek or TUV Rheinland, according to Alibaba's own page. Alibaba also says it does not guarantee product authenticity, so the badge is about the company, not your unit.
  • Photos and video from the seller. Ask for pictures of the exact variant you will receive, not the catalogue images.
  • A live video call. For anything expensive, ask the seller to show the item on a live call. Alibaba's seller blog suggests asking them to show the serial number next to something dated that day, so you know the footage is live, not recorded.

How do you pay a Chinese seller without a Chinese bank account?

You pay through the platform or through someone in Pakistan, never in cash. The options depend on the site.

  • Alibaba.com. Card or bank transfer at checkout. Alibaba's Trade Assurance page, when we checked in November 2026, said you can claim a refund if your order has not been shipped and expect delivery by the scheduled date or delay compensation. Our guide to Alibaba Trade Assurance explains the limits.
  • AliExpress and Temu. A Pakistani card with international use enabled often works. Banks deduct an advance income tax on foreign card payments under section 236Y.
  • Taobao and 1688. These take Chinese payment methods only, so a Pakistani card or wallet will not work. You pay an agent or service in rupees and they pay the seller.

Who receives the item in China if the seller only ships locally?

An agent, a forwarder or a single-item service receives it at an address in China, then sends it on to Pakistan. This is the step people assume needs a trip, and it does not. Taobao, 1688 and Xianyu sellers ship to a Chinese address like any domestic order. What happens next depends on who holds that address: a forwarder ships what you send them, while a full service buys, receives and ships it as one job.

How does customs clearance work if you are not there?

Clearance happens in Pakistan, so being in China never mattered for it. For a personal parcel, the courier, Pakistan Post or the service files the declaration and pays the duty, and you show your CNIC. For a commercial import in your own name, you or a licensed customs agent file a goods declaration on Pakistan Single Window, which needs an NTN and a PSW subscription with NADRA biometric verification.

What are the risks of buying from China remotely?

The main risks are receiving something different from what you expected, paying someone who does not ship, and a customs surprise. Each has a remote fix:

  • Wrong or poor item. Ask for photos of your exact variant, read the specifications table instead of the title, and read the low-rated reviews.
  • Seller does not ship. Pay on the platform, where a dispute route exists, or to a Pakistani business you can identify.
  • Customs surprise. Check the item is allowed, and work out the duty before paying, or use a route where one price includes customs.
  • Language mistakes. Send the model number and variant in writing, and keep the chat.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to visit China to import goods?

No. You can find products online, message sellers, pay through the platform or a Pakistani service, and have the goods shipped and cleared without travelling.

Can I see the product before buying from China?

Not in person, but you can ask the seller for photos and video of your exact variant, and for a live video call on expensive items.

Is it safe to pay a Chinese seller I have never met?

It is safer when you pay on the platform, where a dispute route exists, and never move payment to a private chat or an unknown account.

Can I buy from Taobao without going to China?

Yes, through an agent or service with an address in China, because Taobao sellers ship only within China and take Chinese payment methods.

Do I need a Chinese visa or bank account to import?

No. Neither is needed to buy online. You pay by card, through Alibaba checkout, or in rupees to a Pakistani service.

Who clears customs if I am not in China?

Clearance happens in Pakistan. The courier, Pakistan Post or the service clears a personal parcel, and a customs agent can clear a commercial shipment.
