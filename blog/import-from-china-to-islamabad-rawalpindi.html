How to Import One Item From China to Islamabad or Rawalpindi

By importfromchina.shop teamUpdated October 3, 20266 min read
Quick answer

A single item from China reaches Islamabad or Rawalpindi by air. It lands at Islamabad International Airport or at Karachi and then comes north by road, clears Pakistan Customs, and a local courier delivers it to your address in either city. Door to door, this commonly takes 7 to 15 days after the seller dispatches. A single-item service quotes one rupee price for the whole journey, paid once, before it ships.

Can I get a single item from China delivered in Islamabad or Rawalpindi?

Yes. Islamabad International Airport serves both cities and handles international cargo, so parcels from China can land there directly. Many consolidated lines still route through Karachi, which has more flights, and move parcels north by road. Either way, the final delivery is by a Pakistani courier to your door in F-sectors, G-sectors, E-11, Bahria Town, DHA, Saddar Rawalpindi, Satellite Town or anywhere else across the twin cities. You do not need a company or import licence for one item.

How does the item travel from China to the twin cities?

  1. Seller dispatch in China. The seller sends the item to a consolidation warehouse, usually in Guangzhou, Shenzhen or Yiwu.
  2. Air cargo to Pakistan. The parcel flies into Islamabad International Airport or into Karachi.
  3. Customs clearance. Pakistan Customs assesses it. For an individual, the courier or agent clears it with your CNIC.
  4. Road leg, if needed. Parcels that landed in Karachi travel north to the courier's Islamabad or Rawalpindi hub.
  5. Local delivery. The courier delivers to your address.

How long does delivery to Islamabad or Rawalpindi take?

At the time of writing, 7 to 15 days door to door after dispatch is typical for consolidated air freight, including customs. A parcel routed through Karachi adds a road leg of a day or two, but still commonly lands within that window. Allow more around Chinese New Year, late January to mid February, and right after the 11.11 and 618 sales.

Is delivery to Rawalpindi any different from Islamabad?

In practice, no. Couriers treat the two cities as one delivery zone served from shared hubs. Addresses in Rawalpindi cantonment areas, Saddar, Chaklala or Bahria Town are delivered on the same schedule as Islamabad sectors. The one difference is address clarity. Older parts of Rawalpindi have informal street names, so include a mohalla, a landmark and a reachable phone number.

What do people in the twin cities commonly order from China?

Islamabad and Rawalpindi have smaller electronics and fashion markets than Lahore or Karachi, so more items are simply not available locally. Common imports:

  • Gadgets: earbuds, smartwatches, mechanical keyboards, chargers and cables, phone accessories.
  • Outdoor and hiking gear for the Margalla trails and northern trips: trekking poles, headlamps, compact stoves without fuel, packs.
  • Home office and student items: monitor arms, desk lamps, laptop stands, tablets.
  • Specialist tools and hobby parts, camera accessories, cycling components.
  • Fashion and shoes in specific styles seen on Taobao or Temu-style apps.

Phones need PTA registration. Drones are routinely held. Loose batteries, aerosols and medicines have their own rules. See what you cannot import from China to Pakistan.

What does it cost to get one item to Islamabad or Rawalpindi?

The item price plus air freight plus Pakistan customs duty and taxes. Duty and taxes depend on the tariff classification and commonly add 25 to 60 percent of the customs value. Rates change with budgets and SROs, so confirm the current figure or ask for a delivered price. Freight is based on actual or volumetric weight, whichever is higher. The road leg from Karachi, when it applies, is part of the freight and not an extra bill to you. Read shipping from China to Pakistan: cost and time for the detail.

What do I need to receive a parcel in the twin cities?

  • A full address with sector or area, street and house number, and a landmark for Rawalpindi addresses.
  • A mobile number that picks up. Couriers call before attempting delivery.
  • Your CNIC number for customs clearance.
  • Awareness of security checkpoints. Some gated sectors and cantonment areas restrict courier entry, so give a gate or reception point if needed.
