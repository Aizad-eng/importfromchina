How to Return a Temu Order in Pakistan (Step by Step)

By Muhammad Shamshad AslamUpdated October 26, 20269 min read
Quick answer

To return a Temu order in Pakistan, open Your Orders, tap Return/Refund, choose the items and reason, tap Next Step, pick your refund method and submit, then hand the parcel to the CPEX courier within 14 days. You have 90 days from purchase. The first return from each order is free, and later returns from the same order cost Rs 612.

How do you return a Temu order in Pakistan, step by step?

You start the return inside your Temu account and Temu's courier collects it from your door. These steps follow Temu's Pakistan Return and Refund Policy, last updated 23 March 2026, as we read it in October 2026:

  1. Open your account. Sign in on temu.com or the app, with the region set to Pakistan.
  2. Find the order. Go to "Your Orders" and tap the "Return/Refund" button on that order.
  3. Choose items and a reason. Select every item you want to send back and the reason. Depending on the reason, Temu may ask for more details about the problem.
  4. Tap "Next Step". This moves you to the return and refund options.
  5. Pick the return and refund methods. Choose how the parcel goes back and whether the money goes to your original payment method or to Temu credit, then submit.
  6. Pack and hand over. Pack the items carefully and wait for the courier. Temu says CPEX logistics will pick it up at your door.
  7. Track the refund. Check the status on the order details page, or in Temu's SMS, email and app notifications.

The order matters. Choose every item you want to return in step 3, because returning more items later from the same order costs money, as explained below.

What if you ordered without a Temu account?

You can still return it. Temu's policy says that if you do not have a Temu account, you click "Save your Order" in your Temu order email to make a return. That links the order to an account so the "Return/Refund" button appears. Use the same email address the order was sent to.

Do you always have to send the item back?

No. For some reasons Temu decides no return is needed. Its policy says that if there is no need to return your items, your final step is simply to select your refund method, Temu credit or your original payment method, and tap "Submit". You cannot choose this yourself. It depends on the reason you give and Temu's review. If the screen asks for a return method, a return is required.

Should you choose door pickup or drop-off?

Door pickup is the route Temu's Pakistan policy describes, and it is the one most people will see. The policy says that if you choose pickup, CPEX logistics will collect the package at your door. The policy also mentions an advance refund issued "after you dropped off your return package", which suggests drop-off can appear for some orders, but we could not confirm drop-off points in Pakistan. Use whatever options the app shows for your order.

For a smooth pickup:

  • Pack the items in the original packaging if you still have it, or in a sturdy box. Damage caused by poor packing can reduce your refund.
  • Stick on the label Temu provides. Temu warns that the address on the package you received is NOT the return address.
  • Keep your phone on. The CPEX rider will usually call before coming.
  • Keep the courier receipt or pickup confirmation until the refund arrives.

When does the Rs 612 return charge apply?

The Rs 612 charge applies to the second and later returns from the same order. Your first return from each order is free, whether it has one item or several. Temu's policy says that for the second and subsequent refunds from the same order, you use the return label it provides and pay a Rs 612 shipping fee, deducted from your refund.

How long do you have to return a Temu item?

You have 90 days from purchase to request a return, and then 14 days to send the parcel. Both clocks matter. Temu's policy says you must send your return package back within 14 days of submitting the return request, and you cannot return items after the return window has closed.

A worked example: you pay on 1 November, the parcel arrives on 18 November, and the 90-day window runs from the purchase date, not from delivery. If you request a return on 10 January, you must hand the parcel over by 24 January. Requesting the return and then waiting for a convenient day is the usual way people lose a refund.

What cannot be returned on Temu Pakistan?

Temu's Pakistan policy lists five groups that cannot be returned:

  • Clothing that has been worn, washed or damaged after delivery, or has had its tags or hygiene stickers removed.
  • Grocery and food products.
  • Some health and personal care items.
  • Some free gifts.
  • Some customised products.

"Some" means the listing or the return screen decides. If you are unsure about a personal care item or a customised product, check the return option on the order before you open the packaging. For clothes, try them on with the tags still attached.

When will you get your Temu refund?

Most refunds reach a bank or wallet within 5 to 14 business days of being processed, and up to 30 days depending on your bank. Processing usually starts once Temu receives the items and they pass its quality check. Some buyers get an advance refund instead, issued after the return is dropped off. Temu says this is based on shopping history and at its sole discretion, and that it may charge your original payment method if the items never arrive or fail the check.

Your choice of refund method changes the timing:

  • Original payment method. JazzCash, Easypaisa or card. Slower, but it is real money.
  • Temu credit. Faster. Temu says credits have no expiry date, typically cannot be redeemed for cash, and refunds to credit typically cannot be reversed once processed.

The refund can also be reduced. Temu's policy says that if an item comes back used, damaged, missing parts or accessories, or damaged by poor packing, and this is not Temu's or the seller's fault, the refund is reduced for the lost value.

Is import tax or customs duty refunded when you return a Temu item?

Only when the return is Temu's fault. The policy says the original shipping fee and import tax are not refundable if your return is not a result of Temu's fault. Insurance costs, if any, are also non-refundable. A sign on delivery fee, if any, is not refunded once delivery succeeded.

For most small orders this does not matter, because post and courier parcels worth Rs 1,000 or less pay no duties or taxes under section 19C of the Customs Act. On a bigger order where Temu collected an import tax line at checkout, a change-of-mind return loses that amount. A wrong or faulty item is different, so pick the honest reason that matches what happened.

What slows down or blocks a Temu refund?

Most refund problems come from the return parcel itself. Avoid these:

  • Sending it to the wrong address. Use the return label, not the sender address on your original parcel.
  • Packing items you meant to keep. Temu says it cannot promise to find and return a wrong item, and does not refund or compensate for it.
  • Splitting returns. Each extra return from one order costs Rs 612.
  • Missing parts. Return chargers, cables and manuals that came in the box.
  • Unusual return patterns. Temu says it reviews return behaviour, and that very frequent returns, empty parcels or altered labels can lead to limited return options or account restrictions.

If an item never arrived at all, that is not a return. Use the missing or not-received route in our guide to a Temu order not received in Pakistan.

What if the 90 days have passed, or Temu does not sell what you need?

If a defect shows up after the window, Temu's policy says to contact customer service, as statutory warranty rights can still apply. Frequently asked questions

Is returning a Temu order free in Pakistan?

Your first return from each order is free, with CPEX picking it up from your door. A second or later return from the same order costs Rs 612, taken from the refund.

How many days do I have to return a Temu item?

You can request a return within 90 days of purchase. After you request it, you must send the parcel back within 14 days.

Who picks up Temu returns in Pakistan?

Temu's Pakistan return policy says CPEX logistics picks up the return package at your door when you choose pickup.

How long does a Temu refund take in Pakistan?

Refunds to your original payment method take 5 to 14 business days, and up to 30 days, depending on your bank or wallet. Temu credit is faster.

Can I return clothes I have worn on Temu?

No. Clothing that has been worn, washed or damaged, or has had tags or hygiene stickers removed, cannot be returned under Temu's Pakistan policy.

Do I get the import tax back if I return a Temu item?

Not if the return is not Temu's fault. The original shipping fee and import tax are only refunded when the problem was caused by Temu.
