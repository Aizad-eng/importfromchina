Is PayPal Available in Pakistan? (2026)

By Muhammad Shamshad AslamUpdated November 12, 20268 min read
Quick answer

No. When we checked in November 2026, PayPal still did not offer accounts to people living in Pakistan. It launched in Sri Lanka in May 2026 but not here. Freelancers can receive PayPal-funded payments through Payoneer, and family abroad can send money home through Xoom, a PayPal service. To pay Chinese sites, use a Visa or Mastercard or a local service.

Is PayPal available in Pakistan in 2026?

No. PayPal has no direct operations in Pakistan, and you cannot open a personal or business account as a Pakistan resident. ProPakistani reported on 20 May 2026 that PayPal had launched in Sri Lanka, through Bank of Ceylon, Commercial Bank of Ceylon and Sampath Bank, while Pakistan was still without direct service after years of announcements. We found no PayPal or State Bank of Pakistan announcement of a Pakistan launch when we checked in November 2026.

Most people asking this want one of two things: to get paid from abroad, or to pay for something online. Both have working alternatives, covered below. If you are buying from China, our pillar guide on how to import from China to Pakistan shows the payment options at each step.

Why has PayPal not launched in Pakistan?

PayPal has not given a public reason that we could find. Pakistani governments have announced plans and partnerships several times, and ProPakistani describes the history as countless delays without a definitive result. Because the reasons are not on record from PayPal, we will not guess at them. What matters for you is that nothing has changed for residents. Treat any social media post announcing that "PayPal is now live in Pakistan" with caution until PayPal's own site lists Pakistan, and never enter card or CNIC details on a page linked from such a post.

What was the PayPal and Payoneer arrangement?

It was a way for Pakistani freelancers to receive money from PayPal users abroad, not a PayPal account for Pakistanis. The News reported on 8 January 2024 that PayPal had signed an agreement with Payoneer, which collects freelancers' money from abroad and transfers it to their bank accounts in Pakistan. The minister announcing it said there would be no need to open a PayPal account.

So if a client abroad wants to pay with PayPal, a Payoneer invoice is the usual route. You still cannot use PayPal to buy anything from Pakistan, because you have no PayPal account to pay from.

Can you open a PayPal account with a foreign address or a relative's details?

You can try, but it is a poor idea. PayPal's user agreement, as published on its country sites, says an individual must be a resident of one of the countries listed on PayPal's worldwide page and must give the correct country of residence when opening and keeping an account. An account opened with an address where you do not live breaks that rule, which gives PayPal grounds to limit or close it, possibly with your money still inside.

Using a relative's account abroad has the same problem in a different form. The account belongs to them, any dispute is in their name, and logins from Pakistan can trigger security checks.

Can you receive money through PayPal's Xoom in Pakistan?

Yes, if the sender is abroad. Xoom's Pakistan page says "Xoom is a PayPal service" and lists bank deposits to Pakistani banks, cash pickup, mobile wallets including Easypaisa and JazzCash, and mobile phone reloads. It lists senders in the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, the European Economic Area and Australia. It only works in one direction. You cannot use Xoom to send money from Pakistan to China.

How else can you receive money from abroad without PayPal?

Besides Payoneer and Xoom, a sender abroad can use an ordinary bank remittance or a transfer service that pays into Pakistani accounts. Wise is one example, and its help page on PKR transfers sets out the rules clearly, which makes it a useful checklist for any service:

  • The recipient must be a private individual. Wise says you cannot send to a business account in Pakistan.
  • The account must be a rupee personal account with a 24-character IBAN starting with PK.
  • Some account types are excluded. Wise names Meezan Bank and Allied Bank Express accounts as ones to avoid.
  • Low-limit accounts can reject transfers. Wise says transfers may be rejected if an Asaan account has a Rs 500,000 limit.
  • There is a ceiling per transfer. Wise lists Rs 35,000,000.

This is all about money coming in. None of these services lets you fund a payment from a Pakistani bank account to a seller abroad. Wise's same page says adding PKR from an external bank account is not currently possible. For paying China, see our guide on how to send money to China from Pakistan.

Can you pay AliExpress, Temu or Alibaba without PayPal?

Yes. These sites take cards at checkout, and that is how most Pakistani buyers pay them. A Visa or Mastercard debit card works if international online use is switched on in your bank app. A PayPak-only card will not. Our guide on ordering from China without a credit card goes through each site, and our guide to SadaPay and NayaPay cards on Chinese sites covers virtual cards.

Card payments abroad have limits. The State Bank of Pakistan set an annual cap of USD 30,000 per person on international card transactions from January 2023, as Dawn reported, and cards are meant for personal use. Your bank converts the dollar price at its own rate, and an advance tax applies on top, so the rupee amount on your statement will be higher than the price you saw at checkout.

For Taobao and 1688 the problem is different. Those sites are built around Chinese payment methods and Chinese delivery addresses, so neither PayPal nor a Pakistani card solves the whole order. Most people in Pakistan buy from them through a service that pays in China.

Do you lose buyer protection without PayPal?

Not if you pay inside the platform. PayPal's buyer protection is one kind of cover, but the shopping sites have their own. Alibaba's Trade Assurance holds your payment in escrow until you confirm delivery when you pay through Alibaba. AliExpress and Temu have their own refund processes for orders paid on their checkout. The protection disappears when you pay a seller outside the platform, by bank transfer or wallet, whatever method you use.

What PayPal scams target people in Pakistan?

Because PayPal is not available here, offers to "get you PayPal" are a common hook. Be wary of these patterns:

  • "PayPal agents" who offer to pay for you in exchange for a rupee transfer to a personal account.
  • Ready-made verified accounts for sale. They are opened with someone else's identity and can be closed at any time, with your money inside.
  • Fake PayPal launch pages asking for CNIC or card details to "pre-register".
  • Payment requests from a buyer abroad asking you to pay a fee to unlock money sent to you.

Frequently asked questions

Can I open a PayPal account in Pakistan?

No. PayPal does not offer accounts to people living in Pakistan. Opening one with a foreign address breaks PayPal's residency rule and gives PayPal grounds to limit or close the account.

When is PayPal coming to Pakistan?

There is no confirmed date. PayPal launched in Sri Lanka in May 2026, and we found no announcement for Pakistan when we checked in November 2026.

How can Pakistani freelancers receive PayPal payments?

Through Payoneer. Under the arrangement announced in January 2024, Payoneer collects payments from abroad and transfers them to Pakistani bank accounts.

Is Xoom the same as PayPal?

Xoom is a PayPal service for sending money to Pakistan from abroad, into bank accounts, Easypaisa, JazzCash or for cash pickup. It cannot send money out of Pakistan.

Can I buy from AliExpress without PayPal in Pakistan?

Yes. Use a Visa or Mastercard debit or credit card with international online payments switched on. PayPak-only cards do not work.

Is it safe to buy a verified PayPal account?

No. Such accounts are opened with someone else's identity and can be limited or closed at any time, along with any money in them.
