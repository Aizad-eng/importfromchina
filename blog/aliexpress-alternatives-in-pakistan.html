AliExpress Alternatives for Buyers in Pakistan

By importfromchina.shop teamUpdated October 17, 20269 min read
Quick answer

If AliExpress is not working for you in Pakistan because your card is declined, delivery is too slow, or the item will not ship, the main alternatives are Temu, which delivers in 5 to 13 business days with free standard shipping and accepts mobile wallets, Shein for clothing, Daraz for local stock with cash on delivery, Alibaba.com for single pieces from factories, and Taobao or 1688 through a single-item buying service for specialist items no one ships to Pakistan.

Why do buyers in Pakistan look for AliExpress alternatives?

AliExpress still delivers to Pakistan for most listings, and when we checked in October 2026 it showed prices in rupees from a Pakistani connection. But four problems push people elsewhere:

  • Payment. AliExpress needs a Visa or Mastercard with international transactions enabled. JazzCash, Easypaisa and Raast are not accepted, PayPak cards do not work abroad, and PayPal is not available to people living in Pakistan.
  • Speed. Parcels sent by post commonly take 25 to 60 days, with tracking gaps in the middle.
  • Customs surprises. Duty is assessed on arrival, so you may only learn the full cost when the parcel is held.
  • Listings that won't ship. Sellers choose which countries each listing covers, and some leave Pakistan out. See what to do when an AliExpress item won't ship.

None of these problems means AliExpress is a bad site. It is still the widest catalogue of Chinese goods that ships to Pakistan, and for a cheap item with no deadline it can be the right choice. But if one of these problems is the reason you are reading this, each alternative below fixes some of them and brings its own trade-offs.

Is Temu a good AliExpress alternative in Pakistan?

For everyday goods, often yes. Temu runs a Pakistan site at temu.com/pk-en with prices in rupees. Its Pakistan shipping page lists free standard shipping on all orders, delivered in 5 to 13 business days after 1 to 3 days of processing. Its return policy allows returns within 90 days of purchase, with free return shipping on the first return from each order, and you must send the package back within 14 days of requesting the return.

When Temu's Pakistan site was checked in October 2026, it showed JazzCash, Easypaisa and card logos, and no cash on delivery. The catalogue is narrower than AliExpress for parts and niche electronics, and Temu's terms make you the importer if customs assesses more duty than was collected at checkout. Compare the two in Temu vs AliExpress vs Alibaba.

One practical tip: if you found an item on AliExpress, search Temu Pakistan with the same key words and a size or model number. The same factory product often appears on both sites under different titles, and Temu's free standard shipping can make it cheaper delivered even when the listing price is similar. Check the specs and included parts before assuming two listings are identical.

Should you use Shein instead of AliExpress for clothes?

For clothing and shoes, Shein usually gives a better experience. With the location set to Pakistan in October 2026, Shein's shipping page listed standard shipping of 7 to 12 business days, and its payment page listed JazzCash and Easypaisa. Each listing carries its own size chart, so measure before you order.

Returns are the weak point: the return method shown for Pakistan was self-return, at your own cost, within 30 days of receipt. Swimwear, lingerie, underwear, jewellery and beauty items cannot be returned at all.

Is Daraz better than AliExpress?

For common items you need quickly, usually yes. Daraz is a Pakistani marketplace, so local stock arrives in days and you can pay cash on delivery. Daraz's return policy says return requests must be raised within 14 days of delivery. Change-of-mind returns cover only some categories, such as clothing and shoes, and do not apply to non-DarazMall items shipped from overseas. Cash on delivery orders are refunded to your Daraz Wallet, which you can withdraw to a bank account.

The cost is a local markup, since Daraz sellers import, add their margin and absorb return costs. For specialist items, the Daraz price can be far above the China price, or the item is not listed at all. See AliExpress vs Daraz for a closer look.

What about Alibaba.com?

Alibaba.com and AliExpress belong to the same group, but Alibaba is built for business buyers. Its Ready to Ship section lists in-stock products with low minimum orders, and some suppliers will sell one piece. Trade Assurance gives payment protection on qualifying orders. It is a good route for equipment, tools, lab or medical devices and trade parts.

The weak point for one buyer is shipping. A single piece sent by international courier can cost as much as the item, so ask for the shipping cost to your city before paying.

Can you buy from Taobao or 1688 instead?

Yes, but not directly. Taobao and 1688 are Chinese domestic platforms. They take Chinese payment methods and their sellers ship within China. Many products sold on AliExpress are also listed on 1688 by factories and traders, often at lower prices and in more variants, which makes these sites the best fallback when no AliExpress seller ships to Pakistan.

To buy there you need a buying service with a Chinese account and address, which then ships the item to you. To find an AliExpress item there, save a clear product photo and use the camera icon in the 1688 or Taobao search bar.

What if you don't have an international card?

That alone rules out AliExpress for many people. The workable options are:

  • Temu and Shein, which both list JazzCash and Easypaisa.
  • Daraz, with cash on delivery and wallets.
  • A single-item service that you pay in rupees by bank transfer, Raast or a wallet, and that pays the Chinese seller for you.

See how to order from China without a credit card for more detail.

How do you choose the right alternative, step by step?

  1. Search Daraz first. If the item is there at a fair price, the speed and returns are usually worth it.
  2. Search Temu Pakistan, or Shein for clothing. Check the delivered total in rupees at checkout.
  3. If the item is specialist, search AliExpress by model number and check whether any seller ships to Pakistan.
  4. If nobody ships it, search 1688 or Taobao by image and look at Alibaba Ready to Ship listings.
  5. Get a delivered price for each route, including shipping and customs, and compare totals rather than listing prices.
  6. Factor in time and returns. A slightly higher price on a site that delivers in days and takes returns is often the better buy.

Common mistakes when switching from AliExpress

  • Comparing listing prices, not delivered totals. Free shipping on one site and paid shipping on another changes the answer.
  • Forgetting customs. Post and courier parcels worth Rs 1,000 or less are duty-free under section 19C of the Customs Act. Above that, duty can apply on any site. See customs duty from China to Pakistan.
  • Assuming same photo means same item. Check size, material and included parts on every listing.
  • Paying chat sellers in advance. A page that uses a platform's name is not that platform.
  • Importing a cheap item at high shipping cost. For small items, a local option usually wins.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best alternative to AliExpress in Pakistan?

It depends on the problem. Temu for cheap everyday items with wallet payment, Shein for clothing, Daraz for fast delivery and returns, and 1688 or Taobao through a service for items nobody ships to Pakistan.

Which sites like AliExpress accept JazzCash or Easypaisa?

Temu and Shein both list JazzCash and Easypaisa for Pakistan, and Daraz accepts wallets and cash on delivery. AliExpress itself needs an international Visa or Mastercard.

Is Temu faster than AliExpress for delivery to Pakistan?

Usually. Temu Pakistan lists 5 to 13 business days after 1 to 3 days of processing, while AliExpress parcels by post commonly take 25 to 60 days.

Is Daraz cheaper than AliExpress?

For common items, the gap is often small once shipping and duty are added to the AliExpress price. For specialist items, Daraz can be much more expensive or not carry the item at all.

Can I buy AliExpress products on 1688 instead?

Often yes, because many AliExpress products are also listed on 1688 by factories and traders. You need a buying service, since 1688 takes Chinese payment and ships only within China.

Is there a site like AliExpress with cash on delivery in Pakistan?

Daraz offers cash on delivery, and some local resellers of Chinese goods do too. Temu and Shein did not show cash on delivery for Pakistan when we checked, and AliExpress is paid by card.
