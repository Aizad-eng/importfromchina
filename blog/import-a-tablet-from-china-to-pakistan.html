Importing a Tablet From China to Pakistan

By Muhammad Shamshad AslamUpdated November 12, 20269 min read
Quick answer

Yes, you can import one tablet from China to Pakistan. A Wi-Fi-only tablet does not use a SIM, so it falls outside PTA's DIRBS registration. A tablet with a SIM slot counts as a mobile device under DIRBS, so it must be registered and taxed or it will be blocked on local networks. Also check whether it runs a Chinese ROM without Google Play.

New to importing? Read our step-by-step guide on how to import from China to Pakistan.

Can you import one tablet from China to Pakistan?

Yes. Tablets are small, valuable and easy to ship, which makes them a good single import. Chinese brands such as Xiaomi, Lenovo, Huawei, Honor and OnePlus sell tablets in China, sometimes in versions or at prices not offered in Pakistan, and they are listed one piece at a time on JD.com, Taobao and AliExpress.

Three questions decide whether a particular tablet is a good buy: does it have a SIM slot, which software version does it run, and which accessories and warranty come with it. The rest of this guide takes them in turn. For the wider process of buying from China, see our guide on how to import from China to Pakistan.

Does a tablet need PTA registration?

Only if it uses a SIM. PTA's DIRBS FAQ defines a mobile device as any SIM based device, and names dongles, mobile phones, smart watches and tablets as examples. A tablet with a SIM slot is therefore treated like a phone. A Wi-Fi-only tablet has no IMEI on the mobile network and falls outside that definition.

For a SIM tablet, the FAQ says the device will receive signals for the first 60 days and can be used without registering during that time. After that it needs to be registered, or it is blocked on Pakistani networks. When we checked in November 2026, the FAQ listed three ways to apply:

  • Online at the DIRBS device registration portal.
  • By dialling *8484# from a phone with a local SIM issued on the applicant's CNIC.
  • At a mobile operator's service centre or franchise.

The application creates a payment slip ID, valid for 7 days, and the FAQ says every registered device is subject to the relevant FBR duties, paid at a commercial bank. For devices arriving by post or courier, the FAQ says they are released on proof of payment of duty and taxes through the PSID. The amounts are set by FBR and depend on the device, so we do not quote them. Our guide on importing a phone from China and PTA covers the same process for phones.

Wi-Fi or cellular: which tablet should you buy?

For most people, Wi-Fi only. It avoids PTA registration and its tax, and you can share your phone's hotspot when out. A cellular tablet only makes sense if you need it on mobile data all day and are ready to register it.

What is a China ROM and why does it matter?

A China ROM is the software version made for the Chinese market, and it often comes without Google Play and other Google apps. Liliputing's 2022 report on the Lenovo Legion Y700 described this clearly. The cheaper units sold through AliExpress ran a Chinese version of Android without the Google Play Store, and units sold as "Global ROM" were essentially the same Chinese version with the language switched to English and the Play Store added.

That pattern is common across Chinese tablets. Before you buy, ask the seller three things in writing:

  • Is this the China version or the global version of the tablet?
  • Does it come with Google Play installed, and is the device Google certified?
  • Is English available in the system settings?

Our guide on Xiaomi China version vs global version goes deeper on what changes between the two, and the same questions apply to other brands.

How do you check Google Play certification when the tablet arrives?

Open the Play Store and look at the certification line. Google's help page gives the steps: open the Google Play Store app, tap your profile icon at the top right, tap Settings, then tap About, where the Play Protect certification status appears.

Google says only certified devices can include Google apps such as the Play Store, and that on uncertified devices Google apps are not licensed, may not be real Google apps, and the device may not get Android system or app updates. A tablet that shows as not certified is a warning sign, especially one sold as a "Global ROM" unit. Check this on the first day.

Which specs and accessories should you match?

Match the stylus, keyboard and charger to the exact tablet model, not just the brand. Points to check:

  • Stylus generation. Brands often sell several pen models that only work with certain tablets. Check the pen's listed compatible models.
  • Keyboard cover. These fit one tablet size and model, so check the listed model exactly.
  • Charger. Check that it is rated 100 to 240 V, which suits Pakistan's 230 V supply. The plug may need an adapter.
  • Storage and RAM. Select the variant you want before copying the link, since one listing often has several.
  • Region and language. As above, ask about the ROM and English support.

What duty and tax applies to a tablet?

A tablet pays normal import duty and taxes, and a SIM tablet pays PTA-related FBR duties as well. Parcels worth Rs 1,000 or less pay nothing under section 19C of the Customs Act, which no tablet will be. Above that, customs assesses the whole value by tariff code. For FY 2026-27 customs duty slabs are 0, 5, 10, 15 and 20 percent, with some goods higher, sales tax is 18 percent on the value plus duties, and withholding income tax is added. Customs converts the price at the rates FBR publishes.

What warranty do you get on a tablet from China?

Often only the seller's support, so do not count on a local brand warranty. A China-market tablet may not be accepted for warranty repair by the brand's service centres in Pakistan, so ask before you buy. Keep the seller's invoice, and ask the seller what happens if the tablet fails in the first months. Our refund policy covers items that arrive wrong, damaged or not working.

What should you check when the tablet arrives?

Check the software and the box details on the first day, while any problem is easy to report.

  • Confirm the model, storage and colour match what you ordered, in the settings menu as well as on the box.
  • Check the Play Protect certification status as described above.
  • Confirm English is available and the system updates screen works.
  • For a SIM tablet, note the IMEI from the settings and the box, and make sure they match, since you will need it for PTA registration.
  • Charge it fully with the supplied charger and check the stylus and keyboard pair properly, if you ordered them.

Report anything wrong straight away with clear details.

What are the common mistakes when importing a tablet?

  • Buying a cellular version without planning for PTA registration.
  • Assuming a "Global ROM" listing means an official global version.
  • Not checking Play Store certification on the first day.
  • Ordering a stylus or keyboard made for a different model.
  • Copying the link with the wrong storage or colour variant selected.
  • Expecting local brand service centres to repair a China-market unit under warranty.
