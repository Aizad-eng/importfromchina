Temu vs Daraz in Pakistan: Price, Delivery, Payment and Returns Compared

By importfromchina.shop teamUpdated October 17, 20269 min read
Quick answer

Temu is usually cheaper for small, generic items and its standard shipping to Pakistan is free, but you pay upfront by JazzCash, Easypaisa or card and wait 5 to 13 business days after processing. Daraz is a local marketplace with cash on delivery, a delivery date on every listing and 14-day returns with free return shipping. Daraz also sells overseas items whose stated price includes customs fees and import duties. If you need it soon, want to pay cash, or may return it, use Daraz.

Is Temu or Daraz better in Pakistan?

Neither wins on everything. Temu tends to win on price for small, unbranded items, because shipping is free and the catalogue is factory-direct. Daraz wins on convenience: cash on delivery, local sellers, faster delivery from local stock, and a short, simple return process inside Pakistan.

The right choice depends on three questions. How soon do you need it? How do you want to pay? And how likely are you to send it back? This guide compares both platforms on each point, based on their own Pakistan pages and listings when we checked in October 2026.

Who runs Temu and Daraz?

Temu is run by PDD Holdings, the group that also owns Pinduoduo in China. It launched a Pakistan site at temu.com/pk-en in September 2024, with prices in rupees.

Daraz is a Pakistani marketplace that also operates in Bangladesh, Sri Lanka, Nepal and Myanmar. Alibaba Group acquired Daraz in May 2018, which makes it a sister company of AliExpress, not of Temu. Most Daraz listings come from sellers inside Pakistan, but Daraz also carries listings marked as shipping from overseas.

Which is cheaper, Temu or Daraz?

For generic items such as kitchen tools, phone accessories, storage boxes or decor, Temu is often cheaper at the final total, because there is no delivery charge and the item comes from the factory side of the chain. Many Daraz sellers import the same kinds of goods and add their margin.

For branded goods, compare carefully. Daraz Mall items are sold by brands and authorised sellers and are covered by Daraz's 2x Money Back Guarantee on authenticity. Temu does not focus on well-known brands.

The fair test is simple. Put the same item in both carts with your own address and compare the final number, including Daraz's delivery fee and any import tax line Temu shows. For a wider look at when local is cheaper than China, read whether it is cheaper to buy from China or Pakistan.

Which delivers faster?

Daraz, for items already in Pakistan. On 17 October 2026, with Daraz's default Karachi address, a cable from a seller in Punjab showed delivery by 23 to 26 October. An anime figure on Daraz marked as shipping from overseas showed 26 October to 1 November. Each Daraz listing shows its own date range for your address, so check it before you order.

Temu's Pakistan shipping page lists 5 to 13 business days for standard shipping, after 1 to 3 days of processing. Business days skip weekends, so in calendar terms plan for roughly one and a half to three weeks. The final leg goes to a local carrier, and tracking can pause for a few days.

Can you pay cash on delivery on Temu in Pakistan?

No, not when we checked. Temu's Pakistan site footer showed JazzCash, Easypaisa, Visa, Mastercard and other cards, Apple Pay and Google Pay, but no cash on delivery. You pay before the order ships.

Daraz showed "Cash on Delivery Available" on both listings we opened. Its refund policy also lists debit and credit cards, HBL direct transfer, Easypaisa wallet, Easypaisa shops, JazzCash mobile accounts, Daraz Wallet, Daraz vouchers and instalments on credit cards as payment methods. One detail matters if you pay cash: Daraz refunds cash on delivery, JazzCash and Easypaisa shop orders to your Daraz Wallet only, though you can withdraw from the wallet to a bank account.

Which has better returns?

Temu gives more time. Its Pakistan policy allows returns within 90 days of purchase. The first return from each order is free and the courier picks it up from your door, but you must send it within 14 days of requesting the return. A second return from the same order costs Rs 612, taken from the refund. Refunds to your original payment method take 5 to 14 business days, up to 30 days, depending on your bank or wallet. Worn or washed clothes, food and some personal care items cannot be returned.

Daraz gives a shorter window but a simpler local process. You must raise a return within 14 days of delivery, and there is no charge to send the product back. The main reasons accepted are damaged, defective, incorrect, incomplete or not as described. Change of mind returns apply only in some categories, such as fashion, and not for phones, tablets or chargers. Daraz's refund policy says it does not apply to Daraz Global products, so read the return terms on any overseas listing.

Who pays customs duty on Temu and Daraz orders?

On Temu, you do. Its Pakistan terms say that, where applicable, you act as the importer and authorise Temu to appoint a freight forwarding agent to pay duties on your behalf. Duties collected at checkout are estimates, and if customs assesses more, you are responsible for the difference. Import tax is not refunded if you return an item for a reason that is not Temu's fault.

On Daraz, local listings are already in Pakistan, so there is no import step for you. For overseas listings, Daraz's product page says the stated price includes customs fees and import duties.

Under section 19C of the Customs Act, post and courier parcels worth Rs 1,000 or less are duty-free, as set by the Finance Act 2025. Above that, duty and taxes can apply. Our guide to customs duty from China to Pakistan explains how they are worked out.

What does "Ships from overseas" mean on Daraz?

It means the seller is outside Pakistan, often in China, and the parcel is sent to you across the border. In practice it is Daraz's closest equivalent to Temu. When we searched Daraz for niche items such as anime figures, CNC router bits and sim racing shifters, a large share of results showed the location "Overseas".

The difference from Temu is in the checkout. Daraz overseas listings showed cash on delivery and said the price includes customs and import duties. Temu asks you to pay upfront and treats duty as your responsibility. For more on Daraz against AliExpress, see AliExpress vs Daraz.

Common mistakes when choosing between Temu and Daraz

  • Comparing item prices only. Compare final totals with your address, including delivery fees and any import tax line.
  • Expecting Daraz speed from an overseas listing. Check the location and the date range on the listing before you order.
  • Missing Daraz's 14-day window. It counts from delivery. Open the parcel and check it the same week.
  • Missing Temu's 14-day send-back rule. The 90 days is to request a return. After you request it, you have 14 days to hand it over.
  • Trusting "Temu Pakistan" pages on Facebook or WhatsApp. These are resellers. Buy only through the Temu app or temu.com with the region set to Pakistan. See does Temu deliver to Pakistan for red flags.
