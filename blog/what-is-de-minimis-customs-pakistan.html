What Is the De Minimis Limit for Parcels in Pakistan?

By Muhammad Shamshad AslamUpdated October 23, 20265 min read
Quick answer

The de minimis limit is the value below which a parcel enters Pakistan without duties and taxes. Under section 19C of the Customs Act it is Rs 1,000 for goods arriving by post or courier, cut from Rs 5,000 by the Finance Act 2025. When we checked in October 2026, the 2026-27 budget had not changed it.

What is the de minimis limit in Pakistan?

It is Rs 1,000. Section 19C of the Customs Act, as amended by the Finance Act 2025, says that where goods imported through post or courier services do not exceed one thousand rupees in value, no duties and taxes shall be demanded. The exemption is subject to conditions and restrictions the Board can set in rules.

De minimis is a Latin phrase meaning too small to matter. In customs it means a value so low that collecting tax on it is not worth the paperwork.

How has the limit changed?

It has been cut sharply in recent years. When we checked in October 2026, this was the history:

PeriodLimitBasis
From October 2020Rs 5,000FBR de minimis rules under section 19C, S.R.O. 1109(I)/2020, for postal and air courier goods
From 1 July 2025Rs 1,000Finance Act 2025 amended section 19C. The budget proposal had been Rs 500
2026-27 budgetRs 1,000, unchangedFBR's customs salient features for Budget 2026-27 list no change to section 19C

The 2026-27 customs measures were mostly tariff cuts under the National Tariff Policy, lower additional customs duty and regulatory duty on many lines, and enforcement changes such as legal cover for cargo scanning. A Pakistani customs guide updated in October 2026 also gives the limit as Rs 1,000. Budgets and rules change, so check again before relying on it.

Why was the limit cut to Rs 1,000?

To stop the exemption being used for goods that should be taxed. A Pakistani customs guide updated in October 2026 says the Finance Act 2025 change was meant to close what the government called misuse of the de minimis facility, such as splitting orders into small parcels or under-declaring values to avoid duty on more expensive goods.

Low-value parcels were never invisible to customs. Under the 2020 rules, postal and courier operators did not file a goods declaration for parcels under the limit, but they had to send customs a consolidated monthly statement of those clearances, with copies of the invoices, so the figures could be reconciled.

What value counts towards the Rs 1,000?

The value of the goods in the parcel. Under the 2020 rules, the value on the parcel label or the courier receipt was treated as the declared value, and foreign currency was converted at an official exchange rate. A few points matter in practice:

  • It is a cliff, not an allowance. A parcel worth Rs 1,200 is not taxed on Rs 200. Above the limit, duty and taxes apply to the whole value.
  • It only covers post and courier. The section's wording is limited to goods imported through post or courier services.
  • Splitting an order does not reliably work. Customs can treat several parcels from one sender as one import.
  • Phones are separate. Mobile phones go through PTA registration whatever their value. See importing a phone and PTA.

What happens if your parcel is worth more than Rs 1,000?

It is assessed like any other import. Duty and taxes are worked out on the customs value, which is the item price plus freight and insurance, known as the CIF value. The rates depend on the product's PCT code. The courier or Pakistan Post usually collects the amount before handing over the parcel. Our guide to customs duty from China to Pakistan shows how the taxes stack up, and Pakistan Post vs courier explains who collects what.

Restricted goods are not made legal by a low value. Medicines, drones and some battery items follow their own rules at any price.

What does the Rs 1,000 limit mean for one item?

For most items worth ordering from China on their own, it does not apply. Rs 1,000 covers a small accessory at most. Anything of real value, such as earbuds, a tool or a spare part, is above it, so plan for duty in the total.

Frequently asked questions

What is the duty-free limit for parcels in Pakistan?

Rs 1,000 for goods arriving by post or courier, under section 19C of the Customs Act. It was Rs 5,000 until the Finance Act 2025.

Did the 2026-27 budget change the de minimis limit?

Not that we found. FBR's customs salient features for Budget 2026-27 list no change to section 19C, and the limit stood at Rs 1,000 when we checked in October 2026.

If my parcel is worth Rs 1,500, do I pay duty only on Rs 500?

No. The limit is a cut-off, not an allowance. Above Rs 1,000, duty and taxes apply to the full customs value.

Does the de minimis limit apply to mobile phones?

No. Phones must be registered with PTA through DIRBS, and the PTA tax depends on the model, whatever the parcel value.

Can I split an order into small parcels to stay under Rs 1,000?

It is risky. Customs can treat several parcels from the same sender as one import and assess them together.
