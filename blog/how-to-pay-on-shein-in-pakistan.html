How to Pay on Shein in Pakistan: JazzCash, Easypaisa or Card

By Muhammad Shamshad AslamUpdated November 11, 20267 min read
Quick answer

You pay on Shein from Pakistan inside the Shein checkout, before the order ships, with JazzCash, Easypaisa or an international Visa or Mastercard. With a wallet, you choose it at checkout and then approve the payment request inside the JazzCash or Easypaisa app. Shein prices are in US dollars, there is no cash on delivery for Pakistan, and you should never pay a person or page outside the app.

Which payment methods work on Shein in Pakistan?

Three methods work for most Pakistani buyers: JazzCash, Easypaisa, and a debit or credit card that allows international online payments. Shein's How to Pay page, when we read it on 11 November 2026, had a section for each Pakistani wallet with the steps to approve a payment, alongside its card and PayPal sections.

How do you pay Shein with JazzCash?

You start the payment on Shein and finish it in the JazzCash app. These are the steps on Shein's How to Pay page:

  1. At checkout, choose JazzCash as the payment method and place the order.
  2. Open your JazzCash wallet and log in.
  3. Tap "payment requests".
  4. Enter your MPIN to approve the payment.

Keep enough balance in the wallet before you start, and keep the Shein order page open until the payment shows as successful. If you leave the request unapproved, the order is not paid.

How do you pay Shein with Easypaisa?

Easypaisa works the same way, with different menu names. From Shein's How to Pay page:

  1. At checkout, choose Easypaisa as the payment method and place the order.
  2. Open your Easypaisa wallet and log in.
  3. Tap "my approval".
  4. Tap "accept" to approve the payment.

Approve only a request that matches the Shein order you just placed, with the same amount. If an approval request appears that you did not start, decline it.

How do you pay Shein with a debit or credit card?

Any Visa or Mastercard can work, as long as your bank allows it to be used online and abroad. Before you pay:

  • Switch on e-commerce and international use in your banking app. Many Pakistani debit cards ship with this turned off.
  • Check your limit. Banks set a daily or monthly cap on foreign online spending.
  • Use a Visa or Mastercard. PayPak cards are for use inside Pakistan and do not work on foreign sites.

Shein's page says it does not collect your card number or personal information when you pay, and that questions about a card transaction should go to the bank that issued the card.

What currency do you pay Shein in from Pakistan?

Usually US dollars. Shein's How to Pay page says the currency you pay in depends on the country you pay from and the payment method you choose, and the list of payment currencies on that page did not include Pakistani rupees in November 2026. Shein's site can show rupee prices for display, using its own rate (Rs 277.24 to the dollar that week), but that is a guide, not the amount your bank will charge.

So read the final total on the last screen before you approve. With a card, your bank converts dollars to rupees at its own rate. With a wallet, check the rupee amount on the approval request itself.

Do you pay extra tax when you pay Shein by card?

Possibly, depending on how your bank processes the payment. If your bank treats a Shein card payment as money sent abroad, it collects advance income tax under section 236Y. FBR's withholding tax rate card, updated to 30 June 2026, lists 0.5% for people on the Active Taxpayers List and 1% for those who are not, on amounts remitted abroad through credit, debit or prepaid cards.

On a Rs 10,000 card payment, that is Rs 50 for a filer or Rs 100 for a non-filer. It shows on your bank statement, not on Shein's receipt. Your bank may also add its own foreign transaction charge, so check its schedule of charges. This is separate from any customs duty on the parcel, which is decided when it reaches Pakistan. Can you pay cash on delivery for Shein in Pakistan?

No. When we checked on 11 November 2026, Shein's shipping data for Pakistan showed cash on delivery as not supported for Standard Shipping, the only method offered. Every Shein order to Pakistan is paid in full at checkout.

That makes "Shein cash on delivery" pages a warning sign. Facebook and Instagram pages called "Shein Pakistan" are independent resellers. They set their own prices and return rules, and Shein's policies do not protect you when you pay them. If anyone asks you to send money to a personal JazzCash, Easypaisa or bank account for a Shein order, you are not buying from Shein.

How do Shein coupons and points work at checkout?

Shein's How to Pay page sets these rules:

  • Enter a coupon code exactly as you received it and tap "apply" before you continue, or the discount does not show in the total.
  • Only one coupon code per order. Coupons cannot be combined, and some items are excluded.
  • You can pay up to 50% of a purchase with points, and 100 points equal one US dollar.

Why did my Shein payment fail?

Most failed payments from Pakistan come down to a few causes:

  • Card not enabled for international use. Turn it on in your banking app, then try again.
  • Limit reached. Your bank's foreign online limit, or your wallet's daily or monthly limit, is lower than the order total.
  • Request not approved in time. With JazzCash or Easypaisa, the order only goes through once you approve it in the wallet app.
  • Not enough balance in the wallet or account.
  • VPN on. A VPN can make the site think you are in another country and show other payment options. Turn it off and check the location reads Pakistan.

If money left your account but the order shows unpaid, do not pay again straight away. Contact Shein customer service with the order number and a screenshot of the deduction, and ask your bank or wallet about the transaction too.
