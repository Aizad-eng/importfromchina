Best Products to Import from China to Pakistan for Resale (2026)

By importfromchina.shop teamUpdated October 3, 20269 min read
Quick answer

The best products to import from China to Pakistan are light, high-margin items with steady local demand: phone accessories and earbuds, beauty and skincare tools, kitchen gadgets, home organisers, fashion accessories, LED lighting, and toys. They ship cheaply by air, carry moderate duty, and sell through Daraz, Instagram, WhatsApp and local shops. Avoid heavy, low-value, battery-restricted or heavily regulated goods for your first orders.

What makes a product good to import?

Before any category, apply four filters. A product passes if it is:

  1. Light for its value. Freight is charged per kg. A PKR 2,000 item that weighs 100 g is easy. A PKR 2,000 item that weighs 3 kg is not.
  2. Not heavily taxed. Check the duty rate for the HS code. Some categories carry regulatory duty that kills the margin. See our customs duty guide.
  3. In demand locally. Search Daraz, OLX and Instagram for the item. If people are already selling it, demand exists. Your job is to source it cheaper or better.
  4. Simple to ship. No lithium battery restrictions, no liquids, no permits.

What should a beginner import first?

Start with one product, not a catalogue. Choose something that is:

  • Under 500 g, so air freight stays cheap.
  • Priced where you can sell at 2–3 times the landed cost. A product that lands at PKR 400 and sells at PKR 1,000 to 1,200 leaves room for platform fees, returns and marketing.
  • Already selling in Pakistan, so you are not creating demand from zero.

Phone accessories, kitchen gadgets and beauty tools meet all three for most beginners.

Which products should I avoid?

  • Heavy, cheap items such as ceramic, glass, or large plastic goods, unless you are shipping by sea in volume.
  • Branded or look-alike goods. Customs seizes counterfeits and you lose the shipment and the money.
  • Items needing certification such as medical devices, baby formula, and some electrical products, until you understand the requirements.
  • Pure battery products such as power banks for your first air shipment. They can be done, but need the right shipping line.
  • Trend products at the peak. By the time you receive the stock, the trend may be over and the market flooded.

How do I check if a product will be profitable?

Work the numbers backwards from the local selling price.

  1. Find the typical selling price on Daraz or Instagram.
  2. Divide by 2.5. That is the maximum landed cost you should accept.
  3. Get a landed quote (product + freight + duty + delivery) from a sourcing service.
  4. If the landed cost is under your maximum, order one piece first and check it with your own hands.

Do this before falling in love with a product. Many items that look cheap on Alibaba land at a price that leaves no margin once freight and duty are added.

Where do Pakistani importers sell these products?

  • Daraz for volume and reach, with platform fees and competition.
  • Instagram and TikTok shops for beauty, fashion and home products with visual appeal.
  • WhatsApp and Facebook groups for wholesale to small shopkeepers.
  • Physical shops and markets in Lahore's Hall Road, Karachi's Saddar, Rawalpindi's Raja Bazaar for electronics and accessories.

Frequently asked questions

What is the most profitable product to import from China to Pakistan?

There is no single answer, but phone accessories, wireless earbuds, beauty tools and kitchen gadgets consistently give good margins because they are light, in demand, and sell at two to three times landed cost.

How much money do I need to start importing from China?

A test order of a light product can start from roughly PKR 50,000 to 150,000 including shipping and duty. Larger first orders are not necessary and increase risk before you know the product sells.

Can I import branded products like Apple or Samsung accessories from China?

Not legally unless they are genuine and you are an authorised distributor. Counterfeit branded goods are seized at customs. Unbranded or your own-brand products are the safe route.

Is it better to import one product or many?

Start with one or two. Learn the process, the real landed cost and the selling cycle, then add products. Spreading a small budget across ten products makes every one of them unprofitable.

Can I sell imported products on Daraz?

Yes. Daraz allows unbranded and own-brand products. You need a seller account, product listings and stock in Pakistan, which a door-to-door import service delivers to you.
