Who Can Buy From Taobao for Me in Pakistan?

By Muhammad Shamshad AslamUpdated October 17, 20269 min read
Quick answer

Three kinds of people can buy from Taobao for you. A Taobao shopping agent buys the item, receives it at its warehouse in China, then bills you again for international shipping. A single-item import service buys it and delivers it in Pakistan for one rupee price. Or a friend or relative in China can buy it and post it. Taobao's own overseas shipping lists a few countries, and Pakistan was not among them. For one item worth around Rs 10,000 or more, a one-price service is usually simplest.

Why can't I just buy from Taobao myself?

Taobao has added features for overseas shoppers, so this is a fair question. Here is where things stand at the time of writing.

  • Language. An English interface exists, but guides describe it as available only when your account region is set to one of a list of supported countries. Others get a basic Global mode or use translation.
  • Payment. Taobao payments go through Alipay, which guides say accepts international Visa, Mastercard and JCB cards. A Pakistani card with international use enabled may work. It is not guaranteed.
  • Shipping. This is the real blocker. Taobao's own consolidation and direct shipping lists a small set of places, including Hong Kong, Malaysia, Singapore, the US, Canada, Australia and Japan. Pakistan was not on the lists we found. Many Taobao sellers also ship only inside China.

So even if you can read the listing and pay for it, the item usually needs a Chinese address and someone to send it on. That is the job the options below fill. For the basics of how Taobao works, see our guide on how to buy from Taobao in Pakistan.

Option 1: a Taobao shopping agent

A shopping agent has a Chinese Taobao account, a Chinese payment method and a warehouse. The usual pattern:

  • You send links with the colour and size.
  • The agent invoices the item, the seller's shipping inside China and its commission. You pay.
  • The agent buys and the seller ships to the warehouse.
  • Once your parcels arrive, the agent weighs them, offers shipping options, and you pay a second time.

The strength of this model is consolidation. If you want eight small things from eight Taobao shops, an agent can combine them in one box. The weaknesses are the second bill, which you only see once everything is weighed, and short return windows. Once an item reaches the warehouse, there is commonly only a short period in which a return to the seller can still be arranged. Agents and forwarders also refuse prohibited items, and the buyer then has to seek a refund from the seller.

Ask any agent whether its shipping to Pakistan includes customs, or whether you will be billed again after clearance.

Option 2: a single-item import service

A single-item service quotes one item, delivered. You send the Taobao link, it checks the listing and the variant, works out shipping and Pakistan customs for that specific item, adds its fee, and gives you one total in rupees. You pay once, and the item arrives at your address.

This model does not consolidate many items. What it gives you is certainty: you know the full cost before you pay, and one business is responsible from the seller to your door. It suits a single product you have already chosen, such as a specific pair of headphones, a camera accessory, a hobby kit or a piece of equipment.

Option 3: a friend or relative in China

If you know someone living or studying in China, they can buy from Taobao with their own account and have it delivered to them. Then they post it to Pakistan through a courier or China Post.

This works, but it shifts the hard parts onto them. They pay for international postage, which for one parcel is usually express courier pricing. In Pakistan, the courier or post office collects customs from you before delivery. Parcels worth Rs 1,000 or less are free of duties and taxes under section 19C of the Customs Act. Above that, duty applies, and our customs duty guide explains how it is worked out. It is also a favour you can ask only so often.

Option 4: find the same item somewhere that ships to Pakistan

Many products on Taobao also appear on AliExpress, sometimes from the same factory. AliExpress ships to Pakistan on many listings, though the shipping options depend on the seller and were reportedly cut back during 2025. Temu has delivered to Pakistan since 2024. If the identical item is listed there at a fair price, you may not need anyone to buy for you. Our guide on buying from AliExpress in Pakistan covers that route.

The catch is that Taobao often has the specific version, colour or small brand that the international sites do not.

How do I check a Taobao listing before asking someone to buy it?

Whoever buys for you will buy what you point at, so the listing is worth five minutes of your own time first.

  • Read reviews with photos. Buyer photos show the real colour, finish and size far better than the seller's studio pictures.
  • Look at sales and shop history. A shop with a long record and steady sales is a safer bet than a new one with a single listing.
  • Check the specification table, not just the title. Titles are stuffed with search words. The details sit in the parameters section and the option names.
  • Watch for pre-sale. Some listings ship days or weeks after the order. Ask before you pay.
  • Ask the seller for real photos of the exact option if anything is unclear. Many sellers will send them.

If the listing looks thin, try searching by image for the same product from another shop. Taobao often has several sellers offering the same item.

What should I send whoever buys for me?

Taobao listings often bundle many versions under one link, each with its own price. The person buying needs to know exactly which one.

  1. The link. In the Taobao app, the share button gives a text code or link. Our guide on copying a product link from Chinese apps explains how.
  2. A screenshot of the selected option, showing colour, size and version as they appear in the listing.
  3. Your measurements in centimetres for clothes and shoes, checked against the seller's size chart rather than the size letter.
  4. Anything about batteries, liquids or magnets, which affects whether it can fly.

Which Taobao items are worth bringing in one at a time?

Shipping one item from China usually costs Rs 5,000 to 20,000, so a single-item service makes sense for items worth around Rs 10,000 or more. That covers audio gear, camera accessories, collectibles, PC parts, tools, specialist kitchen gear and quality clothing or shoes from Chinese brands.

For cheap items, the maths changes. A Rs 1,500 phone stand is not worth shipping alone. Either combine it with other items through an agent or look for it locally. Avoid branded goods at replica prices. Pakistan Customs seizes counterfeits, and no buyer can protect you from that.

What are the common mistakes?

  • Paying an agent's first invoice and treating it as the total. International shipping comes later.
  • Sending a link without the option. The buyer may pick the cheapest variant shown, which is often not the one you wanted.
  • Using Taobao's in-app translation to judge quality. Read reviews with photos and check the seller's history.
  • Ordering something that cannot fly, such as a loose power bank or a perfume.

Frequently asked questions

Who can buy from Taobao for me if I live in Pakistan?

A Taobao shopping agent, a single-item import service, or a friend or relative in China. All three have a Chinese account and address, which Taobao's shipping usually requires.

Does Taobao ship directly to Pakistan?

Not on the official lists we checked. Taobao's own consolidation and direct shipping covers a short list of countries, and many sellers only ship inside China, so Pakistani buyers use someone with a China address.

Can I pay Taobao with a Pakistani Visa or Mastercard?

Possibly. Guides say Taobao payments through Alipay accept international Visa and Mastercard. Even if payment works, shipping to Pakistan is usually the problem.

Why does my Taobao agent ask for a second payment?

The first payment covers the item, delivery inside China and the agent's fee. International shipping is only priced once your parcel reaches the agent's warehouse and is weighed.

Is it worth buying one cheap item from Taobao and shipping it to Pakistan?

Usually not. Shipping one item from China commonly costs Rs 5,000 to 20,000, so it makes sense for items worth around Rs 10,000 or more.

Will I pay customs on a Taobao parcel in Pakistan?

Yes, unless the goods are worth Rs 1,000 or less, which are duty-free by post or courier under section 19C. A one-price service includes customs in its quote.
