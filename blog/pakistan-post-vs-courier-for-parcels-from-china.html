Pakistan Post vs Courier for Parcels From China

By importfromchina.shop teamUpdated October 11, 20269 min read
Quick answer

A parcel from China reaches you either by post or by courier. By post, it travels through China Post or a consolidator, Pakistan Customs examines it, and Pakistan Post delivers it and collects any duty manually. By courier, such as DHL or a partner like TCS for UPS, the courier clears customs as a licensed agent, asks for your CNIC and usually lets you pay duty before delivery. Post is cheaper and slower with patchy tracking. Courier is faster, tracked end to end and more expensive.

What is the difference between post and courier for a parcel from China?

They are two separate systems. Post means the national postal services: China Post (including its EMS express service) hands the parcel to Pakistan Post, which delivers it inside Pakistan. Most cheap AliExpress orders travel this way, often after a consolidator such as Cainiao has bundled them. Courier means a private express network such as DHL Express, UPS (through TCS, which announced it as UPS's authorised service contractor in Pakistan in 2016) or FedEx, which carries the parcel on its own aircraft and handles it from pickup to your door.

The route decides almost everything else: how fast it is, how well you can track it, who deals with customs and how you pay any duty.

How can you tell which route your parcel is on?

Look at the tracking number. International postal items use the Universal Postal Union's S10 format: 13 characters made of two letters, eight digits, one check digit and a two-letter country code for the post office that issued it. A number ending in CN was issued by China Post. The first letter hints at the service:

Starts withPostal service type
E (for example EA, EB)EMS express mail
CParcel post
RRegistered letter post (small packets)
LTracked letter post
ULetter post without customer tracking

If your number does not follow this pattern, it is most likely a courier or consolidator number, tracked on that company's own site.

How does tracking differ?

Courier tracking is continuous. The same company scans the parcel at pickup, export, the flight, import customs and delivery, so you see every step on one page.

Postal tracking is handed over between countries. China Post or the consolidator tracks it to departure. Once it reaches Pakistan, Pakistan Post's Express Mail Track and Trace System on ep.gov.pk takes over, using the same tracking number. In between, a postal parcel can go quiet for days or weeks. Small letter-post packets starting with U may have little or no tracking at all once they leave China. Our guide to tracking a parcel from China to Pakistan explains each status.

How does customs handle a postal parcel?

For inbound mail, Pakistan Customs examines dutiable articles and sets the duty based on the value of the goods. Pakistan Post does not set the duty. It only collects it on Customs' behalf, through its own manual system. Pakistan Post says plainly that it has no online system or call centre for collecting duty, and that it never asks for payment through its website, a link, a cash app or a card.

In practice this means a dutiable postal parcel can be held while it is assessed, then delivered with duty to pay, or you may be asked to collect it from a post office. How it works can vary by city and item, so if your parcel is held, call the Pakistan Post helpline on 051-111-111-117 or visit your local post office with your CNIC and tracking number.

How does customs handle a courier parcel?

A courier clears your parcel itself, acting as a licensed clearing agent. DHL's Pakistan guidance says it asks for the receiver's CNIC because Customs needs to verify the receiver's identity before release, and that the name on the airway bill must match the CNIC exactly. A misspelled name can push the parcel into manual processing and delay it.

If duty is due, DHL says guest receivers get an SMS or email with a secure link to pay through its On Demand Delivery system before the parcel is delivered. DHL says clearance is usually done within one to two business days once the documents are right. Other couriers have their own process, so watch for a call or message from the courier named on your tracking page. If someone else will receive the parcel for you, couriers typically ask for an authority letter and both CNICs.

Is there a duty-free limit for small parcels?

Only a very small one. Section 19C of the Customs Act, as amended by the Finance Act 2025, says no duties and taxes are demanded where the value of goods imported through post or courier does not exceed one thousand rupees. It was five thousand rupees before. Above the limit, the whole parcel can be assessed, not just the part above it. Customs can also treat several parcels from one sender as one import. Our customs duty guide explains how duty is worked out once a parcel is above the limit.

What payment scams should you watch for?

Fake "customs fee" messages are common. Pakistan Post warns that it never asks for online payment through its website and that you should not pay through any link, phone call, cash app or card while tracking an item. Its only official sites are ep.gov.pk and pakpost.gov.pk.

  • If a message says your postal parcel is held and asks you to pay through a link, it is not from Pakistan Post.
  • For courier parcels, only pay through the courier's own website or app. Type the address yourself instead of tapping a link if you are unsure.
  • Check the tracking number in the message against the one you were given. Scam messages often use random numbers.

What should you do if a parcel is stuck?

  1. Check which system it is in from the tracking number format.
  2. For post, track it on ep.gov.pk, then call 051-111-111-117 or file a complaint through the Pakistan Post complaints page.
  3. For courier, call the courier with the waybill number and your CNIC ready, and ask whether documents or duty are pending.
  4. Keep the seller's invoice or order screenshot. Customs may ask for proof of value.

Our guide on what to do when a parcel is stuck at customs covers each case.

Whichever route it is on, keep these papers ready:

  • The order or invoice. A courier clearing your parcel needs a commercial invoice showing the declared value, a description of the item, the country of origin and both addresses. For a platform order, keep the order page screenshot showing what you paid.
  • Your CNIC. For a courier, the name on the airway bill must match it. For post, take it with you if you collect.
  • The tracking number and any messages from the seller about dispatch.

Do post and courier treat restricted items differently?

The rules on what can enter Pakistan are the same whichever route the parcel takes. Phones and other SIM devices still need PTA registration through DIRBS after arrival, and medicines need DRAP approval. Pakistan Post's own guide bars explosive, flammable and dangerous substances from the post, and airlines restrict loose lithium batteries and power banks, which affects both routes since both usually fly. Devices with built-in batteries are usually fine. If you are unsure, check our list of what you cannot import from China to Pakistan before ordering.

Which should you choose for one item?

Post makes sense for cheap, light items you are not in a hurry for. Courier makes sense when time matters, when the item is valuable enough that losing track of it would hurt, or when you want customs dealt with for you. The catch is cost: courier rates for one small parcel can be more than the item.

Frequently asked questions

Who collects customs duty on a postal parcel in Pakistan?

Pakistan Customs sets the duty and Pakistan Post collects it on its behalf, through a manual system, never by online link.

Do couriers like DHL clear customs for me?

Yes. Couriers clear parcels as licensed agents. DHL asks for your CNIC and says the name on the airway bill must match it.

How do I know if my parcel is coming by post?

Postal tracking numbers have 13 characters, two letters, nine digits and a country code. One ending in CN was issued by China Post.

Is there a duty-free limit for parcels in Pakistan?

Only one thousand rupees for goods through post or courier, under section 19C of the Customs Act as amended in 2025.

Is a message asking me to pay Pakistan Post customs online real?

No. Pakistan Post says it never asks for online payment through links, calls, cash apps or cards.

Which is faster, post or courier?

Courier. Air express commonly takes 5 to 10 days, while AliExpress-type post to Pakistan commonly takes 25 to 60 days.
