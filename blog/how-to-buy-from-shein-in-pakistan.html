How to Buy From Shein in Pakistan (2026)

By Muhammad Shamshad AslamUpdated October 23, 20269 min read
Quick answer

Yes, you can buy from Shein in Pakistan through its global site and app. When we checked in October 2026 with the location set to Pakistan, Shein listed standard shipping of 7 to 11 business days after 1 to 3 days of processing, free above a minimum order value. Its payment page lists JazzCash, Easypaisa and cards. Shein's terms make you the importer, the return method shown was self-return at your own cost, and parcels worth over Rs 1,000 can attract customs duty.

Does Shein deliver to Pakistan in 2026?

Yes. Shein does not run a separate Pakistan website. You buy through the global site at shein.com or the Shein app, with your location set to Pakistan. When we opened Shein's Shipping Info page in October 2026 with the location set to Pakistan, it showed standard shipping from Shein's global warehouses, an estimated 7 to 11 business days for delivery, and a 1 to 3 day processing time before that.

The same page names Pakistan among the countries covered by Shein's On-Time Delivery Service terms. It also lists countries where Shein has suspended shipping for logistics reasons, and Pakistan was not on that list when we checked.

One line on the page matters for smaller towns: if you cannot type your address during checkout, Shein says delivery is not supported in your area. If that happens, use an address in the nearest larger city.

How do you set up a Shein account for Pakistan?

  1. Download the Shein app or open shein.com. Turn off any VPN so the site does not set you to another country.
  2. Check the location. On the Shipping Info page there is a Location box, and it should read Pakistan.
  3. Register with your email or phone number.
  4. Add your address in full: house and street, area, city and province, with a mobile number you answer. Couriers in Pakistan rely on the phone number for the last leg.
  5. Use your real name as on your CNIC. Shein's terms say consignee names, addresses and payer names must be valid for customs.

How do you order from Shein in Pakistan, step by step?

  1. Find the item and read the size chart. Do not assume your usual local size. Measure a garment you own and compare it with the chart in centimetres. Our Chinese size chart guide helps.
  2. Read reviews with photos. Reviews from buyers who mention their height and weight tell you more than the model photo.
  3. Add to bag and check the shipping line. Shein charges a small shipping fee below a minimum order value and gives free standard shipping above it. Check the actual figure at checkout.
  4. Decide on On-Time Delivery. If you tick the On-Time Delivery box at checkout and the order arrives late, Shein's terms say it may give you 100 Shein Points, worth 1 US dollar, valid for 30 days.
  5. Pick a payment method and pay. See the next section.
  6. Save your order number and track it. The tracking page gives the most current delivery date.

How do you pay Shein from Pakistan?

Shein's Payment Method page, when we read it in October 2026, listed these options relevant to Pakistan:

  • JazzCash. Choose JazzCash on the order page. Then open your JazzCash wallet, go to "payment requests" and enter your MPIN to approve.
  • Easypaisa. Choose Easypaisa on the order page. Then open your Easypaisa wallet, go to "my approval" and tap accept.
  • Debit or credit card. Visa and Mastercard work if international online transactions are switched on in your banking app. Local-only PayPak cards do not work on foreign sites.

The same page also describes PayPal, but PayPal does not offer accounts to people living in Pakistan. Shein's page says the currency you pay in depends on the country you pay from and the method you choose, and rupees were not in its currency list. Check the currency and total on the final screen before you approve. If you pay by card, your bank's exchange rate and charges apply. Do you pay customs duty on a Shein parcel in Pakistan?

Sometimes. Shein's Terms and Conditions say you authorise Shein and its agents to make customs declarations in your name, and that as the importer you are responsible for complying with the laws of your own country.

Pakistan's rule for small parcels is section 19C of the Customs Act. Since the Finance Act 2025, post and courier parcels worth Rs 1,000 or less are duty-free. The Finance Bill had proposed Rs 500, and the Act set Rs 1,000. Above that, duty and taxes can apply. A typical Shein order of a few items is often over Rs 1,000, so do not assume it is free of duty. Our guide to customs duty from China to Pakistan explains how it is worked out.

Shein's On-Time Delivery terms also say it does not cover delays caused by a buyer not paying taxes or duties on time. If a courier asks for duty, ask for the customs assessment document before paying.

Can you return Shein items from Pakistan?

Yes, but returns cost you more than on a local site. Shein's Return Policy page, read with the location set to Pakistan, said:

  • Most items can be returned within 30 days of receipt, in new condition with tags attached.
  • Non-returnable items include bodysuits, swimwear, lingerie, underwear, jewellery, beauty items, cosmetics with a broken seal, customised items and items marked non-returnable.
  • Phones, laptops and other electronics must come back sealed and not activated.
  • The return method shown was Self-Return. You pay the return fee to your own courier, and Shein does not reimburse it.
  • A faulty or damaged item should be reported to customer service within 180 days of the order date.
  • Refunds go to your Shein Wallet or your original payment account.

Because a self-return to an overseas warehouse is expensive, buy carefully on size and avoid returning a cheap item. See refunds and returns when importing from China.

Is a "Shein Pakistan" page on Facebook or Instagram official?

Treat it as a reseller, not Shein. Shein sells to Pakistan through its own app and website. Pages that take orders by chat and ask you to send money to a personal JazzCash, Easypaisa or bank account are running their own business, with their own prices and return terms. Some deliver as promised, but Shein's policies do not protect you. Be wary of advance payment requests and prices far below Shein's own site.

Common mistakes when buying from Shein in Pakistan

  • Ordering your usual local size. Measure and use the chart in centimetres.
  • Using a card with international payments switched off. Turn it on in your banking app first, or pay with a wallet.
  • Splitting orders to dodge duty. Customs can treat related parcels as one import.
  • Buying non-returnable items you are unsure about. Swimwear, lingerie and beauty items cannot go back.
  • Missing the claim deadlines. 90 days from payment for non-delivery, 60 days from delivery for missing items.
