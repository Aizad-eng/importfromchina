Documents Required to Import From China to Pakistan (2026)

By Muhammad Shamshad AslamUpdated November 12, 20269 min read
Quick answer

The documents required to import from China to Pakistan depend on the route. A commercial import needs a commercial invoice, packing list, bill of lading or airway bill, your NTN, usually an STRN, a bank financial instrument, a goods declaration and any permit the product needs. A personal courier parcel usually needs only your CNIC and an invoice or order receipt.

New to importing? Read our step-by-step guide on how to import from China to Pakistan.

What documents do you need to import from China to Pakistan?

You need a set of seller documents, a transport document, your own registration numbers and a customs declaration, plus a permit if the product is regulated. Which of them apply depends on whether you import commercially in your own name or receive one parcel through a courier. This guide is about the paperwork only. What does the commercial invoice need to show?

The commercial invoice must show the real price you paid, in the real currency, for the exact goods in the shipment. Customs uses it as the starting point for the customs value, which is the cost of the goods plus freight and insurance to Pakistan. A useful invoice carries:

  • The seller's and buyer's names and addresses, matching the transport document.
  • A clear description of each item, not just "parts" or "gift".
  • Quantity, unit price, total value and currency.
  • The price term, such as EXW, FOB or CIF, so customs knows whether freight is included.
  • The HS code, if the seller provides one. Treat it as a hint, because Pakistan uses its own eight-digit PCT code.
  • The invoice number and date, which you also enter against the financial instrument on PSW.

Do not ask a seller to write a lower value or mark a purchase as a gift. Customs converts the value at National Bank of Pakistan rates, which FBR publishes, and it can use its own valuation rulings instead of your invoice where one applies. An invoice that looks wrong usually means a slower, closer review, not a lower bill.

What is the difference between a packing list, a bill of lading and an airway bill?

The packing list describes what is inside the cartons, while the bill of lading or airway bill is the carrier's record of what it is moving and to whom. You need one transport document for every shipment, and a packing list for most commercial cargo.

  • Packing list. Carton count, contents per carton, gross and net weight and dimensions. It lets customs match the goods to the invoice without opening every box.
  • Bill of lading. Issued for sea freight by the shipping line or forwarder. It names the shipper and consignee, and an original bill of lading can be the title document you need to collect the goods.
  • Airway bill. Issued for air cargo and courier parcels. It is not a title document, but the consignee name on it must match the importer or receiver.

Since 15 August 2026 the consignee details on these documents carry extra weight. ProPakistani reported in June 2026 that under SRO 918(I)/2026 the importer's NTN or FTN must appear in the Import General Manifest, the carrier's arrival list for customs, and that a CNIC or passport number is accepted for small parcel importers and one-time importers without an NTN. Make sure your seller or forwarder puts the right number on the booking.

When do you need a certificate of origin from China?

You need a certificate of origin only when you want the reduced customs duty under the China-Pakistan Free Trade Agreement. Without one, the normal tariff rate applies even to goods made in China.

The CPFTA has its own certificate format, listed as Annex II of the agreement on China's Ministry of Commerce FTA site. The exporter applies for it in China, and it is issued by an authorised body, commonly the China Council for the Promotion of International Trade. Three points matter:

  • It must be the preferential CPFTA certificate. A general certificate of origin or a line on the invoice saying "made in China" does not give the lower rate.
  • The goods, HS code and quantities on it must match the invoice and the goods declaration.
  • It reduces customs duty only on listed tariff lines. Sales tax and income tax still apply.

What is the goods declaration and who files it?

The goods declaration is the formal statement to Pakistan Customs of what you are importing, its value, its tariff code and the duties due. Every import has one. On Pakistan Single Window it is called the Single Declaration, and PSW's own page lists what goes into it: consignment information, commodity lines, financial instrument details such as currency, delivery term, bank, IBAN and mode of payment, uploaded documents, and finally a system-generated payment slip ID for the duties.

A registered importer or a licensed customs agent files it for commercial cargo. For a courier parcel, the courier or its clearing agent files it under its own registration. Our guide to WeBOC and the goods declaration walks through each part.

Why do NTN and STRN count as import documents?

They identify you as the importer of record, and without them a commercial import cannot be declared in your name. PSW's subscription page lists an NTN, FTN or STRN as the first requirement to subscribe, and you cannot file a Single Declaration without a subscription.

  • NTN. FBR says that for individuals the 13-digit CNIC is used as the NTN once you e-enrol on the IRIS portal. A company or partnership gets its own number.
  • STRN. Your sales tax registration. Most commercial importers hold one, but whether you must depends on your goods and business, so confirm with a tax adviser.
  • PSW subscription. When we checked in November 2026, PSW charged a one-time, non-refundable Rs 500 fee, required biometric verification at a NADRA e-Sahulat centre and kept the user ID valid for two years.

Which products need an NOC or permit before release?

Goods on the restricted list of the Import Policy Order, or under another regulator's rules, need that regulator's permission as well as customs clearance. Paying duty alone does not release them. PSW calls these licences, permits, certificates and other documents LPCOs, and says applying for them through the Single Declaration is mandatory where they apply. Common ones for goods from China:

  • DRAP for medicines and medical devices. For personal use, DRAP takes NOC applications from the patient or applicant on its online portal.
  • PSQCA for goods under compulsory standards. PSW handles PSQCA release orders online, and PSQCA can ask for more documents or a laboratory check before it releases the goods.
  • PTA for phones and other telecom equipment. Commercial importers need PTA type approval and a certificate of compliance. PTA's 2021 regulations, as reported by ProPakistani, allow a person five mobile devices for personal use in a calendar year.
  • Plant, animal and narcotics controls, which have their own parts in Appendix B of the Import Policy Order.

Our explainer on the Import Policy Order shows how to check whether your product is on these lists.

What documents does a personal courier parcel need?

Very few. For one item you buy for yourself, the courier or clearing agent prepares the declaration, and you are usually asked for your CNIC and sometimes for the invoice or order receipt that proves the value. You do not need an NTN, an STRN, a PSW account or a financial instrument. Our guide on whether you need an NTN for one item covers this in detail.

The value still matters. Under section 19C of the Customs Act, as amended by the Finance Act 2025, post and courier parcels worth Rs 1,000 or less pay no duties or taxes. Above that, the whole value is dutiable and the courier collects what customs assesses. If the parcel looks like trade rather than personal use, for example many identical units, FBR treats it as a commercial quantity, and commercial paperwork follows.

How should you keep and match your documents?

Keep every document for a shipment together and make sure the same names, numbers and values appear on all of them. Most document delays come from mismatches, not missing paper. Check these before the goods leave China:

  • The consignee name on the bill of lading or airway bill matches your NTN or CNIC record.
  • The invoice value, packing list quantities and declaration lines agree.
  • The certificate of origin, if used, names the same goods and HS code.
  • The permit or NOC covers the exact product and quantity shipped.
  • Your bank's financial instrument carries the same invoice number and currency.

Frequently asked questions

What documents are needed to import goods from China to Pakistan?

For a commercial import: commercial invoice, packing list, bill of lading or airway bill, NTN, usually an STRN, a bank financial instrument, the goods declaration and any permit the product needs.

Do I need a certificate of origin to import from China?

Only to claim the reduced CPFTA customs duty. It must be the preferential China-Pakistan FTA certificate, matching the invoice and declaration. Without it, the normal tariff rate applies.

What documents does a courier need for a personal parcel from China?

Usually your CNIC and, if asked, the invoice or order receipt. The courier or its clearing agent files the declaration, so you need no NTN or PSW account.

Is a bill of lading the same as an airway bill?

No. A bill of lading covers sea freight and can be a title document. An airway bill covers air cargo and courier parcels and is a receipt and contract, not a title document.

Does my NTN have to appear on the shipping documents?

For commercial cargo, yes. Under SRO 918(I)/2026, from 15 August 2026 the importer's NTN or FTN goes in the import manifest, with CNIC or passport accepted where no NTN is required.

Which goods need an NOC to import into Pakistan?

Medicines and medical devices need DRAP permission, goods under compulsory standards need a PSQCA release order, and phones need PTA approval. Check the Import Policy Order for your product.
