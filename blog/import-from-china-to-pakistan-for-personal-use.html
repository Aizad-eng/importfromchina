Importing From China to Pakistan for Personal Use: Rules and Routes

By Muhammad Shamshad AslamUpdated November 12, 20267 min read
Quick answer

You can import goods from China to Pakistan for personal use with just your CNIC. No NTN or import licence is needed, but customs duty and sales tax apply once a parcel is worth more than Rs 1,000. Phones need PTA registration, medicines need DRAP approval and counterfeits are seized. You can order direct, use a courier, or use a single-item service.

New to importing? Read our step-by-step guide on how to import from China to Pakistan. Also see: Customs duty from China to Pakistan

Can you import from China to Pakistan for personal use?

Yes. Pakistani law lets anyone receive goods from China for their own use, and you need only a CNIC. The courier, Pakistan Post or the service that handles the parcel files the customs declaration for you. You do not need a company, an NTN, an import licence or a Pakistan Single Window account.

Personal use does not mean duty free, and it does not mean any item is allowed. The rules below are the ones that apply to a personal import. For the wider process, including the commercial route, see how to import from China to Pakistan.

What does Pakistan Customs accept as personal use?

Customs accepts goods as personal when they do not look imported for trading or gain. That is how FBR's travel guide defines the opposite case, commercial quantity: goods imported prima facie for trading or pecuniary gain and not for personal use or gift. There is no fixed number of items.

One item, or a few different items for yourself or your family, reads as personal. A tool you use for your own work usually does too. Many identical units, or an invoice addressed to a shop, does not. A few examples help. A laptop for yourself, a pair of shoes in your size and a spare charger together read as personal. Two of the same air purifier for two rooms in one house usually does too. Ten identical purifiers do not, whatever you plan to do with them. Our guide to personal use versus commercial import covers the borderline cases.

How do you receive a personal parcel by courier?

You show your CNIC, and the name on the parcel should match it. An express courier usually pays the duty and taxes to customs on your behalf, then collects them from you, with its own handling charge, before it hands over the parcel. Keep the order confirmation and proof of payment, because if customs questions the value, those are what you show.

How much duty do you pay on a personal item?

You pay the same tariff rates as anyone else. There is no lower personal rate and no flat courier rate. Customs works out the value from the price plus shipping and insurance, converts it at the exchange rate FBR publishes, and may apply a valuation ruling that sets a minimum value for some goods.

When we checked in November 2026, customs duty ran in slabs of 0, 5, 10, 15 and 20%, with higher rates on some goods. Additional customs duty applied on some slabs, regulatory duty only on listed goods and capped at 20% from 1 July 2026, and sales tax at 18% on the duty-paid value. The only exception is the section 19C waiver for post or courier parcels worth Rs 1,000 or less. Our customs duty guide shows a worked calculation.

A simple illustration shows how the taxes stack. Take an item customs values at Rs 20,000 in a 10% duty slab. Customs duty is Rs 2,000. Sales tax at 18% is charged on Rs 22,000, which is Rs 3,960. That already adds Rs 5,960 before any additional customs duty, regulatory duty or advance income tax that may apply to that code. The real figure depends on the tariff code, so treat this as the shape of the sum, not a quote.

Which personal items need extra approval?

  • Phones. Register with PTA through DIRBS and pay the tax on the model. You can check a device's status by sending its 15-digit IMEI to 8484.
  • Medicines and supplements. These need DRAP approval, even in small amounts.
  • Drones. These need prior permission.
  • Batteries and liquids. Loose lithium batteries, power banks, aerosols and perfumes are restricted on flights. Devices with a built-in battery usually ship fine.
  • Branded goods at replica prices. Counterfeits are prohibited and can be seized, personal use or not.

A useful order of checks before you choose a route: first ask whether the item is allowed and needs a permit, then where it is sold, then how soon you need it. The permit question rules routes out. The site and the deadline choose between the rest.

Can you bring personal items from China in your luggage?

Yes, as long as you declare what is above your allowance and the goods are not in commercial quantity. FBR's travel guide says goods brought in commercial quantities are released only on payment of a fine equal to 30% of their value in addition to duty and taxes. For a few items for yourself, a traveller's baggage is a reasonable route if someone you trust is already coming. Phones brought this way still need PTA registration.

Can someone in China send you a personal gift?

Yes. A gift is treated as personal, but it is still dutiable if it is worth more than Rs 1,000. The sender should declare it honestly as a gift with its real value. Writing a lower value to save duty is misdeclaration, and customs can reassess it anyway. The same restricted-item rules apply to gifts, so a relative cannot send a phone, medicines or a drone as a gift to get around PTA, DRAP or permission rules. Ask the sender to keep the receipt, because you may need the value if customs asks.

What mistakes do people make with personal imports?

  • Assuming personal use means no duty.
  • Ordering a phone and forgetting the PTA step.
  • Buying supplements or medicines without checking DRAP rules.
  • Ordering from Taobao or 1688 and expecting direct delivery to Pakistan.
  • Buying a branded item at a fraction of its price.
  • Ordering many identical units and calling them personal.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a licence to import for personal use in Pakistan?

No. A personal parcel clears on your CNIC through the courier, Pakistan Post or a service. Licences and registrations are for commercial imports.

Is there duty on personal items from China?

Yes, once the parcel is worth more than Rs 1,000. Personal items pay the same tariff rates as any import, plus 18% sales tax.

Can I import a phone from China for personal use?

Yes, but it must be registered with PTA through DIRBS and the tax on the model paid, or it will be blocked on Pakistani networks.

Can I import supplements for personal use?

Medicines and supplements need DRAP approval, even in small amounts, so check the DRAP rules before you order.

Who pays customs on a courier parcel?

The receiver. An express courier usually pays customs on your behalf and collects the amount, plus a handling charge, before delivery.

Is a gift from China duty free?

Only if it is worth Rs 1,000 or less. Above that, a gift pays normal duty and taxes on its declared value.
