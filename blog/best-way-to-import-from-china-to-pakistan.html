What Is the Best Way to Import From China to Pakistan?

By Muhammad Shamshad AslamUpdated November 12, 20267 min read
Quick answer

The best way to import from China to Pakistan depends on four things: value, size, urgency and quantity. Small cheap items are best bought on a marketplace that ships here, or locally. One item worth Rs 10,000 or more is usually best through a single-item service. Heavy goods go by air cargo or sea, and quantities to sell need the commercial route.

What is the best way to import from China to Pakistan?

There is no single best way. There is a best way for your item, and four questions find it: what is it worth, how big and heavy is it, how soon do you need it, and how many do you need. Most people pick a route first and then find it does not suit the item. Start with the item instead.

Three examples show how the answers change the route. A cheap, light phone stand is best from a marketplace that ships here, or a local shop. An audio interface worth well over Rs 10,000 and sold only on Taobao needs someone in China to buy it, so a single-item service fits. A 120 kg workshop machine in a crate is a freight job, so air cargo or sea through a forwarder is the realistic choice. Same country, three different best routes.

How does the item's value change the best route?

Value decides whether importing makes sense at all. Section 19C of the Customs Act, as amended in 2025, waives duties and taxes only on post or courier parcels worth Rs 1,000 or less. Above that, duty and 18% sales tax apply to the whole value.

The bigger factor for cheap items is shipping. Getting one item from China to a Pakistani address usually costs Rs 5,000 to 20,000 depending on size and type. A Rs 2,000 item rarely survives that, unless a marketplace like Temu absorbs the shipping. For items worth around Rs 10,000 or more, the shipping becomes a reasonable share and a single-item service starts to make sense.

How do size and weight change the best route?

Size often matters more than weight, because carriers charge by volumetric weight when a parcel is bulky. Express couriers such as DHL, FedEx and UPS divide length times width times height in centimetres by 5,000. Air cargo divides by 6,000. You pay on whichever is higher, the actual weight or the volumetric weight.

A 50 x 40 x 30 cm box holding a 4 kg item shows the effect. By express it counts as 12 kg. By air cargo it counts as 10 kg. Either way you pay for far more than 4 kg. Large crated goods, such as machines, often cost less by sea, which is slower but priced by volume in a shared container.

What is the best route if you need the item urgently?

Express courier is the fastest, commonly 5 to 10 days door to door, but it is the most expensive per kilogram and the courier collects duty and its handling charge before delivery. Consolidated air freight usually lands in 7 to 15 days. Post and budget marketplace shipping take weeks.

Timing in China matters too. Chinese New Year closes many factories and logistics firms for roughly two to four weeks between late January and mid February, and shipping slows after the 11.11 and 618 sales. If you need something by a fixed date, order well before those windows.

What is the best route if you need more than one?

For a few different items for your own use, combine them in one parcel. You pay one shipment instead of several, and each item is assessed at its own duty rate.

For many identical units, the route changes. FBR defines a commercial quantity as goods imported for trading or gain rather than personal use or gift, and customs judges that by quantity and type. Large quantities belong with a cargo company that ships by weight, or on the commercial route in your own name, which needs an NTN and a Pakistan Single Window subscription. We do not handle that kind of order.

Does it matter which Chinese site the item is on?

Yes, because the site decides whether the seller will ship to you and how you can pay. Temu and AliExpress ship to Pakistan directly and take cards. ProPakistani reported in July 2025 that AliExpress stopped offering its budget routes for Pakistan, so the shipping options at checkout may be fewer than before. Alibaba suppliers arrange international shipping, and some list single pieces as Ready to Ship, but courier rates for one piece can be high. Taobao, 1688, Xianyu and JD.com sellers ship within China only, so you need someone there to buy and forward the item.

Does the item need permission before you pick a route?

Check this before anything else, because a permit problem overrides every other choice. Phones must be registered with PTA, medicines and supplements need DRAP approval, and drones need prior permission. Loose lithium batteries, power banks, aerosols and perfumes are restricted on flights, so many air routes refuse them. Counterfeit branded goods are prohibited and seized. What mistakes do people make when choosing an import route?

  • Choosing by item price alone and ignoring shipping, which can be more than the item.
  • Forgetting volumetric weight on a light but bulky box.
  • Ordering from Taobao or 1688 and expecting it to ship to Pakistan.
  • Assuming a marketplace parcel has no duty. Above Rs 1,000 it is dutiable.
  • Ordering just before Chinese New Year and needing it by a date.
  • Sending many identical units through a personal route.

Frequently asked questions

What is the easiest way to import from China to Pakistan?

For one item, buy from a marketplace that ships here if it has what you want. If the item is only on Taobao, 1688 or a factory site, use a single-item service.

Is air or sea better for importing from China?

Air is better for small and urgent items, commonly 7 to 15 days. Sea is usually cheaper for large crated goods but takes commonly 25 to 45 days in a shared container.

Is it worth importing a cheap item from China?

Usually not. Shipping one item commonly costs Rs 5,000 to 20,000, so cheap items make sense only on marketplaces that include shipping, or bought locally.

What is the fastest way to get something from China?

Express courier, commonly 5 to 10 days door to door. It is also the most expensive per kilogram, and duty is collected before delivery.

What is the best way to import many units?

Through a cargo company or the commercial route in your own name with an NTN and a Pakistan Single Window account. Single-item services do not handle quantity orders.

Does the best route change for phones or medicines?

Yes. Phones need PTA registration and medicines need DRAP approval, so check those rules first. Some routes refuse restricted goods entirely.
