Shein Alternatives in Pakistan: Where Else to Buy

By Muhammad Shamshad AslamUpdated November 11, 20267 min read
Quick answer

The main Shein alternatives for buyers in Pakistan are Temu for cheap everyday and fashion items with free shipping and easier returns, Daraz for fast local delivery with cash on delivery, AliExpress for the widest catalogue and specific brands, Alibaba.com for single pieces from manufacturers, and Taobao or 1688 through a buying service for things none of them carry. The best choice depends on speed, how you want to pay and whether you might need to return it.

Why look for a Shein alternative in Pakistan?

Shein works in Pakistan. When we checked on 11 November 2026, its Standard Shipping to Pakistan took 7 to 11 business days after 1 to 3 days of processing, cost US$1.08 below US$14.43 and was free above it, and JazzCash and Easypaisa were accepted. People still look elsewhere for a few reasons:

  • Returns. Shein's only return method for Pakistan is Self-Return, with postage at your cost.
  • No cash on delivery. Every Shein order is paid in full at checkout.
  • Speed. Two to three weeks is too long when you need something for an event this week.
  • Range. Shein is mostly fashion and home. For gadgets, tools or a specific brand, it is the wrong shop.
  • Delivery area. Shein says that if you cannot type your address at checkout, delivery is not supported there.

Is Temu a good Shein alternative?

For most people, it is the closest one. Temu runs a Pakistan site at temu.com/pk-en with prices in rupees, and its Pakistan shipping page listed free standard shipping on all orders, in 5 to 13 business days after 1 to 3 days of processing, when we checked in October 2026. Its clothing range is large, and it adds home, kitchen and gadget items Shein does not focus on.

Returns are the big difference. Temu's Pakistan policy allows returns within 90 days of purchase, and the first return from each order is free, with the courier collecting from your door. Shein gives 30 days and you pay the postage. On payment, Temu takes international cards, and JazzCash and Easypaisa have appeared and disappeared from its checkout, so check yours. Our Shein vs Temu comparison goes line by line.

When is Daraz the better choice?

When you want it fast, want to pay cash on delivery, or want an easy return. Daraz is a Pakistani marketplace, so much of its stock is already in the country. Its return policy says a return request must be raised within 14 days of delivery. Change-of-mind returns apply to some categories, such as clothing and shoes, but not to items shipped from overseas outside DarazMall, and cash-on-delivery orders are refunded to your Daraz Wallet.

Many Daraz sellers stock items from the same Chinese suppliers as Shein and Temu, with a local margin added. For cheap clothes you might return, that margin often buys you peace of mind.

Is AliExpress worth it instead of Shein?

For a specific product, brand or accessory, yes. AliExpress has the widest catalogue of the Chinese sites that ship to Pakistan, including official brand stores. For everyday clothes it is usually slower and harder than Shein. Payment needs a Visa or Mastercard with international use switched on, since JazzCash and Easypaisa are not accepted there, and delivery by post commonly takes 25 to 45 days. Read how to buy from AliExpress in Pakistan first.

What about Alibaba.com, Taobao and 1688?

Alibaba.com is a business marketplace, but some suppliers sell Ready to Ship items with a minimum order of one piece. It suits equipment, tools and parts more than fashion, and courier shipping for one piece is often close to the item price.

Taobao and 1688 are China's domestic platforms. They have far more choice than Shein, including small brands, rare sizes and better grades of the same item, but they take Chinese payment only and ship within China. To buy there from Pakistan you need a buying service with a Chinese account and address. See how to buy from Taobao in Pakistan.

What about Facebook pages selling "Shein Pakistan" items?

They are resellers, not Shein. Some deliver what they promise and offer cash on delivery, but they set their own prices and return rules, and Shein's policies do not protect you. Never send money to a personal JazzCash, Easypaisa or bank account for a "Shein" order. If a page's price is far below Shein's own site, be careful.

Which Shein alternative should you pick?

  • Cheap clothes and home items, with easy returns: Temu.
  • Needed this week, or cash on delivery: Daraz.
  • A specific product, part or brand: AliExpress.
  • Equipment or a single piece from a factory: Alibaba.com.
  • Something none of these carry: Taobao or 1688 through a service.
