Importing a Car Android Panel (Head Unit) From China to Pakistan

By Muhammad Shamshad AslamUpdated November 12, 20269 min read
Quick answer

Yes, you can import a single car Android panel from China to Pakistan, and it often makes sense for a specific screen size, frame or chipset that local shops do not stock. Match the frame and harness to your exact car, check the chipset and memory, avoid relying on a built-in SIM, and expect duty plus Rs 5,000 to 20,000 shipping.

New to importing? Read our step-by-step guide on how to import from China to Pakistan.

Can you import one car Android panel from China to Pakistan?

Yes. A car Android panel, also called an Android head unit or Android screen, is an ordinary consumer electronic item and a single unit can be imported for your own car. Alibaba, AliExpress and 1688 list a far wider range of sizes, frames and chipsets than local shops carry. If you are new to the process, our guide on how to import from China to Pakistan covers the basics first.

The reason to import is choice, not a guaranteed saving. Shops in Pakistan sell many generic panels already. Importing earns its keep when you want a particular chipset, a frame made for your exact model and year, a larger or higher resolution screen, or a known brand that is not stocked here.

What kinds of car panels do people import?

Most buyers want one of four things. The first is a plain double-DIN style panel, commonly 7 inch, for older cars with a standard radio opening. The second is a model-specific 9 or 10 inch panel that comes with a plastic frame shaped for one car, such as a particular Corolla, City, Civic or Suzuki generation. The third is a premium unit with a stronger chipset, more memory and a better audio processor. The fourth is an add-on, such as a reversing camera or a 360 degree camera kit, bought to match a panel already fitted.

If your car already has a factory screen with wired CarPlay or Android Auto, you may not need a new panel at all. A small wireless adapter can be enough, as our guide on the Ottocast U2Air Edge explains.

Which specs should you compare on a listing?

Compare the chipset, memory, screen and audio first, because the outside of most panels looks the same. Sellers often lead with a big number like "8 core" without saying which chip it is. Ask for the exact chipset name in writing.

Will the panel fit your car?

A panel fits only if three parts match: the frame, the wiring harness and, on newer cars, the CAN bus decoder. Getting one of these wrong is the most common reason a panel sits unused.

  • Frame (fascia). Model-specific panels come with a frame for one car and one range of years. Give the seller your car's make, model, year and whether it is a Pakistani-assembled or Japanese-import version, because dashboards differ.
  • Harness. The harness plugs into your car's original radio connectors. A universal harness means cutting and joining wires, which is more work for your installer.
  • CAN bus decoder. On many newer cars, steering wheel buttons, the reversing signal and some displays talk to the radio over CAN bus. The listing should name the decoder for your car.
  • Right-hand drive. Pakistan drives on the left with right-hand-drive cars. Some frames are made for left-hand-drive versions of the same model, so confirm the frame is for a right-hand-drive car.
  • Voltage. Car panels are built for 12V systems. If you drive a 24V truck or bus, ask the seller in writing whether the unit supports it.

Send the seller clear pictures of your current dashboard and the back of the old radio if you can. That one message prevents most fitting mistakes.

Do CarPlay and Android Auto work in Pakistan?

They usually connect, but neither is officially supported in Pakistan. When we checked in November 2026, Apple's iOS feature availability page did not list Pakistan under CarPlay, and Google's Android Auto help page did not list Pakistan among its countries. Google's page says most features won't work outside the listed countries. In practice, that means you should not buy a panel only for CarPlay or Android Auto without accepting some risk, and you should not expect either company to help if something misbehaves.

The phone also matters. Google's help page lists Android 11 or newer, an active data plan and, for wireless use, a phone with 5 GHz Wi-Fi. The good news is that an Android panel runs its own apps, so Google Maps, YouTube Music and similar apps can run on the panel itself even without projection.

What about panels with a 4G SIM slot?

Treat the SIM slot as a bonus you may not be able to use. PTA's DIRBS system covers any SIM-based device, which its FAQ describes as dongles, phones, smart watches, tablets and similar devices. A device with a non-standard IMEI is blocked after one day of first use on a Pakistani network, according to the same FAQ. A device with a valid but unregistered IMEI can be registered by paying duties and taxes within 60 days of first local use.

Many cheap panels do not come with a clear, valid IMEI, and registering one is a separate process with its own cost. The simple route is to run the panel on your phone's hotspot. If you do want to use a SIM, ask the seller for the IMEI before you pay and check it by sending it by SMS to 8484.

How is a car panel taxed at Pakistan customs?

A car panel is taxed like other electronics: duty and taxes on the customs value, according to its PCT code. The customs value is the CIF value, meaning the item plus freight and insurance, converted at the NBP rates published by FBR. When we checked in November 2026, the main charges were:

  • Customs duty in slabs of 0, 5, 10, 15 or 20 percent depending on the PCT code, with some goods higher.
  • Additional customs duty of 0 percent on the 0 to 15 percent slabs and 2 percent on the 20 percent slab, under the rates in force from 1 July 2026.
  • Regulatory duty on listed goods only, capped at 20 percent from 1 July 2026 under SRO 1064(I)/2026.
  • Sales tax of 18 percent on the value plus duties, and advance income tax under section 148, which is 1, 2 or 5.5 percent for filers depending on the goods and doubled for non-filers.

Section 19C lets post and courier parcels worth Rs 1,000 or less through without duties or taxes, but no car panel is that cheap. Our customs duty guide walks through a full calculation.

Is it worth the shipping cost?

It is worth it for a mid-range or premium panel, and rarely for the cheapest ones. Shipping one item from China to Pakistan usually costs Rs 5,000 to 20,000 depending on size and type. A boxed 9 or 10 inch panel with a frame and harness is light but bulky, and air carriers charge by volumetric weight when a box is large for its weight. Add a camera kit or a large frame and the box grows. For that reason the service works best for items worth around Rs 10,000 or more. A basic 7 inch unit that costs little in China may end up close to a local shop price once shipping and duty are added.

What are the common mistakes?

  • Ordering the panel without the frame and harness for your exact model and year.
  • Choosing by "8 core" and memory numbers without a named chipset.
  • Assuming wireless CarPlay or Android Auto is built in because the listing image shows the logo. Ask whether it is wired, wireless or both.
  • Planning around the SIM slot without checking the IMEI.
  • Forgetting that a new panel may not talk to your old reversing camera.
  • Skipping the CAN bus decoder on a car that needs one, then losing steering wheel controls.

Frequently asked questions

Will a Chinese Android panel fit my Suzuki or Toyota?

Usually, if you order the frame and harness made for your exact model and year. Tell the seller whether your car is Pakistani-assembled or a Japanese import, because dashboards differ.

Does CarPlay work on an Android head unit in Pakistan?

It usually connects, but Apple did not list Pakistan under CarPlay when we checked in November 2026, so it is unsupported here. Do not buy a panel only for CarPlay.

Can I use a SIM card in my car Android panel?

Only if the unit has a valid IMEI that you register through PTA's DIRBS. Non-standard IMEIs are blocked after one day of use, so most people use the phone's hotspot instead.

How much RAM should a car Android panel have?

Look for at least two clear numbers, such as 4 + 64 GB, and a named chipset. Low memory makes maps and music apps reload, which is annoying while driving.

Will my old reversing camera work with the new panel?

Not always. Newer panels often expect AHD cameras. Check the panel's camera input in the listing, or order a matching camera with it.

Is customs duty charged on a car panel?

Yes. Duty, sales tax of 18 percent and advance income tax are charged on the customs value. Only parcels worth Rs 1,000 or less are exempt under section 19C.
