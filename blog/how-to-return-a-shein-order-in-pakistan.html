How to Return a Shein Order in Pakistan (and When It Is Worth It)

By Muhammad Shamshad AslamUpdated November 11, 20267 min read
Quick answer

Shein accepts returns within 30 days of receipt for most items in new condition, with tags and hygiene stickers intact. You start the return in My Orders, but the only return method shown for Pakistan is Self-Return: you send the parcel through your own courier and Shein does not refund the postage. International postage from Pakistan can cost more than a cheap item, so check the cost first. Faulty or damaged items are different: report them to customer service within 180 days of the order date.

Can you return a Shein order from Pakistan?

Yes, but it costs you. Shein's Return Policy, as served to us from Pakistan on 11 November 2026, accepts returns "within 30 days of receipt for most items in new condition". The only return method listed was Self-Return: you pay the return fee directly to your courier, and the policy says Shein does "Not reimburse the costs".

Sending a parcel abroad from Pakistan is not cheap, so a return often makes sense only for a higher-priced item. Before you start, get a quote from a courier for sending the parcel to the return address in your return request, and compare it with what you would get back.

What can you not return to Shein?

Shein's policy lists these as non-returnable:

  • Bodysuits, swimwear, lingerie, underwear, jewellery and beauty items.
  • Event and party supplies, DIY supplies, pet supplies and accessories, except scarves, bags and mermaid blankets.
  • Cosmetics with a broken seal.
  • Items marked as non-returnable on the listing.
  • Free gifts, depending on their status at payment.
  • Customised items, which also cannot be cancelled.

For items you can return, the condition rules are strict:

  • Clothing: new, unworn, unwashed, unaltered, with original tags attached and any hygiene sticker intact.
  • Shoes and bags: unworn, in the original branded box with all accessories. Shoes may only have been tried on a soft, clean surface.
  • Electronics: sealed and not activated, with personal data erased, in the original packaging.
  • Bundles: every item in the set goes back together.

How do you return a Shein order, step by step?

These are the steps in Shein's Return Policy:

  1. Sign in to your Shein account.
  2. Go to My Orders and open the order with the item.
  3. Tap "(Combined) Return", select the item or items, give the reason and tap "Next Step".
  4. Choose the Return Method. For Pakistan we saw only Self-Return.
  5. Choose the Refund Method, your Shein Wallet or the original payment account, and tap "Submit".
  6. Find the return label: My Orders, then Returns, then Return & Refund Record, then Details, then "Save the Label".
  7. Pack the items, attach the label, and send the parcel with your courier. Keep the receipt and tracking number.

Two warnings from the policy. Do not send the parcel to the sender address printed on your original package, because that is not the return address and it can affect your refund. And if you reuse the original bag, remove or cover the old shipping label so the parcel is not sent back to you.

The policy also says that in some cases Shein may, at its sole discretion, refund an item without asking you to send it back. You cannot choose this.

How long does a Shein refund take?

Shein's policy gives this timeline once you send the parcel:

StageTime on Shein's policy
Return shipment to Shein's warehouse5 to 7 days
Warehouse receipt and quality check5 to 7 days
Shein issues the refund1 day
Your bank or wallet processes itUsually 1 to 10 business days

From Pakistan the first stage can take longer than 5 to 7 days, depending on the courier you use. If the item fails the quality check, for example because it looks washed or the tags are gone, the policy says Shein will not refund it.

Should you choose Shein Wallet or the original payment method?

Shein lets you pick either. A refund to the original card goes back through your bank, which converts dollars to rupees at its own rate on the day, so the rupee amount may differ from what you paid. A refund to your Shein Wallet stays in Shein as credit, which only helps if you will order again. If you paid by JazzCash or Easypaisa, check which refund options the return screen offers for that payment.

What if the item is faulty, damaged, wrong or missing?

That is not a normal return, and you should not pay to post it back first. Contact Shein customer service instead:

  • Faulty or damaged item: within 180 days of the order date, according to the Return Policy. Shein says it will investigate and offer a solution, including a refund if applicable.
  • Missing or wrong items, or a parcel marked delivered that you never got: within 60 days of the delivery date shown in tracking, according to the Shipping Info page.

Take photos of the parcel, the label and the item as soon as you open it. They are your evidence.

How can you avoid needing a return at all?

Most Shein returns from Pakistan are about size. Measure a garment you already own and compare it with the size chart on the listing in centimetres, and read reviews where buyers give their height and weight.
