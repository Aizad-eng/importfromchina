PTA Tax on Phones Imported From China (2026)

By Muhammad Shamshad AslamUpdated October 23, 20269 min read
Quick answer

A phone imported from China must be registered on PTA's DIRBS system and its tax paid, or it stops working on Pakistani networks after 60 days. The tax is set by FBR, not PTA. It rises in slabs with the dollar value customs uses for your model, and differs between passport and CNIC registration. A phone shipped to you is normally registered on your CNIC.

Do phones imported from China need PTA registration?

Yes. Every phone used on a Pakistani mobile network must be registered on PTA's Device Identification Registration and Blocking System, known as DIRBS. PTA's DIRBS FAQs say a device works for 60 days from the day a SIM is first inserted. After that, an unregistered phone stops receiving mobile service. Wi-Fi keeps working, but calls, SMS and mobile data do not.

The FAQs also say every device that is registered is subject to the relevant FBR duties. Registering is how you pay them. That is what people call the PTA tax.

How does DIRBS registration work?

You register online, by USSD code or at an operator's franchise. When we checked in October 2026, the DIRBS site listed three channels: the registration portal at dirbs.pta.gov.pk/drs, the USSD code *8484#, and franchise centres of the mobile operators. The steps are:

  1. Dial *#06# on the phone to see its IMEI. A dual SIM phone has two IMEIs.
  2. Check the status by sending each IMEI by SMS to 8484, or using the check box on the DIRBS homepage.
  3. Insert your SIM. The 60-day period starts from that day.
  4. Apply on DIRBS with your CNIC, or passport if you qualify, the SIM number and the IMEIs.
  5. Pay the duty against the PSID you receive. PTA's FAQs say the PSID is valid for 7 days from the date you apply.

PTA's FAQs say an individual can register up to five devices in a calendar year. Non-compliant devices, such as phones with an invalid or cloned IMEI, are treated differently. The FAQs say a non-compliant device can be blocked after one day of first use on a Pakistani network.

Who sets the PTA tax?

FBR does. PTA's FAQs put it plainly: tax and duty imposition is FBR's directive. PTA runs the registration system and collects through it, but the amounts come from FBR's customs and tax law. The same FAQs say all individuals pay these duties, whether they are tax filers or not.

That matters when you read news about the PTA tax. A change to the PTA tax is really a change in a budget, an SRO or a customs valuation ruling.

How is the tax worked out?

By value slab, not by a percentage of what you paid the seller. The duty for a phone depends on which US dollar value band it falls in, and different parts of the charge are set per band. The bands used in 2026 reporting are: up to $30, $30 to $100, $100 to $200, $200 to $350, $350 to $500, and above $500.

Within each band, the total is built from several charges. When we checked in October 2026, reporting described these parts:

  • Regulatory duty, a fixed rupee amount per band.
  • Additional customs duty, a percentage.
  • Sales tax. ProPakistani reported in April 2026 that FBR officials told a Finance Committee there was no scope to cut the existing 18% GST on phones, while other 2026 reports describe a higher rate on premium phones. Check the FBR figure for your model rather than assuming a rate.
  • Withholding income tax, which the same report put at roughly Rs 11,500 on expensive devices.

The same April 2026 report said phones above $500 faced around Rs 76,000 in total tax, about 54% of value. That was before the 2026-27 budget changes below, so use it only to understand the scale.

What changed in the 2026-27 budget?

Regulatory duty and additional customs duty on imported phones came down, while sales tax was not cut, according to 2026 reports. ProPakistani reported on 10 September 2026, citing a Commerce Ministry brief, that regulatory duty on fully built smartphones was cut by about 20% in every band for FY2026-27.

Passport or CNIC: which applies to a phone shipped from China?

Normally the CNIC. The passport route is for people who bring phones with them from abroad. PTA's FAQs describe passport registration for international travellers who brought devices while travelling to Pakistan, and a separate temporary registration of 120 days from arrival for overseas Pakistanis and foreign visitors.

Passport and CNIC registration carry different amounts in each band, with passport generally lower, according to 2026 reports from Pakistani news sites. Because the passport route depends on your own arrival in Pakistan, a phone that reaches you by courier from a Chinese seller does not fit it. Plan your sums on the CNIC amount.

How is the dollar value of your phone decided?

By customs, not by your receipt. For used phones, FBR's Directorate General of Customs Valuation sets values by model in valuation rulings. ProPakistani reported in April 2026 that Valuation Ruling No. 2076 of 2026 set new values for used phones of 62 brands, regardless of condition, after an earlier 2026 ruling was rescinded. It listed values such as $225 for a used iPhone 13.

So a phone you bought cheaply on 1688 can still land in a higher band if the assessed value for that model is higher. These rulings changed more than once in 2026, which is why one person's PTA amount for a model may not match yours. Our guide to how customs valuation rulings work explains the system.

How do you check the exact amount before you buy?

Use the official tools, not calculator sites. If the seller can share the IMEI before dispatch, check its status on DIRBS first, so you know it is a valid number before the phone leaves China. Then look up the duty. The DIRBS homepage links to an FBR page called Mobile Device Duty Information on WeBOC, where FBR shows duties for individual devices. The PSID you get when you apply on DIRBS shows the final figure you must pay.

Third-party PTA tax calculators disagree with each other by tens of thousands of rupees for the same phone, and many still show figures from before the 2026-27 budget.

Common mistakes with PTA tax

  • Using last year's figure. Duty bands changed for FY2026-27. Check after July 2026.
  • Planning on the passport rate. It is for travellers. A shipped phone normally goes on your CNIC.
  • Letting the 60 days run out. The count starts when the SIM goes in, not when you open the box.
  • Buying a phone with a cloned or patched IMEI. It is illegal, and DIRBS can block it within a day.
  • Ignoring the second IMEI. Dual SIM phones need both registered.

Is it still worth importing a phone from China?

Sometimes. Add the CNIC tax for your model's band to the China price, shipping and customs, then compare with the Pakistani price for the same model with a local warranty. Importing tends to make sense for models not sold here.

The tax system is built to favour local assembly. ProPakistani reported in April 2026 that imported phones were taxed at about 54% of their value against about 25% for locally manufactured or assembled phones. If a locally assembled version of your model exists, it will almost always cost less to use legally.

Frequently asked questions

How much is the PTA tax on a phone from China?

It depends on the dollar value band customs uses for your model and on CNIC or passport registration. Check the exact amount on FBR's Mobile Device Duty Information page or the PSID from DIRBS before you buy.

Is PTA tax set by PTA or FBR?

By FBR. PTA's DIRBS FAQs say tax and duty imposition is FBR's directive. PTA runs the registration system through which you pay.

How long can I use an imported phone before registering it?

60 days from the day a SIM is first inserted, according to PTA's DIRBS FAQs. After that an unregistered phone loses mobile service.

Can I register a phone shipped from China on my passport?

Normally no. The passport route is for travellers who brought the phone with them while travelling to Pakistan. A phone delivered by courier is normally registered on your CNIC.

Did the 2026-27 budget reduce PTA tax?

Partly. Regulatory duty on imported smartphones was cut by about 20% in each value band and additional customs duty fell from 6% to 4%, but sales tax was not cut, according to 2026 reports.

How many phones can I register on DIRBS in a year?

PTA's FAQs say an individual can register up to five devices in a calendar year.
