AliExpress vs Daraz: Which Is Better for Buyers in Pakistan?

By Muhammad Shamshad AslamUpdated October 17, 20269 min read
Quick answer

For most buyers in Pakistan, Daraz is the easier choice: cash on delivery, a delivery date on every listing, 14-day returns with free return shipping, and overseas listings whose price already includes customs fees and import duties. AliExpress has a far bigger catalogue, more specific models and official brand stores, but you usually need an international Visa or Mastercard, delivery takes weeks, and customs may be charged on arrival. Choose AliExpress when Daraz does not have the exact item you want.

AliExpress or Daraz: which is better for buyers in Pakistan?

Daraz is better for convenience and AliExpress is better for choice. If the exact item is on Daraz at a fair price, Daraz is almost always less hassle: local delivery, cash on delivery and returns handled inside Pakistan. If it is not, or only in the wrong version, AliExpress is where the long tail lives.

Below we compare them on price, delivery, payment, returns and customs, based on what each platform's pages and listings showed when we checked in October 2026.

Are AliExpress and Daraz the same company?

They share an owner. Alibaba Group acquired Daraz in May 2018, and AliExpress is also part of Alibaba Group. They are still separate marketplaces with separate accounts, sellers, payment systems and rules.

Daraz is a local marketplace for Pakistan, Bangladesh, Sri Lanka, Nepal and Myanmar. Most of its sellers are in Pakistan, though it also lists items marked as shipping from overseas. AliExpress is a cross-border marketplace where sellers, mostly in China, ship single items to buyers around the world.

Is AliExpress cheaper than Daraz?

On the item price, often yes, because many Daraz sellers buy from the same Chinese suppliers and add their margin, platform commission and return costs. On the final cost, not always. With AliExpress you may add a shipping line, your bank's exchange rate and card charges, and customs duty if the parcel is assessed.

For cheap, common items with many Daraz sellers, competition usually keeps Daraz prices close enough that the saving is not worth a month's wait. AliExpress tends to win on niche goods with few local sellers, such as specific electronics modules, hobby parts, tools and accessories for less common devices. For a broader look, read whether it is cheaper to buy from China or Pakistan.

How long does each take to deliver?

Daraz shows a date range on each listing for your address. On 17 October 2026, with Daraz's default Karachi address, a cable from a seller in Punjab showed delivery by 23 to 26 October. An item marked as shipping from overseas showed 26 October to 1 November.

AliExpress has no single delivery time for Pakistan, because each seller picks its shipping options. A July 2026 guide from WorldFirst puts most orders to Pakistan at 15 to 45 days, depending on the method chosen at checkout. Tracking often goes quiet between leaving China and reaching a Pakistani carrier.

Replacements matter too. Daraz's returns FAQ says a replacement takes 5 to 22 days to reach you, depending on your location. On AliExpress, a replacement for a wrong item usually means a new international shipment, so the full wait starts again.

How do you pay on AliExpress and on Daraz?

Daraz gives you the most choice. Both listings we opened showed cash on delivery, and Daraz's refund policy lists cards, HBL direct transfer, Easypaisa wallet, Easypaisa shops, JazzCash mobile accounts, Daraz Wallet, vouchers and credit card instalments. Orders paid by cash on delivery, JazzCash or Easypaisa shops are refunded to the Daraz Wallet only. Card refunds take 10 working days and Easypaisa wallet refunds 5 working days, counted after Daraz's quality check on the returned item.

On AliExpress you will usually need a Visa or Mastercard with international online payments switched on. Many Pakistani bank cards have this off by default. PayPak cards do not work on foreign sites, and PayPal is not available in Pakistan. Our guide on how to pay Chinese sellers from Pakistan covers the card settings.

Which has better returns and buyer protection?

Daraz is simpler. You must raise a return within 14 days of delivery, and there is no charge to send a product back. Accepted reasons include damaged, defective, incorrect, incomplete and not as described. Change of mind applies only in some categories, such as fashion, and not for phones, tablets, batteries, chargers or earphones. Refund times depend on payment method, for example 10 working days back to a card. Daraz's refund policy says it does not apply to Daraz Global products, so read the return terms on overseas listings.

AliExpress buyer protection covers items that do not match the description or arrive damaged, if you report it within 15 days of receipt, and non-arrival within the guaranteed delivery window on your order page. You open a dispute from the order. For a wrong or faulty item, you may be asked to send it back to China, which from Pakistan is rarely worth the postage for a cheap item.

Who pays customs duty?

On AliExpress, you do, if the parcel is assessed. Duty and taxes are usually collected by Pakistan Post or the courier before release, and you only learn the amount when the parcel arrives. Under section 19C of the Customs Act, as set by the Finance Act 2025, post and courier parcels worth Rs 1,000 or less are duty-free. Above that, duty can apply. See customs duty from China to Pakistan for how it is calculated.

On Daraz, local listings are already in the country. For overseas listings, the product page says the stated price includes customs fees and import duties, so there is nothing extra to pay at the door beyond what the checkout shows.

Daraz overseas listings vs AliExpress: what is the difference?

Both bring an item from abroad, often from China. The differences are in how you pay and who carries the risk.

  • Payment: the Daraz overseas listing we opened offered cash on delivery. AliExpress usually needs a card upfront.
  • Duty: Daraz says the overseas price includes customs and import duties. AliExpress duty, if any, is collected on arrival.
  • Selection: AliExpress has far more listings, variants and brand stores. Daraz overseas is a smaller set.
  • Price: the same product can differ either way. Compare the final total.

When we searched Daraz for niche items such as anime figures and CNC router bits, many results showed the location "Overseas", so check Daraz before going to AliExpress.

Which should you choose for your item?

  • You need it this week or want to pay cash: Daraz, from a local seller.
  • It is a branded item and authenticity matters: Daraz Mall, which Daraz covers with its 2x Money Back Guarantee, or the brand's official store on AliExpress.
  • It is a specific model, part or variant Daraz does not have: AliExpress.
  • The AliExpress listing does not ship to Pakistan: see what to do when an AliExpress item will not ship to Pakistan.

Common mistakes when comparing AliExpress and Daraz

  • Comparing the item price only. Add delivery, card charges and possible duty on the AliExpress side.
  • Assuming a Daraz item is local. Check the location on the listing. Overseas items take longer.
  • Waiting too long to check a Daraz parcel. The 14 days count from delivery.
  • Letting an AliExpress protection window lapse. Open a dispute before the countdown on the order page ends.
  • Buying fake branded goods on either site. Pakistan Customs seizes counterfeits, and low prices on big brands are a warning sign.

Frequently asked questions

Is AliExpress better than Daraz for shopping in Pakistan?

Daraz is usually better for speed, cash on delivery and easy returns. AliExpress is better when you need a specific model, part or brand that Daraz sellers do not stock.

Is Daraz owned by AliExpress?

Not exactly. Both belong to Alibaba Group, which acquired Daraz in May 2018, but they are separate marketplaces with separate accounts and rules.

Can I pay cash on delivery on AliExpress in Pakistan?

Generally no. A 2026 payments guide says cash on delivery is rarely offered on cross-border AliExpress orders, so plan to pay by an international Visa or Mastercard.

Why is the same item cheaper on AliExpress than on Daraz?

Many Daraz sellers buy from the same Chinese suppliers and add their margin, commission and return costs. Once you add AliExpress shipping, card charges and possible duty, the gap often shrinks.

Do Daraz overseas items include customs duty?

Yes, according to Daraz's product page, which says the stated price for items shipped from overseas includes customs fees and import duties.

How long do I have to return a Daraz order?

Daraz says a return request must be raised within 14 days of delivery, and there is no charge to send the product back. Change of mind only applies in some categories.
