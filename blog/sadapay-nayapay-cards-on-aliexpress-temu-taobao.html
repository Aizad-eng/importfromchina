Do SadaPay and NayaPay Cards Work on AliExpress, Temu and Taobao?

By Muhammad Shamshad AslamUpdated October 23, 20269 min read
Quick answer

Partly. SadaPay's help centre names AliExpress as a site where its Mastercard works, and NayaPay says its Visa card works for international online payments wherever Visa is accepted. When we checked in October 2026, neither company confirmed Temu or Taobao by name, so those are not confirmed. Use the virtual card, switch on international payments and expect a card fee plus advance tax.

Do SadaPay and NayaPay cards work on AliExpress, Temu and Taobao?

Some of the time, and only AliExpress is confirmed by name. SadaPay's own help centre lists AliExpress as a platform where its Mastercard can be used. NayaPay's help centre says its Visa debit card can be used for local and international online transactions where Visa is accepted, but it names no shops. For Temu and Taobao we found no statement from either company when we checked in October 2026, so the honest answer for those two is not confirmed.

That gap matters because a card being on the Visa or Mastercard network does not guarantee that a given Chinese checkout will accept it. Fraud filters, the country your account is set to and your app's own card settings all play a part. The rest of this guide sets out what each company actually says, what it costs and how to give yourself the best chance of the payment going through.

Does a SadaPay card work on AliExpress?

Yes, according to SadaPay. Its help article on where you can use SadaPay debit cards says the cards run on Mastercard and can be used anywhere Mastercard is accepted, as long as payments from Pakistan are not blocked by the merchant. It names AliExpress, Netflix and Amazon as examples. The article is dated May 2023, so it is not new, but we found nothing from SadaPay since then that withdraws it.

The same page recommends the virtual card for online payments and the physical card for ATM withdrawals and shop counters. That is sensible for a Chinese site. A virtual card can be frozen in the app without affecting your physical card, and you can keep only the amount you need for one order in the account.

For the AliExpress side of the order, such as choosing a seller and a shipping method, see our guide on how to buy from AliExpress in Pakistan.

Does a NayaPay card work on AliExpress?

Probably, but NayaPay does not say so by name. Its help centre says the Visa debit card is accepted at over 46 million retail locations and online merchants worldwide, and that it can be used for all local and international online transactions where Visa is accepted. AliExpress accepts Visa, so on paper the card qualifies. We did not find a NayaPay page or a recent reputable report that confirms AliExpress specifically, so we have left it as not confirmed by name.

One NayaPay card does not work at all for this. NayaPay's help centre says its PayPak debit card can only be used for in-store purchases and ATM withdrawals in Pakistan, and is not enabled for online payments. If your NayaPay app shows two cards, the one to use on a foreign site is the Visa card.

Can you pay on Temu Pakistan with SadaPay or NayaPay?

We could not confirm it either way. When we opened Temu's Pakistan pages in October 2026, the accepted payment logos included Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, Maestro, Diners Club, JCB, UnionPay, Apple Pay and Google Pay. Both SadaPay and NayaPay issue cards on networks in that list. Neither company and neither Temu page mentioned the other by name, and we found no recent reputable report settling it.

There is one fee point worth knowing before you try. SadaPay's help page on rupee transactions with international merchants says that charges billed in rupees by a foreign merchant are settled in US dollars and treated the same as a regular international transaction, with the same fee. So if a foreign site bills you in rupees, that does not by itself mean you avoid the international fee.

Temu's Pakistan terms of use also say that, where applicable, you act as the importer, and that duties collected at checkout are estimates. If customs assesses more, the difference is yours to pay. Our guide on whether Temu delivers to Pakistan covers delivery, returns and the other payment options.

Can you use SadaPay or NayaPay on Taobao?

Not confirmed, and for most people it is the wrong question. A Wise guide to buying from Taobao explains that Taobao does not link bank cards directly. You add a foreign Visa, Mastercard or JCB card to Alipay, and Alipay pays Taobao. Whether Alipay accepts a SadaPay or NayaPay card issued in Pakistan is something we could not confirm from any official page.

The bigger problem is delivery. The same Wise guide lists the regions Taobao serves for international or consolidated shipping, and Pakistan is not on that list. Most Taobao sellers ship only to addresses in China. Even if your card went through, the parcel would still need a Chinese address and someone to send it on to Pakistan. That is why people in Pakistan usually buy Taobao items through a Taobao buying service and pay in rupees instead.

What does a foreign payment on SadaPay or NayaPay cost?

More than the price on the screen. There are three layers: the app's own fee, the exchange rate it uses, and the advance tax the government collects on card payments abroad.

  • SadaPay fee. SadaPay's help page on non-rupee international transactions lists an international transaction fee of 6% of the base amount, excluding FED, plus a fixed fee of Rs 55 on transactions up to Rs 800. Its own worked example charges 6.96% in total, which is the 6% fee once tax on the fee is added. SadaPay says the conversion rate is passed on to it by its partner banks.
  • NayaPay fee. NayaPay's schedule of charges for July to December 2026 lists an international card transaction fee of US$0.25, charged on both successful and failed transactions. NayaPay's help centre separately describes FX settlement costs, the cost of buying foreign currency to settle your payment, without giving a percentage.
  • Card issue fees. The same NayaPay schedule lists Rs 600 for the Visa virtual card and Rs 1,000 for the physical Visa card including delivery.
  • Advance tax. Under section 236Y of the Income Tax Ordinance, card payments to merchants outside Pakistan carry advance tax. FBR's rate card for tax year 2027 shows 0.5% for people on the Active Taxpayers List and 1% for others, from 1 July 2026. SadaPay's July to December 2026 schedule still printed the older 5% and 10% when we checked. Our guide to tax on international card payments explains the difference and works through an example.

There is also a ceiling. The State Bank of Pakistan caps card-based international transactions at US$30,000 per person per year, across all your cards together. For one order it will not matter.

How do you set up the card before paying a Chinese site?

Do this before you reach the checkout, because repeated declines can freeze a card and, on NayaPay, each failed international attempt carries the same US$0.25 fee as a successful one.

  1. Use the Visa or Mastercard virtual card. On NayaPay, make sure you are not using the PayPak card. On SadaPay, the virtual card is the one SadaPay recommends for online payments.
  2. Check the card settings in the app. NayaPay's app lets you enable or disable international transactions and online payments. Turn both on for the card you will use.
  3. Load the order amount plus a margin. On SadaPay, allow at least 7% for the fee and its tax, plus the advance tax, plus some room for rate movement between checkout and settlement.
  4. Pay inside the platform's own checkout. Never type card details into a link sent by a seller in chat.
  5. Approve any one-time code. Some checkouts ask for a code before the payment completes. Keep your phone with you.
  6. Check the transaction afterwards. Open the transaction in the app and look at the fee and tax lines. If the advance tax looks like 5% or 10% on a payment made after 1 July 2026, ask the app to explain it in writing.

Why does a SadaPay or NayaPay payment get declined?

Usually because of a setting, a balance shortfall or the merchant's own filters, not because the card is banned. The common causes are:

  • International or online payments switched off on the card in the app.
  • Using the wrong card. A PayPak card will not work on a foreign site.
  • Not enough balance for the fee and tax. The order total alone is not enough on SadaPay, because the 6% fee and tax come on top.
  • The merchant's fraud check. A first payment from Pakistan to a new merchant can be refused by the merchant's side. Trying again many times in a row rarely helps.
  • A region mismatch. If your account on the shopping site is set to another country, the checkout may expect a different kind of card or billing address.

Common mistakes with fintech cards on Chinese sites

  • Assuming a rupee price means no international fee. SadaPay says rupee charges from international merchants are treated as international transactions.
  • Retrying a declined payment again and again. On NayaPay each failed international attempt still carries the card transaction fee, and repeated declines can lock the card.
  • Keeping your whole balance in the app. Load only what the order needs. If the card details leak, your loss is limited.
  • Forgetting customs. The card payment covers the item and the site's shipping. Duty assessed in Pakistan can still be due later, depending on the site's terms and the parcel's value.
  • Trying Taobao by card. Even if the payment worked, the seller will usually ship only inside China.
