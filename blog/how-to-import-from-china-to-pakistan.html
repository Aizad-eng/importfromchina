How to Import From China to Pakistan: Two Routes, Step by Step

By importfromchina.shop teamUpdated October 11, 202612 min read
Quick answer

There are two ways to import from China to Pakistan. For commercial quantities in your own name you need an NTN, usually sales tax registration and a Pakistan Single Window subscription, then you pay the supplier through your bank, ship by air or sea and file a goods declaration. For a single item, a courier, a marketplace that ships here, or a single-item service clears it for you on your CNIC. Either way most goods attract duty and taxes, so work out the landed cost before you pay.

What are the two ways to import goods from China to Pakistan?

Every import from China ends with someone filing a declaration with Pakistan Customs and paying the duty and taxes. The real choice is who that someone is. Either you import in your own name as a registered importer, or a courier, marketplace or service clears the goods for you.

If you plan to sell goods regularly, the first route gives you proper import records in your name. If you want one thing for yourself, the second route is far simpler. A useful test is to ask whether you will import again next month. If yes, the one-time setup pays off. If not, let someone already registered carry the paperwork. The rest of this guide shows how each route works.

How do you import from China in your own name, step by step?

This is the commercial route. It takes some setup the first time, then each shipment follows the same pattern.

  1. Get an NTN. E-enrol on FBR's IRIS portal. For an individual the 13 digit CNIC becomes the NTN. A company or partnership gets a 7 digit NTN.
  2. Check sales tax registration. Most commercial importers also hold a sales tax registration number (STRN). Whether you must depends on your goods and business, so confirm with a tax adviser.
  3. Subscribe on Pakistan Single Window. PSW is the portal where declarations, permits and payments are handled. Subscription needs your NTN, CNIC, a SIM in your own name, the email in your IRIS record, a one-time fee and biometric verification at a NADRA e-Sahulat centre. Our guide to Pakistan Single Window walks through it.
  4. Link your bank to PSW. Associate your bank profile by IBAN so payment details can flow between the bank and PSW.
  5. Find the HS code and the rules for it. The code sets the duty and tells you whether another agency, such as DRAP or PSQCA, must approve the goods. See how to find the HS code for your product.
  6. Choose a supplier and agree terms. Get a proforma invoice stating the price term (EXW, FOB or CIF), packing and lead time. Our guide on finding reliable Chinese suppliers covers the checks.
  7. Pay through your bank. The payment is tied to your declaration as a financial instrument, such as an advance payment, a letter of credit, or a contract or collection arrangement.
  8. Book freight. Air or sea, through a forwarder or the supplier. You receive an airway bill or bill of lading.
  9. File the goods declaration. On PSW you, or a licensed customs agent, file the Single Declaration with the invoice, packing list, transport document and any permits, then pay duties and taxes against the system PSID.
  10. Clearance and delivery. Customs assesses the declaration and, based on risk, clears it directly or checks documents or goods first. After release you collect from the port or airport.

Keep these documents together for every shipment, because the declaration and any later query from customs rely on them:

  • The commercial invoice, showing the real price paid, currency and price term.
  • The packing list, with box count, weights and dimensions.
  • The airway bill or bill of lading.
  • Proof of payment through your bank, matching the financial instrument on PSW.
  • Any licence, permit or certificate the HS code requires, and a certificate of origin if you claim free trade agreement rates.

Many first-time importers hire a licensed customs agent for the first few shipments and learn the PSW screens alongside them. The agent files under their own registration, so you never need to hand over your own login. The airport side is explained in our air cargo clearance guide.

How do you pay a Chinese supplier from Pakistan?

Payment is where many first imports stall.

  • Commercial bank payment. For an import in your own name, your bank pays the supplier. On PSW the payment is linked to your goods declaration as a financial instrument. PSW replaced the older Electronic Import Form (EIF) with this system, and the bank settles the instrument after the goods clear.
  • Alibaba checkout. Cards and bank transfer through Alibaba work for many buyers, though some Pakistani cards are declined.
  • Through an agent or service. You pay in rupees locally, and the agent pays the seller in RMB.

PayPal is not available in Pakistan, and JazzCash, Easypaisa and Raast are not accepted on Chinese platforms. Our guide on how to pay Chinese sellers from Pakistan goes through each method.

How does customs work for imports from China?

Every import is classified under a Pakistan Customs Tariff code, and that code sets the duty. Customs assesses the declaration and, depending on the system's risk selection, clears it straight away or checks documents or goods first.

There is one small waiver for parcels. Section 19C of the Customs Act, as substituted by the Finance Act 2025 and still shown in FBR's consolidated Act amended up to 30 June 2026, says that where the value of goods imported through post or courier does not exceed one thousand rupees, no duties and taxes shall be demanded, subject to conditions set by the Board. Above that limit, and for any cargo shipment, duties and taxes apply.

Two other points often affect China imports:

  • The China-Pakistan Free Trade Agreement. Listed goods can get reduced or zero customs duty with a valid certificate of origin, but sales tax and income tax still apply. See our CPFTA guide.
  • Phones. A phone or SIM device must be registered with PTA through DIRBS within the grace period, with tax paid on the model, or it gets blocked on Pakistani networks.

What can't you import from China, or only with permission?

The Import Policy Order 2022, issued by the Ministry of Commerce as SRO 545(I)/2022, lists banned goods in Appendix A, goods allowed only on conditions in Appendix B, and restrictions on used goods in Appendix C. It is amended often, so check the current version for your product. The common problem areas for China imports are:

  • Drones, which need prior permission.
  • Medicines and supplements, which need DRAP approval.
  • Counterfeit branded goods, which customs seizes.
  • Weapons and replica weapons, which are banned.
  • Loose lithium batteries and power banks, aerosols, perfumes and flammable liquids, which are restricted on flights. Devices with built-in batteries usually ship fine.
  • Phones and SIM devices, which need PTA registration.

What are the most common import mistakes?

  • Comparing the product price with a local shop price. Compare the landed price instead, after freight, duty and taxes.
  • Ignoring volumetric weight. A light but bulky box is charged on its size.
  • Under-declaring value. It is a customs offence, and valuation rulings mean customs often uses its own minimum values anyway.
  • Ordering from 1688 or Taobao and expecting delivery to Pakistan. Those sellers ship within China only.
  • Buying a 110V appliance. Pakistan runs on 220 to 240V at 50Hz with C and D plugs. China-market items are 220V, but US-market versions need a converter.
  • Skipping the permit check. Goods needing DRAP, PSQCA or other approval are not released on duty payment alone.
  • Paying an unverified seller in full. Use platform protection where you can, and read whether it is safe to order from China.
  • Ordering just before Chinese New Year. Expect weeks of delay.

Frequently asked questions

Can an individual import from China to Pakistan without a company?

Yes. Single items come through a courier, post or an import service on your CNIC. To import commercially in your own name you need an NTN, usually sales tax registration and a PSW subscription, but not a company.

Is there a duty-free limit for imports from China?

Only a small one. Section 19C of the Customs Act, as amended by the Finance Act 2025, waives duties and taxes where goods through post or courier are worth no more than one thousand rupees.

How long does it take to import from China to Pakistan?

Air freight typically takes 7 to 15 days door to door including customs, and sea takes about 25 to 45 days for a shared container. Add the supplier's preparation time.

Do I need Pakistan Single Window to import from China?

Only if you import commercially in your own name. For a single parcel, the courier or service that clears it uses its own registration.

What is the minimum order to import from China?

There is no legal minimum. Suppliers set their own minimum order quantities, and some list single pieces. Our service handles exactly one item per order and no bulk.

Can I pay a Chinese seller with JazzCash or Easypaisa?

Not directly. Chinese platforms do not accept JazzCash, Easypaisa or Raast. You can use them to pay a local agent or service, which then pays the seller.
