How to Import from China to Pakistan: Complete Guide (2026)

By importfromchina.shop teamUpdated October 3, 20269 min read
Quick answer

To import from China to Pakistan you find a supplier (Alibaba, 1688 or a sourcing agent), confirm samples and price, pay the supplier, ship by air (7–15 days) or sea (25–45 days), clear Pakistan Customs through WeBOC or a clearing agent, pay duty and sales tax, and receive the goods. If you only want one item, you skip all the paperwork by using a door-to-door sourcing service that handles everything for one landed price. No minimum order.

What are the steps to import from China to Pakistan?

Every import, whether it is a single gadget or a container of tiles, goes through the same six stages. The difference is only who does the work: you, or a sourcing partner.

  1. Find the product and supplier. Search Alibaba, 1688, Made-in-China or ask a sourcing agent. Compare at least three suppliers on price, minimum order quantity (MOQ) and reviews.
  2. Confirm specs, samples and price. Ask for photos, a sample if the order is large, and a quote that states the price term (EXW, FOB or CIF). Agree on packaging and labelling.
  3. Pay the supplier. Alibaba Trade Assurance accepts cards. 1688 needs a Chinese payment method, which is why most Pakistani buyers use an agent for 1688 orders.
  4. Ship the goods. Air express for small, urgent orders. Air freight for 50–500 kg. Sea freight (LCL or FCL) for anything heavy or bulky.
  5. Clear customs in Pakistan. Goods are declared on WeBOC or the Pakistan Single Window. Duty, additional duty, sales tax and advance income tax are assessed on the declared value.
  6. Receive and inspect. Delivery to your address in Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad or anywhere else, then count and check against the order.

Do I need a licence or registration to import?

It depends on how you import.

  • Commercial import in your own name requires an NTN (National Tax Number), a Sales Tax Registration Number (STRN) for most goods, and WeBOC registration. A customs clearing agent files the Goods Declaration for you.
  • Small shipments through a courier or a sourcing service are cleared by the courier or the service under their own registration. You only provide your CNIC and address. This is how most first-time and small-volume buyers import.

If you want one item, or anything under roughly 100 kg, the second route is far faster and cheaper than setting up your own import registration.

Should I ship by air or by sea?

Use air when the shipment is light, valuable or urgent. Use sea when it is heavy, bulky or low-margin.

  • Air express (DHL, FedEx, courier consolidators): 5–10 days door to door. Best under 50 kg.
  • Air freight: 7–15 days including clearance. Best for 50–500 kg.
  • Sea LCL (shared container): 25–45 days. Best for 1–15 CBM.
  • Sea FCL (your own container): 20–35 days. Best above 15 CBM.

How do I pay a Chinese supplier from Pakistan?

This is the step that stops most beginners. Pakistani bank cards often fail on Chinese platforms, and bank transfers (TT) require documentation and a bank that will process the remittance.

  • Alibaba Trade Assurance: Pay by card or bank transfer through Alibaba. Your money is held until you confirm the goods. Works for many Pakistani cards, not all.
  • Bank TT: Standard for larger commercial orders. Requires a proforma invoice and your bank's import documentation.
  • Through a sourcing agent: You pay the agent in PKR by bank transfer, JazzCash, Easypaisa or Raast. The agent pays the supplier in RMB. This is the only practical way to buy from 1688.

What goes wrong most often, and how do I avoid it?

  • Wrong product or quality. Always get photos or a video of your actual order before it ships. A pre-shipment inspection costs little compared with a rejected batch.
  • Surprise duty. Know the HS code and duty rate before ordering. Under-declaring value is a customs offence and leads to penalties and delays.
  • Restricted items. Some goods need permits (medical devices, certain electronics with batteries, chemicals, used machinery). Check before you pay.
  • Supplier scams. Verify the company, start with a small order, and never pay 100% upfront to an unverified seller. Read our guide on finding reliable Chinese suppliers.

Is it easier to use a sourcing agent?

For a single item or any order under a few thousand dollars, almost always yes. An agent finds the supplier, negotiates, checks the goods, ships, clears customs and delivers to your door for one quoted price. You skip the Alibaba account, the payment problems, the clearing agent and the port visits. For large recurring container orders, setting up your own import registration becomes worthwhile. Frequently asked questions

Can an individual import from China to Pakistan without a company?

Yes. Individuals can receive small shipments through couriers or a sourcing service that clears customs under its own registration. You provide your CNIC and address. Importing commercially in your own name requires an NTN, usually an STRN, and WeBOC registration.

How long does it take to import from China to Pakistan?

Air shipments take 7 to 15 days door to door including customs. Sea shipments take 25 to 45 days. Add supplier production time, which ranges from a few days for stock items to 30 days or more for custom orders.

What is the minimum order to import from China?

There is no legal minimum. Suppliers set their own MOQ, often 50 to 500 units for factories and as low as 1 unit on 1688. A single-item sourcing service like ours has no minimum at all: you can order exactly one piece.

Is importing from China to Pakistan profitable?

It can be, if the landed cost including duty and freight leaves a margin at local market prices. Electronics accessories, beauty tools, kitchen gadgets and fashion accessories commonly have good margins. Heavy, low-value items often do not, because freight and duty eat the margin.

Which city in Pakistan is best for importing?

Karachi has the seaports and the largest customs operation, so sea shipments clear there. Air shipments can clear at Karachi, Lahore or Islamabad airports. For door-to-door services, your city does not matter, since delivery is included.
