Best Chinese Clothing Apps That Deliver to Pakistan (2026)

By Muhammad Shamshad AslamUpdated November 11, 20267 min read
Quick answer

For clothes from China, Shein and Temu are the two apps that work best from Pakistan in 2026. Both deliver, both quote roughly one to three weeks, and both have accepted Pakistani wallets. Shein has the deeper fashion range and charges a small shipping fee below about Rs 4,000; Temu ships free and has easier returns. AliExpress is slower and card-only. Taobao has the widest choice of all but needs a buying service. Check the size chart in centimetres whichever you use.

Which Chinese clothing apps deliver to Pakistan?

Three Chinese apps with large clothing ranges deliver to a Pakistani address on their own: Shein, Temu and AliExpress. Taobao, which has more clothing than any of them, does not deliver to Pakistan, but you can reach it through a buying service. Daraz, a Pakistani marketplace, also lists overseas clothing from Chinese sellers.

We have only included apps whose Pakistan delivery we checked on their own pages. Other fashion apps exist, but we have not listed any we could not confirm.

Is Shein the best app for clothes from China?

For fashion range, yes. Shein is built around clothing, shoes and accessories, and each listing carries its own size chart. From Pakistan it is straightforward to pay, since its How to Pay page has steps for JazzCash and Easypaisa as well as cards. Its delivery to Pakistan is quoted at 7 to 11 business days after 1 to 3 days of processing.

The weak points are returns and the extra fee on small orders. The only return method shown for Pakistan is Self-Return, with postage at your cost, and swimwear, lingerie, underwear, bodysuits and jewellery cannot be returned. Orders under US$14.43, about Rs 4,000, pay US$1.08 shipping. See how to buy from Shein in Pakistan.

Is Temu good for clothes in Pakistan?

Yes, especially for basics, kids' clothes and accessories. Temu shows rupee prices on its Pakistan site, ships free on all orders, and its returns are the most forgiving here: 90 days, with the first return from each order free and collected from your door. Worn or washed clothing, or clothing with tags or hygiene stickers removed, cannot be returned. Its fashion range is wide but less focused than Shein's. Compare the two in Shein vs Temu in Pakistan.

Should you buy clothes on AliExpress from Pakistan?

Usually only for something specific, such as a brand's official store, a costume, or a size the others do not stock. AliExpress delivery to Pakistan by post commonly takes 25 to 45 days, payment needs an international Visa or Mastercard, and sending clothes back to China rarely makes sense. Sizes vary a lot between sellers, so read each listing's chart.

What about Taobao for clothes?

Taobao has the widest clothing choice of any Chinese platform, from small independent brands to traditional wear and very specific sizes and fabrics. It sells only inside China, with Chinese payment and Chinese addresses, so from Pakistan you need a buying service. That adds cost, so it suits one special item more than everyday basics. How do you get the right size from a Chinese clothing app?

Chinese sizes often run smaller than the same letter size locally, and they vary between sellers. To get it right:

  • Measure a garment you own that fits well, flat, in centimetres: chest, waist, length, sleeve.
  • Compare those numbers with the size chart on the listing, not the letter size.
  • Check whether the chart gives body measurements or garment measurements. They are different.
  • Read reviews where buyers mention their height and weight, and look at customer photos.
  • For fitted items, choose the bigger size when you are between two, since returns from Pakistan cost money on most apps.

Do you pay customs duty on clothes from China?

You can. Under section 19C of the Customs Act, post and courier parcels worth Rs 1,000 or less pay no duty or tax. Above that, customs can charge duty and taxes on the whole value, and clothing often sits in higher-duty headings. Customs decides the amount when the parcel arrives, so it cannot be known exactly in advance. See customs duty on Shein orders.

Which clothing app should you use?

  • Rare sizes, small brands or traditional wear: Taobao through a service.
