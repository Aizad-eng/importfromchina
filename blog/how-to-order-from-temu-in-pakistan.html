How to Order From Temu in Pakistan, Step by Step

By Muhammad Shamshad AslamUpdated October 23, 20269 min read
Quick answer

To order from Temu in Pakistan, install the Temu app or open temu.com/pk-en, check that prices show in Rs, sign up and add items to your cart. Enter a full address with a landmark and a phone number you answer, then pay with JazzCash, Easypaisa or a card, as there is no cash on delivery. Free standard shipping takes 5 to 13 business days after processing.

How do you order from Temu in Pakistan?

You order from Temu in Pakistan through its Pakistan site or app, where prices are in rupees and standard shipping is free. If you only want to know whether Temu delivers here at all, our guide on whether Temu delivers to Pakistan answers that. This guide is the walk-through: what to tap, what to type and what to check at each stage, based on Temu's Pakistan pages when we checked in October 2026.

The whole process in eight steps:

  1. Install the Temu app or open temu.com/pk-en, and make sure the region is Pakistan.
  2. Create an account.
  3. Find the item, pick the exact variant and add it to your cart.
  4. Check the cart total in Rs.
  5. Enter your address in full, with a landmark and a working phone number.
  6. Pay with JazzCash, Easypaisa or a card.
  7. Track the parcel from your order history.
  8. Return anything that is wrong through "Your Orders".

Each step is explained below.

Step 1: How do you get the Pakistan version of Temu?

Download the Temu app from the App Store or Google Play, or open temu.com/pk-en in a browser, and confirm the region reads Pakistan. Temu is one app with many country sites. The country you land on depends on the region setting, not only on where you are.

  • Turn off any VPN first. A VPN or an office network can drop you on another country's site.
  • Check the region selector. On the website it sits in the top bar, and the Pakistan option reads "Pakistan English".
  • Check the prices. They should show in Rs. When we opened the Pakistan site in October 2026, its currency was set to Pakistani rupees and its time zone to Karachi.

If prices still show in dollars or dirhams, sign out, close the app and open it again with the VPN off. Do not try to buy from another country's Temu site with a Pakistani address. Each country site uses its own carriers, taxes and return pickups.

Step 2: How do you create a Temu account?

Tap Sign in / Register and enter your email address, or choose one of the other sign-in options the screen offers. It takes a minute. A few points matter more than people expect:

  • You must be 18 or older. Temu's terms say users must be at least eighteen and that children under 18 are not permitted to use the service.
  • Use your real name. The same name goes on the parcel, and Temu's terms make you the importer of what you buy, so it should match the person receiving it.
  • Use an email you check. Temu sends order confirmations, shipping notices and refund updates by email, SMS and app notifications.

Buy only inside the official app or temu.com. Facebook pages and WhatsApp numbers calling themselves "Temu Pakistan" are resellers, and Temu's return policy does not cover what you buy from them.

Steps 3 and 4: How do you pick the item and check the cart?

Open the listing, choose the exact colour, size or model, and add it to your cart, then check the total in rupees before you pay. Most wrong orders on Temu come from the variant menu, not from the seller.

  • Select every option. One listing can hold many sizes, colours and plug types at different prices. The picture at the top is not always the variant you selected.
  • Read the size chart in centimetres. For clothes and shoes, measure something you own and compare.
  • Read the listing notes. Temu says merchant partners may state in a listing that they cannot ship to P.O. boxes, and some items need a physical address or a signature.
  • Read reviews from buyers who describe the size or fit. They tell you more than the product images.
  • Look at the cart total, not the item price. On bigger orders, check whether an import tax line appears.

You can keep several items in one cart. Temu's terms say you may pay for multiple orders in one transaction and that multiple orders may be delivered together in one package. Some items still arrive as separate parcels with their own tracking.

Step 5: How should you write your Pakistani address on Temu?

Write the address the way a local rider would need it: house and street, area, city, a landmark and a mobile number you answer. Temu hands the last leg to local carriers, and in Pakistan that rider will usually call before coming.

And items cannot be delivered to security-restricted areas such as military bases, so if you live inside a cantonment or a guarded official colony, use an address outside it.

Step 6: How do you pay Temu with JazzCash, Easypaisa or a card?

Choose your payment method on the checkout screen and approve the payment. When we checked the Pakistan site in October 2026, the payment options shown were JazzCash, Easypaisa, Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, Maestro, Diners Club, JCB, UnionPay, Apple Pay and Google Pay. There was no cash on delivery.

With JazzCash or Easypaisa:

  1. Make sure your wallet has enough balance for the full total.
  2. Select JazzCash or Easypaisa at checkout and enter the mobile number linked to the wallet.
  3. Approve the payment the way the screen tells you, usually inside your wallet app or with your wallet PIN.
  4. Wait for Temu's order confirmation before closing the app.

With a debit or credit card:

  1. Switch on international or online payments in your banking app first. Many Pakistani cards come with this turned off.
  2. Enter the card details at checkout and complete the bank's one-time code step.
  3. Check your bank SMS. Temu shows prices in Rs, but your bank may still treat it as a foreign transaction and add its own charges.

Never send money by hand to a wallet number for a Temu order. Real Temu payments happen inside the Temu checkout.

One more thing on the total. Temu's Pakistan terms say that, where applicable, you act as the importer, that duties collected at checkout are estimates, and that if customs assesses more, you pay the difference. Under section 19C of the Customs Act, post and courier parcels worth Rs 1,000 or less are duty-free. Above that, duty and taxes can apply. Step 7: How do you track a Temu order to Pakistan?

Open "Your Orders" in the app and tap the package. Temu's shipping page says you get a message when the order ships, a tracking number when it is ready, and a delivery time shown under each package in your order history.

  • Processing takes 1 to 3 days before shipping. Count the 5 to 13 business days from dispatch, not from payment.
  • Business days skip weekends, so in calendar terms plan for roughly two to three weeks.
  • Tracking can go quiet once the parcel reaches the local carrier. Temu says tracking may not be available every step of the way.
  • Keep your phone on in the delivery window. Missed calls from the rider are the most common reason for a failed delivery.

If the parcel never arrives, or tracking says delivered but you have nothing, Temu asks you to contact customer service within 90 days of the order date. For parcels that change carriers along the way, see how to track a parcel from China to Pakistan.

Step 8: How do you return a Temu item from Pakistan?

Go to "Your Orders", find the order, tap "Return/Refund", choose the items and the reason, tap "Next Step" and pick your refund method. Temu's Pakistan return policy, last updated 23 March 2026, sets these rules:

  • Window: returns within 90 days of purchase.
  • Deadline after requesting: you must send the package back within 14 days of submitting the request.
  • Pickup: if you choose pickup, CPEX collects the parcel from your door.
  • Cost: your first return from each order is free. A second return from the same order costs Rs 612, deducted from your refund, so send everything back together.
  • Not returnable: worn or washed clothing, clothing with tags or hygiene stickers removed, food, some health and personal care items, some free gifts and some customised products.
  • Refund time: 5 to 14 business days to your original payment method, up to 30 days depending on your bank or wallet. Temu credit is faster and does not expire, but it usually cannot be turned back into cash.
  • Import tax: not refunded if the return is not Temu's fault.

Send the return to the address on Temu's return label, not the sender address printed on your parcel.

Common mistakes when ordering from Temu in Pakistan

  • Building a cart on the wrong country site. If prices are not in Rs, fix the region before adding anything.
  • Leaving out the landmark or phone number. Local riders rely on both.
  • Using a cantonment or restricted address. Temu cannot deliver to security-restricted areas.
  • Paying a "Temu Pakistan" page on social media. That is a reseller, not Temu.
  • Returning items in two batches. The second return from the same order costs Rs 612.
  • Missing the 14-day send-back rule. Requesting a return is not enough. The parcel has to go back within 14 days.
  • Ignoring the import tax line on a large order. Customs can assess more than Temu's estimate, and you pay the difference.

Frequently asked questions

Can I order from Temu in Pakistan without a credit card?

Yes. When we checked in October 2026, Temu's Pakistan checkout showed JazzCash and Easypaisa alongside cards, so a mobile wallet is enough.

Does Temu have cash on delivery in Pakistan?

No. Cash on delivery was not among the payment options on Temu's Pakistan site when we checked. You pay upfront by wallet or card.

How long does a Temu order take to reach Pakistan?

Temu's Pakistan shipping page lists free standard shipping in 5 to 13 business days, after 1 to 3 days of processing. In calendar terms that is roughly two to three weeks.

Why does the Temu app show dollars instead of rupees?

The region is set to another country, often because of a VPN or an old account. Turn off the VPN and choose Pakistan English in the region selector.

How do I return a Temu order from Pakistan?

Open Your Orders, tap Return/Refund, choose the items and reason, and pick a refund method. The first return per order is free with CPEX door pickup, within 90 days of purchase.

Do I pay customs duty on a Temu order?

Parcels worth Rs 1,000 or less by post or courier are duty-free under section 19C. Above that, duty can apply, and Temu's terms say you pay any amount customs assesses beyond its estimate.
