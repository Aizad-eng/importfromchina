Can I get a single item from China delivered in Faisalabad?

Yes. One item for your own use, or for your own workshop or mill, can come from China to a Faisalabad address without a company registration or an import licence in your name. The parcel travels by air, clears Pakistan Customs and a courier delivers it. You do not need to visit an airport, a customs office or the city's dry port for a single item.

What makes Faisalabad a little different from Lahore or Karachi is the kind of address. Many orders here go to a home in a colony, a shop in one of the old bazaars, or a factory gate. Each needs slightly different details, which we cover below.

Which airport serves Faisalabad, and does it handle international cargo?

Faisalabad International Airport on Jhang Road, about 10 km southwest of the city centre, is the city's airport. It is run by the Pakistan Airports Authority and has customs and immigration for its international flights. It also has a cargo complex next to the passenger terminal, leased to PIA.

The cargo side is small. Pakistan Civil Aviation Authority figures quoted on the airport's Wikipedia page put cargo handled at 52 metric tons for July 2024 to June 2025. The international routes listed there go to the Gulf and Saudi Arabia, and none went to China when we checked in October 2026. So a parcel from China does not fly straight into Faisalabad. In practice it lands at a larger gateway airport and reaches the city through the courier network by road. You do not arrange that leg yourself, and it does not change the price we quote.

How does one item get from China to a Faisalabad address?

It follows five steps, and you only take part in the first one.

  1. You send us the product link from Alibaba, 1688, Taobao, AliExpress or another Chinese site.
  2. We buy the item and the seller dispatches it to the shipping line's warehouse in China.
  3. The parcel flies to Pakistan together with other parcels.
  4. Pakistan Customs assesses it. The duty and taxes are already part of the one price you paid, so no bill follows you home.
  5. A courier carries it to Faisalabad and delivers it to the address you gave.

National couriers such as TCS and Leopards have long described their networks as covering all cities and major towns of Pakistan, and Faisalabad is one of the largest of those cities, so the last leg is routine.

How long does delivery to Faisalabad take?

Usually 7 to 15 days after the seller dispatches, the same window we quote for anywhere in Pakistan. Before dispatch there is the seller's own handling time, which the listing normally states and which is longer for made-to-order parts. We give you an expected delivery date in writing when you pay.

What kind of single items suit Faisalabad's mills and workshops?

Parts and tools for textile and engineering work are a natural fit. The district government describes Faisalabad as a major textile hub, and its Wikipedia entry lists hosiery, dyes, industrial chemicals, agricultural equipment and engineering works among the city's industries. Some examples of single items that fit that kind of work:

  • A replacement part for an industrial sewing or embroidery machine, when the exact part number is not available locally.
  • A sensor, control board or display for a machine, ordered by the model number printed on the old part.
  • A GSM round cutter or a thread counting glass for checking fabric.
  • A clamp meter or a specific multimeter for a factory electrician.
  • Personal items too, such as a particular keyboard, earbuds or a phone accessory.

These are examples of what one item can be, not a claim about what people order. For parts, always match the model and part number before you send the link. Our guide on importing machine and appliance replacement parts shows how to find the right listing.

We bring one item, or a few different single items together. If a mill needs the same part in quantity, that is a commercial import for a cargo company or clearing agent. We do not do bulk or resale.

Should I check Faisalabad's bazaars before importing?

Yes, for anything common. The city centre was laid out as eight roads running out from the Clock Tower, and each bazaar grew its own trade. Katchery Bazaar is known for mobile phones and accessories, Bhawana Bazaar for electrical and electronic goods, and Montgomery Bazaar for yarn and raw cloth. If the item is there at a fair price, buying locally is quicker and you can take it back to the shop.

Importing makes sense when the exact model or part is not on the shelves, or when the local price is far above the Chinese one. It works best for items worth around Rs 10,000 or more. How should I write a Faisalabad address so the courier finds it?

Write it the way a rider on a motorbike would look for it, with the colony, number and a landmark. Specific tips for Faisalabad:

  • Name the colony and its number exactly. Peoples Colony No. 1 and Peoples Colony No. 2 are separate areas.
  • Add a landmark every courier knows, such as D-Ground, the Clock Tower, Susan Road or a named chowk, mosque or school.
  • For a shop near the Clock Tower, give the bazaar name, the shop name and the shop number. The lanes are busy and shop numbers alone are easy to miss.
  • For a mill or factory, give the gate number, the person's name and the number of someone at the gate, so the parcel is not left with a guard who does not know who it is for.
  • For a gated housing scheme, tell the gate staff to expect a courier.
  • Give a mobile number that answers calls from unknown numbers. Couriers usually call before they deliver.

What are the common mistakes with Faisalabad orders?

Most problems come from the order details, not the distance.

  • Ordering a machine part by its picture on the listing instead of its part number.
  • Choosing a variant made for another country, such as a 110V version of a tool, without noticing.
  • Importing an item that costs less than its shipping.
  • Leaving out the colony number or the factory gate, which can send the parcel back to the courier's depot.
