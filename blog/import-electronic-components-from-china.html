Can you import Arduino, ESP32 and sensors from China to Pakistan?

Yes. Development boards, modules, sensors and small tools are ordinary goods and can be imported for your own projects, studies or repairs. Chinese sites list boards, breakouts and kits that are not stocked locally. Our guide on how to import from China to Pakistan explains the overall process.

The honest catch is cost. Most single components are cheap, and shipping is not. This guide helps you decide when to buy locally, when to order direct from a platform that ships to Pakistan, and when a single-item service makes sense.

Which ESP32 should you choose?

Choose by radio and processing needs, because the ESP32 family now has several quite different chips. When we checked Espressif's pages in November 2026, they described them like this:

ChipCPURadioGood for
ESP32 (original)Dual-core Xtensa, 240 MHzWi-Fi and BluetoothMost tutorials and older libraries
ESP32-S3Dual-core Xtensa LX7, 240 MHz2.4 GHz Wi-Fi 802.11 b/g/n, Bluetooth 5 (LE)Cameras, displays and small AI tasks, with 45 GPIOs and vector instructions
ESP32-C3Single-core RISC-V, 160 MHzWi-Fi and Bluetooth LESmall, low-cost sensor nodes
ESP32-C6Single-core RISC-V, 160 MHz2.4 GHz Wi-Fi 6, Bluetooth 5 (LE), IEEE 802.15.4 for Thread and ZigbeeMatter and Zigbee smart home projects
ESP32-C5Single-core RISC-V, 240 MHzDual-band 2.4 and 5 GHz Wi-Fi 6Projects that need 5 GHz Wi-Fi

One electrical point catches beginners: ESP32 boards use 3.3V logic, while the UNO R4 WiFi described below works at 5V. Connecting a 5V sensor output straight to an ESP32 pin can damage it, so use a level shifter or a sensor rated for 3.3V.

If you follow online tutorials, the original ESP32 or the ESP32-S3 has the widest support. For smart home work, the C6 is the one with a Thread and Zigbee radio. Also check the board around the chip: flash and PSRAM sizes, the USB connector, and whether it uses a USB-to-serial chip or the chip's own USB.

Is a cheap Arduino a fake or a clone?

It depends on the branding. Arduino's own blog separates the categories. Official boards are the only ones that can use the Arduino name. Clones are near-exact copies under a different brand, derivatives change the design for a purpose, and compatibles share some connectors or software support. Counterfeits copy the design and the Arduino branding, logo and board graphics, and Arduino says they come with no support.

A clone under its own brand is a legitimate, cheap way to learn. A board printed with the Arduino logo at a fraction of the official price is a counterfeit, and counterfeit goods can be seized at customs. If you need a specific official board, check its documentation first. Arduino's page for the UNO R4 WiFi, for example, lists a Renesas RA4M1 (Arm Cortex-M4) at 48 MHz, an ESP32-S3 module for Wi-Fi and Bluetooth, 5V logic and a 6 to 24V input through VIN or the barrel jack.

Why does my clone board not show up on my computer?

Many clone boards use a CH340 USB-to-serial chip, which may need a driver. SparkFun's guide describes the CH340 as a WCH chip that converts USB data to serial UART and back. It says the driver should install automatically on most operating systems, but you may need to install it the first time or after an operating system update, and on macOS it advises removing old drivers first. Download the driver from the chip maker or a trusted guide, not a random mirror. A charge-only USB cable is the other common cause, so try a cable you know carries data. Boards with the chip's own USB, as on many ESP32-S3 boards, may need to be put into download mode with the BOOT button the first time you upload code.

Which sensors and modules are worth ordering?

The common, cheap modules are usually best bought locally, because the electronics markets in Pakistan's big cities stock them and delivery is quick. China is worth it for items that are rare or more expensive here:

  • Specific sensor breakouts, such as particular gas, air quality, radar presence or time-of-flight sensors.
  • Display modules in an exact size or interface your code expects.
  • Development kits with a matching camera, screen or case.
  • Tools such as logic analysers, USB oscilloscopes, smart soldering irons and bench power supplies.

When you plan an order, write the list first: board, sensors, display, cables and any headers or connectors. Ordering everything for one project at once keeps it to one shipment instead of several, and it is easier to see whether the total justifies the shipping cost.

Always note the exact part number and check the datasheet. Two modules with the same listing image can carry different chips and pin layouts. Our guide on the Pinecil V2 soldering iron is an example of a tool worth importing on its own.

Can lithium batteries for projects be shipped?

Loose cells and packs, such as 18650 cells or LiPo packs, are the hardest kind of lithium battery to ship by air. They travel as batteries on their own, and many sellers and couriers will not send them to Pakistan. A small battery already built into a device ships much more easily. Buy loose cells locally where you can, and read our lithium battery shipping guide before ordering anything with a battery.

How are components taxed, and what is the Rs 1,000 rule?

Under section 19C of the Customs Act, as amended by the Finance Act 2025, post or courier parcels worth Rs 1,000 or less pay no duties or taxes. Above that, the whole value is dutiable. Duty is charged on the customs value, which is the CIF value converted at NBP rates published by FBR. When we checked in November 2026, customs duty slabs ran from 0 to 20 percent with some goods higher, additional customs duty was 0 percent on the 0 to 15 percent slabs and 2 percent on the 20 percent slab, regulatory duty applied only to listed goods and was capped at 20 percent from 1 July 2026, and sales tax was 18 percent on the value plus duties, with advance income tax under section 148 on top.

In practice, a small direct order from a platform that ships to Pakistan, kept under Rs 1,000, is the cheapest route for a cheap module. A larger order of different items crosses the threshold and is taxed normally.

When does a single-item service make sense for electronics?

It makes sense when the item is not sold on a platform that ships to Pakistan, or when it is worth around Rs 10,000 or more. Shipping from China usually costs Rs 5,000 to 20,000 depending on size and type, so a single sensor is never worth it. A development kit, a USB oscilloscope, a 3D printer part or a set of different boards for one project can be. We do single items, or a few different single items together. Parts in quantity for a product are a commercial import, which also brings in PTA, since PTA requires type approval for terminal equipment imported for commercial purposes. For that, use a cargo company or the commercial route.

What are the common mistakes?

  • Ordering the wrong ESP32 variant for the tutorial or library you plan to use.
  • Mixing 3.3V and 5V boards and damaging a module.
  • Buying a "genuine Arduino" at a counterfeit price.
  • Ordering loose lithium cells by air.
  • Paying Rs 5,000 or more in shipping for a few hundred rupees of parts.

Frequently asked questions

Which ESP32 is best for beginners?

The original ESP32 or the ESP32-S3, because most tutorials and libraries support them. Choose the ESP32-C6 if you want Thread or Zigbee for smart home projects.

Are cheap Arduino boards from China fake?

Not always. Clones under their own brand are legitimate. Boards carrying the Arduino logo at a very low price are counterfeits and can be seized.

Why is my Arduino clone not detected by my laptop?

It probably uses a CH340 USB-to-serial chip that needs a driver, or the cable is charge-only. Install the driver and use a data cable.

Is there customs duty on small electronics parcels?

Post or courier parcels worth Rs 1,000 or less pay no duties or taxes under section 19C. Above that, the whole value is dutiable.

Can I import 18650 cells from China?

It is difficult. Loose lithium cells are the hardest batteries to ship by air, and many sellers refuse. Buy them locally if you can.

Can you import components for my product in quantity?

No. We only handle single items or a few different single items. Quantities for a product need a cargo company or the commercial import route, including PTA type approval for radio modules.
