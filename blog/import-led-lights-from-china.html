Importing LED Lights From China to Pakistan

By Muhammad Shamshad AslamUpdated November 12, 20269 min read
Quick answer

Yes, you can import LED lights from China to Pakistan, but it only makes sense for special fittings, not everyday bulbs. Check the base (B22 or E27), a 220 to 240V driver, colour temperature and CRI, and battery rules for solar lights. LED lamps are on PSQCA's mandatory list, and shipping of Rs 5,000 to 20,000 rules out cheap items.

New to importing? Read our step-by-step guide on how to import from China to Pakistan.

Can you import LED lights from China to Pakistan?

Yes. LED lights are ordinary goods and a single fitting, or a few different fittings for one room, can be imported for your own use. Chinese platforms list a choice of designer pendants, track lights, architectural strips, smart bulbs and large chandeliers is far wider than in Pakistani markets. Our pillar guide on how to import from China to Pakistan covers the general steps.

Be realistic about which lights are worth it. Plain bulbs and tube lights are made and sold everywhere in Pakistan, and importing them one at a time makes no sense. The case for importing is a fitting you cannot find locally.

What LED lights are worth importing one at a time?

Lights that are unusual, large or technical are worth considering. Common examples:

  • Designer pendants, chandeliers and wall lights in a style not stocked locally.
  • Magnetic track lighting systems where the rail, connectors and heads must all match.
  • Addressable RGB strips and controllers for a home theatre, gaming room or shop display at home.
  • High-CRI lamps for artwork, a hobby studio or a clinic.
  • Grow lights with stated spectrum data.
  • Replacement LED panels or drivers for a fitting you already own.

Lights needed in quantity for a building project are a different matter. We only do single items, so for a full project order, a cargo company or the commercial import route fits better.

Which specs should you check on an LED listing?

Check the base, input voltage, colour temperature, colour quality and real power. Most problems with imported lights come from one of these.

Will a Chinese LED light work on Pakistani electricity?

Usually yes, because both countries run at around 230V and 50Hz. The worldstandards.eu table lists Pakistan at 230V, 50Hz with type C and D plugs, and China at 230V, 50Hz with type A and I plugs. The risk is a listing that defaults to a US version with a 110V driver. Ask for the 220V version in writing.

For lamp bases, a PSQCA notification on self-ballasted LED lamps covers B22d, E26 and E27 caps for 100 to 250V, which matches what you see in Pakistani shops. Look at your existing holder before ordering: a bayonet base pushes in and twists, a screw base screws in.

Voltage swings and load-shedding are hard on cheap drivers. A fitting with a decent constant current driver, or one rated for a wide input range, tends to survive longer. A plug-in light with a type A or I plug needs a simple adapter for Pakistani sockets.

What should you know about LED strips?

LED strips run on low voltage, so the strip, its power supply and its controller must all match. Strips are sold in 5V, 12V and 24V versions. Longer runs usually do better on 24V, because low-voltage strips dim and change colour towards the far end. Addressable strips, where each LED can show its own colour, need a controller that supports that strip's chip. The power supply is the part that plugs into the mains, so it must accept 220 to 240V input, and it should be rated comfortably above the strip's total wattage. Buying the strip, controller and supply from the same seller as one set avoids most mismatch problems. Note the length you need before ordering, measured along the actual path the strip will follow, and check where it can be cut, because most strips can only be cut at marked points.

Can solar LED lights with batteries be shipped by air?

Usually, if the battery is fitted inside the light. Air rules treat a battery installed in equipment as the easiest lithium category. A separate battery pack in the same box counts as packed with equipment, and The Compliance Center notes that a 30 percent state of charge limit for lithium-ion batteries packed with equipment became mandatory on 1 January 2026 for batteries above 2.7 Wh. A spare battery sold on its own is the hardest case. Our guide to lithium battery shipping rules explains the categories. For solar floodlights, also be wary of headline wattage. Compare the panel size, battery capacity and LED count instead.

Are there PSQCA or energy-label rules for LED lights?

Yes, for lights made or sold in Pakistan. PSQCA's list of mandatory items notified by the government includes self-ballasted LED lamps for general lighting (PS-5252) and LED modules for general lighting (PS-5253 and PS-5211). Separately, CLASP's policy database records Pakistan's energy label regulations for LED lights, administered by NEECA, as setting mandatory minimum energy performance standards and labels for tubular, non-directional and directional LED lamps, with a revision issued as SRO 415(I)/2024.

These rules matter most to anyone bringing in lights to sell, which is one more reason quantities belong on the commercial route with the right certifications. For one fitting for your own home, the practical steps are to describe the item accurately and keep the seller's specifications.

How are LED lights taxed at customs?

LED lights pay customs duty under their PCT code plus taxes on the customs value. That value is the CIF value, converted at NBP rates published by FBR, and customs can use a valuation ruling instead of your invoice where one exists for the goods. When we checked in November 2026, customs duty slabs ran from 0 to 20 percent with a few goods higher, additional customs duty was 0 percent on the 0 to 15 percent slabs and 2 percent on the 20 percent slab, regulatory duty applied only to listed goods and was capped at 20 percent from 1 July 2026, sales tax was 18 percent on the value plus duties, and advance income tax under section 148 applied on top. Section 19C exempts post and courier parcels worth Rs 1,000 or less. Our guide on how customs valuation rulings work explains why the invoice price is not always the value used.

What are the common mistakes with imported lights?

  • Ordering E27 bulbs for B22 holders, or the reverse.
  • Accepting the US version with a 110V driver.
  • Buying a dimmable fitting without checking it matches your dimmer.
  • Paying more for shipping than the light is worth.
  • Trusting headline watts on solar floodlights.
  • Ordering a strip without a matching power supply and controller.

Frequently asked questions

Do Chinese LED bulbs fit Pakistani holders?

Yes, if you pick the right base. Pakistani holders use both B22 bayonet and E27 screw types, so check your holder before you order.

Will a Chinese LED light work on 230V in Pakistan?

Yes, if its driver is rated for 220 to 240V or 100 to 240V at 50Hz. Avoid US versions with 110V drivers.

Is it worth importing LED bulbs from China?

Not for ordinary bulbs, which are sold everywhere in Pakistan. Importing makes sense for special fittings worth around Rs 10,000 or more.

Can solar lights with batteries be shipped from China?

Usually yes when the battery is built in. Separate battery packs ship at reduced charge, and loose spare batteries are the hardest to send by air.

Do LED lights need PSQCA certification in Pakistan?

Self-ballasted LED lamps and LED modules are on PSQCA's mandatory list, which matters mainly for lights made or sold here. Quantities for sale belong on the commercial route.

Which colour temperature should I choose?

Around 3000K gives warm light for living rooms and bedrooms, and 6500K gives cool daylight. Use one value per room so the light looks even.
