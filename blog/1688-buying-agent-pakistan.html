Can I Order From 1688 From Pakistan? Agents and Options Explained

By Muhammad Shamshad AslamUpdated October 17, 20269 min read
Quick answer

You can browse 1688 from Pakistan, but ordering directly is hard. The site is in Chinese, most sellers ship only inside China, and its official route for overseas payment runs through a partner account rather than a Pakistani card. So almost everyone uses one of three options: your own account plus a freight forwarder, a 1688 buying agent, or a single-item service that buys and delivers one piece for one rupee price. For one item worth around Rs 10,000 or more, the single-item route is usually simplest.

What actually stops a direct order from 1688?

1688.com is Alibaba Group's marketplace for Chinese domestic trade. Payments guides written for overseas buyers are open about the fact that it was not built with them in mind. Four things get in the way.

  • Language. The site is in Chinese. Chrome's translation makes it readable, but not everything translates, and some verification screens stay in Chinese.
  • Account setup. Overseas buyers register as individuals, and the account has to be verified through a linked Alipay account.
  • Payment. The official overseas payment method is 1688's cross-border pay, known in Chinese as 跨境宝, offered with the payments company WorldFirst. It is not available on every supplier, item or order type. We could not confirm whether its accounts are open to residents of Pakistan, so check before relying on it.
  • Shipping. Most 1688 suppliers ship within China only. Even with an account and a payment route, you still need a Chinese address.

1688 also has a cross-border supply section, labelled 跨境专供, which features suppliers selling to cross-border buyers. It is still in Chinese and does not remove the shipping problem.

Option A: your own 1688 account and a freight forwarder

If you can set up an account and a payment route, you order like a Chinese buyer and enter a forwarder's warehouse address in China. The forwarder receives the goods, ships them to Pakistan, and arranges clearance.

This suits regular buyers who are comfortable working in Chinese, talking to suppliers on WeChat, and handling their own disputes. Most 1688 suppliers move conversations to WeChat once an order is under way. It also suits commercial importers who already use a forwarder for other goods. For a one-off single item, the setup is a lot of effort.

Option B: a 1688 buying agent

A 1688 buying agent, often called a sourcing agent, has Chinese accounts, pays suppliers in RMB, receives goods at its warehouse and ships them on. Many also negotiate with suppliers, ask for lower minimums on a first order and combine goods from several suppliers into one shipment.

This is the normal route for resellers and small businesses buying stock. Agents commonly charge a service fee on the order plus freight, and freight is often a separate bill once goods are weighed. Ask whether Pakistan customs is included or collected after clearance. Our guide to China sourcing agents covers how they work on larger orders.

Option C: a single-item service

A single-item service buys one piece from a 1688 listing and delivers it to your door in Pakistan for one price. It checks whether the seller will sell a single unit, confirms the variant, and quotes the item, shipping, customs and its fee as one rupee total. You pay once.

It will not buy 30 units or mix ten suppliers into one box. It suits the person who found one specific product on 1688, often after seeing it on Daraz or AliExpress at a higher price, and wants just that.

Will a 1688 seller sell me just one piece?

Often, for finished goods in stock. Minimum order quantity varies by supplier and product, and many suppliers accept one or two units for standard stock items. Factory listings for raw materials, packaging and made-to-order goods have real minimums that nobody can waive.

Most 1688 listings show a price ladder that falls as quantity rises. A single piece pays the top tier, which is still often below the international sites. Our guide on how to buy from 1688 in Pakistan explains how to read the minimum and the price tiers.

To find the item on 1688 in the first place, three methods work well.

  • Search by image. The 1688 app has a camera search. A screenshot from AliExpress, Daraz or a video often finds the same product. See searching Taobao and 1688 by image.
  • Search in Chinese. English searches return poor results. Translate the product name into Chinese and paste it in.
  • Start from another link. If you have an AliExpress or Alibaba link, the factory may list the same item on 1688. Send that link to whoever is buying for you.

How do I get one item from 1688 through a single-item service?

  1. Copy the 1688 link from the browser or the app's share button.
  2. Note the exact variant: colour, size, model and any bundle.
  3. Send the link through the service's quote form, with the variant.
  4. Get one price in rupees, with the fee stated, before paying anything.
  5. Pay once, by bank transfer, Raast, JazzCash or Easypaisa.
  6. The service buys, the seller ships to China, and the item flies to Pakistan and clears customs.
  7. Receive it at your address. Nothing extra is collected at the door.

When is a 1688 buying agent the better choice?

A single-item service and a buying agent can both get things off 1688, but they are built for different orders. A buying agent is usually the better fit when:

  • You want stock to resell. Ten, fifty or a hundred units of the same thing is agent work. Single-item services do not handle it.
  • You are combining many cheap items. Twenty accessories from six shops only make sense in one consolidated box.
  • You need negotiation. Lower minimums, custom packaging, a logo or a change to the product all need someone talking to the supplier over time.
  • You buy every month. A regular arrangement with an agent can bring better freight rates.

A single-item service is the better fit when you want one specific product, you want to know the full delivered cost before paying, and you would rather not deal with a second shipping bill or customs yourself. Our checklist for choosing a China buying service helps whichever way you go.

What does customs mean for a 1688 item?

Parcels by post or courier worth Rs 1,000 or less are free of duties and taxes under section 19C of the Customs Act, as set by the Finance Act 2025. A 1688 item worth importing alone is almost always above that, so duty and taxes apply. With your own forwarder, you or the forwarder's clearing agent deal with the assessment. With a one-price service, customs is already in the price. Rates depend on the product's tariff code, and our customs duty guide explains how they are worked out.

What are the common mistakes?

  • Believing a guide that says foreigners cannot use 1688 at all. There is an official overseas payment route. The practical problem is shipping and language.
  • Buying a verified account from a stranger. Accounts verified under someone else's identity can break the platform's terms and can be closed with your balance in them.
  • Forgetting the China shipping leg. 1688 prices usually exclude delivery from the seller to the warehouse.
  • Ordering made-to-order goods for one piece. Ask for stock items.
  • Comparing a 1688 price with a delivered Daraz price. Add shipping and customs first.
  • Branded goods at a fraction of retail. They are replicas and Pakistan Customs seizes them.
