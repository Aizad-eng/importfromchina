Importing Bicycle and Cycling Parts From China to Pakistan

By Muhammad Shamshad AslamUpdated November 12, 20269 min read
Quick answer

Yes, you can import bicycle and cycling parts from China to Pakistan as single items, and it works best for drivetrain parts, wheels, carbon components, lights and bike computers worth around Rs 10,000 or more. Match parts by standard, not by inch size: ETRTO tyre size, speed count, freehub type and brake mount. Frames and whole bikes are bulky and cost far more to fly.

New to importing? Read our step-by-step guide on how to import from China to Pakistan.

Which cycling parts do people in Pakistan import from China?

People mostly import parts that are hard to find locally in the right specification: groupset parts, wheels, carbon handlebars and seatposts, lights, bike computers and good tyres. Local bicycle markets in Pakistan stock a lot of basic parts for common bikes, but anything in a specific speed count, a newer standard or a lighter material is often missing. Many of the world's bicycle components are made in China, and many are listed one piece at a time on Taobao, 1688, AliExpress and JD.com.

The parts that make the most sense as single imports share three things: they are small, they are worth a fair amount, and they fit by a published standard. A rear derailleur, a cassette, a set of brake rotors or a carbon seatpost fits all three. A steel mudguard or a basic saddle usually does not, because shipping costs more than the part.

For the wider picture of buying anything from China as a person rather than a company, start with our guide on how to import from China to Pakistan. This page is only about cycling parts.

How do you read tyre and wheel sizes so the part fits?

Use the ETRTO number printed on the tyre sidewall, not the inch size. Schwalbe's own size guide explains that ETRTO gives the width and the inner diameter in millimetres, so 37-622 means a tyre 37 mm wide that fits a rim with a 622 mm bead seat. Schwalbe calls this designation unambiguous, which is exactly what you want when you cannot return a tyre easily.

Inch sizes are the trap. Schwalbe notes that three different diameters, 559, 571 and 590 mm, are all called 26 inch, and that both 622 and 635 mm tyres are called 28 inch. A seller in China listing "26 inch" may mean a different wheel from the one on your bike. French sizes such as 700 x 35C are clearer, because the letter tells you the diameter, but not every size has a French name.

How do you match drivetrain parts by speed and freehub?

Match the speed count first, then the freehub and cassette standard, then the derailleur's capacity for the cassette's largest sprocket. Shimano publishes compatibility charts for exactly this on its product information site. When we checked in November 2026, its index grouped charts for 9, 10 and 11 speed rear drivetrains, FREEHUB and cassette spline compatibility, rear derailleur and cassette gear teeth, and LINKGLIDE cassettes and freehubs.

In practice, before you order a drivetrain part, write down:

  • Speed count. Count the sprockets on your cassette. A 10 speed shifter and an 11 speed derailleur are not a safe mix unless a chart says so.
  • Freehub body. Note what your rear hub takes. Some cassettes need a specific freehub, and Shimano lists LINKGLIDE as its own chart for this reason.
  • Largest sprocket. A wide range cassette needs a derailleur rated for it. The gear teeth charts list the maximum for each derailleur.
  • Chain width. Chains are sold by speed count. Buy the chain for the speed of the cassette.

Look up your own part numbers in the maker's chart and send them with your link. The same approach works for other brands that publish compatibility tables.

What else must match: brakes, bottom brackets, axles and seatposts?

Almost every bike part is defined by a size or standard, and each one must match your frame or existing parts. Check these before you order:

  • Disc brakes. Rotor diameter, mount type (centre lock or six bolt) and the caliper mount on your frame or fork. Shimano's disc brake charts also cover brake fluid type, which matters if you mix parts from different makers.
  • Bottom bracket. The shell standard of your frame and the spindle of your crankset. These differ widely, so read the marking on your current bottom bracket.
  • Axles. Quick release or thru axle, plus the axle width and length.
  • Seatpost. Diameter to a tenth of a millimetre, for example 27.2 or 31.6 mm. It is usually stamped on the old post.
  • Handlebar and stem. Clamp diameter, steerer diameter and, for carbon parts, the torque limit printed on the part.

Is it worth shipping a frame, wheelset or whole bicycle by air?

Usually not for a whole bike, sometimes for a wheelset, and rarely for a frame unless it is a high value carbon frame. Couriers charge on the larger of actual weight and volumetric weight. DHL's own formula is length times width times height in centimetres, divided by 5,000. Big boxes become expensive even when they are light.

Which cycling items cause trouble in shipping or at customs?

Separate lithium batteries, liquids and fake branded parts cause most of the trouble. Lights and bike computers with a built-in battery usually ship without issue. Loose e-bike battery packs are a different matter and rarely fly. Our guide on e-bike and EV parts and batteries explains why.

  • Liquids and sprays. Chain lube, degreaser sprays, tubeless sealant and brake fluid can be refused for air shipping. Ask before you add them to an order.
  • Counterfeits. A famous brand name on a very cheap listing is a warning sign. Counterfeit goods can be seized by customs, and a fake brake or carbon part is also a safety risk. Buy branded parts from the brand's own store on the platform, or buy an honestly unbranded part.
  • CO2 cartridges. Pressurised cartridges for tyre inflators can be refused for air shipping, so buy them locally.

What duty and tax applies to bicycle parts?

Bicycle parts pay normal import duty and taxes once the parcel is worth more than Rs 1,000. Under section 19C of the Customs Act, as amended by the Finance Act 2025, post and courier parcels worth Rs 1,000 or less pay no duties or taxes. Above that, the whole value is assessed.

For FY 2026-27, customs duty is charged by tariff code in slabs of 0, 5, 10, 15 and 20 percent, with a few goods higher. Additional customs duty is 0 on the 0 to 15 percent slabs and 2 percent on the 20 percent slab. Regulatory duty applies only to listed goods and was capped at 20 percent from 1 July 2026. Sales tax of 18 percent is then charged on the value plus those duties, and withholding income tax is added for most parcels. The exact rate depends on the code for your part, so see our breakdown of customs duty from China to Pakistan.

What are the common mistakes with cycling parts from China?

  • Ordering by inch size instead of the ETRTO number.
  • Mixing speed counts between shifter, derailleur, cassette and chain.
  • Forgetting the freehub standard when buying a cassette or a rear wheel.
  • Buying a wide range cassette for a derailleur that cannot reach the largest sprocket.
  • Choosing the wrong seatpost diameter by half a millimetre.
  • Adding lube, sealant or CO2 to an air parcel.
  • Flying a whole bike or a cheap frame when the shipping cost is more than the saving.
  • Trusting a big brand name on a price that is far below the brand's own store.

Frequently asked questions

Can I import a single bicycle part from China to Pakistan?

Yes. Single parts such as derailleurs, cassettes, wheels, rotors and carbon components are practical imports, especially when they are worth around Rs 10,000 or more.

How do I know which tyre size to order?

Read the ETRTO number on your tyre sidewall, such as 37-622. Inch sizes like 26 or 28 inch cover several different rim diameters, so they are not reliable.

Will an 11 speed derailleur work with my 10 speed shifter?

Not unless the maker's compatibility chart says so. Match shifter, derailleur, cassette and chain to the same speed count, and check Shimano's charts for Shimano parts.

Is it worth importing a whole bicycle from China by air?

Usually not. A boxed bicycle has a large volumetric weight, so air shipping often costs more than any saving. A cargo company by sea or a local shop is normally better.

Can bike lights and computers with batteries be shipped?

Lights and computers with a built-in battery usually ship by air without trouble. Separate e-bike battery packs are restricted and rarely fly.

Is there duty on bicycle parts?

Parcels worth more than Rs 1,000 pay customs duty by tariff code plus 18 percent sales tax and income tax. Parcels of Rs 1,000 or less are exempt under section 19C.
