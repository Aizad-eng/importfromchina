How it works

Fill in the form with the product link. Within 24 hours we send you one price in rupees on WhatsApp. It covers the item, shipping, Pakistan customs and our fee. You pay once, before it ships.

Single items only. No bulk.No wholesale, no cartons, no resale stock. One item per request.
Best for items worth Rs 10,000 or more.Shipping from China usually costs Rs 5,000 to 20,000 plus our fee, which can be more than a cheaper item is worth.

What happens after you send the form

  1. We check the item. We look at the listing, the seller and whether it can come into Pakistan.
  2. You get one price within 24 hours, on the WhatsApp number you gave us, in rupees.
  3. You pay once, before it ships, by bank transfer, Raast, JazzCash or Easypaisa. We give you an expected delivery date in writing.
  4. It arrives at your door, usually 7 to 15 days after the seller dispatches, anywhere in Pakistan.
